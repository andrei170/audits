What this means for a new roofer
The bar is lower than it looks
  • Two of five have a Google Ads tag on their site (Slate & Slate, Jensen), a sign they run, or have run, Google ads. The other three show no sign of it in their page code.
  • Only two of five build pages per town or per service. That is how a firm gets found for “roofer Keizer” or “roof leak repair Silverton”.
  • None of the five shows a review count on its own site. Each one asks you to take the rating on trust.
  • Only Slate & Slate’s form asks what kind of job it is. Asking the job type and zip up front is the cheapest way to separate a small repair from a full replacement before you drive out.
⚠ What this page does not contain

Review counts on Google were not read (Google Maps cannot be read by our tools), so no Google review numbers appear here. The Google Ads Transparency Center was not read for the same reason. A tag in a site's code shows an ads account is connected; it does not show how much anyone spends.
