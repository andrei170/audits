What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ You are starting from zero, which means the order matters more than anything else. Ads sent to a business with no profile, no reviews and no way to catch a missed call waste money. So the free and cheap foundations come first.

The goal your team noted, 20 to 30 roofing jobs plus 10 to 20 repairs a month, is two different kinds of work, and the plan treats them that way.

1. Get findable, with one name · Days

A Google Business Profile, verified, under the exact name on your CCB licence, with your CCB number, service towns and phone. The same name and number on Facebook and the main directories.

⭐ This is free, and it is what shows up when a referral searches for you before calling.

2. A website built to bring in estimates · Whoever builds it

A form at the top of every page asking the job type and zip first, tap-to-call on every page, a page per service (repairs, replacement) and per town, and your CCB number visible.

⚠ If someone is already building it, this becomes a checklist to hand them. The sample site in this pack shows each item.

3. Measurement before any ad money · Days

Analytics, a call-tracking number and an ad conversion tag on every form and every call button.

Without this, nobody can tell which ad brought which job.

4. Answer and follow up every enquiry · One to two weeks

A simple CRM: every call and form lands in one place, a missed call gets an automatic text back, and every estimate gets followed up until it is a yes or a no.

⭐ When you are on a roof, this is what stops a lead going to the next roofer on the list.

5. Reviews from day one · Ongoing

An automatic review request after every finished job, starting with the referral jobs you are doing now.

Every competitor we read leans on its reviews. Yours start with the jobs you are already doing.

6. Then paid leads, split by job type · When 1 to 4 are live

Google search ads for urgent repairs (“roof leak repair Salem”), and separate campaigns for replacements, which take longer to decide and need more follow-up.

⚠ Repairs and replacements are different buyers, so they get different ads, landing pages and follow-up.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know what a repair or a roof is worth to you, and a business this new has no history to measure. Anyone giving you a job forecast today would be inventing it.
