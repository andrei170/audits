Tap the arrow to move a candidate on. Each move can send the next message on its own: interview link, trial day details, offer.

How we would run it, in plain English
Step 1
Agree the role with you. What the job is, the hours, the pay [set by you] and your must-haves, such as F-gas and a driving licence.
Step 2
Write the ads and the short form. In your words, with your own photos or TikTok clips. You approve them before anything runs.
Step 3
Run the ads locally. Facebook and Instagram, around Exeter and the towns you choose. You set the budget.
Step 4
Screening runs on its own. Applicants answer the questions, the rules sort them, and only the shortlist reaches you with a booked call.
Step 5
Interview and trial day. You meet the shortlist and run a paid trial day [set by you] on a real install.
Step 6
You decide. Nobody is hired without you, and the ads stop as soon as you have who you need.
⚠ What this page does not say. It does not say how many people will apply, how fast, or what anyone will cost. Those depend on the role, the area and the time of year, and we would not guess them.
