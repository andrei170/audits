Positioning in one line

“You don’t need more work, you need your evenings back. We measure where your admin goes, automate the biggest piece first, in your name and under your control, and only move on once it’s actually saving you time.” Do not forecast hours. Week 1 time log is the anchor.

Ask in this order
1. Walk me through yesterday's admin, start to finish. When did it happen (evenings, weekends)? Let him talk; that is the brief.
2. What software does he use now? Xero / QuickBooks? Spreadsheets? Any job management (ServiceM8, Tradify, simPRO, Jobber)? Who set it up and does he like it?
3. Who answers enquiries? Just him, by phone, between jobs? With hundreds a month, how does he decide who to call back?
4. How are quotes built and sent? Survey needed for most? Template or from scratch? How long per quote?
5. How many quotes are outstanding right now? Does he follow them up?
6. How does he chase payments? Deposits? Commercial clients on 30-day terms? Any late payers now?
7. F-gas / annual service reminders: is there a list of installs and due dates, or does it rely on memory? Lost revenue there?
8. Out-of-area and can't-fit-in enquiries: what does he tell them, how long does that take? (Site says 'entire UK'/'nationwide' while he wants local work. Easy first fix in the form and FAQ.)
9. Team: CH filed average employees = 1 (both years), but reviews say 'Brett's team' / 'the guys'. Subcontractors? He said he does not need staff, so do not pitch hiring.
10. Where does manufacturer warranty registration fit (7-year warranty on site)? Another admin step per install?
Objections
“I’ve tried software before and it didn’t stick.”Ask what it was and why it failed (usually too much setup, too many features, nobody to help). Our angle: one problem at a time, built around how he already works, and we do the setup. Nothing moves to step 2 until step 1 saves him time.
“I don’t have time to set it up.”Exactly why we do it, not him. His input is the week-1 time log and a short check before anything goes live. Start with the enquiry form: it touches nothing else in the business.
“I’m booked anyway, why bother?”Being booked is the problem we solve: the admin is happening in his evenings because the days are full. Fully booked with hundreds of enquiries means most of the admin is saying no or 'not yet' by hand. Automate that first.
“I don’t want robots talking to my customers.”Every message is in his words, from his name, approved by him, and can be switched off. His reviews praise him for clear communication; we protect that, not replace it.
“Send me something to look at.”Send the hub link (assets.html), but book the next call before hanging up.
