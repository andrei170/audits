What we would build, in order

⭐ The order below is a starting point. Week 1 decides the real order: whatever takes most of your time gets built first. One thing at a time, each one checked with you before the next.

Week 1. Measure first · Before anything is built

A simple log of where your admin time actually goes for one week, plus a look at the software you already use.

⭐ So we start with your biggest time sink, not ours.

1. One enquiry form that does the sorting · First build

One form across the site: postcode, job type, property type, number of rooms or units, photos, and when they want it. Out-of-area postcodes and people you cannot fit in yet get an honest automatic reply and a place on a waiting list.

So most enquiries arrive ready to price, without a call back.

2. Instant replies and missed-call text back · First build

Every form and missed call gets a reply from your name within seconds, with your FAQ answers and a clear note on timing.

So customers stop chasing and you answer fewer repeat questions.

3. Survey booking · Next

A booking link showing only the survey slots you open, with automatic reminders.

No more back-and-forth to find a time.

4. Quote templates and follow-ups · Next

Templates for your common jobs and a polite follow-up sequence that stops when they reply.

You check and send, rather than write and remember.

5. A simple job pipeline · Then

One board from enquiry to quote to booked to done to paid, on your phone.

You can see at a glance what is waiting on you.

6. Job sheets, invoices and payment chasers · Then

On-site job records with F-gas details, invoices raised when a job is done and reminders if unpaid, connected to your accounts software if you use one.

Paperwork done once, on site.

7. Servicing reminders and review requests · Then

Yearly service and leak-check reminders from each install date, and a review request after every job.

Repeat work and reviews without you remembering.

How we would work with you
  • You keep control. Nothing is sent to a customer that you have not approved, and every automation can be switched off.
  • You own the data. Customer lists, quotes and job records sit in accounts in your name and stay yours.
  • Biggest time sink first, and nothing else gets built until that one is working for you.
  • We fit around the software you already use rather than making you start again.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about hours saved. We do not yet know your week, your software or which job takes the most time. Anyone giving you a forecast before knowing those would be inventing it.
