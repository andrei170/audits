⚠ One small thing on paperwork

Your terms and privacy pages call the business “BSP Air Conditioning Limited”. The company on the register is Building Services Projects (UK) Limited. Worth making the two match on quotes, invoices and terms, so customers know exactly who they are contracting with.

How an enquiry reaches you today

From what the site shows, a new customer can do one of four things: ring your mobile (on every page), email (address in the footer), or fill in one of three forms. There is no text, WhatsApp, chat or online booking.

⚠ What has to happen next, every time

None of the three forms collects enough to price a job or to know if it is in your area. So the next step for almost every enquiry is you, calling or messaging them back to ask where it is, what it is, how many rooms, and when they want it. Then, often, a survey visit arranged by phone. Then the quote. That chain is the admin, and most of it is the same questions every time.

What the site tells customers before they contact you
⭐ Good: an FAQ that already answers the basics

Install time (1 to 2 days), listed buildings, servicing once a year, brands, warranty and free quotes. That is a real head start: those answers can be reused in an automatic reply.

⚠ Missing: the answers that cause the follow-up calls
  • Nothing on reply times or how far ahead you are booking, while the site promises a “fast response”.
  • Mixed messages on area: Exeter, Devon and the South West on the home page, “the entire UK” in the FAQ and “nationwide” on the about page.
  • Nothing on what happens after an enquiry: a call, a survey, a written quote, how long each takes.
  • The FAQ block appears twice on the same page.
What we could not check
⚠ Left out rather than guessed
  • What happens after someone presses send: we did not submit a test enquiry, so we do not know if customers get an automatic reply or where the form goes.
  • Your back office: accounts, quoting, diary and job software are not visible from outside.
  • Your Google Business Profile and total Google reviews: our tools cannot read them.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok: linked from your site, not read.
Why this matters for your admin

You told us most enquiries come from TikTok and social media, sit in WhatsApp and email, and many go unanswered. You pick jobs by distance first, then wall-mounted over loft or ducted, and do the admin yourself after work (your words, not measured).

Each gap above adds to that: no postcode means a call back to find where the job is, no system type means a call back to see if you want it, and UK-wide wording brings in enquiries you will only decline. The CRM demo shows this sorted before you look at it.
