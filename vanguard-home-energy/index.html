What we found
🔴🔴 The headline, and you can check it yourself

Every page of vanguardhomeenergyllc.com contains a line called a canonical tag. It tells Google which address is the real, official version of that page.

🔴 On your site, every one of those tags points to aguardlight123.vercel.app, which returns a "404 not found" error. So does your sitemap, the file that lists your pages for Google. We counted 52 references.

⚠ It looks like the address your developer used while building the site, left in when it went live. Google may ignore a broken instruction like this, or it may follow it. Either way it is the opposite of what you want, and it is usually a few minutes for whoever built the site to fix.

⭐ To see it yourself: open your homepage, right-click, choose View page source, and search for vercel.

🔴 Nothing is counting

No analytics and no advertising tag of any kind. Nothing records how many people visit, which page they read, or which ones asked for a quote.

⚠ You told our team you want leads. Before spending a dollar on getting them, you need to be able to see which ones arrive and where from.

⚠ Two things a Phoenix homeowner notices
  • Your phone number is (435), a Utah area code, on a Phoenix business. Worth asking whether that costs you calls from people who assume you are out of state
  • The email shown is a Gmail address, on a site with its own domain
⭐ What is already strong
  • AZ ROC licensed and insured, stated across the site
  • 17 pages, with a page for each service: electrical installation, repair, troubleshooting and wiring; EV charger installation, repair and removal; solar installation, repair and maintenance
  • A quote form on the homepage, services and contact pages
  • Business markup that tells Google your address and the cities you serve
  • A gallery and Google reviews on the site

⭐ The site itself is good. The plumbing behind it is broken.

⚠ Not assessed: site speed, your Google review count, and whether Google currently shows your pages. We have not guessed.
