Behind the pages
⚠ Code and set-up
  • Local business markup: none. Checked on all 20 pages.
  • Tracking: none. No Google Analytics 4 ID, no Google Tag Manager container, no Google Ads tag (AW-), no Meta pixel.
  • Headings: 3 main headings on every page, 2 of them "Free Quote"; 48 on the Services page.
  • Descriptions: 5 pages (Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Contact) share one description, which starts "Welcome to Eco Systems Mechanical Inc, your premier provider…".
  • Images: 50 images on the home page; 15 carry the alt text "logo Eco Systems Mechanical Inc" and 4 carry none, so Google cannot tell a rooftop job photo from a logo.
  • Maps: the maps embedded on the site are set to Spanish (the map code says es-419), so labels such as "Valle Central de California" can show to English-speaking visitors.
  • Quote form: a pop-up loaded by a script from your website provider. It sends to a Gmail address through an outside server. We did not submit it, so we cannot say whether quotes arrive.
  • Size: the home page code is about 495 KB and the logo is a 718 KB animated GIF. We did not run a speed test.

⚠ Why the phone number matters

Google checks that your name, address and phone match across the web. When they differ, it is less sure the listing is you. More practically, a customer who finds you on Google rings a different number from one who finds your website. If both numbers are yours and both ring you, that is fine to keep, but it is worth knowing which one people use.
