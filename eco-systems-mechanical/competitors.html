What this means for you
1Every one of them has pages for the towns you serve. You have none.

The Little Guy’s has a page for each of 14 towns, including Ripon, Salida, Escalon, Lathrop, Stockton and Modesto. Dycus has addresses such as /ac-repair-ripon-ca/ and /ac-replacement-salida-ca/ (132 addresses naming a town). Star has 159, including Escalon and Lathrop pages for each service.

Source: Each firm’s sitemap.xml, read November 10, 2026. "Town pages" counts addresses that name a local town, so it includes service-plus-town pages.

2They have 130 to 260 times your Google reviews.

Souza’s in Ripon has 261 reviews at 5.0. Star in Manteca has 520 at 4.9. You have 2 at 5.0. Your rating is as good as theirs; the gap is the count, and only customers can close it.

Source: Google Maps searches for "hvac Manteca CA", "air conditioning contractor near Ripon, CA" and "heating and air conditioning Modesto", November 10, 2026.

3Four of the five let a customer book online.

Souza’s, Star, The Little Guy’s and Essentra load a booking system (Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or LeadConnector) on the home page. Your quote request goes to a Gmail inbox.

Source: Home page code of each site, read November 10, 2026. Dycus showed no booking script in its home page code.

4Two of them are set up to measure Google Ads; you are not set up to measure anything.

Souza’s and Dycus both carry a Google Ads tag inside Google Tag Manager, which means they can see which ads bring calls and forms. Star runs Google Analytics 4. We found no tracking of any kind on your site.

Source: Google Tag Manager containers and home page code, read November 10, 2026. A tag shows a firm is set up to advertise, not how much it spends.
