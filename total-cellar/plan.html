The order we would do it in
1Tidy what is live, this week

Take down the placeholder team section and the Sample Page, write a real page title and description for each page, fix the footer postcode and the contact map, add a privacy notice. Small jobs.

2Get on Google Maps

Set up and verify a Google Business Profile for Total Cellar Services, with your categories, areas served, photos and phone number, so a manager searching the map can find you.

3Start counting

Install Google Analytics 4 and call and form tracking, so every later step can be judged on numbers.

4Ask for reviews

A short message with your Google review link to the venues you look after. Only customers can add reviews.

5Pages that bring in bookings

A booking form at the top of every page asking the job and the venue postcode first, a page for each service including event bars, a page for each area, and real proof once you have it. The demo shows what that looks like.

6Then, and only with tracking in place, Google Ads

Searches like "beer line cleaning Surrey" and "beer not pouring", measured so you decide with numbers whether to keep going.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of calls, contracts or venues. What we can do is show you each enquiry, where it came from and what it led to, so you decide with numbers. Your current web designer can build any of this; the demo is a blueprint, not a demand to switch.
