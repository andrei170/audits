The order we would do it in

Steps 1 to 7 are getting you out of WhatsApp; step 8 is getting found. You can try the CRM demo to see steps 2 to 7 working with sample data.

1Map how your comms run

One session with you and your office manager: how jobs arrive, the questions you ask on a callout, your engineers’ areas and hours, job types and how you charge, and where your venue list lives.

2One inbox and one phone line

WhatsApp Business, calls, Gmail and your website form into one shared inbox tied to your venues, with missed calls answered by text. Your WhatsApp is checked with you before anything moves.

3AI triage and booking

The AI answers and triages callouts and books the nearest free engineer, under rules you set; you and the office approve.

4Engineer dispatch

Jobs on your 3 engineers’ phones with venue notes and photos, sign-off and job sheets from photos and a voice note.

5Line-clean schedules

A cycle per venue, WhatsApp reminders the venue can answer, and an overdue list.

6Invoicing and chasing

Invoices on sign-off, reminders until paid, connected to your accounting software if you use one.

7Brewery and installation pipeline

A separate board from enquiry and survey to handover and payment.

8Getting found

Tidy the live website (placeholder team, Sample Page, titles, privacy notice), a Google Business Profile and tracking, then pages and a booking form that feed the same inbox. The demo website shows what that looks like.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise hours saved or a set amount of admin cut. What the build does is put every message, job, engineer and invoice in one place and take the typing off you, so you can see the difference for yourself. Your current web designer can build the website part; the demo is a blueprint, not a demand to switch.
