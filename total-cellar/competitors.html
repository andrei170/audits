What this means for you
1The biggest name in your patch sells against the weekly in-house clean.

Clear Brew’s Kingston & Wimbledon page names its local owner, Dan Cassen, lists 19 areas from Kingston to Cobham and Leatherhead, and offers a free clean. Its pitch is moving venues "away from weekly in-house line cleans". Your Line Cleaning page says lines should be cleaned every seven days, and stops there.

Source: clearbrew.co.uk/branches/kingston/ and its sitemap, read 12 November 2026. Clear Brew also has branch pages for Richmond & Twickenham and Basingstoke & Guildford.

2Morepour covers your counties and your event work, and it is set up to measure ads.

Morepour, near Horley, has 4.8 stars from 28 reviews, a "Book a Technician" page, mobile bar hire, and articles about dispense work in Sussex, London and Hampshire. Its site carries Google Analytics 4 and a Google Ads tag, so it can see which ads bring enquiries. Yours carries no tracking of any kind.

Source: morepour.com home page code and sitemap, and Google Maps, 12 November 2026. A tag shows a firm is set up to advertise, not how much it spends.

3You are the only one of the five we could not find on Google Maps.

Morepour (28 reviews), Cellar Buoys (6) and BevCool (7) all show stars on Google Maps. A pub manager searching the map for help with a dispense problem sees them, not you.

Source: Google Maps searches from Staines, Reading, Guildford, Windsor and London, 12 November 2026.

4Every competitor names a person or a booking route. Your About page shows placeholder text.

Clear Brew names its branch owners; Morepour has a booking page; Cellar Buoys leads with a cleaning schedule. Your site names no one, and the team section is template filler.

Source: Each firm’s site, read 12 November 2026.

Where you are ahead
  • You do the whole job. Maintenance, breakdowns, new installations and line cleaning from one firm, plus event bars. Clear Brew and Cellar Buoys lead with cleaning; BevCool leads with products.
  • Real photos of installs. Fonts, taps and cellar panels you can point to.
  • A Staines base between west London, Surrey and Berkshire, the patch these competitors split between them.
