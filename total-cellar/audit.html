The About page, as a visitor sees it today
⚠ The one to fix first

Under "Our Team", the page reads, for each of four people: "Project Manager. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur adipiscing elit sed do eiusmod tempor…". Lorem ipsum is the filler text a designer puts in before the real words arrive. We cannot tell from outside whether these four names are real people; if they are not your team, a licensee checking you out before letting you into their cellar may wonder what else on the site is not real. It is a five-minute fix.

Behind the pages
⚠ Code and set-up
  • Platform: WordPress with the Avada theme and a child theme. Plugins seen in the code: Contact Form 7, a cookie notice plugin (Cookie Law Info), Fusion Builder, Slider Revolution and Slide Anything. Footer credit: Telsa Media Ltd.
  • Local business markup: none. Checked on all 10 pages; only a breadcrumb trail is marked up.
  • Tracking: none. No Google Analytics 4 ID, no Universal Analytics ID, no Google Tag Manager container, no Google Ads tag (AW-) and no Meta pixel on any page.
  • Headings: 2 main headings on 9 pages and 3 on the Contact page; "Our Clients" is a main heading on every page.
  • Descriptions: none written. The home page’s is cut from body text and ends "Request a".
  • Images: 14 on the home page, 13 with no alt text, including the four client logos.
  • Age: the home page code carries an "updated" date of 18 April 2019, and every image we saw was uploaded in March or April 2019. There is no news or blog section (/blog/ returns not found).
  • robots.txt: sets "Crawl-delay: 30", which asks search engines that follow it (Bing does; Google ignores the setting) to wait 30 seconds between pages.
  • Privacy: the forms collect name, email and phone; we found no privacy notice linked from the pages we read, and /privacy-policy/ returns not found.
  • Security and addresses: the site loads over HTTPS, and both http:// and www. versions redirect to it correctly.
