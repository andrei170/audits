The turnover question
  • The numbers do not line up. 173 jobs x about GBP 220 = about GBP 38k a month = about GBP 0.45M a year, not 2.6M.
  • Installs could close the gap: brewery fit-outs are far bigger tickets than a callout or clean, and may not be in the 173 or the 220 average.
  • Companies House (FY to 30 Apr 2025): average employees 3 including directors, cash about 5k, net liabilities 1,507, debtors 65,164, creditors under a year 85,197. No turnover filed. That is a much smaller picture than 2.6M, though it is 18 months old and he now says a team of 5.
  • Treat the 2.6M as unverified. Do not repeat it back to him. Ask: what share of revenue is installs versus callouts and cleans, and roughly how many installs a year?
  • Sizing: the setter-reported scale suggests he can afford a proper build, and the pain is real and daily. Keep the CH caution: propose a first phase (inbox + phone line + AI triage/booking + dispatch) that pays for itself in office time, then add cleans, invoicing and installs.
Discovery questions, in this order
  1. "What are you looking for in streamlining your comms?" Open with this, then stop talking. Let him define it.
  2. Which "other system" does he use besides WhatsApp (Jordon’s words)? What does it do, who uses it?
  3. Who answers out of hours, including the WhatsApps until 9pm? Him, the office lady, nobody?
  4. How do jobs reach the engineers now? Forwarded WhatsApp, a call, a morning list? How do they report back?
  5. How do invoices go out, who raises them, and who chases? (Debtors 65k vs 5k cash at Apr 2025: chasing may hurt. Ask, do not quote.)
  6. What does a missed callout cost? A lost venue, a competitor called instead? Let him put the number on it.
  7. How many venues are on regular line-clean contracts, and how is "who is due" tracked?
  8. How do the brewery install projects run end to end, and what share of revenue are installs versus callouts and cleans?
  9. The office lady’s day: what does she do hour by hour, and what would she hand to the system first?
  10. "Cut it in half": what would that free him to do? More installs, more engineers, time off, sales?
Objection notes
