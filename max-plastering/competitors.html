What this means for you
You already win on content. The gap is elsewhere.
  • Your site has more town pages, guides and case studies than any of these. That is why the SEO enquiries come in.
  • Sussex Damp Experts measures and advertises (Tag Manager plus Google search ads); GRF measures (Google Analytics and a Meta pixel). You do neither yet, so they can see which search brings a job and you cannot.
  • Your 5.0 from 90 is the best rating in the Hove and Worthing map results, ahead of Brighton Damp Proofers’ 4.8 from 95. A steady review request after every job keeps that lead moving.
  • The only form that asks what the problem is sits on thedampspecialists.co.uk. Knowing the job before you call back lets you sort a survey from a skim before you drive out.
  • Other firms are paying to sit above you: Google’s paid local listings (Nordic Damp Proofing, M. Ross Building Services), Surrey Hills Damp text ads, and paid map listings (BWT Southern, MPC Plastering), seen on the searches on the SEO page.
