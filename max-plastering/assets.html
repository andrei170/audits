Questions for the call
1. Which name do you want customers to know you by?Your site and Google profile say Max Damp Proofing. Facebook still says Max Plastering & Drywall, Instagram says Max Plastering & Damp Proofing, our booking note said Max Plastering & Decorating, and maxplasteringdrywallco.com shows nothing yet. One name used everywhere keeps reviews and searches pointing at one business.
2. Is The Damp Specialists yours, and who answers 01903 444 614?Its website footer carries Sussex Preservation LTD and your Garland Point address, and its Google listing sat one place above you in the Worthing map results.
3. When a form arrives while you are on a job, who replies, and how quickly?A review on your site mentions Tina in the office. Does every form and WhatsApp message land with one person, or in several places?
4. What happens to a call to 01273 044 013 that nobody picks up?Voicemail, a call back later, or a text straight away?
5. After you send a quote, what happens if the customer goes quiet?Is there a follow-up, and who does it?
6. Out of the SEO enquiries, roughly what share become a survey, and what share become a job?No exact numbers needed. This tells us whether the gap is getting enquiries or converting them.
7. Which jobs do you want more of?Damp and timber, plastering and rendering, or both? Your team said small to medium jobs within about 15 to 20 miles. Does decorating still feature?
8. Can you tell which search, page or source brought each job?We found no analytics or ad tag in your page code. Is anything tracking enquiries another way?
9. Have you run Google or Facebook ads before?We found none in either ad library. If you tried them, what happened?
10. Who looks after the website and the SEO today?The site is clearly well built. Anything we suggest on the site goes through them, not around them.
