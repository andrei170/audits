The site itself is strong: it is the part that brings your SEO enquiries. The low grades sit around it: what happens after someone finds you, and what happens if rankings move.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (22 Oct 2026)
APages for every town and service
⭐ 211 addresses in the site map (118 pages, 30 guides, 13 case studies, 50 image entries), including a damp proofing page for each of 70+ Sussex towns and a page for each service. This is the work that brings your SEO enquiries.
AProof of work
⭐ Case studies with street names and photos (Church Road, Portslade; Wordsworth Street, Hove; Sugden Road, Worthing and more), linked from the homepage.
AGoogle reviews
⭐ 5.0 from 90 Google reviews, shown in Google’s own map results and in the reviews panel on every page of your site. The highest rating, or joint highest, in every set of map results we saw you in.
A-Local business markup
⭐ LocalBusiness markup with your address, phone, opening hours and company number on every page read.
A-Who you are
⭐ A “Meet Max” section on the homepage and a full About page.
AProfessional email
⭐ info@maxdampproofing.co.uk, matching the website domain.
BGoogle visibility (tested searches)
Your website was on page one of Google for 5 of the 7 searches where we could read the whole page, and 2nd for both “damp proofing Shoreham-by-Sea” and “plasterer Shoreham-by-Sea”. In the map results you sat 3rd of 3 for Hove and Worthing. Paid listings sat above you on five searches.
CTap-to-call on a phone
⚠ 6 call links on the homepage and a phone button in the desktop header. But on a phone-sized screen the first screen has no call button: just a menu icon and a WhatsApp bubble.
DMain enquiry form
⚠ A quick form sits under the top section of every page. It makes email compulsory and the phone number optional, and does not ask what the job is. So the fastest way to reply, a call or a text, is the one thing you may not get.
DAccreditation shown
⚠ No trade body or vetting scheme shown on the homepage (no PCA, TrustMark, Checkatrade or similar). It names Biokill, Triton and BS 8102 as materials and standards. Three of the four competitors we read show one (Checkatrade or CSRT).
FOne name everywhere
🔴 Old names still on Google’s page one: Checkatrade “Max Plastering & Drywall Co”, Yell “Max Plastering & Damp Proofing”, facebook.com/maxplasteringdrywallco, and a Trustpilot profile on maxplasteringdampproofing.co.uk (that address no longer loads). The Damp Specialists has its own Google listing and site with a different number. maxplasteringdrywallco.com is registered but shows nothing.
FMeasurement
🔴 No analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads or Meta tag in the page code we read. ⚠ One could be added another way; worth asking. Without it, nobody can say which page or search brings the jobs.
FSecond lead channel (paid)
🔴 No ads found for Max Damp Proofing on Meta or Google, at any time and in any region we checked. Meanwhile paid listings from other firms sat above you on five of the Google searches we read. Today every new enquiry depends on rankings holding.

⭐⭐ Every low grade is about the system around the site, not about your work or the site’s content. Fix measurement, the mobile call route, the quick form, one name everywhere and a second channel, and the same checks would grade about A-, 83 out of 100. That is a re-grade of the same checks, not a forecast of enquiries.

⚠ Not graded: reply speed (not tested), site speed (Google’s test was unavailable to us), Google review volume read from Google itself, and social activity. They are left out of the score rather than guessed at.
