⚠ Google now puts an AI answer above the results

An AI Overview appeared at the top of Google for “damp proofing Shoreham-by-Sea”, “damp proofing Brighton”, “rising damp treatment Hove” and “damp proofing Lancing”. For Brighton it builds a table of local damp firms and cites Sussex Damp Experts. ⚠ We could not expand the full AI text, so we cannot say whether you appear further down that table.

⚠ You were not named in any AI answer we could read in full

We also asked an AI search tool three questions on 22 October 2026 and read its written answers:

  • “Best damp proofing company Brighton and Hove”: it named Brighton Damp Proofers (citing 449 Checkatrade reviews) and Sussex Damp Experts (citing Trustpilot). Not you.
  • “Who should I call for damp and replastering near Shoreham-by-Sea”: it named Portslade Plastering and pointed to MyBuilder. Not you, even though you are based in Shoreham and do both.
  • “Max Damp Proofing Shoreham”: it found only the old Trustpilot profile on maxplasteringdampproofing.co.uk, with one review, and could not confirm a Shoreham base.

⚠ Limits: this was one AI search tool, not ChatGPT, which we could not query. Answers change from day to day. Treat it as a spot check, not a ranking.

Why the AI tools miss you, and what we would do
  • AI answers lean on review platforms and directories (Checkatrade, Trustpilot, MyBuilder). Your 90 reviews sit on Google; the only other profile the tool found is under an old name on a dead address. Fix: update or close the old profiles so they point at Max Damp Proofing and maxdampproofing.co.uk.
  • Your pages answer the questions people ask, but none of the pages we read marks them up as FAQs or services (only the business details are marked up). Fix: add FAQ and service markup to the pages that already carry the answers. No rewrite needed.
  • One name everywhere. When the same business appears as four names, answer tools treat them as different firms, or skip them.
  • A steady flow of new reviews that mention the job and the town (“rising damp, Hove”), which is exactly what answer tools quote.
