Where enquiries can leak
After someone finds you
  • On a phone, the first screen has no call button. A visitor sees a menu icon and a WhatsApp bubble. The number is in the menu and further down the page.
  • The quick form on every page makes the phone number optional and email compulsory, and does not ask what the job is. The longer form on the Contact page does ask for a phone number.
  • Enquiries arrive three ways (form, phone, WhatsApp). The Contact page promises a reply within 24 hours. We did not test how fast replies actually come: we did not call or submit a form.
  • No analytics or ad tag in the page code, so there is no record of which page or search produced which enquiry.
⚠ Four names in use online
  • Site and Google profile: Max Damp Proofing.
  • Facebook: facebook.com/maxplasteringdrywallco. Instagram: maxplasteringdampproofing.
  • On Google’s page one for “plasterer Shoreham-by-Sea”: a Checkatrade profile for “Max Plastering & Drywall Co” and a Yell listing for “Max Plastering & Damp Proofing” (5.0 from 4).
  • An AI search tool asked about “Max Damp Proofing” came back with an old Trustpilot profile for maxplasteringdampproofing.co.uk holding one review. That address no longer loads.
  • Our booking note recorded Max Plastering & Decorating.

Old names are normal after a rename. Pointing every one at the current name keeps reviews, searches and AI answers on one business.

⚠ A second site carrying your company details

thedampspecialists.co.uk names Sussex Preservation LTD and Garland Point, Shoreham-by-Sea, BN43 5PF in its footer, alongside the company’s previous registered office in Western Road, Brighton. It uses a different number, 01903 444 614. It also has its own Google listing, “The Damp Specialists (Worthing Brighton Hove Eastbourne...)”, 4.7 from 14, which sat 2nd in the map results for “damp proofing Worthing”, one place above you.

It also makes claims that do not appear on maxdampproofing.co.uk and that we could not check, such as “since 2005” and a count of homeowners served. If it is yours, it is worth making sure every claim matches what you can show, and that its calls reach the same place. If it is not, someone is using your company details.
