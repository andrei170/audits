What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ You do not have an enquiry problem. You have a lot of them. So the order is: catch and convert more of what already arrives, then add a second source so the business does not rest on rankings alone.

1. Measure what you already get · Week 1

Analytics on the site, a tracked number, and a record of every form, call and WhatsApp tap.

⭐ The first time you will see which pages and towns actually bring the jobs.

2. Reply to every enquiry within minutes · Week 1 to 2

Every form and WhatsApp message lands in one inbox on your phone. The customer gets a text straight away: who you are, that you have their message, and when you will call.

Speed to reply is the cheapest win there is when enquiries already arrive.

3. Missed-call text back · Week 1 to 2

A call to 01273 044 013 that nobody answers sends an automatic text: “Sorry we missed you, we are on a job. What is the problem and where is the property?”

⭐ When you are on a ladder or mid-skim, this stops the caller ringing the next name on Google.

4. Quote follow-up · Week 2

After a survey and a written quote, a short sequence of texts and emails until it is a yes or a no, with your case studies attached.

Quotes that go quiet are usually forgotten, not refused.

5. Review requests after every job · Week 2

An automatic review request when a job is signed off, asking the customer to mention the job and the town.

Keeps the 90 reviews growing, and feeds the answer tools.

6. Small fixes on the current site · With whoever runs it

A call button that stays on a phone screen, phone number compulsory on the quick form, a job-type question, FAQ markup, and one name on every profile.

⚠ A checklist for your web person, not a rebuild. The sample website shows each item.

7. Then a second channel · When 1 to 5 are live

Google search ads on damp terms in your radius, measured from day one. Facebook and Instagram later, to people who have already visited.

⚠ Only once every enquiry is tracked and answered, so the spend can be judged.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your job values or how many enquiries become jobs today. Anyone giving you a forecast before measuring would be inventing it.
