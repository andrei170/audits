What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ You do not need more enquiries of every size. You need more of the big ones, booked far enough ahead to keep the team busy. So the order is: measure, sort, get found, follow up, and only then pay for ads.

1. Track first · Days

Analytics, call tracking on the 0808 number and a tag on the form, so every enquiry has a source.

⭐ This also tells you, for the first time, what your current SEO is producing.

2. A form that sorts big projects from small · With the site

Project type, rough budget in bands, start date, property type and whether drawings exist, on every page, with a text back to the homeowner straight away.

Your time goes on surveys for the projects you want.

3. Pages to be found on · Two to four weeks

A page for each of your seven towns, and for lofts by type, extensions, garage conversions and loft-plus-extension. Your Checkatrade score and real review quotes on every page, and your business details in the code.

This is what search engines and AI assistants read to decide who does what, where.

4. Follow-up that does not depend on you being off site · One to two weeks

Every call and form in one place, missed calls texted back, and every quote followed up until it is a yes or a no.

Large projects take weeks to decide; most are won or lost in the follow-up.

5. Then ads, aimed at big projects · When 1 to 4 are live

Google search ads for people already searching for lofts and extensions in your towns, and Meta ads to homeowners in those towns. Both land on the form that asks the budget.

⚠ Ads sent to a site with no tracking and no budget question tend to buy small enquiries. That is why they come last.

About your current SEO

We are not suggesting you drop anyone today. Ask your provider three things: your Google positions for “loft conversion Sutton” and “loft conversion Cheam”, what has changed on the site since March, and how they measure enquiries without tracking on the site. The answers make the decision for you.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. There is no tracking on the site today, so there is no baseline to forecast from. Anyone giving you an enquiry forecast today would be inventing it.
