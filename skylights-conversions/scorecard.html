Strong reputation, a thin website. The things a homeowner trusts are in place; the things that bring in and sort enquiries are mostly missing.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (20 October 2026)
AReviews on Checkatrade
⭐ 9.67 out of 10 from 90 reviews, approved member since December 2009. Recent reviews (May, July and August 2026) praise the finish and the communication, and one customer has used you three times. This is the strongest thing about the business online.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ The free 0808 number is a tap-to-call link in the header of every page. ⚠ There is no call button that stays on screen as a visitor scrolls on a phone.
BLayout on a phone
Checked in a 500-pixel-wide browser: text fits, the menu collapses properly, the “Free Quote” button is near the top. It only links to the contact page, though; there is nowhere to start an enquiry on the page itself.
BPage response and images
Pages came back in 0.3 to 1.1 seconds; photos are served as modern WebP files in several sizes. ⚠ Google’s full speed test (Lighthouse) refused our requests today, so this is a partial check, not a speed score.
DSEO basics on the page
Yoast SEO is installed: every page has its own title, description and canonical tag, and there is a sitemap. But the homepage headline (“Maximise Space. Elevate Living.”) names no service and no town, the Gallery and Contact pages have no main heading at all, and none of the 21 photos across the six pages has alt text, so Google cannot tell what they show.
FPages for each town and service
🔴 The whole site is six pages. One town page (Epsom). No page for Sutton, Carshalton, Ewell, Morden or Stoneleigh, even though all five are on your own area list. No page for extensions or garage conversions, although your Checkatrade profile lists both and your reviews mention them.
DWhere you show up for money searches
“loft conversion Cheam”: 4th on both Bing and DuckDuckGo. “loft conversion Sutton”: not in the first nine on either; CS Lofts, Sutton Lofts and Nuloft are. ⚠ Google itself could not be read, so treat this as a direction, not a Google position.
FNamed in AI answers
🔴 Two AI-style questions (“best loft conversion companies in Sutton and Cheam”, “who should I use for a dormer loft conversion in Epsom or Ewell”) did not name Sky Build. They named Croft Loft Conversions, MS Loft Conversion, Top Division Lofts and Checkatrade-listed builders instead.
DBusiness details for Google (schema)
The page code tells Google it is a web page and carries an FAQ block, which is good. It never says it is a building business: no business type, address, phone number, opening hours or link to your Checkatrade reviews in the code.
FYour reviews, on your own site
🔴 The only mention is a small “Member of Checkatrade.com” link at the top. No score, no review count, no quotes. A homeowner deciding on a large project never sees the 9.67.
FEnquiry form
🔴 The only form is on the Contact page. The homepage, both loft pages and the Epsom page send people there with a link. It asks name, phone, email, area and a message, then “Submit”.
FSorting big projects from small ones
🔴 Nothing on the site asks what the project is, roughly what the budget is, or when they want to start. So every enquiry, from a loft to a small repair, arrives looking the same and has to be found out on the phone or at a survey. One competitor asks budget in bands on its first form.
FTracking
🔴 No Google Analytics, no Tag Manager, no Google Ads or Meta tag in the code of any of the six pages. Nobody, including your SEO provider, can see from the site how many people visited, called or sent the form.
FOne consistent story
⚠ The site gives four different ages for the business: “trading since 2017” (homepage), “trading since 2003” (loft conversions page), “more than two decades” (Epsom and Gallery) and “decades of experience”. Checkatrade says member since 2009. A careful buyer notices.

⭐⭐ The pattern is clear: reputation A, website D or F. The hard part, years of good work and 90 reviews, is done. What is missing is cheap by comparison: pages to be found on, a form that sorts enquiries, and tracking.

⚠ What this grade is not: Google rankings, Google reviews, your Google Business Profile and a full Lighthouse speed score could not be read today, so they are left out of the score rather than guessed at.
