Questions for the call
1. Your current projects: where did each one come from?Checkatrade, a referral, a repeat customer, or the website. That tells us which channel to build on first.
2. When do the current builds finish, and when do you want the next ones starting?Your own FAQ says design and approval takes 8 to 12 weeks. So a survey booked now is a start date in the new year.
3. Which projects do you want more of?Lofts only, or extensions, garage conversions and loft-plus-extension too? Your Checkatrade lists all three; your website only shows lofts.
4. Who does your SEO, what do they send you each month, and which searches did they say they would get you onto?With no tracking on the site, any report cannot include the enquiries the site produced.
5. Roughly how many enquiries a month come in, and how many are too small to be worth a survey?This decides how hard the form should filter.
6. What would you ask a homeowner before you drive out to survey?Those questions become the form, so the answers arrive before you call.
7. How far do you want to travel for the right project?The site says a 5-mile radius of Cheam; recent reviews are from CR0, SM4 and KT22.
8. What has put you off ads so far?A bad past experience, cost, or worry about time-wasters. Each has a different answer.
9. Which company name do you want customers to see?Skybuild (Surrey) Ltd is on the site and Checkatrade; Skylights Conversions Ltd is on construction.co.uk with one 1-star review.
10. When you are on site and the 0808 number rings, who answers?A missed call on a large project usually rings the next firm.
