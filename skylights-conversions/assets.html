Questions for the call
1. Which projects do you want more of?Lofts only, or extensions and loft plus extension too? Your Checkatrade lists all three; the website shows lofts.
2. What is the smallest project worth a survey?That decides how hard the form filters.
3. When do the current builds finish, and when should the next ones start?Design and approval take 8 to 12 weeks by your own FAQ.
4. Where did your current projects come from?Checkatrade, referral, repeat customer or the website. That tells us what to build on.
5. Who does your SEO, and what do they send you each month?No page has changed since March and there is no tracking on the site.
6. What does your written cover include, and for how long?Two competitors state 10 years; your site states no length.
7. Who answers the 0808 number when you are on site?A missed call on a large project usually rings the next firm.
