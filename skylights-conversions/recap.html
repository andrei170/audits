Where we are

Mark, this page pulls together where things stand before Friday. We put the first pack together on 20 October and re-checked everything on 22 October. Nothing on your website has changed in between, so the findings still stand. Friday is the conversation: what you want more of, and whether what we found is worth fixing.

What we found, in three lines
⭐ The reputation is done

9.67 out of 10 from 90 Checkatrade reviews, approved member since December 2009, the most recent dated 20 August 2026 (re-read 22 October). Customers come back for second and third projects.

🔴 The website does not use it

Six pages, no page changed since 11 March 2026 (your own sitemap, re-read 22 October), no tracking of any kind, no page for extensions or garage conversions, one town page, and your 9.67 shown nowhere on the site.

⚠ Every enquiry arrives looking the same

The only form is on the Contact page and asks nothing about the project, the budget or the start date. So a loft and a small repair look identical until you are on the phone or at a survey.

What the sample website shows
  • A survey form on every page that asks project, rough budget, start date and property type first.
  • Your 9.67 and real review quotes up front, read from Checkatrade.
  • Pages for lofts, extensions and garage conversions, and one for each of your seven towns.
  • A call bar that stays on screen on a phone. Anything we could not check is marked as a placeholder.

What we have not done
