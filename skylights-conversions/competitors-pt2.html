What it shows
⭐⭐ Finding 1 · Your reputation holds up. Your site hides it

Where a firm shows a count, only CS Lofts (126 on Google) shows more than your 90. Sutton Lofts, the only competitor whose Checkatrade profile we could read, joined in 2022 with 41 reviews; you joined in 2009. But every one of them says more about its reviews on its own site than you do.

🔴 Finding 2 · They sell certainty, in writing

CS Lofts and Simply Loft both state a fixed price and a 10-year cover. CS Lofts puts the build time in weeks in writing and takes £500 off for every week it overruns. Your site promises cover “subject to terms and conditions” and says nothing about price or time. On a project this size, a homeowner compares those promises line by line.

⭐ You already work to clear timings (8 to 12 weeks design, 6 to 10 weeks build, from your own FAQ). Saying what you already do, in writing, is the cheapest change on this page.

⚠ Finding 3 · Simply Loft is winning your own town

It has a page for Cheam and came 1st for “loft conversion Cheam” on Bing and DuckDuckGo. You came 4th, with no Cheam or Sutton page. A page per town, carrying your reviews, is how that gap closes.

⚠ Finding 4 · Only one of them filters before the survey

Touchstone asks budget in bands and start date on its first form. Nobody else does, including you. For a firm that wants bigger projects, that one form is the difference between driving to surveys and choosing them.

The short version: you are not behind on quality or reputation. You are behind on saying it and being found for it. The sample website does both.
