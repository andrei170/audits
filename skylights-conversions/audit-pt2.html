Each point your team raised, checked again

When the call was booked, our team noted four things about Sky Build. This page takes each one in turn: what we found when we looked again on 22 October, and what would fix it. These are our team’s notes, not your words, so put us right on Friday where they are wrong.

1 · Noted when you booked

“On longer, high-value builds, and wants more of the same”

What we found: Your Checkatrade profile lists extensions, loft conversions and garage conversions, and recent reviews mention a rear extension and an outhouse. The website only sells lofts: two loft pages, one Epsom page, no extension or garage conversion page. And your own FAQ says design and approval take 8 to 12 weeks before a 6 to 10 week build, so the next big project has to be found months before the current one ends.

What fixes it: Pages for the bigger work (extensions, loft plus extension, garage conversions) and a form that asks budget and start date, so the large projects stand out the day they arrive and surveys get booked far enough ahead.

2 · Noted when you booked

“Already has SEO in place”

What we found: Re-checked 22 October: still six pages, and your sitemap shows no page changed since 11 March 2026. Still no Google Analytics, Tag Manager or any other tracking in the code. On 20 October you were 4th for “loft conversion Cheam” and not in the first nine for “loft conversion Sutton” (Bing and DuckDuckGo). Simply Loft, which has its own Cheam page, was 1st in your town.

What fixes it: Tracking first, so you can see what the SEO produces. Then ask your provider three things: your Google positions for those two searches, what has changed on the site since March, and how they count enquiries with no tracking on the site. We are not suggesting you drop anyone.

3 · Noted when you booked

“Not sure about ads”

What we found: No ads running for skybuild.co.uk (Google Ads Transparency Centre, UK, checked 20 October). Loft firms around you have the tracking in place to advertise. The real risk with ads for a high-value trade is paying for small enquiries.

What fixes it: Ads last, not first. Only once tracking and the budget question are live. Search ads for people already searching for lofts and extensions in your towns, on a daily budget you can turn down when the diary is full.

4 · Noted when you booked

“Full team plus subcontractors”

What we found: The team exists, so the limit is not who builds; it is how the work comes in. The site’s only number is the free 0808 line, and nothing on the site follows up an enquiry if nobody answers while you are on site.

What fixes it: A missed call texted back straight away, every call and form in one place, and every quote followed up until it is a yes or a no. Large projects take weeks to decide, and most are won or lost in that follow-up.

⭐ And one thing nobody raised, but it is your biggest asset

90 Checkatrade reviews at 9.67, member since 2009, re-read 22 October. On your website it appears only as a small “Checkatrade.com” link. No score, no count, no quotes. Putting it on every page costs nothing and is what a homeowner checks first.

⚠ Two things worth tidying before any of the above
  • Your cover: the site promises cover on every loft, “subject to terms and conditions”, but never says what it covers or for how long. Two competitors state a 10-year figure (see Competitors Pt.2). The one public 1-star review, from November 2024, is about exactly that question. Site wording that matches your written terms closes it off.
  • Your age: the homepage still says “trading since 2017”; the loft page says 2003; Checkatrade says member since 2009. Companies House shows Skylights Conversions Ltd from April 2016 and Skybuild (Surrey) Ltd from July 2017. One story, used everywhere.
Questions that change what we would build
  • Which projects do you want more of: lofts only, or extensions and loft plus extension too?
  • What is the smallest project worth a survey? That sets how hard the form filters.
  • When do the current builds finish, and when should the next ones start?
  • Who does your SEO, and what do they report each month?
  • Who answers the 0808 number when you are on site?
  • What does your written cover actually include, and for how long?
  • Which company name do you want customers to see?
