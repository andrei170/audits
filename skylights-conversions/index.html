1. Is the SEO you pay for working?
Some foundations, very little to rank with

The site has the basics an SEO provider usually installs: Yoast SEO, a title and description per page, a sitemap and an FAQ block. Here is what it is missing, and what we saw in search:

  • Six pages in total (home, loft rooms, loft conversions, Epsom, gallery, contact). Only one town page. No Sutton, Carshalton, Ewell, Morden or Stoneleigh page, though all five are on your own area list.
  • No extension or garage conversion page, so the site cannot be found for those searches at all.
  • The sitemap shows no page has changed since 11 March 2026. Seven months without a new or updated page is unusual for an active SEO service; worth asking your provider what they have done since.
  • The homepage headline names no service and no town; two pages have no main heading; none of the 21 photos has alt text.
  • No tracking of any kind on the site, so no report can tell you how many calls or forms the website produced.

2. AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews)
Not named in either answer we tested

We asked two questions the way a homeowner would ask an AI assistant: “best loft conversion companies in Sutton and Cheam Surrey” and “who should I use for a dormer loft conversion in Epsom or Ewell”. Neither answer named Sky Build. They named Croft Loft Conversions, MS Loft Conversion, Top Division Lofts, and Checkatrade-listed builders.

⭐ Why it matters for you: these answers lean on review sites and on pages that state plainly who does what, where. You have the reviews; the site does not state the rest. A page per town and service, your Checkatrade score on the site, and business details in the page code are what we would add.

Two questions on one AI search tool on one day is a snapshot, not a measurement of every assistant.

3. Ads
No ads running today
  • Google Ads Transparency Centre, UK, any time: “0 ads” for skybuild.co.uk.
  • Meta Ad Library, UK, keyword “skybuild”: five results, none yours (a property developer in Cyprus and an app). ⚠ We searched by keyword; checking your Facebook page directly on the call settles it.

⭐ So there is nothing to fix, only an opportunity. People searching “loft conversion Sutton” are already looking; Simply Loft, CS Lofts and Touchstone all have the tracking in place to advertise to them. The risk with ads for a high-value trade is paying for small enquiries, which is why the form comes first (section 4).

4. Your enquiry route
Every enquiry arrives looking the same
  • The only form is on the Contact page; every other page links to it. It asks name, phone, email, area and a message.
  • No question about the project, the budget or the start date. A loft, an extension and a small repair all arrive identical, and you find out which on the phone or at the survey.
  • Your 9.67 Checkatrade score is not shown anywhere on the site.

⭐ Touchstone Lofts asks budget in bands on its first form. The sample website does the same, plus project type, start date, property type and whether drawings exist, so the big projects stand out before you pick up the phone.

5. Two things worth tidying
Small, but a careful buyer notices
  • The age of the business: “trading since 2017” on the homepage, “since 2003” on the loft page, “more than two decades” on two others. Checkatrade says member since 2009. Pick one and use it everywhere.
  • Two company names: Skybuild (Surrey) Ltd (Companies House 10861415, at 9 Matlock Place) on the site and Checkatrade; Skylights Conversions Ltd (10144364) on construction.co.uk, with one unanswered 1-star review from November 2024. A short, calm public reply there, and site wording that matches your written terms on cover, closes it off.
