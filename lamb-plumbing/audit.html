The three gaps that matter most

With no live page and no system, the list of things that could be built is long, so here are the three that would do the most, in order, for the problem Ben actually flagged.

1. A page people can reach, with one obvious way to get in touch

Right now a customer who looks you up lands on a holding page and leaves. A short, honest page that says what Lamb Plumbing does, where, and who runs it, with a job-type-and-postcode enquiry form and a tap-to-call number, turns a search into an enquiry. It is the front door, and nothing else can catch work until it exists. There is a worked example of one in this pack.

2. A system that catches every enquiry the moment it lands

This is the part Ben flagged. An enquiry that comes in while you are on a job needs to be answered instantly, an automatic reply to the form, a text back to a missed call, so the customer knows you exist and waits for you instead of ringing the next plumber. The same system holds the booking, the customer and the job in one place, so nothing is lost and nothing is remembered off the top of your head.

3. The admin that runs itself after the job: invoice, chase, review

Once the work is done, a system can send the invoice, chase it if it is late, and ask the happy customer for a review, all without you sitting down at a laptop in the evening. That is the admin load off your plate and, quietly, more reviews and faster payment. None of it is possible until there is a page and a system to hang it on, which is why those come first.

What is already strong

  • You own the right web address. lambplumbing.services is a clear match for your name, already registered, secured with HTTPS, and set up on a proper builder. That is the first thing most firms have to go and sort; you are past it.
  • You run professional email on it. Google Workspace business email on your own domain. It marks you out as a real, established, paying business, which is exactly the reassurance a cautious customer looks for, and it is what a system plugs into to send and log every reply.
  • There is nothing to undo. A clean start is the fastest kind. No old site to untangle, no bad content to rewrite, no legacy system to migrate off.
