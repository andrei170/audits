HOW TO PLAY IT (read first)

This is not a "your website is terrible" call, and it is not a website call at all. lambplumbing.services shows a Squarespace "Coming Soon" page, so a build is already in hand. The F is the size of the opportunity, not a failing. The brief is CRM / Admin: the page is just the front door, the pitch is the system behind it that catches every enquiry and runs the paperwork. Lead with the two positives (owned, secured domain on a real builder; proper Google Workspace email) and tie straight to what Ben sold, catching enquiries and cutting admin, because a missed call mid-job and an evening of invoicing are what it quietly costs them.
