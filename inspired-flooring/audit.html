The three gaps that matter most

Most of this site is in good shape and the store comes through clearly. Three things are quietly capping how much growth it turns from the interest it already earns, and they are exactly the sort of thing a busy owner has no reason to have noticed.

1. Nothing records which clicks become booked visits or sales

Your analytics counts visits, which is the groundwork, but there is no conversion tag at all. That means you cannot run Google advertising for the fitted floors or the furniture and know what it earned, and you cannot see which pages or searches bring the higher-value jobs. It is the first thing to put right, because paid search, which is the obvious lever for a store expanding its range, is guesswork without it.

2. The site is missing from the local map results it should own

You have a real showroom on Brandon Street, set opening hours, and a clear list of areas you cover, but the website carries only general organisation markup, not the local-business or store tag that puts a shop into Google's map pack for "flooring shop near me" or "carpet shop Motherwell". For a business whose customers often want to come in and see and feel the product, being found on the map is a direct line to footfall, and it is a quick fix.

3. No customer reviews are shown on the page

You are an award-winning store and you clearly have Google reviews, but a visitor has to leave your site to find them. Flooring and furniture are considered purchases people research carefully, and other people's word for the work is what tips the decision. A few of your real Google reviews, pulled onto the homepage and the product pages as short quotes, would reassure the exact buyer who is weighing you against another store. They have to be your real reviews; we would never put an invented one on your site.

What is already strong

  • You are already measuring. Google Analytics 4 is live through a tag manager, with no dead legacy tag. Most firms we look at are flying blind; you are not.
  • You capture the enquiry on the page. Forms on the homepage and the contact page, tap-to-call throughout, and a WhatsApp button. The ready customer has a way in.
  • Real, shown credibility. Two Prestige Awards and recognised brands like Karndean and Quick-Step, on an established company trading from a real Motherwell showroom.
  • A proper, broad build: flooring, furniture and home styling all laid out clearly, your own domain email, and the areas you cover stated plainly.
