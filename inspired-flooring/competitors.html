This page is not about who is the better store. On range, on your awards and on twenty-five years of experience, you are already ahead of most of the field. It is about who a customer in Motherwell, Hamilton, Uddingston, Glasgow or East Kilbride finds when they search "flooring shop near me", "LVT flooring Motherwell", "carpet fitters Hamilton" or "furniture store Lanarkshire", scan a few sites and map listings for a couple of minutes, and decide which showroom to visit or who to invite out to measure, usually before they have spoken to anyone.

The pattern. The stores that win that search do not necessarily stock a better floor than you. They show up in the local map pack, they put their reviews where the customer can see them, and the ones growing on purpose run paid search with tracking so they can afford to sit at the top for the searches worth having. You already have the hardest parts to earn: a real showroom, two industry awards, and a broad, well-presented range. The gap is that the website is missing from the map results it should own, your reviews are a click away instead of on the page, and nothing is set up to run or measure advertising for the fitted jobs and the new home range.

What the stores winning the search put on the page

Across flooring and interiors retailers that rank and convert in markets like Lanarkshire, the same ingredients show up again and again:

  • A strong local map presence, driven by local-business markup plus a complete Google Business Profile, so they appear for "flooring shop near me". This is the piece your site is missing.
  • Visible customer reviews on the landing page itself, not only a link out to Google. You have the reviews; they are just not on the page yet.
  • A short booking or quote form on the page the search lands on, in the hero, so the ready buyer can book a home visit or a showroom appointment without hunting. You already have forms; the refinement is qualifying them.
  • A page per product and area, so "LVT flooring", "carpets Hamilton" and "furniture Motherwell" each have a page to rank. You have the product structure already.
  • Paid search with conversion tracking for the fitted jobs and the bigger furniture buys, so they can measure what an enquiry costs and keep bidding where it pays. This is the piece you do not yet have.
