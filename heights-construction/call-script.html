The call, in order
  1. Open: "Thanks Jacob. Before I show anything, what made you take the call, and what would make this half hour worth it?"
  2. Where work comes from: "3 to 4 enquiries a week, mostly word of mouth. How many of those become jobs? Which ones do you wish you had more of?"
  3. The dream project: "Talk me through your ideal garden room or summer house job. What does it come to, and how long does it take?"
  4. Capacity: "You said 5 to 10 more jobs. Who builds them: you, a team, subcontractors? How quiet does January to March get?"
  5. What has been tried: "What does RankMyLocal look after? Has anyone run adverts for you before?" Never knock RankMyLocal.
  6. Pain check: "You have wanted this for 2 to 3 months. 1 to 10, how much do you want the winter full this year?"
  7. Summary back, wait for a clear yes, then share the audit and scorecard, then the plan.
  8. Show: strong site (credit it), then no ads, no Meta pixel, garden rooms buried, Reviews page showing 0.
Finding capacity and revenue without sounding salesy
  • "On a garden room, how many weeks from deposit to handover? How many can you run at once?"
  • "If the right enquiries came in, how many extra projects could you start before spring?"
  • "What does a typical project come to: garden office, or the premium room with bi-folds?"
  • Then let HIM do the sum: "So one extra project a month is worth roughly what to you?"
How to pitch it
  • Frame: "You have a strong website and a partner keeping it found on Google. What is missing is anything that puts your garden rooms in front of homeowners before they start searching. That is what we do."
  • His angle: a NICEIC builder and electrician, so the garden room comes wired, heated and certified by one team.
  • Fit: Growth if he wants Google as well as Meta; Starter if Meta only. Figures are in the brief. Say them only if asked, never in writing.
  • No promise of enquiries, jobs or dates.
Close
  • "If we had a garden room page live and adverts running to homeowners near you, with every enquiry counted, is there any reason not to start before the quiet months?"
  • "Then I need 5 to 10 photos of rooms you have built, and the areas you want."
  • Not ready? Book the follow-up before you hang up. Recap by email, no pricing.
