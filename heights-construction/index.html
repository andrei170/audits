The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A genuinely strong website: a quote form on every page, a quote builder, a survey booking page, tap-to-call and WhatsApp.
  • Prices out in the open: your garden rooms page shows garden offices from £12,000 to £20,000, studios and gyms £15,000 to £25,000, premium rooms £25,000 to £35,000. That filters out time-wasters before they ring.
  • Real proof: case studies with costs and timelines, NICEIC Approved Contractor, £5M insurance shown, Google tag in place.
  • Pages for every area you cover, from Hampton and Twickenham to Wimbledon and Islington.
⚠ What is missing
  • Nothing is bringing new people to the site. You told our team there are no paid adverts, so it relies on word of mouth and Google finding you.
  • The homepage leads with extensions, lofts, rewiring and EV chargers. Garden rooms and summer houses, the work you most want, are one of 17 services. The phrase "summer house" does not appear on the homepage at all.
  • No Meta (Facebook and Instagram) pixel, so a Facebook or Instagram advert could not learn who enquires.
  • Your Reviews page shows "0 reviews" in its own counter, even though the homepage shows a 5.0 Google rating. A careful buyer who clicks through sees zero.
A note on your website partner

Your site says it is designed, built and managed by RankMyLocal, who also look after your Google Business Profile, local SEO and reviews. That is good work and we would not touch it. What we do sits on top: paid adverts that put your garden rooms in front of homeowners who are not searching yet, sent to pages your partner already built.
