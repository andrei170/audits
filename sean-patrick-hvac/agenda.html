Questions we would like to ask you
1. How many jobs a week do you do now, and how many are repairs versus new systems?So we know what 3 more a week means in your business.
2. What is your average job worth, roughly, for a repair and for a replacement?We have not guessed this. It decides what each lead is worth to you.
3. Out of 10 calls that come in, how many become a booked job?This tells us how many extra calls 3 extra jobs really need.
4. Roughly how much goes to Angi each month, and how many jobs does that bring?Your numbers stay with you. We only want the cost per booked job.
5. Who runs your Google Ads, and can you see which calls came from an ad?We found no Google Ads conversion tag on the website, so we would like to understand how it is being measured.
6. Who rebuilt your website in October?It is a real improvement, and we would build on it rather than replace it.
7. Are you on Google's Local Services Ads (the "Google Screened" ads at the very top)?We could not check this from outside.
8. Which towns do you most want more work in?Your site lists 20. We would start with the ones you want most.
9. Do you offer financing, or a written workmanship warranty?Neither is on the main pages today.
10. Is the business open on Sundays or not?The site says both, so customers and Google get mixed signals.
