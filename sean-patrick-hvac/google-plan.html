Rented leads and owned leads

You said Angi makes you money but feels like a scam. Both can be true. Angi's business is matching a homeowner with several pros, so the same request can go to other contractors as well, and the customer relationship starts on Angi's platform. Leads you own come to you and only you: someone finds you on Google, calls your number, and becomes your customer.

We are not saying drop Angi. While it makes you money, keep it. The goal is to get to a point where you can see what a booked job costs from each source, and then decide with numbers.

1. Count every call and form
  • Google Ads conversion tracking. Your Tag Manager is already installed, so adding the Google Ads tag for calls from the website, form submissions and calls from the ads is quick. Then Google Ads learns which searches become jobs.
  • A tracked number per source. One number on Google Ads, one on your Google listing, one on Angi (where Angi allows it), one on the website. All ring your phone. Now you can see cost per booked job by source.
  • A simple weekly sheet. Calls in, jobs booked, by source. Five minutes a week.
2. Google Ads that land on the right page
  • Send each ad to the page for that job (AC repair, furnace repair, replacement), not the home page.
  • Put a short quote form on every one of those pages, next to the number.
  • Run ads for the towns you most want, with a matching town page.
  • Use your strongest true lines: "speak directly with an owner" and "24/7".
3. Google Local Services Ads

These are the ads at the very top of Google with the "Google Screened" badge. You pay per lead, not per click, and the lead comes to you alone. Google checks your licence and insurance first, which you already have. We could not see whether you run these today, so we will ask on Sunday.

4. Your Google listing and reviews
  • Make your hours the same on Google, your site and everywhere else (today your site says both 24/7 and "Sun: closed").
  • Ask every finished job for a Google review, by text, the same day. Your site already has the review link.
  • Add photos of real installs to the listing and the gallery page regularly.
  • Answer every review, good or bad.
5. Town pages

A page for each town you serve, starting near home: Glendora, San Dimas, West Covina, Claremont, Monrovia, Arcadia and Pasadena are all on your own Areas list. Azusa, Covina and La Verne sit right next to Glendora but we did not see them on the list, so we would ask you about them. Each page names the town, the jobs you do there, and has the form and the number.

What this does not promise

None of this promises a number of calls, jobs or a ranking. It makes sure that every lead you pay for is counted, that the ones from Google are yours, and that you can judge Angi on facts.
