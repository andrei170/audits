The starting point

Your site was rebuilt in October and November, and the 15 service pages are good material. We would keep and build on them, not throw them away. What we would add is everything around them that turns a visit into a counted, booked job.

You can see the idea working in the demo site.

What the site would include
A quote form on every page
Short, on the service pages too: what is wrong, system type and age, how urgent, ZIP, name, phone. You see what the job is before you call back.
Tracking on every call and form
Google Ads conversion tags through your existing Tag Manager, and tracked numbers by source.
Pages for ads
A page per main job for Google Ads, so the person who searched "furnace repair" lands on furnace repair.
Town pages
One page per town you want work in, starting with the ones closest to Glendora.
Your proof, on the page
CSLB licence number in the footer, your Google rating (read live from Google), NATE, years in the trade, real install photos.
One set of facts everywhere
Same hours, same number, same address on the site, Google and directories. Fixed map pin and social links.
Financing and warranty, if you offer them
Only if you do. We would not add anything you do not offer.
A simple weekly report
Calls and forms by source, so you can compare Google, your listing and Angi.
How we would work with you
  1. A short call to agree which towns and jobs matter most.
  2. Tracking first, so everything after it can be measured.
  3. Forms and ad pages next, then town pages.
  4. A weekly check-in on what came in and from where.
Not included in this pack

No prices, no timelines and no promised results. Those come after Sunday's call, and only if it makes sense to you.
