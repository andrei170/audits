The short version

You do not have a website problem any more. You have a counting problem and an ownership problem. Angi makes you money because it hands you people ready to buy, but those people were often sent to other pros too, and the relationship lives on Angi. Google Ads can give you leads that are yours alone, but only if every call and form from an ad is counted, so Google can learn which searches turn into jobs.

3 more jobs a week is about 156 more jobs a year. On the call we want to work backwards from your real numbers (average job, how many calls you win) to see how many of those could come from leads you own.

The order we would do it in
  1. Count everything first. A Google Ads conversion tag for calls and forms, and a separate tracked number for Google Ads, Google listing, Angi and the website, so you can see what a booked job costs from each.
  2. Give ad clicks somewhere to land. A quote form on every service page, and ad pages built for the exact job (for example "AC repair in San Dimas").
  3. Fix the small things that cost trust. One set of opening hours everywhere, your CSLB number in the footer, the Instagram link, the map pin in your markup.
  4. Town pages for the towns you already list, starting with the ones closest to Glendora.
  5. Google listing and reviews. Ask every finished job for a Google review, and keep your Google Business Profile up to date.
  6. Then decide on Angi with numbers, not a feeling. Once every source is counted, you can see whether Angi earns its place.
