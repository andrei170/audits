Dave, thanks for making time on Monday. You told our team that work is not the problem: finding good engineers is. Indeed brings you poor applicants, the groups have not worked, and the engineers worth having are busy in winter. So this pack is about finding, filtering and testing good gas and air conditioning engineers, and keeping your apprenticeship route full. It is not about more customers. Before the call we read archived copies of your website, your Google, Checkatrade and Which? listings, Companies House, six local heating and air conditioning firms and the job boards near you, and built a demo careers site. Nothing here is priced, and none of it is a verdict on your work.

Where: Zoom, us06web.zoom.us/j/83816723539.

⭐ What is already working
  • A reputation good engineers respect: 4.9 on Google, 10/10 on Checkatrade, Endorsed by Which?.
  • A real team on show, with your British Gas background and Mason’s apprenticeship.
  • Growth on record: average staff up from 6 to 11 in three years.
What we found
  • Nothing on your site tells an engineer you are hiring: no careers page and nowhere to apply, on any archived page.
  • Your apprenticeship success is never turned into an invitation, and no gas apprenticeship is advertised within 15 miles of Acton on the government service.
  • Air conditioning is invisible to an F-Gas engineer: not on the archived pages and not a category on your Google listing.
  • Recruiting grade today D, 50 out of 100; the same checks after the build, A-, 87.
