What this means for you
1You are competing with British Gas and the agencies for the same engineers.

The loudest adverts near you come from British Gas and from recruitment agencies quoting forty to seventy thousand. On a job board your advert sits in that list, which is why Indeed brings you two applicants a day and most are poor: the good ones are not reading it.

Source: Reed searches around Ealing, 20 November 2026.

2No local firm is selling the job, so the first one that does stands out.

Heywood, West London Gas, Phoenix, Boiler Boys, Reliant LDN and CapCool have strong reviews and no careers page we could find. A good engineer quietly wondering about a move has nowhere to look. A page that shows your team, your standard and your growth would be the only one of its kind in West London.

Source: Home pages of each firm, 20 November 2026.

3Apprenticeships are wide open, and they are what already works for you.

No gas engineer apprenticeship is advertised within 15 miles of Acton on the government service. You have put five people through and they stay. A steady, visible apprenticeship route (school leavers, career changers, improvers) is ground nobody near you is working.

Source: Find an Apprenticeship searches, 20 November 2026.

4Air conditioning engineers are found differently from gas engineers.

F-Gas engineers are spread across commercial, refrigeration and facilities work, and agencies are training up their own at entry level. To attract one, the air conditioning side of Gasworks has to be visible: on your site, on Google and in the advert itself.

Source: Reed air conditioning and F-Gas searches, 20 November 2026.

5Winter is the wrong time to find someone, and the right time to start looking.

You said it yourself: good engineers are busy now. A page and a small advert left running collect names through the winter, so when the air conditioning season starts you already have people to call.

Source: What you told our team.
