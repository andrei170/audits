The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A reputation engineers respect: 4.9 on Google from 135 reviews, 10/10 on Checkatrade from 288, Endorsed by Which?.
  • A real team on show: names, roles and professional photos, plus your own British Gas background.
  • Your apprenticeships work: Mason came through his apprenticeship with you and is now one of your engineers.
  • A growing firm on record: Companies House shows average staff rising from 6 to 7, then 10, then 11 in the year to March 2026.
⚠ What holds it back for hiring
  • Nothing on the site is aimed at an engineer. No careers page, no vacancy line and no way to apply, on any archived page.
  • The apprenticeship story is told, then dropped. Nothing invites the next apprentice, although that is your best route.
  • Air conditioning is invisible to an F-Gas engineer: not on the archived pages and not a category on your Google listing.
  • No Facebook pixel, so a recruitment advert could not learn who applies.
