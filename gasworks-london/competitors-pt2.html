What this means for you
1Almost nobody is advertising a two or three day air conditioning job.

Nearly every air conditioning advert near you is full time, at forty to fifty thousand a year, from agencies and big firms. Across the whole of London we found two part-time listings, and neither was air conditioning. So an advert that says "two or three days a week, experienced, references required" is not competing with that list. It speaks to a different person: an experienced engineer who runs his own small air conditioning business, or one who wants fewer days. That person is not reading full-time adverts.

Source: Reed searches, 23 November 2026.

2The firms your customers compare you with are easy to reach out of hours.

Four of the six show 24/7 or out-of-hours cover, and four have a WhatsApp button (West London Gas, Phoenix, Boiler Boys and CapCool). Boiler Boys also has an online booking form and a chat box on its site. Every call to Gasworks goes to two people in the office. Missed-call text back, WhatsApp replies and an AI answering overflow calls (on Audit Pt.2) would put you level with them without adding to the office team.

Source: Each firm’s own website, read 23 November 2026.

3Your website did not load for us.

On 23 November gasworkslondon.co.uk returned a server error each time we tried. It may be temporary, and it is also why our first audit had to use archived copies. It is worth checking with whoever hosts it: a careers page and any adverts, and any customer looking you up, all need the site working first.

Source: Our own checks of gasworkslondon.co.uk, 20 and 23 November 2026.

4Air conditioning engineers have choices, so the offer is what stands out.

Five of the six firms sell air conditioning alongside gas and heating, and CapCool does nothing else. An F-Gas engineer looking around sees plenty of firms doing the same work. What none of them offer on their sites is what you can: two or three days to start, a trial that either side can walk away from, a team that has grown from 6 to 11 and a 4.9 reputation. Those are the things to put in the advert.

Source: Each firm’s own website, 23 November 2026; your Google listing and Companies House, 20 November 2026.

5Still nobody near you talks about apprentices.

None of the six mentions apprentices on its site, and no gas apprenticeship was advertised within 15 miles of Acton when we checked. Your apprentices stay. That route is still open to you alone.

Source: Each firm’s own website, 23 November 2026; Find an Apprenticeship, 20 November 2026.
