To compare it with

A recruitment agency typically charges 15 to 20% of a first year’s salary. On the forty to fifty thousand air conditioning roles advertised near you, that is about £6,000 to £10,000 for one engineer, and you own nothing afterwards. Everything below stays yours: the page, the list of engineers and the ad account.
