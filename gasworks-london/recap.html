How you judge a new engineer
  • A trial day, then the next day, then the next week. For the first few weeks either side can walk away, because you do not want to commit too early.
  • A questionnaire of about 50 questions to see what they know, then easy jobs with you alongside.
  • Problems show up later: some people impress on day one and get sloppy after three or four weeks. You think you should spend more time with each one in the first two days.
  • What you look for: consistent, reliable, good quality, good with customers, experienced and smart.
  • References from previous employers are the best early check. We agreed to make them required before anyone can apply: fewer applications, but more of the right people.
