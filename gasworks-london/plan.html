The plan, in order

Open the door, filter hard, then test on a real job. The demo careers site shows steps 2 and 3 working, including what you would receive for each applicant.

1Agree the roles and terms (on Monday)

Which roles first: a qualified gas engineer, an F-Gas air conditioning engineer, an apprentice, or all three. Employed or self-employed, the pay band, van, hours, on-call and the trial day. We do not guess any of these; you set them, and the page says them plainly.

2One careers page that opens the door

A "Work at Gasworks" page on your own site, in your words: the team, your British Gas background, the reviews, the air conditioning growth, Mason’s story. The demo shows it. Left open all year, it is there when a good engineer finally looks.

3An application that does the first sift

Five minutes on a phone. The questions check Gas Safe registration and categories, F-Gas, years on boilers and systems, driving licence, area and right to work, and politely turn away anyone who is not qualified for the role they chose. Everyone gets an instant reply. You only see the ones who pass.

4Two small, steady adverts

One for qualified engineers: Facebook and Instagram adverts shown to people within reach of Acton who follow heating, gas and air conditioning trade pages, built from your own team photos. One for apprentices and improvers, plus a free listing on the government’s Find an Apprenticeship service. Aimed by area and interest, never by age or gender.

5Add the tracking first

A Meta pixel on the careers page, so the adverts learn which people actually apply, not just who clicks.

6Make air conditioning visible

Add the air conditioning category to your Google listing and an air conditioning section to the careers page. An F-Gas engineer needs to see that the work is real and growing.

7A screen that protects your time

We read the answers first, check Gas Safe numbers on the public register and ask for F-Gas certificates, then book one 15-minute call per shortlisted person into one slot in your week.

8The trial day you already offer

You said you would give someone a trial any day soon. One paid day alongside James or Manveer on a real job tells you what an Indeed CV never will.

9A bench for the spring

Every good applicant who is not ready now goes on a list. When air conditioning season starts, you have names to ring instead of starting from scratch.

10See it working

Each week: applications, how many passed, calls held, trial days booked. You can see it working without chasing anyone.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise a hire, a number of applicants or a date. Good gas and F-Gas engineers are scarce in winter, which is the whole problem. What this plan does is put your firm in front of good engineers who are not looking, turn away the wrong ones before they cost you an hour, keep an apprentice route open, and test the right person on a real job before you rely on him. On employment status, contracts and apprenticeship funding, your accountant and the apprenticeship provider are the right people to ask.
