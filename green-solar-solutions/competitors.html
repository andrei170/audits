What this means for you
1Reviews are the biggest visible gap, and the easiest to start closing.

L J Norton shows 4.9 from 280 on Google and Apex Doma 5.0 from 150. You have 5.0 from 3. Your score is as good as anyone’s; the count is what a homeowner notices. Every finished install is a chance to ask, and at your current pace that is a few a month.

Source: Google Maps from central Stockport, 17 November 2026.

2Two of them have built a page for each town. You have the words already, inside your service pages.

Apex Doma has 11 solar pages for single towns around you, including Bramhall, Hazel Grove and Alderley Edge; Renewable Planet has 71 town pages. Your service pages already describe Stockport, Marple, Wilmslow and Altrincham; turning those sections into their own pages is a quick win.

Source: Each firm’s sitemap, 17 November 2026.

3Four of the six measure their visitors; one is set up for Google Ads. None of the six has a Meta pixel.

L J Norton and Manchester Renewables run Analytics and Tag Manager, Apex Doma runs Analytics and a Google Ads tag, Renewable Planet runs Tag Manager. Nobody has the Facebook and Instagram pixel, so if your social ads go out properly measured, you are first in this patch.

Source: Home page code of each site, 17 November 2026. A tag shows a firm is set up to measure or advertise, not how much it spends.

4Only one asks the postcode, and two talk about finance.

Renewable Planet’s form asks the postcode; none of the others do. Renewable Planet and Manchester Renewables both have finance pages. Whether you offer finance is a question for the call; if you do, it belongs on the site.

Source: Each firm’s home page, 17 November 2026.

5Most of them do more than solar. You can be the solar and battery specialist.

L J Norton and Solarus mix solar with EV chargers and general electrical work; Renewable Planet leads with heat pumps. Your site is only solar, batteries and servicing, which is a clear message if the site says it plainly.

Source: Each firm’s home page, 17 November 2026.

Where you are already ahead

  • Local business markup with your address and hours, which only some of them have.
  • A perfect 5.0, and a review that names you personally.
