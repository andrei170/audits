The three campaigns in one line
Panels and batteries for existing homes, batteries for homes that already have solar, and a reminder for anyone who looked.

These are the campaigns behind the ads plan. Each has its own adverts so you can see which one brings surveys, all built from your own photos and drone footage, all sending people to one survey page.

Campaign by campaign
1Panels and a battery, for homes around Stockport

The goal: survey bookings for the jobs you said you want more of: turn up, install, go.

  • Who sees it: homeowners in the areas you pick around Stockport and North Cheshire. Aimed by area, never by age or gender.
  • What it shows: a drone video of a finished roof, a set of real install photos, and one of you saying who Green Solar is and that every system is quoted to the house.
  • Where it sends them: the survey page: postcode, type of home, what they want and roughly when.

Why this one: this is the main job, and the one that is best for your cash flow, rather than extensions where the profit waits for the builders.

2A battery for homes that already have panels

The goal: quicker, smaller jobs from people who already understand solar.

  • Who sees it: homeowners in the same areas. Over time, the people who respond to it teach the adverts who else to show it to.
  • What it shows: a battery install from one of your jobs, and the plain point: store what your panels make in the day, use it in the evening.
  • Where it sends them: the survey page: postcode, type of home, what they want and roughly when.

Why this one: a battery on an existing system is a shorter decision and a shorter install. It keeps the van busy between bigger jobs.

3A reminder for anyone who looked

The goal: bring back people who watched the video or opened the survey page but did not book.

  • Who sees it: only people who have already watched or visited.
  • What it shows: your Google reviews, a finished job, and “book your free survey”.
  • Where it sends them: the survey page: postcode, type of home, what they want and roughly when.

Why this one: solar is a considered buy. People compare, as you saw when one install went to Solarus. A reminder keeps Green Solar in the comparison.

What that means against one job

You told us a typical job keeps you about £3,000. If an enquiry cost £40 and only one in ten became a job, that job would have cost about £400 in adverts. Your own close rate is the number that turns this into something real, and the weekly check shows it.
