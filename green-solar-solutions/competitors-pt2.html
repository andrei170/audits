What this means for you
1Only one local installer is talking about the Warm Homes Loan Scheme yet.

Renewable Planet has a page for it. The other five we checked do not. If the loans open widely, homeowners will search for installers who can take them, and there is room to be the clear local answer. Source: each firm’s website, 20 November 2026.

2Two of the six offer a way to spread the cost. You do not, yet.

Renewable Planet and Manchester Renewables both offer finance. On a £10,000 install, a monthly figure is often what makes someone say yes. Source: their websites, 20 November 2026.

3Every installer here also sells commercial.

All six, plus the specialists nearby, advertise commercial solar alongside domestic. Nobody locally owns it as their only focus, so a clear commercial page is not crowded ground. Source: each firm’s website, 20 November 2026.

4Reviews are still the biggest visible gap.
