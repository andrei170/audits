What you want
  • In the next 90 days: about 6 jobs a month, while you bring in an extra person. Twelve a month would be ideal, but six first is fine. Grid (DNO) applications can take up to two months, so installs lag sales.
  • Then: around 3 jobs a week, which is what you can already install.
  • Steady work all year, not waves, and cover for the January and February dip in word of mouth.
  • Social media advertising is the main thing you want help with, using your real installs, not AI.
  • Less time lost to quoting and paperwork: one job starting next week has been re-quoted nine times, about three hours each.
  • More Google reviews, kept genuine. You ask every customer and are thinking about a small thank-you for leaving one.

Your own numbers, as a guide only: at about £10,000 a job and around 30% net, each extra job is worth roughly £3,000 to you. 6 jobs a month is roughly £60,000 of work against the £4,000 to £5,500 of the last two months. This is arithmetic on what you told us, not a forecast.

A commercial lead company you spoke to charges about £400 a survey at around 20% conversion, roughly £2,000 a job before materials, and commercial cash flow is harder than domestic, where a 10% deposit and 40% on delivery cover the materials.
