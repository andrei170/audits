The plan in one line

Your real installs, in front of homeowners in the areas you choose, sent to one page that asks the postcode and the home first, with every enquiry counted.

This is the strategy behind the Ads Only option on Deliverables & Pricing, written down so you can see what you are paying for at every step. Your website stays exactly as it is.

1. Who the adverts are aimed at
  • Homeowners in the areas you pick around Stockport and North Cheshire, not the whole UK.
  • Leading with the jobs you want: panels and batteries on existing homes, and batteries for people who already have solar. Turn up, install, go: the jobs that are best for your cash flow.
  • Not extensions or new builds, where the profit waits for the builders.
2. What the adverts look like
  • Built only from your own photos and drone footage of real installs. Never AI images.
  • A few versions, not one: a short drone video of a finished roof, a set of install photos, and one with you explaining who Green Solar is. Running a few side by side is how we find out which one homeowners respond to.
  • One clear next step: book a free survey, because every system is quoted to the house.
  • Nothing goes live until you have approved it.
3. Where the adverts send people
  • One landing page for the adverts, with a short survey form: postcode, type of home, what they want (panels, a battery, both, or a battery for existing solar) and roughly when.
  • Far-away enquiries get an honest answer straight away, so you are not driving to Leeds for a quote.
  • Tracking set up first: the Meta pixel, plus taps on call and WhatsApp counted, so every enquiry shows which advert it came from. If the pixel you paid for exists, we check it and use it; if not, we set one up in your name.
4. The budget
  • You set it, on your own card, in your own Meta account. We never touch the money.
  • You mentioned £200 to £300 a month, about £7 to £10 a day. That is enough to start and learn. On a small budget results show more slowly, and we will tell you honestly in each weekly check what the numbers can and cannot tell us yet.
  • If something works, you decide whether to put more behind it. If something does not, we stop it.
5. Week by week
WhenWhat happens
Day 1You send your photos, drone footage and access to your Meta business account.
Days 2 to 7We build the adverts, the landing page and the tracking. You approve them.
Weeks 2 and 3Live. Meta needs a little time to learn who responds, so the first days move around. Weekly check-in: what is working, what we stop.
End of month 1A short report: enquiries, where each came from, and which became surveys and jobs.
Month 2More of what worked, less of what did not, still checked every week.
End of month 2The second report, and we decide together what happens next.
6. What we need from you
  • Photos of your installs, and your drone footage (originals, as you have them).
  • Access to your Meta business account and ad account, or we set them up in your name.
  • The areas you most want work in, and the ones to leave out.
  • About 15 minutes a week for the check-in.
7. After the two months
  • If the adverts are working, they carry on month to month, at a fee sized to the business and agreed in writing before month three. Nothing renews automatically.
  • Everything stays in your name: the ad account, the pixel, the landing page and the video files.
  • What this does not include: changes to your website, SEO, and regular social media posting. We can talk about those once the adverts are paying for themselves.
