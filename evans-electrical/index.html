What we found
⭐ The headline

This is a well-built site. 113 pages, real prices, online EICR booking with payment, a tappable number everywhere, NICEIC on every page, and 239 five-star Google reviews according to Top Rated Electricians. Most electricians we audit have none of this.

So this audit is not about rebuilding anything. It is about the gap between paying for visitors and turning them into booked jobs.

🔴🔴 1. Ad traffic lands on pages with no form

There is a Google Ads tag in your page code, which usually means ads are running. But none of your 13 service pages, and not your homepage, has an enquiry form. Forms exist only on Contact, the EICR booking page and the EV quote page.

🔴 Somebody who clicks an ad for “consumer unit upgrade” can ring or WhatsApp, or go hunting for the Contact page. A visitor who would rather not ring right then has nowhere to leave their details.

🔴 2. Nobody can say which ads turn into jobs

From outside we can see the tags, not what they count. If calls, forms and WhatsApp taps are not set up as conversions, Google is optimising for clicks, and you cannot tell a good campaign from an expensive one.

⚠ Worth checking together: open Google Ads, and see what the “Conversions” column is counting.

⚠ 3. Your strongest proof is credited to someone else

239 five-star Google reviews is a number most competitors cannot touch. Your site says “200+” and links to Top Rated Electricians, and does not link to your Google profile at all.

⚠ A live Google reviews feed, with the count, on every page would do more work than any badge.

⚠ 4. Small things that leak enquiries
  • The homepage WhatsApp button opens a chat with the landline (01606 645964), not a mobile
  • EICR certificates are “within 24 hours” on one page and “48-hour” on another
  • OZEV is not named, though EV chargers are a headline service and a directory lists you as OZEV approved
  • evanselec.uk, your email domain, shows a “not connected to a website” page. Anyone who types it from your email address lands nowhere
⚠ 5. No proof of the work itself

No gallery, no case studies, no team page. Matt, Liam, Jacob, Jack, Scott and Tom are named in your reviews and nowhere else on the site. For a rewire or a consumer unit, people want to see the work and the people.

⚠ 6. Sixty pages that say the same thing

60 town pages are built from one template, at about 500 words each with near-identical wording, alongside 17 proper location pages for the same towns. Google tends to treat near-duplicates as one page, so they add less than their number suggests.

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile itself, your Google Ads account, and where you rank in search. We have not guessed.
