Steve, thanks for making time on Monday. You told us work is not the problem; finding a joiner who is still good after the first month is. And you are not in a rush. So this pack is about one good self-employed joiner, found slowly and tested properly. It is not about more jobs. Before the call we read your website (all 28 pages), your Google, Guild, Facebook and TikTok pages, Companies House, twelve joinery firms around Swindon and two job boards, and built a demo work-with-us page. Nothing here is priced, and none of it is a verdict on your work.

Where: Zoom, about 30 minutes. The link comes from our team.

⭐ What is already working
  • A website most joinery firms would envy: your story, your standard and real photos, across 28 pages.
  • 5.0 on Google and on your own site, and Guild of Master Craftsmen membership.
  • Workshop-made bespoke work fitted to engineering tolerances: exactly what a careful joiner wants to be part of.
What we found
  • Nothing on your site tells a joiner you would take one on: no careers page and no way to apply, on any of 28 pages.
  • Nobody near you is advertising for a bespoke fitting joiner, so the good ones are not on job boards. They have to be shown your work.
  • Your Facebook pixel may not be counting visits, and Google shows your listing as possibly unclaimed.
  • Website grade today C, 60 out of 100; the same checks after the build, A-, 85.
