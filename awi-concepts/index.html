The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A genuinely good homeowner website: 28 pages, real photos, your story, nine service pages, nine town pages and proper markup for Google.
  • Your standard is written down: engineering tolerances, hand scribing, shadow gaps, "Build it once. Build it right."
  • 5.0 on Google (8 reviews) and on your own site (9), and Guild of Master Craftsmen membership since March 2026.
  • Tap-to-call on every page, a WhatsApp link, and a clear 30-mile area from Wroughton.
⚠ What holds it back for hiring
  • Nothing on the site is aimed at a joiner. No careers page, no vacancy line, no way to apply, on any of the 28 pages.
  • The only form is a quote form for homeowners, with no option for someone wanting work.
  • Tracking may not be counting visits: the Facebook pixel only switches on at www.awiconcepts.com, and the site runs without the www. No Google Analytics.
  • Your Google listing may be unclaimed: Google still shows "Own this business?".

Behind the pages
⚠ Code and set-up
  • Platform: a modern JavaScript site that serves Google a ready-made copy of every page. Well built.
  • Forms and reviews: the quote form and the review widget come from ScaleSuite and Reputation Hub, which run on the GoHighLevel platform. Leads from the form already go into a CRM.
  • Tracking: Meta pixel 1429002692453640 is in the site code, set to start only when the address is www.awiconcepts.com. awiconcepts.co.uk redirects to awiconcepts.com, and every page tells Google to use awiconcepts.com, so visits there are not counted. No Google Analytics, Tag Manager or Google Ads tag.
  • Markup: LocalBusiness, opening hours, areas, FAQ and review score. One main heading on every page.
  • Images: all 339 have descriptions; 25 on Our Work are phone file names.
  • Your robots file blocks several AI crawlers, which is a sensible choice, not a fault.
