Questions we would like to ask you
  • What would the joiner actually do: fitting only, or bench work in the workshop too?
  • Self-employed: a day rate or price work? How many days a week could you keep him busy, steadily?
  • The agency’s three, and the hires who were good for a month or two: what went wrong each time?
  • What would you need to see before you trusted someone alone in a customer’s house?
  • Where would he ideally live? How far from Wroughton is too far?
  • Own van and tools, and any cards or insurance you would expect?
  • Who looks after your website, Google listing and Facebook today?
