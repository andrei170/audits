What this means for you
1Nobody near you is advertising for a bespoke fitting joiner, which means the good ones are not job-hunting.

Neither board showed a self-employed or fitted-furniture joiner role around Swindon. The joiners you want are working, for themselves or for someone else. They will not see a job board. They might see an advert in their own feed showing a walnut walk-in wardrobe, and then look you up.

Source: Reed and Totaljobs searches, 18 November 2026.

2The firms that do recruit ask for a CV, and that is the wrong test for you.

One Step Joinery and Edmont both say "send your CV". Your agency hires came with CVs too. A CV cannot show scribing or a shadow gap; photos of a joiner’s own fitted work can, before you spend an hour on him.

Source: One Step Joinery and Edmont vacancy pages, 18 November 2026.

3Agencies are working the same small pool.

The skilled cabinet maker and the furniture sprayer near you were both advertised by agencies for unnamed workshops in Marlborough. Good bespoke people around Swindon are scarce enough that firms pay someone else to look.

Source: Reed adverts 57180779 and 57546361, 18 November 2026.

4Your website is already ahead of theirs. Use it.

Most of these firms have short home pages (about 170 to 600 words) and little about who they are. Yours tells a joiner exactly who you are and what standard you hold. Add one page and one form, and it becomes the best place for a good joiner near Swindon to land.

Source: Home pages of each firm, 18 November 2026.

5Reviews: they have more, you have the same score.

BJW (70), Absolute (56), MRY (54) and Fine Grain (44) have many more Google reviews than your 8, all at 4.9 or 5.0. For a candidate this matters less than for a customer, but it is worth asking every happy client.

Source: Google Maps from central Swindon, 18 November 2026.
