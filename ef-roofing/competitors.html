Who you are really bidding against
🔴🔴 Before the table, the thing worth hearing first

You are not bad at hiring. The men are not there.

The NFRC's State of the UK Roofing Industry, Winter 2026, surveyed roofing contractors and found:

  • 47% said recruiting skilled labour was harder than a year earlier
  • 58% said skilled labour shortages were limiting the amount of work they could take on
  • Around 70% said recruitment difficulties were limiting their ability to take on work
  • ⚠ The worst shortages are slaters, tilers and felt roofers, which is precisely the qualified hand you described wanting

⭐⭐ So two thirds of your competitors are turning down work for the same reason you are. That is not consolation, it is the market you are recruiting in, and it changes what a sensible plan looks like.

🔴🔴 Your real competitor for labour is London, and it pays a great deal more

🔴 A roofer in London averages £53,415. The UK average is £30,441. That is a gap of roughly £23,000 a year for the same trade.

⚠ Milton Keynes is about fifty miles from London and on a direct train. Every qualified roofer within reach of you can see that number too.

⭐⭐ So when a good hand does not take your job, it is usually not because they did not see the advert. It is because the advert had to beat London, and you are competing on a figure you may never have been shown.

🔴 And this is why Indeed was never going to work for you

You told our team you tried Indeed, your own adverts and several other channels, and that none produced a qualified hand.

⭐⭐ A job board reaches people who are looking for work. In a trade with a 58% shortage, the qualified men are not looking: they are already employed, and they are being asked to move by somebody every month.

⚠ Five businesses told us the same thing this week. It is not a flaw in how you wrote the advert.

🔴🔴 The part that is actually yours to fix, and it costs nothing

You have no careers page. We checked the whole site.

So the sequence you are currently running is: pay a job board, catch a roofer's attention, send him to a website with five real pages and 290 words that never mentions working for you, and hope.

⭐ Every rival employer competing for that same man has somewhere for him to land. You do not. ⚠ It is the cheapest item on this entire list and it is the one standing between an advert and a conversation.

⚠ What a qualified roofer is weighing up, and what you can put against it
  • Money. You may not beat London, and you may not need to. The travel, the hours and the hassle are worth real money to a man with a family
  • Steady work. ⭐ You told our team you want 3 to 4 roofs a week at £2,800 to £10,600 each. That is a full order book, and a full order book is one of the strongest things an employer can offer. It is not mentioned anywhere on your site
  • Who he would be working for. There are no photographs of your crew, no named supervisor and no account of the firm
  • ⚠ Accreditation. The site names none. For a qualified older hand, who he is signing on with matters
⚠ One observation about your own site, and it is a question rather than a claim

Your contact address is published as "20 Newington Gate, Simpson, Milton Keynes MK6 4AN, Regatul Unit". Regatul Unit is Romanian.

⭐⭐ If part of your network is Romanian-speaking, that is a recruitment channel, and it is one Indeed cannot reach at all. Qualified tradesmen move through word of mouth and closed Facebook groups far more than through job boards, and a firm that can speak to that network directly has an advantage over the London contractors who cannot.

⚠ We are not assuming anything about your network. We noticed the wording on your own site and it is worth two minutes of the call.
