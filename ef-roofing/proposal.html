What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ This order is not arbitrary. Measurement goes first because every judgement after it depends on having it. Anything that needs a decision from you is marked.

First, and measured in days

Tap-to-call on every page

The phone number made tappable in the header of every page.

⭐ Cheapest item in the whole list. Most people read a trade site on a phone.

Graded F today - ⚠ 1 tap-to-call link across 36 pages. Most people read a trade site on a phone.

Install measurement

Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager, with a conversion event on every enquiry form and every tap-to-call link on the site.

⭐ This is first because every other decision depends on it. Until it is in, nobody can tell a quiet month from an invisible website.

Graded F today - 🔴🔴 No analytics of any kind. Nothing records how many people reach the site or what they do on it.

Show the accreditations

Accreditation logos and certificate numbers displayed, with certificates viewable.

⭐ If any accreditation is held, it is being paid for and not shown.

Graded F today - ⚠ No accreditation named anywhere. If any is held, it is being paid for and not shown.

Email on your own domain

Move the published address off a free provider onto the domain already owned.

⚠ For domestic work this changes nothing. For commercial and contract work it reads smaller than the business is.

Graded F today - ⚠ A free email address is published on a site with its own domain.

Next, once the first is live

Advertising tag, installed but not switched on

A Google Ads conversion tag placed and tested, so that if advertising ever starts, the spend is measurable from day one.

⚠ Installing the tag is not starting to advertise. It costs nothing to have it ready and it is the difference between measured spend and guesswork.

Graded F today - 🔴 No advertising tag of any kind. Spend could not be measured today even if it started tomorrow.

Local business markup

Address, geographic coordinates, opening hours and trade category written into the page code where search engines read them.

⭐ For every “near me” search this is the single biggest structural gap, and it is hours of work rather than days.

Graded F today - 🔴 Structured data exists but only describes the website (SearchAction, WebSite). No address, no coordinates, no opening hours.

Say who you are

A short page naming who runs the business and how long it has traded.

Graded F today - ⚠ Nothing saying who runs the business.

Then, the conversion work

Enquiry routes

A short enquiry form in the hero of every page, asking the job type and postcode before the phone number.

⚠ A form below the fold is a form most visitors never reach.

Graded F today - 🔴 One form across 36 pages. The content does the persuading and then has nowhere to land.

A careers page

A vacancies page aimed at tradespeople, with a short application form.

⭐ Several firms we speak to cannot find staff and have nowhere for an applicant to land.

Graded F today - 🔴 None. Nothing aimed at somebody who might want to work there.

Slowest, and the part that compounds

Something that earns search traffic

A small number of genuinely useful pages answering the questions buyers actually ask.

Graded F today - ⚠ None. Nothing that earns search traffic over time.

What we would not touch

These already pass, and changing them would be change for its own sake:

  • Code structure - One main heading per page, as it should be.
  • Reviews on site - Present. ⚠ Volume and recency not assessed.
  • Proof of work - A gallery or project section is present.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not know what a job is worth to you yet, and we have no baseline for your site because nothing has ever measured it. Anyone giving you an enquiry forecast today would be inventing it.

⭐ Both of those become real the moment you tell us what a typical job pays, which is the first thing we will ask.
