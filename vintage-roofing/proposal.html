In the order we would actually do it

Sequenced, and the order is the argument. The free things and the credibility things come first, because spending money to send commercial buyers to a site that says you fit roofs on houses would waste it.

⭐ Before anything else, and it costs nothing
  • Settle the trading history. Five years or twenty. Pick the true one and make the whole site say it
  • Put Checkatrade on your own website. Badge, rating and a link. You already earned it
  • Put up any accreditation you hold. If you hold none, say so on the call and it becomes the first real decision rather than an oversight
  • Find out whether that Google Ads tag is still spending. Ten minutes, and it may be costing you money every month for a campaign you forgot about
🔴 1. A commercial page, because you currently have no way to be chosen for the work you want

One page that says plainly what you take on, what sizes, what sectors, and who to ring. Right now a commercial buyer has nowhere on your site to land.

🔴 2. Then the service and town pages

Four towns, Swindon, Bath, Bristol and Chippenham, multiplied by the work you want. Total Cladding and Roofing has four commercial pages in Swindon alone. This is the part of the gap that is pure mechanics rather than reputation.

3. Local business markup and a proper email address

Address, hours and service area in a form Google reads. And an address on the domain you already own, rather than Gmail.

4. Only then, advertising, and only if you want it

⚠ We are not pitching you a campaign today. You told our team you have been burnt before, and the honest position is that advertising into the site as it stands would repeat that. ⭐ The tracking is already installed, which is more than most prospects start with, so when it is worth turning on you are closer than you think.

⚠ Nothing in this pack is priced, and nothing here promises a number of jobs. What a job pays you and how many you could carry is the first thing we need from you, because every judgement about what is worth doing depends on it.
