What we measured

Read automatically from vintageroofingservices.co.uk on the morning of 7 October 2026. Seven pages, 818 words in total. Nothing below is an opinion about your roofing. It is what a buyer, or Google, can see.

🔴🔴 The headline, and everything else is downstream of it

You want large jobs. Your website sells small ones.

The services it lists are new roofs, roof repairs, roof line care and general maintenance, and the copy is written to a homeowner throughout: "whether you're building a new home, adding an extension, or replacing an old roof".

🔴 There is no commercial page, no industrial page, and no mention of commercial or industrial work anywhere on the site.

⚠ And both channels you named to our team, Checkatrade and Google, are where homeowners look. A facilities manager, a main contractor and a commercial surveyor do not use Checkatrade.

🔴 Nothing on the site says you are allowed to do large work

We searched 244KB of your own pages for NFRC, CHAS, Constructionline, SafeContractor, CompetentRoofer and TrustMark. None of them appears.

⚠ For domestic work that costs you very little. For commercial and contract work it is the gate: many tenders will not accept a contractor without one, and a surveyor checking you before instructing will look for exactly these.

⭐ If you hold any of them and simply have not displayed them, tell us on the call. That single fact changes the competitor page more than anything else in this pack.

⚠ Your own proof is missing from your own site

You told our team you get enquiries through Checkatrade. Your website never mentions Checkatrade once.

⭐ So someone who finds you on Checkatrade sees your reviews, and someone who finds you on Google sees none of them. The same firm looks substantially weaker through one door than the other, and the weaker door is the one you own.

⚠ Two different answers to how long you have been trading

One part of your site says over 5 years of experience. Another says over 20 years of industry experience.

⚠ We do not know which is correct and have not assumed. But both are published at once, and for a buyer weighing a large contract that is the sort of thing that quietly ends a shortlist.

⚠ Someone has already been here

Your site carries Google Analytics 4 and a Google Ads tag, and no Meta pixel and no Tag Manager.

⭐⭐ You told our team you have been burnt by marketing people before. The Ads tag is the fingerprint of that. ⚠ We cannot tell from outside whether it is still spending money, and we are not going to pretend we can. It is the first thing worth checking today.

⚠ A Gmail address on a firm that owns its domain

The only email published anywhere on the site is vintageroofingservices@gmail.com.

⚠ Harmless for a homeowner. For a commercial buyer it reads as a smaller operation than 10 to 15 men, and you already own the domain the address could sit on.

⚠ The smaller technical ones, for completeness
  • No local business markup. Your address, hours and service area are not published in a form Google reads, so a search for a roofer in Chippenham has less to go on
  • Seven pages and 818 words in total across Swindon, Bath, Bristol and Chippenham. That is not enough surface to rank for the range of work you do across four towns
  • Two enquiry forms and four tap-to-call links, which is the one part of this that is genuinely fine
  • No careers page, with a 10 to 15 man team you may want to grow
  • Reviews, a team section and a gallery are all present. The raw materials are here. They are pointed at the wrong customer

⚠ What we have NOT assessed: your site's speed, how many reviews you hold on Checkatrade or Google, your social activity, your insurance level, or the quality of your roofing. None of those are scored, because we did not measure them.
