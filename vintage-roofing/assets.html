⭐⭐ The one sentence this whole pack comes down to

You told our team you can take on as much large work as you can get, and that you have 10 to 15 men doing 1 to 3 jobs a week.

🔴 Your website sells only small domestic jobs. New roofs, repairs, roofline care, general maintenance. There is no commercial or industrial work mentioned anywhere on it, and both the channels you named, Checkatrade and Google, are places homeowners look.

⚠ So the large work you want is not being turned down. It is not arriving, and nothing you currently have is pointed at it.

⚠ Three things we will ask you to correct us on
  • Your site gives two different answers for how long you have been trading. One section says over 5 years of experience and another says over 20 years of industry experience. We do not know which is right, and a commercial buyer reading both will not either
  • You told our team you get enquiries through Checkatrade. Your website never mentions Checkatrade once. Your strongest piece of outside proof is invisible to anyone who lands on your site directly
  • We found no accreditation of any kind on your site. Not NFRC, CHAS, Constructionline, SafeContractor, CompetentRoofer or TrustMark. If you hold one and simply have not put it up, that is a ten minute fix and it changes the competitor page considerably
⚠ Two things before the call that would make it far more useful
  • What does a large job actually pay you? You told our team the average varies too much to give a number. A range for the big ones is enough
  • Is anything still running on Google Ads? We found a Google Ads tag live on your site. We cannot tell from outside whether it is spending. If a previous agency left it behind, that is worth knowing today
