🔴🔴 The one to actually look at is Total Cladding and Roofing

They are in Swindon, the same as you. And they do not have one commercial page. They have a page for Commercial Roofing Company Swindon, another for Commercial Roof Repairs Swindon, another for Industrial and Commercial Roofing Swindon, and another for Commercial Roofing Contractors Bristol.

⭐⭐ That is four pages aimed at four separate searches, in your own town, for the exact work you told us you want more of. When a Swindon facilities manager searches any of those phrases, they are the answer and you are not on the page.

⚠ They also publish CHAS, SafeContractor and NFRC together, which is precisely the set a tender asks for.

⚠ Where you are genuinely stronger, and it is worth saying
  • You have 10 to 15 men. Several of these are specialist industrial outfits; a directly employed crew of that size is a real advantage on anything with a programme attached
  • You already do domestic well enough to get work from Checkatrade. That is a live reputation, which most pure commercial contractors do not have
  • You have reviews, a team page and a gallery already on the site. The raw material for the commercial argument exists. It is pointed at homeowners
