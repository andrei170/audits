In the order we would do it

The order is the argument. Advertising a site whose form is not connected wastes every pound. So the repairs come first, and they are quick.

⭐ 1. Connect the enquiry form: done

Both forms are now wired to a form service (re-read 20 October). Next: send one test together, and have each enquiry also text you so none sits unseen in an inbox.

🔴 2. Finish the site

The tab title, your real prices in the slider, your real phone number for Google, one main heading per page, a sitemap, and the logos sorted either way.

🔴 3. Measure it

Count every call, every form and every quiz answer, and where each person came from.

And a simple CRM: every quote followed up, and every customer’s retest date stored with a reminder before it is due.

4. Proof a buyer can check

Your qualification and insurance on the first screen, and a review from each client you already have. You have the customers. The site needs their words.

5. A page for each town and each kind of customer

Sunderland, Newcastle, Durham, Blyth. Landlords, schools, care homes. Each one is a search somebody is making today, and the kind of page AI answers quote. See them in the demo.

6. Then advertising, measured

Google search ads in your own account for the customer you most want, sent to the page written for them. When it stops, everything built stays with you. Why Google first.

⚠ Nothing here forecasts a number of jobs. Who you most want as a customer, and how many sites you can take on, are the first things we need.
