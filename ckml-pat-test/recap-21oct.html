⭐ What CKML already has going for it
  • 14 years in business and a strong list of repeat clients
  • £5M public liability insurance and fully DBS-checked engineers, which schools, care homes and landlords look for
  • Room for more work: the team can take on more jobs than come in today
  • The website form now works, thanks to your IT person
🎯 The goal Neil set

Grow turnover by around 20%, and over time make CKML a business that runs without depending on one person, so it could be sold one day.

⚠ What is holding growth back
  • New customers cannot find CKML. We asked an AI search tool the questions a buyer would ask, like “PAT testing in Blyth”. CKML came up in none of them, while competitors did
  • Why: AI tools and Google lean on review sites and directories (Trustpilot, Yell, Bark, FreeIndex). In 14 years CKML has not collected reviews, so there is nothing for them to find
  • The website is one page, with no sitemap and no pages for schools, landlords, care homes or the towns you cover. The phone number given to Google is still a placeholder
  • Quotes, re-tests and review requests are all done by hand, so some slip through
🛠 The plan, in order
  1. Fix the follow-up first: the CRM. Every enquiry, call and text in one place. Quotes followed up automatically, re-test reminders sent before each test is due, a review request after every job, and one shared calendar so nobody double-books. It also means the business runs on a system rather than on memory, which is what makes it sellable
  2. Build the website properly. A page each for businesses, landlords, schools and care homes, a page for each area you cover (Blyth, Newcastle, Sunderland, Durham, Northumberland and more), and an About page with the team, insurance and DBS checks. Neil suggested more photos of the engineers and equipment, and client logos instead of names
  3. Then bring in new enquiries with Google Ads. Each search, like “PAT testing for schools Sunderland”, goes to the page written for it. DRA is already running Google ads, which shows buyers are searching
❓ Worth thinking about before Monday
  • Who answers new enquiries day to day, and who should get the alerts?
  • Which customers do you want more of: offices, landlords, schools, care homes or multi-site contracts?
  • Which client logos and photos are you happy for us to use on the site?
  • Any questions from the FAQ videos?
