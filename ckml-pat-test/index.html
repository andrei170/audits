What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

The website reads well and looks modern. But it was published before it was finished, and the unfinished parts are the ones that bring in work.

🔴🔴 1. The enquiry forms go nowhere

There are two forms. In the page code, both are set to send to "YOUR-EMAIL@example.com", the placeholder a site builder leaves for the owner's address.

🔴 As it stands, somebody who fills in "Get your free, no-obligation quote" and presses Send is not reaching you. And nothing tells them, or you.

⚠ We read this from the page code and did not send a test. A test enquiry takes one minute and settles it.

🔴 2. Four more placeholders a customer or Google can see
  • The browser tab reads "CKML PAT Testing - Full Site Preview". That is what Google shows as your headline
  • The price slider says, on the page: "Example pricing shown for illustration - replace with CKML's real rates before publishing"
  • The phone number given to Google is "+44-XXXX-XXXXXX"
  • The client list says logos are being added "as we confirm brand usage permissions". ⚠ Six logo files are already in the page. Worth making sure permission is in place for each
🔴 3. Nothing is being counted

No Google Analytics, no Google Ads tag, no Meta tag. So nobody can say how many people have visited, rung, or tried the form.

⚠ 4. One page has to do the work of twenty

Everything is on a single page. You list 12 towns and five kinds of customer, but there is no page for "PAT testing Sunderland" or "PAT testing for landlords", which is how people search.

⚠ The largest local competitor has a page for each area, including one for Blyth, your own town.

⚠ 5. Big names, but no proof a stranger can check

Subway, Virgin Media, Best Western, Crowne Plaza, the NHS and Siemens is a strong list. But there is no review, no testimonial, and no qualification or insurance named anywhere.

⚠ A facilities manager choosing a tester has to show their insurer who they used. They look for the certificate before the price.

⭐ What is already strong
  • The wording. Clear, honest, and it explains the law without scaring anybody
  • Tap-to-call works, in four places
  • Free socket testing and microwave leakage checks with a visit. A real reason to choose you, stated plainly
  • A named, local, two-person contact: Clair and Neil, on your own email domain
  • The "Do you need PAT testing?" quiz is a good idea that most competitors do not have

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, social pages, and where you rank in search. We have not guessed.
