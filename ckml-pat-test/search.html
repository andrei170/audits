🔴🔴 The headline: when a buyer asks, CKML does not come up

On 20 October we asked an AI search tool six questions a facilities manager, school business manager or landlord might ask. CKML was named in none of them, not even when we asked for CKML by name or typed in your web address.

More buyers now ask ChatGPT or read Google’s AI summary before they click anything. If the AI answer does not know you exist, you are not on the shortlist.

⚠ Who it recommends in your own town

For Blyth and for Northumberland it named LMTS PAT Testing, from a directory profile it says was last updated in April 2023. When we opened that profile on 20 October it no longer exists (the directory returns “gone”). So AI tools are sending Blyth buyers to a listing that has been taken down, and not to you.

Why the AI tools cannot see you
  • They read directories and review sites (FreeIndex, Trustpilot, Yell, Bark) and well-built pages. We found no CKML listing with reviews
  • Your site is one page, titled “Full Site Preview”, with six main headings where one is expected
  • No sitemap to tell Google what pages exist, and the phone number given to Google is XXXX
  • No page answers a specific question, like “PAT testing for schools in Sunderland” or “how often should a care home PAT test?”
⭐ What we would do about it
  • A page for each kind of customer and each town, as in the demo site, each answering the questions buyers actually ask
  • Fix the basics: a real title, one main heading per page, a sitemap, and your real phone number in the details Google reads
  • Get listed where AI tools look: Google Business Profile, FreeIndex, Yell and Bark, with the same name, address and phone on each
  • Ask your repeat customers for a Google review. You keep 90 to 95% of them, so they are the quickest proof there is
  • Re-ask the same six questions every month and show you the answers, so you can see whether CKML starts appearing
Advertising
You: no ads running, as you told us

Google Ads Transparency Centre (United Kingdom), searched for ckmlpattest.co.uk on 20 October: 0 ads. Meta Ad Library (United Kingdom), searched for “CKML”: two results, neither of them yours.

🔴 DRA is paying for Google

The same Google library shows 7 ads pointing at draelectricals.co.uk from DRA Pat Testing Limited, a verified advertiser. That fits what you told us: DRA advertises, and it is busy enough to offer you its overflow work.

⚠ Facebook is the wrong place to start

Meta’s library shows about 57 active UK ads using the phrase “PAT testing”. The ones at the top are training courses selling to people who want to learn PAT testing, not testers selling to businesses. Buyers of PAT testing search for it on Google when a test is due.

⭐ How we would advertise, once the site is ready
  • Google search ads only, to start, on searches like “PAT testing Sunderland”, “PAT testing for schools” and “landlord PAT test”
  • Each ad goes to the page written for that search, not the home page
  • Every call and form counted, so you can see which searches and towns bring work
  • Sell what is included, not the price: free socket and microwave checks on the same visit, a local engineer, no call centre
  • In your own Google account, so everything built stays with you

⚠ We have not estimated cost per enquiry or a number of jobs. We would set a small test budget, measure it, and decide from real numbers.
