Our first call, Tuesday 13 October

Everything we covered on Tuesday, in one place, before our next call. If Clair is joining, this is the page to send her first.

⭐⭐ What you told me
  • CKML is your second business. Clair runs the day to day, and you put the schedules together
  • About 14 years trading, grown by word of mouth, with no advertising at all
  • Commercial is the main work: offices, schools, colleges, multi-storey head offices. Regular work comes from facilities companies and the Virgin Media contract
  • About 10 to 12 jobs a month, anything from 15 minutes to four weeks. The minimum call-out is £65, for up to about 60 items
  • You could take on 30 to 40 jobs a month, or 60 to 70 of a typical job of around 125 tests if they are in the same area
  • Your price is around 85p to £1 an item: cheaper than DRA, dearer than ATCO
  • 90 to 95% of the work is repeat customers, and sales have been flat for about three years
  • There is no CRM. In your words: “We basically respond to them as they come in and do the quotes. If we get the job, great. If not, then that’s it.”
  • The goal: grow it, and possibly make it a saleable asset
  • You have been thinking about adding fire safety and emergency lighting
What I showed you
  • The graded scorecard: 40 out of 100 on the day (43 now the form is fixed). Forms not connected, nothing measuring visitors or enquiries, no accreditations or insurance shown, the price marked as an example, and one long page instead of a page per town and per customer
  • Five competitors: DRA, North East PAT Testing, ATCO, Enlighten and HT PAT Testing. You knew three of them
  • Where you are stronger: free socket testing and microwave leakage checks included, a local engineer, and a client list with real names
  • The order of work: fix the form, finish the site, measure everything, add proof and a page per town, and only then advertise
⭐ Fixed since: the enquiry form

You said your IT person had fixed the form and a test came through. On 20 October we re-read ckmlpattest.co.uk and both forms are now wired to a form service with real keys. On 15 October they still pointed at a placeholder, so this is recent.
