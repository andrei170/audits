🔴🔴 Finding 1 · The gap is proof, not quality

You keep 90 to 95% of your customers year after year. That is the strongest evidence of good work in this table, and none of it is visible to a new buyer. DRA shows 147 reviews on one directory alone. We found none for CKML.

Your repeat customers are the quickest fix: ask them for a review.

⚠ Finding 2 · You’re in the middle on price, so sell what’s included

ATCO leads with 60p. DRA charges more and wins anyway, on reviews and on being found. You can’t win on price and shouldn’t try.

⭐ What none of them say: free socket testing and microwave leakage checks, in the same visit, from a local engineer. That is your headline.

⭐⭐ Finding 3 · The demand is there

DRA is paying for Google Ads (the Google ad library showed 7 of its ads on 20 October), posting on LinkedIn, and has asked you to take its overflow work. A firm doesn’t keep paying to advertise if it isn’t working, and it doesn’t offer out work if it has nothing to do.

So the North East has more PAT testing than DRA can handle, and you have spare capacity.

Finding 4 · One visit beats three suppliers

HT PAT Testing bundles PAT, fire safety and emergency lighting. For a facilities manager, that is one booking, one engineer and one invoice instead of three. If you add fire safety and emergency lighting, sell it the same way.

The short version: you’re not losing on quality or price. You’re losing on being seen and being provable, and those are the cheapest things to fix.
