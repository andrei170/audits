What this means for you
Your work beats most of these. Your web presence does not.
  • The firms that show up have a page per service, and some have a page per town. S Peart, ten minutes from you, has separate pages for barn conversions, new builds and stonework, which is exactly your work.
  • None of the six is running Google Ads on its own web address (0 ads in Google’s public records for each). For a builder doing new builds and conversions, that is an open lane.
  • Three of the six show trade bodies on their site (FMB, CHAS, TrustMark, CSCS). You are an FMB member and do not show it.
  • Two of the six have a commercial page (Clinton and County Durham Building and Joinery). You do commercial and agricultural work and your site does not mention it.
  • ⭐ None of them shows a review score like your 10/10 from 49. That is your strongest card, and it is currently sitting on Checkatrade instead of your own site.
