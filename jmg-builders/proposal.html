What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ The aim is not more of any job. It is that the people who look you up find a builder as good online as your reviews say you are, and that every enquiry is answered, sorted and followed up without you stopping work.

1. Fix the two dead links · Week 1, free

Point the Website button on Checkatrade and the FMB at your real site, and decide what happens to jmgbuilders.co.uk and jmgbuilders.uk (forward both to the live site if they are still yours).

⭐ We would do this with you on the call if you like. It costs nothing and it is the biggest single fix.

2. One phone number, one address, everywhere · Week 1

Agree which mobile and which web address customers should use, and make every listing match.

Customers, and search engines, trust a business whose details agree.

3. Google Business Profile and reviews · Weeks 1 to 2

Check or set up your Google profile with your service area, services and photos. Then a review request by text after every job, so your Checkatrade-standard reviews also appear on Google.

This decides the map results, and it is what AI answers quote.

4. A site that shows what you build · Weeks 1 to 3

A page for each kind of work you want more of, town pages, your names, your FMB membership, your 10/10 from 49 on the home page, project write-ups with your photos, and an estimate form that asks the job, postcode, stage and timescale and takes drawings.

⚠ If you want to keep your current web company, this becomes a checklist for them. The sample site shows every item.

5. Answer and follow up without picking up · Weeks 2 to 3

Missed-call text-back within seconds, an instant reply to every form, site-visit reminders, and follow-up on every quote until it is a yes or a no.

⭐ This is the part that saves time when you and Matthew are on site.

6. Measurement · Week 3

Analytics, conversion tracking, call tracking and a source on every enquiry.

So you can see where your best jobs actually come from.

7. Test Google Ads, small and tight · When you have capacity

Only the work you choose, only the area you choose, sent to the page about that work, with a budget sized to how busy you are.

⚠ Only once tracking is in, and only if you want more of a particular kind of job.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your job values, how far ahead you are booked or where your work comes from. Anyone giving you a forecast before seeing those would be inventing it.
