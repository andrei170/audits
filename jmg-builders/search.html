What stands out
  • Directories own the top of almost every search. Your Checkatrade profile does some of the work for you, which is why its Website button matters so much.
  • The builders who do appear have a page for the thing that was searched: garage conversions, Teesdale, Barnard Castle.
  • On your own name, the blank jmgbuilders.uk page ranks and your real site does not.

⭐ An open lane

None of the local builders we checked is running Google Ads on its own web address. The paid spots for building searches round here are mostly taken by directories and national firms. A small, tightly targeted campaign for the work you want, in the area you want, sending people to a page about that work, would not be competing with your local rivals at all.

⚠ That is a reason to test, not a promise of results, and it only makes sense once the site can track enquiries.

How we would run it, if you want it
  1. Only the jobs you choose, each with its own page (for example new builds, barn conversions, extensions).
  2. Only the area you choose, set to people who are in it.
  3. Negative keywords from day one: jobs, apprentice, DIY, cheap, free, plans only.
  4. Every call and form tracked, so you can see what each enquiry cost and which turned into work.
  5. A budget sized to your capacity, reviewed weekly. No prices on this page; that is a conversation.
