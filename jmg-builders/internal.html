Objections
“I get all my work from word of mouth / I’m busy enough.”Agree: the reviews prove the work. Then: word of mouth still ends with the friend looking him up. Today that look-up lands on a parked page from Checkatrade and a blank page from the FMB. Fixing that protects the word of mouth he already has. If he is booked solid, the pitch is the follow-up system and choosing better jobs, not more jobs.
“I’ve already got a website / a web guy.”Do not knock New World. The site is decent and was updated in August. The gaps are specific and fixable: service pages switched off, no tracking, no names, no FMB badge, no review score, form without postcode or job type. Offer the sample site as a checklist for them. Our value is the system around it: listings, Google profile, reviews, text-back, follow-up, tracked ads.
“Checkatrade gets me enough.”Keep it. But its Website button points at a parked domain, and only 2 of his 49 reviews are from the last 12 months. Ask every customer for a Google review too.
“Ads don’t work for builders / for big jobs.”Agree a cold ad does not close a new build. Then: none of the six local competitors runs Google Ads, so the paid spots are directories and nationals. A small test on barn conversions or new builds, sent to a page about exactly that, with follow-up behind it, is low risk. And it comes after the free fixes, not before.
“I need to talk to Matthew.”Expected: two equal directors. Offer a second short call with both, and leave the hub link (assets.html) for Matthew to read first.
“No time.”What we need from him: 30 minutes, his best photos and a line on each, which number and which domain, and access to the listings. Everything else is done for him.
