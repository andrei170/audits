Questions for the call
1. What kind of job do you want more of?New builds, extensions, barn conversions, agricultural work? And which jobs would you rather do less of?
2. Where does most of your work come from today?Checkatrade, the FMB, repeat customers, word of mouth? Your site says most of it is recommendations.
3. Which web address do you think of as yours?We found three: jmgbuilders.co.uk, jmgbuilders.uk and jmgbuildersnorthern.co.uk. Only the last one works.
4. Which mobile should customers ring?Your listings show 07847 953069, 07429 945574 and 07841 352679.
5. Who looks after your website now, and are you happy with it?We would rather work with them than start again, if that suits you.
6. How far ahead are you booked?So we only suggest something that fits your capacity.
7. When you are on site and the phone rings, what happens?Voicemail, Matthew, call back at night?
8. Do you have a Google Business Profile, and do you ask customers for Google reviews?We could not check Google Maps ourselves.
9. Are you happy for your FMB membership, and TrustMark and CSCS if current, to be shown on your site?Checkatrade lists all three; your site shows none.
10. Do you have photos and a few words on your favourite jobs?A barn conversion or a stone house written up properly is worth more than 70 uncaptioned photos.
Bigger jobs: a few questions
11. What were your biggest jobs in the last year or two, and who were they for?A farm, an estate, a self-builder, an insurer?
12. How did those big jobs find you?So we add to the route that works rather than replace it.
13. From first enquiry to a yes, how long does a new build or a farm building usually take?And how many quotes are out with someone deciding right now?
14. What happens after you send a big quote?Who follows it up, and when.
15. What do farmers, estates or insurers ask for before they say yes?Insurance, risk assessments, references? Whatever they ask for can be on the site before they have to ask.
