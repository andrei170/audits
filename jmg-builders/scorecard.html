Digital Presence Scorecard
F
34 / 100
1 of 18 checks passed. 11 failed.
The work is excellent and the reviews prove it: 10/10 from 49. But the two biggest directories you are on send people to web addresses that do not work, and your real site does not show up when someone searches for a builder.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (25 Oct 2026)
FWebsite links on your listings
Checkatrade’s “Website” button goes to jmgbuilders.co.uk, which is a parked domain page. The FMB’s “Visit website” goes to jmgbuilders.uk, which shows a blank hosting page saying “please upload your website”. Neither listing links to your real site, jmgbuildersnorthern.co.uk.
DEnquiry form
⭐ A short step-by-step quote form on every page (name, phone, email, how to contact you, a line about the project). It does not ask the postcode, the type of job from a list, the stage (plans or not) or the timescale, and drawings or photos cannot be attached. The phone box only accepts digits, so a number typed with spaces or +44 is refused.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ 07847 953069 is a tap-to-call link at the top of all 7 pages.
FPages and service depth
7 pages in total (per your own sitemap). New builds, extensions, porches, garage conversions and groundwork share one services page. The menu links to separate service pages are switched off in the page code. No page for barn conversions, new builds, stonework or agricultural work, and no page for any town.
DPage titles and descriptions
The home page title and description are good (“Building Services in County Durham”). The other six pages all share one description: “Building company from County Durham.”
CHeadings
One H1 per page, which is right. The home H1 names County Durham but no service; the contact page H1 is “Have any questions?”.
FLocal business markup
No structured data on any page, so search engines and AI tools have to guess your services, area and hours.
FMeasurement
No Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads tag or Meta pixel on any of the 7 pages. Nobody can see how many people visit, where from, or how many fill in the form.
FAccreditations shown
You are an FMB member (on the FMB’s own site) and Checkatrade also lists TrustMark and CSCS. Your website shows none of them. The only badge-type link is to Checkatrade in the footer.
CReviews
⭐ 10/10 from 49 verified reviews on Checkatrade, several from repeat customers. Your site shows 5 of the review texts on a testimonials page, but the 10/10 and the 49 are not shown anywhere on your site.
DProof of work
⭐ A 70-photo gallery on your site and 145 photos on Checkatrade, including stone new builds and a barn conversion. No project has a write-up (where, what, how long), and every photo is captioned with a generic line.
FWho you are
Your site never names you or Matthew. The “family run, two brothers” story that sells a builder is only on Checkatrade and the FMB.
FShowing up in local searches
Your site did not appear in any of 8 local searches (builders Darlington, builders Barnard Castle, house extension Darlington, and five more). Your Checkatrade profile was 6th for “builders Darlington”.
FBrand search
Searching “JMG Builders Darlington”: Checkatrade first, Yell second, your real site not in the top 10, and the blank jmgbuilders.uk page 9th. “JMG Builders Northern” finds your site 2nd.
FProfessional email
jmgbuilders@yahoo.co.uk. An address on your own domain looks more established to bigger clients.
FContact details that match
Three different mobiles across your listings (07847 953069 on your site and the FMB, 07429 945574 on Checkatrade, 07841 352679 as an alternative on your site) and three different web addresses.
DSite upkeep
The sitemap was updated in August 2026 (good). But the privacy page still reads “Last updated : #date”, a template line never filled in, and the main page tells Google its address is the older http version.
FReady for ads
No ads running for any of your web addresses (checked in Google’s public ad records), which is fine. But with no tracking and no page per service, an ad today would land on a general page and nobody could tell which clicks became enquiries.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about the web presence, not about your work. The thing that takes years to earn, 49 customers saying 10/10, is already done.

After the build: the directory links, form, pages, descriptions, markup, measurement, accreditations, review score, project write-ups, who you are, contact details and upkeep rows are all fixed by the build itself. Search positions and Google reviews take weeks of steady effort, so we do not project those.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google Business Profile and Google reviews, Facebook and Instagram, and anything inside an ads account.
