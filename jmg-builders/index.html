⚠ The most important finding in this pack, and it is free to fix

Your Checkatrade profile is where many customers check you out, and the FMB’s directory is where people look for a vetted builder. Both send anyone who clicks “Website” to a page that does not work. A homeowner comparing three builders clicks through, lands on a parked page, and is left wondering if you are still trading. Updating both links to your real site takes minutes.

Your live site, page by page
jmgbuildersnorthern.co.uk: 7 pages, last updated August 2026
  • Pages: home, services, testimonials, recent projects, contact, FAQ, privacy. New builds, extensions, porches, garage conversions and groundwork are listed together on one services page.
  • Service pages are switched off: the menu links to them exist in the page code but are hidden, and lead nowhere.
  • The form is a short three-step quote request. It asks name, phone, email, how to contact you and a line about the project. No postcode, no job type list, no stage or timescale, and no way to attach drawings.
  • No tracking: no Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads tag or Meta pixel on any page.
  • No structured data telling search engines your services, area and hours.
  • Your name and Matthew’s are not on the site, and neither are the FMB, TrustMark or CSCS.
  • ⭐ Good: tap-to-call on every page, a clear three-step “how we work”, 5 real reviews, a 70-photo gallery, an FAQ with your hours (Monday to Friday 9 to 5, Saturday 9 to 3) and your area.
  • Small things: the privacy page still reads “Last updated : #date”, and six of seven pages share one description in Google.
Search and AI answers

We ran eight searches a homeowner or self-builder would type. Your site did not come up in any of them. Full results are on the search page.

Why you do not show up
  • No page per service. A search for “barn conversion builders County Durham” is matched to a page about barn conversions. You do not have one; a competitor ten minutes away does.
  • No town named beyond County Durham and Barnard Castle.
  • The directories point at dead addresses, so the links that should be sending people and trust to your site are not.
  • No markup for search engines and AI answers to read.
⚠ What we could not measure
  • Google itself, Google Maps and your Google Business Profile: our tools cannot read them, so we do not know if you have a profile or Google reviews. Searches were run on DuckDuckGo with the UK region set.
  • Facebook and Instagram ads: Meta’s ad library refused our reader. We found no Facebook page in search and your site links to none.
  • Page speed: Google’s speed test returned no data, so it is not graded.
  • Your company accounts are micro accounts, which do not show turnover.
