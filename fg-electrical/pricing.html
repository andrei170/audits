Before the numbers, the thing I am not selling you

I am not going to put an advertising retainer on this page.

You told me you keep £3,000 to £4,000 a month. My advertising packages start at £3,000 a month, and because EV and electrical are expensive keywords, you would properly belong one tier above that.

🔴🔴 So the honest position is that my own ads product would cost you more than your business currently keeps. You have already paid £6,000 to somebody who sold you something that could not work at the size you bought it. I am not going to be the second one.

⭐ Everything below is one-time. There is a monthly option further down, but it is deliberately at the bottom and it is deliberately small, and I would rather you did not take it yet.

⚠⚠ What “free month of ads” does and does not mean

It means I do not charge you my £500 management fee for the first month.

🔴 It does not mean free advertising. The money that goes to Google or Meta is still yours, it comes off your own card, on your own account, and you choose how much it is. I am waiving my fee, not buying your clicks, and I would rather say that in writing now than have you discover it on an invoice.

⭐ You also set the spend, and you can set it at nothing. If after the build you decide not to advertise at all, the free month simply goes unused and you have lost nothing.

⚠ Neither option is a new website. Your site has seven enquiry forms and twenty-four tap-to-call links across twenty pages, which is the best-converting setup of anything I audited last week. North Dime did a good job and I am not going to sell you a rebuild you do not need. Both options above are additions to what you already have.

What neither option includes, so there is no surprise
  • No advertising spend, and no advertising management. See above.
  • No guarantee of a number of jobs, enquiries, or a ranking position. You have been given that promise before and it cost you six thousand pounds. Anybody who gives you a figure after two phone calls is inventing it, including me.
  • No monthly fee, no contract, and no notice period. It is a one-time piece of work.
  • No ownership of anything. The tracking, the tags and the pages are yours, on your domain, with your web company. If you never speak to me again you keep all of it.

⭐⭐ The reason to split the fee from the spend at your size is simple arithmetic. If I charged you £500 and spent half of it on ads, that is £250 of media a month. On EV charger keywords that buys almost nothing, and I would be taking £250 a month to administer a budget too small to work. Separating them means your money goes to the platform and you can see it, and I am paid for the work rather than for holding your card.

How you should judge whichever you pick

Four weeks after the Foundation goes in, you should be able to answer three questions you cannot answer today:

  • Where do the high-ticket enquiries actually come from? You believe it is the website. You will know.
  • How many people reach the site and leave without contacting you? That number is the size of the leak.
  • Which pages do your best enquiries land on first? Because that tells us which seventy pages are worth having, rather than guessing.

⭐ If after four weeks it turns out the website produces almost nothing and it is all word of mouth, that is a useful answer too - and it would mean the right next move is the commercial testing and the new-build estates, which are phone calls and relationships rather than anything I would charge you a monthly fee for.
