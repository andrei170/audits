🔴🔴 The price floor, and why it argues AGAINST chasing domestic work

Here is what is publicly advertised in your market right now:

  • £89 for an EICR, from eicrcertificatebelfast.co.uk
  • £99 from electrical-certificate.co.uk
  • £180 plus VAT from Glow Electric, who are NICEIC qualified
  • From £250 for a commercial property EICR

🔴 There is a race to the bottom happening on domestic certificates and you will not win it. Somebody is doing an EICR for £89.

⭐⭐ Which is exactly why what you said on the call was right. Your words were that you do not want to be "that electrician that you call for if you want one light fitting". The numbers back you up: the domestic end is being fought over at £89, and the commercial end starts at £250 and goes up with the size of the building.

⚠ So the commercial page, the landlord portfolio page and the town pages are not nice-to-haves. They are how you get out of the £89 fight.

⚠ Where you are genuinely stronger, and it is worth Natalia hearing it
  • You are NICEIC approved and you do say so. Several of the cheapest advertisers do not name any registration at all
  • You do the full range. EICR, commercial, landlord, EV charging, rewires, new build. Most of the firms above sell one certificate. Kane Electrical is the only one we found doing EICR and EV charging together, which is your combination
  • Around 300 EV installs. That is a genuine specialism and your current site barely mentions it
  • You have an office. Natalia takes every website enquiry before it reaches Jonny. ⭐ Most firms this size do not have anyone answering, and that is worth more for reaching people than any of this
