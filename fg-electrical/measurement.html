The problem, stated plainly

You told our team the EV charger flow feels like it is slowing. Nothing on your website can confirm or deny that, because there is no analytics, no tag manager, no advertising tag and no pixel on it.

⭐⭐ So there are three completely different things that could be true right now, and no way to tell them apart:

  • Fewer people are finding the EV page.
  • The same number are finding it and fewer are getting in touch.
  • Nothing has changed at all and a slow fortnight felt like a trend.

🔴 Each one has a different fix, and two of them cost nothing. Below is the shape of what you would be looking at instead.

What we are deliberately not selling you

⭐⭐ We are not going to suggest rebuilding your website, and that is deliberate. Twenty pages, seven forms, twenty-four tap-to-call links, NICEIC shown, a team page, commercial and landlord work both named. It already does the job. Rebuilding it would be us selling you something rather than fixing what is actually wrong.

What it takes

Analytics and a tag manager installed, then a conversion event placed on every enquiry form and every tap-to-call link on the site, then an advertising tag sitting ready but switched off.
