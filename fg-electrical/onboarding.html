Step 1 · about 10 minutes

Your calendars

  • Each of you signs in to your own calendar, live on the call. We never see your password
  • Which do you each use: Outlook, Google or Apple?
  • Once connected, bookings show in the calendar you already use, and the system knows when you are busy
Step 2 · about 15 minutes

Your phones

  • Call order: Natalia first, then Jonny after a set number of seconds, or both phones at once. You choose
  • Your working hours, so calls stop ringing when you are closed
  • What a caller hears or receives out of hours: a message, a text back, or both
  • A holiday switch Natalia can turn on herself, for holidays and days you are not working
  • Your business number: which provider it is with, so calls can be passed to the new system
Step 3 · about 10 minutes

Your customers

  • Your customer list, in whatever form you have it. A spreadsheet, your phone contacts or an export from another system are all fine
  • We bring it across so every customer is in one place, and the jobs show as a pipeline you can follow
  • Bulk emails: before the first one goes out, we set up your own sending address, so emails come from your domain and land in inboxes, not spam
Step 4 · about 10 minutes

Your domain

  • Who registered fgelectricalltd.co.uk? For example GoDaddy, 123 Reg or Namecheap, usually whoever put the current site live
  • If it sits in their account, ask them to move it into your name. It is your domain and you should hold it
  • 🔴 This is the one thing that can hold the website up. The new site cannot go live on your address without it
  • Who provides your business email (for example Microsoft 365 or Google). No password needed. We only change the website records, never your email
Step 5 · about 10 minutes

The website: what we still need

  • Photos of finished jobs. Phone photos are fine, the more the better
  • A photo of Jonny for the About page
  • Your Google reviews, or a yes so we can pull them in
  • Your insurance cover level
  • PAT testing: do you want it on the commercial page?
  • “From” prices: do you want them shown, on which services, and what they are?
  • Your area figures (EV chargers and EICRs in Banbridge, over 300 EICRs in Belfast): are they right?
  • County Louth: are you registered with Safe Electric for work in the South?
  • The Lighthouse charity: a few lines in Jonny’s own words
  • Google Business Profile: add andrei@ag1consulting.co as a Manager
Step 6 · about 5 minutes

What happens next

  • We set up the phone and email system first, then build the live website, then switch on tracking
  • You see everything before it goes live, and nothing goes live until you say yes
  • Anything you cannot answer today is fine. Send it when you have it, and I will chase the rest
Your support once you are live
  • First 2 months: a Zoom call with Andrei is available every week
  • From month 3: one every two weeks
  • You book them yourselves, whenever suits you, and only when you need one: edits, help with the setup, or anything that comes up
