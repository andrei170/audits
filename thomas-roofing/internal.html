Positioning in one line

“Your site is a good base. Right now it sends people who check you out to your competitors. Fix that for free, then get you found in the towns near you, and only spend on ads once you can see what they bring.” Trust is the sale here: lead with the free fix, ownership and transparency.

Ask in this order
1. What exactly burned him before? (Who, what promised, what delivered, contract length, did he own his site and accounts?) Let him tell it fully. That story is the brief for this call.
2. Does he still have a TrustATrader profile? The link on his site redirects to the Prescot category list, so it has likely lapsed or been renamed. If lapsed: did he stop paying? How many reviews did it have?
3. Google Business Profile: does he have one, who controls it, how many Google reviews?
4. Who built the site (WordPress/Elementor/Yoast, Site Kit)? Is that person still involved? Do not knock them.
5. Jobs he wants more of and typical values: full reroof vs repairs vs flat roofs. Which pay best?
6. Radius: base is Whiston/Prescot (L35). Does he want Prescot/Huyton/St Helens/Knowsley or all of Liverpool and the Wirral?
7. How quiet is it right now, and how many jobs a month would make a difference?
8. Team size and capacity: is it just him or a crew? (Sole director; site says 'team'.)
9. What happens to calls when he is on a roof?
Objections
“I’ve been burned by companies like you before.”Agree, and do not defend the industry. Ask what happened. Then: start with the free fixes on the call (reviews button, area page), he owns every account, he sees every enquiry and where it came from, and nothing grows until it is working. Offer to fix the reviews link live as proof.
“I get my work from word of mouth / TrustATrader.”Keep it. But the friend who recommends him, and the customer who checks him out, both end up on his site, and the reviews button there sends them to Woolton and Homestyle.
“I’ve already got a website.”Agree, and say it is better than most (nine real service pages). We are not selling a rebuild. The gaps are specific: reviews, area pages, the form, tracking.
“Ads are a waste of money.”Often they are, when nothing is tracked. That is why ads come last, only after tracking, only for the jobs and towns he picks, with a budget he sets.
“Send me something to look at.”Send the hub link (assets.html), but book the next call before hanging up.
