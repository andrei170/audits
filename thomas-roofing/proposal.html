What we would do, in order

⭐ You have been let down before, so this starts with things you can check yourself. The first steps are fixes you will see change on your own site. Everything after that is measured, so you can see what is working and stop anything that is not.

1. Fix the reviews button · Week 1, free

Point it at your own TrustATrader profile, or if that has lapsed, at your Google reviews. Put your star score and a few real reviews on the home page.

⭐ We can do this with you on the call. It stops your own site sending customers to your competitors.

2. Fix the missing area page · Week 1, free

Rebuild “Areas We Cover” and add a short page each for the towns you want work in: Prescot, Whiston, Huyton, St Helens, Knowsley, Liverpool.

So people searching nearby find you, and you are found in the towns you choose, closest first.

3. Russell’s wrapping on your pages · Week 1

The job and the town in every page title and main heading, a search description on every page, a free roof survey offer, and CORC and insurance at the top.

Your content is already there; this makes it read like the job a customer searched for. The full list is in Audit Pt.2.

4. One name everywhere · Week 1

Use Thomas Roofing (NW) Ltd on the site, Google, TrustATrader and Facebook.

So customers and Google know they are all the same business.

5. A form that sorts the enquiry · Week 2

Ask the job type, postcode and roof type, let people send a photo, and score each enquiry 1 to 4.

The 3s and 4s come straight to your phone; you stop calling back every small job.

6. Answer while you are on a roof · Week 2

A text back within seconds when you miss a call, and an instant reply to every form, from your name and number.

So a customer does not ring the next roofer while you are up a ladder.

7. Measurement · Week 2

Tracking on calls and forms, so every enquiry shows where it came from.

So you can see what actually works before you spend anything on ads.

8. Small, local Google Ads · When you want more work

Only the jobs you choose, only the towns you choose, sent to the page about that job, with a budget you set.

⚠ Only once tracking is in, so you can see exactly what you are getting.

How we would work with you
  • Your website, your accounts, your data. Everything is set up in your name and stays yours.
  • You see the numbers: every call and form, and where it came from.
  • Start small and grow it only when it is working.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your typical private job value or how many you want a month. Anyone giving you a forecast before knowing those would be inventing it.
