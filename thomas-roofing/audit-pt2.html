What you told us you need, gone through one at a time

On Tuesday you told us what you want and what has gone wrong before. This page takes each point, says what we found when we looked on 3 November 2026, and what would fix it.

The eight points
1 · You said

“Roof replacements and roof repairs, like Russell”

What we found: Russell Roofing’s site is only six pages: home, about, contact, privacy, Roof Repairs and Roof Replacements. Every page title says the job and the city (“Roof Repairs Liverpool”), every page has a search description, each service page has one main heading with the job and the city in it, and every page offers a free roof survey. It shows CORC membership and “fully insured” in its first lines, and two local 0151 numbers.
⭐ Here is the surprise: your own pages use the words more than Russell’s do. Your roof repairs page says “repair” 30 times to Russell’s 20, and your replacement page says “replacement” 19 times to Russell’s 11. Your titles say “Roof Repairs - Thomas Roofing Ltd”, with no town and no search description.

What fixes it: Keep your pages and copy Russell’s wrapping: titles and main headings with the job and the town, a search description on every page, a free roof survey offer on every page, and CORC and insurance at the top. Then a replacement page and a repair page for each of your main towns, which Russell does not have.

2 · You said

“Got to be a certain value”

What we found: Your form asks name, phone, email and a message, the same as Russell’s. Neither can tell a £300 slate job from a full reroof until you call back.

What fixes it: A form that asks the job type, postcode, roof type and a photo, and scores each enquiry 1 to 4. The 3s and 4s, the bigger jobs in your area, come straight to your phone. Small or far-away jobs get a polite reply without taking your time. We need your number for “a certain value” to set it.

3 · You said

“Two slates fell off in Manchester”

What we found: Checkatrade came top in all four searches we ran on DuckDuckGo on 3 November 2026 (roof repairs, roof replacement and roofers in Liverpool, roofers in Prescot). A directory sends the same enquiry to several roofers and is full of small jobs, which is what you got.

What fixes it: Your own pages for replacements and repairs, in the towns you pick, so a homeowner with a big job finds you and only you. Searches for a full roof are fewer than for a quick repair, but each one is worth more to you.

4 · You said

“Doesn’t mean anything if your phone’s not going”

What we found: Your site counts visits (a Google tag) and nothing else. Nothing records which calls or forms came from the website, so a company could tell you that you are “at the top” and you could not check it.

What fixes it: Every call and every form counted, with the town, the job and where it came from. You see the list yourself. That is the number that matters, not rankings.

5 · You said

“There’s no one out there that you can trust”

What we found: You were on a contract you did not know about, for a site that brought no work.

What fixes it: Everything in your name: your domain, your Google profile, your ads account, your tracking. You see every enquiry as it comes in. We start with the free fixes so you can check the work on your own site before anything else.

6 · You said

“As long as you don’t have to sleep overnight”

What we found: Your site names only Liverpool and the Wirral, and its area page is missing. You told us you will go out to Cheshire, Blackpool, Flint and parts of Wales, Manchester and Macclesfield.

What fixes it: A town list in the order you want work, closest first: Prescot, Whiston, Huyton, Knowsley, St Helens, Liverpool, Widnes, Warrington, then further out. A replacement and repair page for each, and ads that only show inside that radius.

7 · You told us

A new web designer is redoing your site for free, and has sent something over

What we found: We have not seen it yet. Whatever it looks like, the parts that bring work are the same: town pages, titles, the form, reviews on show and tracking.

What fixes it: We are happy to work with whoever builds it. We can give him the list (pages, titles, form, tags) to build in, or do it ourselves. Either way the tracking and the area pages go on, and the site stays in your name.

8 · You said

“So I sign my own work off”

What we found: Your site shows no trade body at all. Russell shows CORC on its home page, and Skyline shows CORC and CHAS. You are already a CORC member.

What fixes it: Show CORC membership and your insurance at the top of every page now, and add NFRC the day it comes through. For a homeowner choosing who to trust with a full roof, that is the quickest win on the site.

Questions that change what we would build
  • What is a typical private job worth, and how many a month would you want?
  • What is the smallest job worth the drive? That sets the form’s scoring.
  • Can your gangs take private work alongside the new contract, and from when?
  • Replacements or repairs first? Or both from day one?
  • How many Google reviews do you have, and who can log in to your Google Business Profile?
  • Who controls your domain name, and can we see what the new designer sent?
