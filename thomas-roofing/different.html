In short

On Tuesday you said “there’s no one out there that you can trust”. Fair enough. So here is what went wrong before, in your words, and what we would do instead. No names, no promises, nothing priced.

Side by side
What happened before (in quotes: your words)How we would work
“You’re at the top of this, at the top of that”We report calls and forms: names, numbers, jobs. Never rankings. (What that looks like)
“Never got any work off it”If it is not bringing enquiries by month two, we say so, and you can stop.
A contract of about two years you didn’t realise you were inNo long contract. Cancel any month.
Came round your house and “fed me a load of” talkFree fixes first, in week 1, on your own site: the reviews button, the broken area page, CORC up top. You check them yourself before anything else.
“Like a shop that looks good on the front but nothing inside”Every page built around one job: getting the right calls. A form that sorts big jobs from small ones.
Directory jobs: “two slates fell off in Manchester”, sent to several roofers at onceEnquiries from your own pages, in the towns you pick, come to you alone.
You could not see what you were paying forAd account, website changes and tracking all set up in your name. If we stop, it all stays with you.
“Half the time I don’t even bother” answeringYou only get interrupted by the 3s and 4s. Missed calls get a text back for you.
What we will not do
  • Promise you rankings, a number of jobs, or a figure for next year. Nobody honest can.
  • Lock you into a contract.
  • Replace your new web guy. The blueprint is his to build from, or we build it with him.
  • Spend a pound on ads before tracking is in, so you can see what each pound brought.
One question for you

What would you need to see in month one to know it’s working?

Whatever you say, that becomes the first thing on your month-one report.
