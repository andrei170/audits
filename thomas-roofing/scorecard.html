Digital Presence Scorecard
F
37 / 100
2 of 14 checks passed. 10 failed.
The service pages are a solid base, better than most. But your reviews button sends customers to a list of other roofers, your area page is broken, and nothing on the site shows a customer why to pick you.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (2 November 2026)
FWhere your reviews button goes
The “Trustatrader Reviews, Click Here” button on your site does not open your profile. It lands on TrustATrader’s list of other roofers in Prescot: Woolton Roofing (193 reviews), Homestyle Roofing (117), Skyline, SC Roofing and more. Thomas Roofing is not on that list.
FReviews shown on your site
Your testimonials page has no reviews and no star score on it, only the button above. A customer comparing roofers sees nothing from your past customers.
FEnquiry form
A form on every page asking name, phone, email and a message. ⭐ Good that it is everywhere. It does not ask the postcode, the type of job or for a photo of the roof, so every enquiry needs a call back to find out if it is worth a visit.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ 07889 025 690 is a tap-to-call link at the top of every page.
BService pages
⭐ Nine service pages (roof repairs, replacement, reroofing, flat, rubber, fibreglass, chimneys, fascias and soffits, general roofing), each around 750 to 800 words. This is better than most roofers we check.
FArea and town pages
The “Areas We Cover” link in your footer opens a “page not found”. Only Liverpool and the Wirral are named. No page for Prescot, Whiston, Huyton, St Helens or Knowsley, where you are based.
FSearch descriptions
None of your 14 pages has a search description, so Google writes its own snippet from whatever text it picks.
FHeadings
The home page has seven main headings instead of one, and each service page has two (the second is “Contact Us”). Search engines read the main heading to work out what a page is about.
FLocal business markup
Basic markup from your SEO plugin says “web page” and “organisation”. Nothing tells search engines you are a roofer, where you work or your hours.
DMeasurement
⭐ A Google tag is installed, so visits are being counted. No Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel, so if you ran ads there would be no way to see which ones brought calls.
FTrust shown on the site
A CORC logo sits at the bottom of each page, linked to CORC’s home page, not to your membership, and with no member number. No insurance or workmanship cover shown. The home page says “over twenty years of combined experience”, which is good, but there is nothing to back it up on the page.
FYour name, the same everywhere
Your site calls the business “Thomas Roofing Ltd” in the title and footer and “Thomas Roofing NW” in the text. Your company is registered as Thomas Roofing (NW) Limited, and a different roofing company in Holywell is registered as Thomas Roofing Limited.
DProof of work
⭐ A gallery of around 47 photos. No job has a write-up (where it was, what was done, how long it took), which is what turns a photo into a reason to call.
FReady for ads
No Ads tag, no area pages and a form that cannot tell a Prescot repair from a job 30 miles away. An ad today would land on a general page and nobody could tell which clicks became work.

⭐ Every failure above is fixable without rebuilding your site. The service pages are the expensive part and they are already done.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google Business Profile and Google reviews, Facebook, and anything inside an ads account.
