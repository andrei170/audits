What we found
⭐⭐ Finding 1 · Russell is clear, not big

Six pages. Two of them are the jobs it wants, and every title and main heading says the job and Liverpool. Every page offers a free roof survey and says CORC and fully insured near the top. That is the whole trick.

Your site already has more than Russell’s: nine service pages, and more of the right words on them. What it is missing is the wrapping. That is a set of edits to your pages, not a new website. Line by line in the Audit Pt.2 checklist.

⚠ Finding 2 · Neither of you showed up in the searches we could run

We ran four searches on DuckDuckGo (UK) on 3 November 2026: roof repairs Liverpool, roof replacement Liverpool, roofers Liverpool and roofers Prescot. Checkatrade was first in all four. Neither Russell nor you was in any top 10. What did show up were directories and roofers with a page for each job in each town, like “roof repairs Liverpool” or “areas we cover: Prescot”.

⚠ DuckDuckGo is not Google, and we cannot see Google results from here. So if Russell does well, it may be on Google or from word of mouth. Worth asking: how do you know it does well?

⚠ Finding 3 · Reviews decide who gets the call

Woolton has 193 reviews on TrustATrader, and an old-style website with no form. It still wins work, because a homeowner comparing roofers sees 193 reviews. SC Roofing puts its star ratings in its main heading. Russell and you show none. Your reviews on show, and a review request after every private job, closes that gap.

Finding 4 · Nobody local is set up to measure Google Ads

Of these five, only Homestyle has a Google Ads tag, and it is based in Brigg, Lincolnshire, with its town pages aimed at Lincoln and Scunthorpe. Skyline can follow up its visitors on Facebook. None of the Liverpool firms here could tell which ad brought which call.

⚠ On ad spend: you asked us to look into what they spend. Nobody can see another firm’s spend from outside. What can be seen is which tags are live, shown above. We did not check Google’s or Meta’s public ad libraries this time: both need a browser our tools cannot run, so we would rather say so than guess.

⚠ Correction to Part 1: Part 1 said Homestyle was next door in Huyton and that Woolton had a Meta pixel. On re-checking, Homestyle is based in Brigg, and we found no Meta pixel on Woolton’s site.

The short version

You are not behind on content, experience or the jobs you can do. You are behind on being found for the towns you want, and on looking trusted. Russell shows the wrapping; Woolton shows the reviews. Put both on your site and you have something none of them has.
