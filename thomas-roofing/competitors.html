What this means for you
Your site is already better built than some of these. They win on proof.
  • Every one of them has its reviews on show on TrustATrader, where a customer checking roofers looks. Yours are behind a button that sends people to them.
  • Homestyle has a Google Ads tag on its site, but a re-check on 3 November showed it is based in Brigg, Lincolnshire, with town pages for Lincoln and Scunthorpe, so it is not aiming at your patch.
  • Skyline can follow up its visitors on Facebook; you cannot yet.
  • ⭐ Your nine service pages are more than most of them have. That is a strong base to build on, not something to throw away.
