Our first call, Tuesday 3 November

Here is everything we covered on Tuesday, in one place, so Wednesday can start where we left off. Anything in quotation marks is how you put it.

What you told us
⭐ The business today
  • Most of your work, about 90%, is social housing. You called it your “bread and butter”.
  • A new social housing framework starts just before Christmas: around 50 houses or more, mostly pitched roofs, to a Marley spec, with solar fitted by another firm.
  • You want NFRC accreditation, “so I sign my own work off”, and you hope this contract gets you there. You are already a CORC member.
  • The other 10% is private work from word of mouth, jobs of around £5k, £10k, £20k, sometimes £50k. You called them “little tiny jobs not worth shouting about”.
  • This year has been flatter than last, and you want to get back to last year’s level next year.
  • Your team: around 10 regular subcontractors, with more gangs brought in for big schemes. You mostly answer the phone yourself rather than being on the roof.
What you want more of
  • “Roof replacements and roof repairs”, the way Russell Roofing goes after them.
  • Full roofs, flat roofs, “whatever you’ve got”, but it has “got to be a certain value”.
  • How far: from Prescot out to Cheshire, Blackpool, Flint and parts of Wales, Manchester, and Macclesfield soon, “as long as you don’t have to sleep overnight”.
⚠ What has let you down before
  • Checkatrade, around £380 a month for a couple of years and still paying: “two slates fell off in Manchester” type jobs, maybe one or two. You plan to cancel it when the contract starts.
  • Rated People and TrustATrader: in your words, all no good.
  • A website company on a contract of around two years you did not realise you were in, and you “never got any work off it”. As you put it: “like a shop that looks good on the front but nothing inside”.
  • “There’s no one out there that you can trust.”
  • Now a new web designer is redoing your site for free. He has sent something over that you have not opened yet.
What we showed you
  • The graded scorecard: F, 37 out of 100. Your service pages are a good base; the gaps are reviews, area pages, tracking and trust.
  • Your reviews button sends people to a TrustATrader list of other roofers, and your “Areas We Cover” page is a page not found.
  • One name everywhere: your site says “Thomas Roofing Ltd” in places and “Thomas Roofing NW” in others.
  • Your competitors: Woolton, Homestyle, Skyline and SC Roofing. You said Woolton is “a good one to look at and model after”. Since then we re-checked: Homestyle is based in Brigg, Lincolnshire, and we found no Meta pixel on Woolton’s site (details in Competitors Pt.2).
  • The plan: fix the reviews button, town pages, show CORC and insurance (and NFRC later), an enquiry form that scores each job 1 to 4 so only the 3s and 4s come straight to you, a text back when you miss a call, tracking, then small local Google Ads with a budget you set.
What you asked us

“What goes on in the back of” Russell Roofing’s site, because it “does seem to do quite well”. We have taken it apart in Audit Pt.2 and Competitors Pt.2.

Still broken, re-checked on 3 November
  • The TrustATrader button on your testimonials page still opens the list of other Prescot roofers.
  • /areas-we-cover/ still opens a page not found.
  • No reviews, no CORC and no insurance shown on the site, and no search descriptions on any page.
  • No Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel, so ads could not be measured today.
What Wednesday is for
  • Go through Russell Roofing and what you would copy from it.
  • Answer the open questions on the start page: job values, the smallest job worth the drive, and timing around the new contract.
  • Agree whether there is a fit, and what a first step looks like.
  • 📎 If you can, send over what your new web designer sent you, so we can look at it before or on the call.
