What it tells us
1. Every firm Google showed has a Google profile with 150+ reviews. You have no profile at all.

Those three-firm results sit above every website on the page for a “roofer near me” search. Without a profile you cannot appear there, however good the website is.

2. Two of the five get into those results with very thin websites.

A&L Roofing’s site is 329 words with no tracking and no sitemap; James Roofing’s is a single 372-word page. Their profiles and reviews put them there, not their sites. ⭐ So the profile comes first, before the website.

3. A new licence is not the barrier. Valley Roofing’s current licence is only six weeks older than yours.

Valley Roofing LLC was first registered on 2 February 2026, you on 18 March 2026. Its site has 115 addresses in its sitemap, town pages for Salem, Keizer and Silverton, RoofingContractor markup and call tracking. (Its Google profile says 10+ years, so the people behind it trade longer than the LLC.)

4. Nobody in those results has an emergency or storm page. You already advertise one.

Your homepage mentions 24/7 emergency leak response 10 times. Pfeifer’s 105 addresses and Moser’s 19 contain no emergency, storm or leak page. In a wet Willamette Valley winter that is the open lane.

Where this comes from
Sources and dates
  • Ratings and reviews: the three-firm local results Google showed on 10 November 2026 (US results, logged out) for “roofing contractor Salem OR”, “roofer Keizer OR” and “roof repair Salem Oregon”. These five firms are every firm that appeared across the three.
  • Licences: Oregon CCB active licence register (data.oregon.gov, dataset g77e-6bhs), searched by business name and phone on 10 November 2026. Licence age is from the first-registered date to 10 November 2026.
  • Websites: each homepage and sitemap read on 10 November 2026. Tracking tags are what was in the page code; a tag shows something is set up, not how much anyone spends.
