Suggested questions, in order
  1. What does a typical job pay you? Split it if it varies: an emergency leak call-out, a repair, a full re-roof. This decides which pages and searches are worth going after first.
  2. Roughly how many jobs a month are you doing now, and in the summer? Even a rough figure. Nothing on the site measures it, so your number is the only one there is.
  3. Who is on the crew? The CCB register lists you as Exempt, which normally means no employees on the books. We need to know how many jobs a week you can actually take before creating demand.
  4. Which work do you want more of first: emergency leaks and repairs this winter, or re-roofs booked for spring? They are different pages and different searches.
  5. Which towns do you actually want to drive to? Your site lists Portland, Salem and Eugene. The demo uses nine towns around Salem. You choose.
  6. Do you have a Google Business Profile we did not find? If one exists under another name or address, it changes the first step.
  7. Who should the site speak for: you, Ramon, or both? Your site tells the family story; the licence is in your name.

Then: has anyone mixed him up with 4 Seasons Roofing? Is the 971 number his mobile (who answers when he is on a roof)? Does he do insurance or storm claims work?
