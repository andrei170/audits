The pattern
Two ways to win here, and the cheap one is not the website
  • Pfeifer and Valley win with a full website: town pages, a testimonials or reviews section, financing, tracking, markup.
  • A&L and James win with reviews alone, on websites thinner than yours.
  • ⭐ Nobody does both an emergency page and a gutter page. You already sell both.
  • ⚠ You currently have neither route: no Google profile for the reviews route, one page for the website route.
Where this comes from
Sources and dates
  • Ratings and reviews: the three-firm local results Google showed on 10 November 2026 (US results, logged out) for “roofing contractor Salem OR”, “roofer Keizer OR” and “roof repair Salem Oregon”. These five firms are every firm that appeared across the three.
  • Licences: Oregon CCB active licence register (data.oregon.gov, dataset g77e-6bhs), searched by business name and phone on 10 November 2026. Licence age is from the first-registered date to 10 November 2026.
  • Websites: each homepage and sitemap read on 10 November 2026. Tracking tags are what was in the page code; a tag shows something is set up, not how much anyone spends.
  • ⚠ Google’s “years in business” is the firm’s own claim on its profile, and can be older than its current licence (Valley Roofing’s licence is from February 2026; its profile says 10+ years).
