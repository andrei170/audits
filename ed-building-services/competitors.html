What this means for you
1On Google Maps, they exist and you do not.

Waymore Home shows 4.9 from 73 Google reviews, Top Grade Build 5.0 from 32 and Decohomes 4.8 from 26. You have no claimed listing. When a homeowner in Twickenham searches for an extension builder, the map is the first thing they see, and nobody else is sent the same enquiry.

Source: Google Maps searched from central Twickenham, 19 November 2026.

2At least one of them is already paying for those searches.

Top Grade Build’s site carries a Google Ads tag. That tells us local builders are buying the searches for extensions and lofts in your area; it does not tell us how much they spend.

Source: Home page code of each site, 19 November 2026.

3They show more pages for Google to find.

Decohomes has 73 pages, Top Grade 71 and Twickenham Builders 32, with pages named for towns and services. You have 9, and your areas page is empty. Ten honest pages, one per service and town you want, would close most of that gap.

Source: Each firm’s sitemap, 19 November 2026.

4Nobody asks the job size, and nobody shows real proof first.

None of the five asks the postcode on its form; you already do. None asks the size of the job. Your Checkatrade album is better proof than several of these sites show, once it is on your own website.

Source: Home page forms of each site, 19 November 2026.

Where you are already ahead
  • Commercial experience: fit-outs, dilapidations and maintenance, and a background in airport and data centre work. None of the five leads with commercial work.
  • A postcode question on your form, which none of them has.
  • Real photos of real jobs across extensions, lofts, kitchens, bathrooms and joinery.
