Enquiries that come to you alone

Each idea below sends enquiries only to you, so you are not one of five builders quoting for the same job. They work best once the website shows your real work and asks the right questions, which is why the demo comes first.

1The St Margarets story

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), homeowners within a few miles of Twickenham.

  • One real job told properly: the St Margarets rear extension, loft conversion and full refurbishment, with before, during and after photos.
  • The advert goes to homeowners in TW1, TW2, TW11 and TW9 only. The form asks the job, its size, the postcode and the start date, so small jobs and out-of-area enquiries are filtered before you call.
  • Every enquiry comes to you alone. Nobody else is sent it.
2Extension and loft searches

Google Search, your own patch.

  • Searches like "house extension builders Twickenham" and "loft conversion Richmond", sent to the matching page of the new site, not the home page.
  • At least one local rival already has a Google Ads tag on its site, so these searches are being bought. We would set a daily cap you choose and report what each enquiry cost.
3Commercial tenders and managing agents

For the weekly tenders you mentioned.

  • Public contracts are published on Contracts Finder and Find a Tender, and many councils and housing associations run their own portals. A weekly shortlist of small fit-out, refurbishment and maintenance work in your area, matched to what you can take on.
  • A short, honest approach to local managing agents and facilities managers for dilapidations and reactive maintenance, pointing at the commercial page.
  • Tender buyers usually ask for insurance, health and safety paperwork and memberships such as Constructionline. We could not verify your Constructionline status; that is a question for the call.
4Your own Google reviews

Free, and it compounds.

  • Claim the Google listing, then ask every finished customer for a Google review with a direct link. Your three Checkatrade customers are the place to start.
  • Local rivals show 26 to 73 Google reviews. Reviews on Google belong to you, not to a platform.
5Past customers and neighbours

Word of mouth, organised.

  • The Isleworth family that had three rounds of extensions is exactly who to ask for an introduction.
  • A short letter to the neighbours around each finished job, with a photo of the work, while the scaffold is fresh in their minds.
What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of enquiries, jobs or tenders won. Advertising costs money and results vary by month and by area. Every campaign would start small, with a cap you set, and be judged on what it brings in.
