The gap in one line
⚠ Rented leads, shared with everyone

Today almost every enquiry reaches you through a platform that sends the same homeowner to several builders, so the job goes to whoever is cheapest or quickest. To get bigger jobs that are yours alone, people need to find you directly: on Google Maps, on a website that shows your real work, and through campaigns that send enquiries only to you. The plan and the campaign ideas are about exactly that.

How a homeowner gets in touch today
⚠ The path from looking to a site visit
  • Phone: your mobile at the top of every page, but you cannot tap it to call on a phone (except on the old holding page).
  • Form: on Home and Contact: name, email, phone, postcode, message and a small sum.
  • Nothing asks what the job is, roughly how big it is, or when they want to start.
  • Email: a mailto link to ed@edbuildingservices.co.uk.
  • Contact page promise: "one of our team will be in contact within 24 hours". Good; worth keeping on every page.
Proof of your work: the photos are on the wrong site
  • Your Gallery page has 4 photos, and their file names (iStock-955980436 and similar) show they come from a photo library.
  • The photo on the home page is a library image too.
