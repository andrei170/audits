The order we would do it in

Own your enquiries first, then pay to grow them. The demo website shows Stage 3 working.

1Stage 1: be findable, this month

So people who are looking for a builder find you, not a platform.

  • Claim and complete a Google Business Profile for E.D Building & Construction, with your services, areas, hours and real photos.
  • Check whether the unclaimed "Ed Building Services" listing in Twickenham is yours, and merge or remove it.
  • Pick one name and one base, and use them on the website, Google, Checkatrade and TrustATrader.
2Stage 2: free fixes to the current site, this week

So nobody who checks you out is put off.

  • Swap the 4 library photos in the Gallery for your own Checkatrade photos, at full size.
  • Remove the old "under construction" page and the empty Areas page, or fill the Areas page.
  • Put numbers in the four counters, or remove them, and make the phone number tap-to-call.
  • Change "won’t be beaten on price" to something about quality and project management.
3Stage 3: a website that wins the bigger job

Shown working in the demo.

  • A form on every page asking the job, its size, the postcode and the start date, with an honest yes or no by area.
  • A page for each service you want more of, a commercial page for tenders, and a page for each area you work in.
  • Proper project pages with your own photos, starting with St Margarets.
  • Calls and form sends counted as enquiries, so you can see where each job came from.
4Stage 4: campaigns that send work only to you

From the campaign ideas.

  • Start with one local campaign and the weekly tender shortlist, then add Google Search once you can see what an enquiry costs.
  • Every enquiry gets a text back straight away, and a follow-up if they go quiet after the quote.
5Stage 5: rely less on the platforms

Your call, when the numbers say so.

  • Once your own enquiries are steady, decide whether the platform fees are still worth it. That decision is yours.
What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of enquiries, jobs or tenders won. What this does is make sure people who are looking for a builder like you can find you, see your real work, and send you the details you need before you visit. Many of the Stage 1 and 2 fixes you could do yourself; the demo is a blueprint, not a demand to switch.
