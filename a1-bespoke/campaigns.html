You asked on Thursday: where would the ads run, who would they reach, and what does it cost?

In this order, switched on one at a time, so each one can show whether it is working before the next one starts.

⭐ Campaign 1 · Flat roofs · Google search · first

The job you want most: rubber and flat roofs, usually £3,000 to £4,000, in and out in a few days.

Searches like: flat roof Altrincham · flat roof replacement Hale · rubber roof installers · EPDM roofing near me · garage roof replacement · leaking flat roof

Goes to: your flat roof page, with your 91 Which? reviews, your guarantee and your drone survey, and a quote form that asks how urgent it is.

⭐ Campaign 2 · Repairs and bay roofs · Google search · second

Searches like: roof leak repair Altrincham · bay window roof repair · roof repair Hale · emergency roof repair

Goes to: your repairs page. Scored 1 to 4, so the urgent ones are rung first.

Campaign 3 · Facebook and Instagram · homeowners in your postcodes · third

Shown to: homeowners in the postcodes you choose, with photos of your own flat roofs, the drone survey, and the 91 reviews. Plus retargeting: people who visited your pages and did not fill in the form see you again.

Later · only when you have the hands for it

New pitched roofs, the £20,000 jobs. Worth more, but you said a small crew is better off with jobs you are in and out of.

⚠ Searches we block from day one

DIY, materials, jobs and careers, “cheap”, and anything outside the postcodes. You pay only when somebody clicks, so the clicks you do not want are kept out.
