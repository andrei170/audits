What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

Your website has had 63 new articles since June, and no way of telling you what they did.

There is no Google Analytics, no Google Ads tag and no Meta tag in the page code. So nobody can say how many people read those articles, or how many then got in touch.

⚠ Wix keeps some visitor numbers of its own that we cannot see from outside. Worth opening on the call.

🔴 A phone number nobody can tap

Your numbers, 0161 883 0845 and 07770 785114, are printed at the top of every page. Not one of them is a link, on any of the 8 pages we read.

🔴 Most people looking for a roofer are on a phone. Somebody with a leak has to memorise your number, leave the page and type it in. Four of the firms you compete with let them tap once.

🔴 Nothing tells Google where you work

The homepage is titled "Home | A1 Bespoke", with no description. There is no page for any town: not Bury, not Radcliffe, not Whitefield.

⚠ Your articles mention Manchester, which helps. But the pages that sell the work, New Roofs and Flat Roofs, never say where you do it.

⚠ The site looks older than the business is

The footer says "©2017". The first thing a visitor reads is an award from February 2016. The Lofts section switches from "we" to "my clients are my number one priority", which reads like template wording left in.

⚠ A customer comparing three roofers will not know the blog was updated yesterday. They will see 2016 and 2017.

⚠ Your best proof is shown as a number, not a voice

You say 5 Star on Which? Trusted Traders, 100% on MyBuilder and 5 Star on Rated People, correct as of 1 June 2026. That is a record most roofers cannot match.

⚠ But the site shows the scores only. Not one customer's own words, and no link to check them.

⭐ What is already strong
  • Which? Trusted Trader, and Trader of the Month
  • A limited company since July 2012, VAT registered, with the company number shown openly
  • A real specialism: Firestone EPDM rubber flat roofs, and loft boarding built off the roof structure rather than the ceiling joists
  • An enquiry form that asks for the address, on the homepage and the Contact page
  • Google is given your name, address and phone number in the right format

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, your Facebook page, where you rank in search, and Wix's own visitor stats. We have not guessed.
