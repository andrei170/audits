What you told me you need, gone through properly

On Thursday we went through the website. This page goes back through what you actually said, one point at a time, and what each one means for getting you your own enquiries, which is the part you asked about.

1 · You said

“Decent leads.”

What we found: About 40% of your work comes from lead sites, where “six or seven people going for the same job” each pay about £40. And on the Which? list for roofers in Altrincham you appear 9th, shown as 17 miles from the centre. Directories sort by distance, so in the towns you want most, you start near the bottom.

What fixes it: Your own adverts, for the searches you want, in the towns you pick. An advert is not sorted by distance, and the person who fills in your form has asked nobody else.

2 · You said

“In Greater Manchester, I only go for nice towns.”

What we found: Your site has no list of the areas you cover and no town pages. Four of the firms that turn up for Altrincham have a page for Altrincham or Hale: C&M Roofing, TRS, Daniel Scott and Paul Bradley.

What fixes it: Ads are set by postcode, not by a circle on a map, so Hale (WA15) and Altrincham (WA14) can be in and the areas you avoid can be out. For the new website, a page for each of those towns goes on the list for your web person.

3 · You said

“Oh, I just found you on the internet.”

What we found: We found no analytics, no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel in your site’s code (it is a Wix site, which can add some of these after the page loads, so this is a strong sign, not proof). Either way, nothing tells you which page, search or town an enquiry came from.

What fixes it: Every call and form tracked to where it came from, and a report every 30 days by job type and town. You stop asking customers where they found you.

4 · You said

“I should contact them faster, I sometimes don't.”

What we found: You are quoting, running the jobs and doing the invoicing. An enquiry that waits a few hours has often rung someone else.

What fixes it: An instant text reply to every form, in your name, a text back to every missed call, and every enquiry scored 1 to 4 so a leak this week is rung before a price-check for spring. It can sit alongside Zoho, or feed into it.

5 · You said

“We're trying to get all the reviews copied onto a reviews section.”

What we found: Which?: 5.0 from 91 reviews. Checkatrade: 9.77 from 44. That is more than almost every firm we compared you with (Paul Bradley 48 on Which?, C&M 118 on its own site). Your site shows “99.9%” and no number.

What fixes it: Put the number everywhere: “91 reviews on Which?, 5.0” on every page and in the adverts. It is the strongest thing you have and it is currently hidden.

6 · You said

“Rubber roofs, flat roofs are very, very good for us.”

What we found: The firms you compete with for flat roofs lead with a guarantee or a price: Daniel Scott publishes prices and a 10-year guarantee, Weather Shield sells Firestone-approved roofs with a 10-year guarantee and finance, C&M offers up to 20 years. Your site does not state a guarantee.

What fixes it: Say what you guarantee a flat roof for, and which system you fit, on the flat roof page and in the ads. Plus your drone survey with a written report, which a customer can picture before you arrive.

7 · You said

“Claude had recommended us as the top choice.”

What we found: That is covered in full on your AI and search audit: how it happened, and what makes it happen more often.

What fixes it: The same pages that make ads work, with your towns, services, reviews and guarantee written plainly, are what AI tools read too.

8 · You said

“He's already underway.”

What we found: Your web person is building the new site, and none of this needs him to stop.

What fixes it: The ads go to their own pages, on a web address you own, so neither of you waits for the other. We send him one short list: a tappable number, a tracking tag, the reviews on the site, a page per town.

⚠ Questions that change what we would build
  • Which towns, exactly? Hale, Altrincham, and which parts of Bolton? And which local ones: Prestwich, Whitefield, Bury?
  • What do you guarantee a flat roof for, and which system do you fit?
  • How many flat roofs a week can the crew take right now?
  • Roughly what do the lead sites cost you a month? That is the number anything new has to beat
  • Can your web person add a tracking tag to the new site?
