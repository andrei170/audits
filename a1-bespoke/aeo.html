How AI assistants and Google find you
⭐ Why this page exists

You told me a customer found you because Claude recommended you as the best local roofer, and you never paid for it. This page measures why that happened, and what would make it happen on purpose, in the areas you actually want.

🔴🔴 The finding

AI search finds you through other people’s websites, not your own. We ran live searches on 15 October:

  • “EPDM rubber flat roof installer Altrincham Hale”: you came up, through your MyBuilder profile and its 350+ reviews across five platforms
  • “Best roofer Radcliffe Bury flat roof repair”: you did not appear at all. Bark, Checkatrade and other roofers did

⚠ So your reviews on other sites are doing the work. Your own website gives an AI almost nothing to quote about where you work or what you do there.

⚠ What would make it happen on purpose
  • A page for each area you want work from: Hale, Altrincham, Bowdon, Wilmslow, Bolton, each saying what you do there
  • A search description on every page, so you write the summary rather than Google or an AI
  • FAQ markup on your articles, so the answers you already write can be lifted word for word
  • Your Google profile and review sites linked in your business markup, so an AI can connect all 350+ reviews to you
  • A proper llms.txt: your services, areas, phone number and best pages, in plain words for AI tools to read
  • A tappable number, so the person an AI sends you can ring in one tap

⭐ All of these are a list your web person can work from. None of them needs a rebuild.

⚠ Not measured: what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini answer on any given day (it changes), where you rank, page speed, and your Google Business Profile. We have not guessed.
