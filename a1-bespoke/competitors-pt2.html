⭐⭐ Finding 1 · Your reviews beat almost all of them, and nobody can see it

91 reviews at 5.0 on Which? Paul Bradley, with the same badge, has 48. Only C&M shows more, on its own site. Your website shows a percentage and no number, and your adverts would show both.

🔴 Finding 2 · On the directories, distance decides who is seen first

On Which?’s list of roofers in Altrincham you are 9th, at 17 miles. However good the reviews, a directory puts the nearest firms first. An advert in Hale is not sorted by how far away you are.

⚠ Finding 3 · Only two firms are advertising right now, and both are in Bolton

Weather Shield and Paul Bradley have adverts running this month. WA14 Roofing’s were last seen in February, Daniel Scott’s and Millbrook’s in August. We found nobody advertising from Hale or Altrincham today. That is room, not a promise of cheap clicks.

⚠ Finding 4 · They compete on guarantee and price

10 years (Daniel Scott, Weather Shield), up to 20 (C&M), prices on the page (Daniel Scott), finance (Weather Shield). Your site states none of these. One line about your guarantee costs nothing and answers the first thing a Hale homeowner compares.

The short version: you are not losing on reputation. You are losing on being seen in the towns you want, and on saying plainly what you guarantee. Both are fixed by your own adverts, going to a page that says it.
