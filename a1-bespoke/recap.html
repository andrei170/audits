Our first call, Thursday 15 October
⭐⭐ What you told me
  • Where the work comes from: about 40% lead sites (Rated People, MyBuilder, Which?), 30% word of mouth and past customers, 20% your website
  • Shared leads are getting expensive: six or seven roofers paying for the same job, with no idea who wins it
  • A customer found you through Claude, an AI assistant, which recommended you as the best local roofer. It started as a small repair and has led to more work
  • The jobs that suit you now: flat roofs, usually £3,000 to £4,000, bay roofs and repairs. In and out in a few days
  • Bigger jobs, like a new pitched roof, are worth more but take longer than a small team can easily carry
  • You choose areas by the customer, not the map: the better parts of Greater Manchester, like Hale, Altrincham and parts of Bolton
  • The goal: a steady flow of decent enquiries you can chase down, and maybe another pair of hands once the work is there
  • You already use Zoho CRM, and you run drone surveys with written reports, which most roofers do not
  • Google has verified you for local advertising, and you have five-star Google reviews
  • A new website is being built by someone you know, and is waiting on your list of services and some wording
What I showed you
  • The graded scorecard: 46 out of 100. No tappable phone number, nothing measuring visitors, no page for any town, and a 2017 footer
  • Six competitors: four let a customer tap to call, three have town pages, three measure their visitors
  • The order of work: tappable number, tracking, bring the site up to date, a page per area, then advertising
  • The demo site: a quote form on every page that asks how urgent the job is, and a page for each service and area
⚠ What you asked
  • “How do you go into lead generation if someone else is doing the website?” Fair question, and it is what Monday is for. The website changes are a list your web person can work from. Our main work is the advertising
  • “Is it mainly Google ads?” Google, Facebook and Instagram, chosen by what works in your areas. That is the research I am bringing on Monday

For Monday: where your ads would run, who they would reach in the areas you want, and what it costs. Your AI and search audit covers how that customer found you through Claude, and how to make it happen on purpose.
