In the order we would do it

The order is the argument. Advertising with nowhere to send people is how Angi came to own your leads.

🔴 1. A website, leading with tree removal

Tree removal and storm damage first, then roofing, siding and windows. Your A+ BBB rating and 24 years on the first screen, a form that asks the job and the zip code, and a page for each town you cover.

🔴 2. Storm damage as the headline offer

Tree removal and roof repair from one firm. It is the one thing no tree service can match, and storms are exactly when people search.

3. Advertising you own, instead of Angi

Ads in your own account, sending people to your own site, with every enquiry tracked to its source. When it stops, everything you built stays with you, which is the opposite of Angi.

4. Your Google Business Profile, with tree service on it

So that a map search for tree removal near Springfield shows you, with your reviews.

⚠ Nothing here forecasts a number of jobs. What a tree job pays you is the first thing we need, because every judgement depends on it.
