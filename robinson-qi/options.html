It is not pay per lead. The leads come from your own ads, to your own website, so nobody else gets sent them. Everything is built in your name and stays yours.

Step 1 · Setup, paid once

$2,000 one time

We fix what is broken and build what the ads need, before a dollar goes on advertising. Running ads to a broken website is paying to send people to an error page.
