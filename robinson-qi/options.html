Two ways to do this
⭐⭐ Where we got to on Monday
  • This is about the tree service only. The home improvement side is full, with around 200 rental units you look after
  • 15 to 20 tree jobs a month, about $2,000 a job, and you keep roughly $1,300 to $1,400 of it. You could take on double
  • Three quarters of the work comes from knocking on doors. About 10% word of mouth, 10% Angi, 5% Yelp
  • You ring a new lead back in about 24 minutes on average
  • You want a steady flow, not a rush in week one and then three quiet weeks
  • 🔴 robinsonsqualitytreecare.com still shows "Site Not Found", re-checked this morning, 14 October

You asked two things: what it costs, and whether it is so many leads for so many dollars. Both are answered below.

It is not pay per lead. You have had that from Angi and Yelp, where the same lead is sold to three firms. Everything here is built in your name and stays yours.

⭐⭐ Option 1 · Done for you · the one I would pick

$6,000 for the first 90 days, paid once

We build it, run it and answer for it.

  • A tree service website in your name, with a page for tree removal, storm damage, trimming and stump grinding
  • A page for each town you want work from, starting with Springfield
  • The storm damage offer on the front page: one call, one crew, one invoice, for the tree and the roof
  • A quote form that scores every enquiry 1 to 4 by how soon they need it, so you ring the urgent ones first
  • Tracking on every call and form, so you can see where each job came from
  • Your Google Business Profile set up for tree work, so you show on the map
  • Google search advertising, built and run by us for the full 90 days, in your own account
  • A missed-call text back, so somebody who rings while you are up a tree gets an answer
  • A call with me every two weeks, and a report each month showing enquiries, cost and booked jobs

⚠ Advertising spend is separate, paid by you straight to Google, on your own card. You set the amount.

Option 2 · Done with you

$1,500 one time

We build the foundations. You run it.

  • The tree service website, with the service pages and the quote form
  • Tracking on every call and form
  • Your Google Business Profile set up for tree work
  • A written plan for the advertising, which you or somebody on your side runs
  • Two calls with me to hand it over

⚠ Not included: us running the advertising, the town pages, the missed-call text back, or the fortnightly calls.

⚠ The arithmetic, on your own numbers

You told me a tree job is about $2,000 and you keep about $1,300 to $1,400 of it.

So Option 1 is covered by five jobs across the 90 days. You do 15 to 20 a month now and told me you could take on double.

Option 2 is covered by two jobs.

⚠ That is arithmetic on your figures to show what would have to be true. It is not a forecast of how many jobs this brings in, and nobody can honestly give you one.

The steady flow you asked for

You said you do not want everything in week one and then three quiet weeks. Search advertising runs on a daily budget, so enquiries arrive across the month, and it can be turned down in a busy week and back up in a quiet one.

What happens after the 90 days

You own the website, the tracking and the advertising account whatever you decide. If you want us to keep running it, we agree a monthly figure before day 90, with no contract.

To start: the first payment, access to your Google Business Profile, your service area in your own words, and photographs of your crew and your work. We also need to sort out robinsonsqualitytreecare.com, which still shows "Site Not Found" (checked 12 and 14 October).
