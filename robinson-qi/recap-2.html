Our second call, Thursday 15 October
⭐⭐ What you told me
  • Nothing had changed since Monday. Tree side only
  • The two businesses stay separate. In your words: “two different contracts, because it’s also two different insurances and two different liabilities.”
  • You have already won both sides of a storm job: a tree that fell on a house, with one estimate to the insurer for the tree and one for the roof and trusses. You got both contracts
  • Your confidence in the system was a 7 out of 10 before the walkthrough, and after it: “You got my interest.”
⭐ What it takes to be a 10, in your words
  • Easy to use, for you and for the customer. After seeing the pipeline, calendar and conversations: “Easy enough to navigate.”
  • Data and tracking: knowing what share of the leads actually turn into jobs
  • Control over the automatic message. Not a vague “we’ll be in touch”, but your name, your number and the company name, and why they are getting it
  • And why a tree is priced on site: power lines, a house or barn underneath, a well or a gas line all change the price
What I showed you
  • Audit Pt.2 and Competitors Pt.2: the gap is reviews, not quality, and the roof is the thing none of them have
  • The system itself: the lead pipeline, the shared calendar and the conversations inbox, as we use them ourselves
  • The pricing: a one-time setup of $2,000, which also covers the first month, then optional monthly support at $1,000 (Standard) or $1,750 (Priority). Ad spend is paid by you, separately
Where we left it

You said the setup was the one you were leaning to, and that you needed to get back from California and finish a couple of jobs first. We start a project with half the setup up front. We booked today, Thursday 22 October, to decide.

✅ What I said I would do, and what is done
  • Every automatic message is written by us and approved by you before it goes live. The first draft is on the demo quote page, with your name, number and company in it
  • The demo is rebuilt for the tree side only, and the storm offer now reads “one call, tree and roof both handled”, with the roof on Robinson’s Quality Improvements’ own contract, so the two businesses stay separate
  • Your own words on pricing are on the site: power lines, structures, wells and gas lines, so the price-shoppers understand before you drive out
  • Ads on a daily budget, so the work comes in across the month, not all in week one
