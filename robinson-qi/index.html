What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

Robinson’s Quality Improvements has no website. Searching the name returns the BBB profile and directory listings, and nothing of your own.

⭐ The BBB profile is genuinely strong: A+, accredited since November 2019, no complaints closed in the last three years, trading since June 2001. That is the reputation of a firm people should be finding.

🔴 And tree removal appears nowhere. The BBB profile lists residential home improvement. Someone in Springfield searching for tree removal has no way to know you do it.

🔴 What Angi actually did, in your numbers

You told our team Angi brought 15 leads a month, all of them in the first week, and that 2 became jobs. That is about one in seven.

  • ⚠ All 15 arriving in the first week usually means the month's budget or lead cap is used up early, so the other three weeks bring nothing
  • ⚠ Angi commonly sends the same request to more than one contractor, so you are often racing others for a person who is shopping on price
  • ⭐ You own none of it. When the contract ends, the leads stop and nothing you paid for stays with you

⭐⭐ Your contract is ending, which makes this the cheapest moment there will be to replace it with something you own.

⚠ The size of the gap

You told our team you are doing about $25,000 a month and want $20,000 more, nearly double, and that the tree side struggles most of roofing, siding, windows and trees.

⚠ We do not know your tree job value yet, so we have not turned that into a number of jobs. It is the first thing we will ask.

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, Facebook page and Google reviews. We could not confirm them and have not guessed.
