Our first call, Monday 12 October

Monday was rushed at the end, because I had to leave for a training session. So here’s everything we covered, in one place, before today’s call.

⭐⭐ What you told me
  • Two businesses: home improvement since 1999, and the tree service since 2017
  • You only want help with the tree side. The home improvement side is full from word of mouth and repeat customers, including around 200 rental units you look after
  • 15 to 18 tree jobs a month, 20 at most, at about $2,000 a job, keeping roughly $1,300 to $1,400 of it
  • You could take on double
  • Where the work comes from: about 75% door knocking, 10% word of mouth, 10% Angi, 5% Yelp. In your words, Angi and Yelp are “horrible”
  • What you’ve tried before: billboards, a website, Angi and Yelp. You haven’t worked with a marketing firm
  • Your callback time on Angi leads averages 24 minutes, but most of the time people don’t pick up or call back
  • You don’t want to be flooded in week one and then wait three weeks for the next lot
  • You’re working seven days a week
What I showed you
  • The graded scorecard: no website showing tree work, nowhere to enquire about tree removal, and nothing online saying you remove trees. You added that reviews should be more visible
  • Your competitors: C&S, Huffman, Haley’s, Guyton and Woodlands. You knew all of them
  • The storm damage idea: one call, one crew, one invoice for the tree and the roof. You said “that’s fine”
  • The demo website: led by tree removal, with a quote form that asks how urgent the job is, so the emergencies get called first
  • The plan: website first, storm damage as the headline offer, advertising in your own account instead of Angi, and your Google Business Profile set up for tree work
🔴 What we need to sort out

You asked if we’d seen robinsonsqualitytreecare.com. We checked it on 12 October and again on 14 October. Both times it showed “Site Not Found”. Anyone who has that address on a card, a truck or an old advert is landing on an error page.

⚠ What you asked me
  • “What do you guys charge? Do you do it monthly or weekly?”
  • “Is it 10 leads for $200?” No. We don’t sell leads. Everything is built in your name and stays yours. I said I’d come back with pricing built around what you actually need, and nothing you don’t

Since Monday: I’ve gone back through what you said in more depth. Audit Pt.2 takes each point one at a time, and Competitors Pt.2 compares your reviews, size and prices with five Springfield tree services. Today we’ll go through how it works and what it costs.
