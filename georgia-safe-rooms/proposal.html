In the order we would do it

The order is set by the calendar, not by us. Black Friday is 27 November. The grand opening is 7 November. Everything works backwards from those two dates.

🔴 Before 7 November: a page that captures the opening

Store address, hours, phone, the grand opening, and “Join the Black Friday early access list”. A sign-up at the counter and a QR code at the door point to it.

🔴 1 to 15 November: the full site

A page for firearms, safes and vaults, safe rooms and bunkers, and FFL transfers if you offer them. Google Business Profile set up with photos from opening day.

🔴 16 to 26 November: the countdown to the list

Three to five emails: a sneak peek, early access for the list, then the deals. Your list hears first; everyone else hears on the day.

27 to 30 November: Black Friday to Cyber Monday

In store, plus whatever can legally be sold online, with safes, accessories and safe room consultations as the advertisable offers.

⚠ Nothing here forecasts sales. What you will stock and offer is the first thing we need.
