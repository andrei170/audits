Risks
  • 🔴 Raffle legality. O.C.G.A. 16-12-22.1: only licensed nonprofits may run raffles, the license needs the group to have existed for 24 months, and an unlicensed raffle is commercial gambling. A raffle is a prize by chance for people who "paid or promised consideration", so a free draw with no purchase needed avoids it. Source: the statute text printed in the Floyd County Sheriff raffle application (2014 code text), read 24 Oct 2026. Not legal advice. He should confirm with his attorney.
  • 🔴 A firearm as a prize. ATF says when a licensee gives the gun to the winner, the licensee completes Form 4473 and runs the NICS check first. Source: ATF Q&A on licensees and firearm raffles, read 24 Oct 2026. Tell entrants this up front.
