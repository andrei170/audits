Why Black Friday is different for you
⭐ The biggest day of the year

Black Friday is the busiest day of the year for firearm background checks in the US. The FBI recorded 203,086 checks on Black Friday 2017, the first time any day passed 200,000, and reports say 2023 set a new single-day record.

⚠ Background checks are not sales, since one check can cover several guns. But they show where the demand is.

🔴🔴 The catch: you cannot buy your way in
  • Google does not allow ads for firearms or ammunition. A narrow pilot for some accessories started in the US in October 2026
  • Facebook and Instagram do not allow ads that sell weapons or ammunition. Safety courses and non-weapon products for over-18s are treated differently
  • Text messages about firearms are heavily restricted by the phone carriers. Some providers allow it with age checks, others block it entirely

So the stores that win Black Friday are the ones with their own list before the day. Email is the one channel nobody can switch off.

⚠ Why mid-November is too late for the website

A website that goes live in mid-November misses the grand opening, which is when most of your Black Friday list gets built. A new site also takes time to appear on Google. The first page has to be live before 1 November. The full site can follow.
