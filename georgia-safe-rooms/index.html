What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

gasaferooms.com describes a safe room and bunker contractor. It does not say there is a store, where it is, when it opens, or that you sell firearms or safes. Read on 16 October 2026.

🔴 Anyone who hears about the grand opening and looks you up will find a bunker builder with no address, no phone number and no hours.

🔴 1. Nobody can find the store

No street address, no map, no opening hours, no phone number. The only contact is a Gmail address and a quote form for safe room builds.

🔴 2. Nothing captures a visitor for later

There is no way to join a list. For a store that cannot advertise its main products on Google or Facebook, its own list is the marketing.

⚠ 3. One page, built on an AI site builder

The site is a single page made with Sintra. There is no page for each product line, no online store, and Google is given no business details.

⭐ What is already strong
  • The safe room and bunker copy is specific: FEMA P-320 and P-361, NBC air filtration, EMP shielding, vault doors
  • MNDA privacy on every build, a real reason to choose you
  • A Google tag is already installed
  • The domain gasaferooms.com is yours and already points at the site

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, social media, inventory and pricing. We have not guessed.
