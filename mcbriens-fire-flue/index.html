  • McBrien’s Fire & Flue, 183 Londonderry Turnpike, Unit 4, Hooksett, NH 03106 (from your MapQuest listing). Phone (888) 581-9005.

The three things holding you back on Google
⚠ 1. Google cannot tell where you work

Your site never says Hooksett, Manchester, Bedford, New Hampshire or NH. It says “New England”. The Contact page map is the whole of New England. Someone searching “chimney repair Hooksett NH” is shown the firms whose pages say Hooksett.

⚠ 2. Three pages for a full chimney business

Home, Service and Contact. Cleaning, liners, rebuilds, masonry, caps and inspections share one page. The competitors that came up in our searches have a page per service and a page per town: Black Moose Chimney’s sitemap lists about 450 pages and Ceaser Chimney’s about 90.

⚠ 3. Nothing is measured

No Google tag, no Analytics, no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel. If you started Google Ads tomorrow there would be no way to tell which calls came from them.

Your site, page by page
mcbriensfireandflue.com: 3 pages, last updated August 2026
  • ⭐ Good: tap-to-call on every page, a form on every page, your hours, emergency service, discounts and 16 reviews on the home page.
  • Built from a website template (“Construz”), and some of it still shows: rating badges using the template’s logos, a link back to the template’s demo site, and social icons that open facebook.com and twitter.com instead of your pages.
  • Several photos are AI-generated images (their file names start “ChatGPT-Image”). Your real job photos, like the rebuild above the water and the liner install in the snow, are much stronger and there are only a handful.
  • Titles are just “Home”, “Service” and “Contact”. The home page has six main headings instead of one.
  • Two names: “McBrien’s Chimney and Flue” in the titles and “McBrien’s Fire & Flue” in the footer.
  • The form asks name, email, phone and message. No town, zip or job type.
  • A small typo on the home page: “Ned Help?”.
Search

We ran five searches on DuckDuckGo with the US region set. Your site was not in the top 10 for any of them, including two for your own town:

  • “chimney sweep Hooksett NH”: Mighty Moose Chimney first, then Anything Chimney, a directory, 603 Chimney and Crown Chimney.
  • “chimney repair Hooksett NH”: Crown Chimney first (also based on Londonderry Turnpike, Hooksett), then Angi, BBB, Mighty Moose and Granite State Chimney.
  • “chimney repair Manchester NH”: Granite State Chimney, Yelp, Ceaser Chimney, Shamrock Chimney.
  • “chimney sweep Bedford NH”: Yelp, Anything Chimney, a directory, Chimcare, Mighty Moose.
  • “McBrien’s Fire & Flue Hooksett”: ⭐ your site comes first when someone already knows your name.

⭐ Every firm that ranks has a page named for the town and the job. That is the pattern.

⚠ What we could not measure
  • Google itself, Google Maps and your Google Business Profile: our tools cannot read them, so we do not know your Google review count or your map position. A public directory that lists Google ratings for Hooksett chimney companies shows Crown Chimney and Ace Chimney Services, and does not list you, which is worth checking together.
  • Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor and BBB: they blocked our tool, so we have not quoted their review counts.
  • Ads: nobody can see another firm’s ad spend from outside.
  • Page speed: Google’s speed test refused our requests that day, so it is not graded.
