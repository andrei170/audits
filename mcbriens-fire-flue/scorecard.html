Digital Presence Scorecard
F
41 / 100
2 of 15 checks passed. 8 failed.
Your reviews and your offers are a strong base. But the site is three pages long, never says Hooksett or New Hampshire, and has no tracking, so Google has very little to show and you cannot see what the site brings in.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (5 November 2026)
FPages on the site
Three pages in total: Home, Service and Contact (from your own sitemap). Every chimney job you do is squeezed onto one Service page, so Google has one page to show for “chimney repair”, “chimney liner”, “chimney rebuild” and everything else.
FWhere you are
The words Hooksett, New Hampshire and NH do not appear anywhere on your site. It says “New England” only. There is no street address, and the map on the Contact page is a map of the whole of New England.
FTown pages
None. No page for Hooksett, Manchester, Bedford, Concord or any town you work in. The firms that show up for “chimney repair Hooksett NH” all have a page for that town.
DService pages
Eight services are described (cleaning, liners, installs, rebuilds, masonry, caps, inspections, dampers and crowns), each in two or three lines on the one Service page. ⭐ The list is right. What is missing is a page for each one, with photos of your own jobs.
FPage titles
Your three titles are “Home”, “Service” and “Contact” plus the business name. None names a service or a town, which is the first thing Google reads.
CSearch descriptions
⭐ All three pages have one. They say “New England”, not Hooksett or Southern New Hampshire.
FHeadings
The home page has six main headings instead of one (“Family Owned &”, “Operated”, “Complete Chimney Services” and so on). Search engines use the main heading to work out what a page is about.
FLocal business markup
Only the basic markup your SEO plugin adds (“web page”, “organization”). Nothing tells Google you are a chimney company, where you are, your hours or your phone.
FMeasurement
No Google tag, no Google Analytics, no Google Ads tag, no Tag Manager and no Meta pixel on any page. Today nobody can see how many people visit, or which calls came from the website.
B-Tap-to-call
⭐ (888) 581-9005 is a tap-to-call link at the top and bottom of every page. It is a toll-free number, not a local 603 number, which is worth a conversation (see the Google plan).
DReviews on the site
⭐ 16 real-looking written reviews, several naming Omar. But they have no source and no link to Google, and the “4.9 Rating, 5.0 Rating, A+ Rating” badges at the top carry the logos from the website template, one of them the template’s own “Construz” logo.
DYour name, the same everywhere
Page titles say “McBrien’s Chimney and Flue”, the footer says “McBrien’s Fire & Flue”, and Angi lists you as “McBriens Flue & Fire”. Google matches a business across sites partly by its name.
FSocial links
The Facebook, LinkedIn and X icons go to the front pages of facebook.com, linkedin.com and twitter.com, not to your pages. The Instagram icon goes to @mcbriensfire; the account we found in search is @mcbriensfireandflue.
DEnquiry form
Name, email, phone and a message. ⭐ Good that it is on every page. It does not ask the town or zip, the type of job, or for a photo, so every enquiry needs a call back to find out if it is a sweep or a rebuild.
A-Offers and hours
⭐ Hours, 24-hour emergency service, and discounts for first responders, military and seniors are all on the home page. That is more than most.

⭐ Every failure above is fixable without throwing the site away. Your services, hours, offers and reviews are already written.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed (Google’s speed test refused our requests that day), your Google Business Profile and Google reviews, Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor and BBB pages, and anything inside an ads account.
