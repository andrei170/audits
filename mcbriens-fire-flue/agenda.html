The call
First call · date and time to be confirmed · about 45 minutes
  1. You first · 10 min
    The business today, the jobs you want more of, and what “crushing the competition” looks like for you.
  2. What a customer finds today · 10 min
    Your site, your search results, and the other chimney companies they find (the audit, competitors).
  3. Your Google plan · 10 min
    Google profile, reviews, Local Services Ads, Google Ads and SEO, in order (the plan).
  4. The demo site · 5 min
    What your pages could look like (demo).
  5. Your questions and ours · 10 min
    The list below.
  6. Next step ·
    Only if it makes sense to you.
Questions we want to ask you
1. Which jobs do you want more of: rebuilds and masonry, liners, cleanings and inspections, or all of them?
2. What is a typical job worth for each, roughly?
3. Which towns do you want work in, and how far will you drive for a sweep compared with a rebuild?
4. Where does your work come from today: Google, HomeAdvisor and Angi, word of mouth, or something else?
5. Do you have a Google Business Profile, who controls it, and how many Google reviews does it have?
6. Are you paying for leads anywhere now, and how are they?
7. How many more jobs a month could you and your crew take on this season?
8. Who answers the phone when you are on a roof, and what happens to the calls you miss?
9. Are you CSIA or NCSG certified, and is your NH licence and insurance something we can show?
10. What would you need to see in the first month to know it is working?
