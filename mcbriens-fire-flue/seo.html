⭐ The pattern

The AI answers named firms that appear in directories with checkable reviews (Angi, BBB, Birdeye, the chimney safety and chimney sweep guild directories) and whose pages say plainly what they do and in which town. Every one of those is something you can add.

Three questions on one AI search tool on one day is a snapshot, not a measurement of every assistant.

What is holding the site back
  • Three pages: Home, Service and Contact. Nothing on its own page for sweeping, inspections, liners, rebuilds, caps or masonry, so there is nothing for a “chimney liner Concord” search to land on.
  • No town is named in the text of any page. The descriptions say “New England”. Hooksett, Manchester, Bedford and Concord do not appear.
  • The page titles say “McBrien’s Chimney and Flue”, not McBrien’s Fire & Flue, so Google sees two names for one business.
  • The home page has six main headings (“Family Owned &”, “Operated”, “Complete Chimney Services”…). Search engines expect one that says what the page is about.
  • No business details in the code for search engines and AI to read: business type, address, phone, opening hours and the towns you cover. There is only a general “organisation” tag.
  • Your Angi and HomeAdvisor listings place you in Vermont, Massachusetts and Maine. AI tools read those listings.
  • Your site links to @mcbriensfire on Instagram; your active account is @mcbriensfireandflue.
What we would do
Pages that answer the exact question a homeowner asks
  • A page per service: sweeping and cleaning, inspections, stainless liners, rebuilds, caps and crowns, masonry repair.
  • A page per town you cover, starting with Hooksett, Manchester, Bedford and Concord.
  • One clear title, description and heading on every page, and your business details in the code.
  • Your name, address and phone made the same everywhere, with the wrong states taken off Angi and HomeAdvisor.
  • A review request after every job, so reviews keep building on Google.
  • Listings where AI looks: BBB, the chimney safety and guild directories if you hold the certifications, Birdeye and the main directories.
  • Short answer pages for questions people ask: “How often should a chimney be swept?”, “Do I need a liner for a wood stove?”

⭐ Why it matters alongside ads: ads stop the moment you stop paying. These pages keep bringing calls, and they make every ad cheaper because Google rates the landing page higher.
