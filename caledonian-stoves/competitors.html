Who is hiring stove and HETAS engineers in Scotland
Where we looked (17 November 2026)What we found
Totaljobs: stove installer jobs in ScotlandIt reported 5 matches. None was a stove job: three fire engineer posts (Glasgow and Edinburgh), a heating engineer in Glasgow advertised at forty-five to forty-seven thousand a year through an agency, and a heat pump installer in Glasgow.
Reed: stove installer jobs, UK-wideNo stove installer jobs. The results were gas engineers, kitchen fitters, a glazing installer, a multi-skilled joiner, heat pump and HVAC installers.
Nine central-belt stove firms’ own websitesEight have no careers page at all. Calido in Stirling has a Vacancies page with nothing on it.

We searched what anyone could see without logging in. Indeed asked us to sign in, so it is not included.

What this means for you
1Nobody in your trade is visibly hiring, which means the people you want are not job-hunting.

There is no live advert for a stove fitter in Scotland on either board, and no stove firm near you advertises a job on its own site. The engineers you want are already working, mostly for someone else. They will not see a job board. They might see a Facebook or Instagram advert showing a listed-building job in their own area, and then look you up.

Source: Totaljobs and Reed searches, and nine firms’ websites, 17 November 2026.

2Your reputation beats theirs, and none of them puts heritage work first.

Your 5.0 from 74 is the best score in the group; Calido (4.9 from 127) and The Glasgow Stove Centre (4.9 from 112) have more reviews but a lower score. Not one of the nine sites we read mentions listed buildings. The work that sets you apart for a craftsman is not being shown by anyone, including you.

Source: Google Maps and each firm’s home page, 17 November 2026.

3Most of them are showrooms. You are not.

Six of the seven firms in the table sell from a showroom or shop. You do home visits and the work itself. For a skilled engineer, that difference is the job: building, not selling boxes.

Source: Each firm’s home page and Google categories, 17 November 2026.

4The adjacent trades are paying well and hiring.

Heating and heat pump engineers in Glasgow are being advertised for. A good joiner, stonemason or heating engineer who wants more craft in his work is a realistic second pool, if you would train the right one on stoves.

Source: Totaljobs and Reed results, 17 November 2026.

5Only one of them measures its adverts properly.

Scottish Stove Centre runs Tag Manager, a Google Ads tag and a Meta pixel. Calido runs Google Analytics 4 and a Google Ads tag. The rest, like you, measure little or nothing.

Source: Home page code of each site, 17 November 2026. A tag shows a firm is set up to measure or advertise, not what it spends.
