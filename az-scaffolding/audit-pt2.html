What you said you needed, and where it stands
1. “Get more work.” Another 5 to 10 jobs a week.
What we found now (10 November 2026)

The new site gives people far more reasons to call: town pages, reviews, real projects and a proper quote form. But nothing on the site records a visit, a call or a form, so neither of us can see whether it is bringing in more work, or which pages do. We found no sign of ads running from the site either (we did not check Google’s or Meta’s ad libraries).

What fixes it

Add tracking first: calls from the site, form enquiries and which town or service page they came from. After a few weeks you have a real starting number. Only then is it worth talking about ads, aimed at the towns and job types you want.

2. “I’d probably say more big.” More of the bigger jobs.
What we found now (10 November 2026)

The site now has pages for Commercial, Industrial, New Build, Temporary Roof and Edge Protection. On the homepage and services page we found no mention of NASC, CHAS, Constructionline, SMAS, TG20, RAMS, handover certificates or insurance figures. A main contractor checking you out for a package usually looks for those.

What fixes it

If you hold them, a short compliance section and a one-page sheet they can download: cards held, memberships, insurance figures, how you inspect and hand over. Your web person can add it; it does not need a new site.

3. “I need a new website. I’ve got it in the pipeline.”
What we found now (10 November 2026)

Done. See the table above. The one form on the site is on the contact page, and it asks job type and location but not height or how long the scaffold is needed.

What fixes it

Small additions: the same quote form on the homepage, and optional height and duration fields so you can price before you ring back.

4. “You actually got to get the jobs done here as well.”
What we found now (10 November 2026)

Agreed. More enquiries are only worth something if they are answered and followed up. Today there is no way to see how many calls are missed or how many quotes go quiet.

What fixes it

Call tracking that shows missed calls, and a simple list of open quotes with a reminder to follow up. This works with or without ads.

5. “Maybe another two, at least two.” Part 2 scaffolders.
What we found now (10 November 2026)

The new site has no jobs or careers page (14 pages and 3 blog posts in its sitemap).

What fixes it

If you are still hiring, a short “work with us” page saying what you need (Part 2, two years’ experience) gives anyone who rings about work somewhere to look.
