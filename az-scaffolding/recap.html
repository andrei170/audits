What you told us on 25 August
  • You have been going 10 years, covering most of Yorkshire, with a team of eight.
  • Most work is repeat customers you have worked for over the years, plus some people who find you on Google. You had not done any marketing and had not worked with anyone to get more jobs.
  • Around 20 jobs a week: at least ten small ones, the rest bigger. Jobs run from about £400 to £10,000, and a lot of your work is £2k to £4k jobs for roofers.
  • On what you wanted: “Get more work.” You said you could take another 5 to 10 jobs a week, and “I’d probably say more big”, while still happy to take the smaller ones as fill-in work.
  • On hiring: “Maybe another two, at least two.” Part 2 scaffolders with at least a couple of years’ experience.
  • If you got 10 enquiries you would expect to turn “at least five” into jobs.
  • “I need a new website. I’ve got it in the pipeline.” You said Chris at Freedom Search was working on it.
What we showed you
  • The August audit of your old site (22 out of 100). The homepage had no phone number or form, none of your accreditations or insurance were on the site, and the contact form had no phone field. On the homepage point you said “No, I didn’t know that, no.” On insurance: “we do. it’s not there either.”
  • A demo site with a quote form asking what the job is, height, how long for and the address. You said “That looks good mate, looks good.”
  • A rough on-screen illustration of how ads might work using your own close rate and job values. That was an illustration, not a forecast.
  • Results from other clients, and the ad packages we offer.
What you asked
  • How people were finding you on Google, given Google already shows a call button for your number.
  • Whether the audit was about the website only (it was; ads had not been covered at that point).
  • Whether it would be better for us to speak to your website person.
  • “What’s the name of these companies then? … can I check them out?” about the client results.
  • The costs, and what it would all involve.
What we agreed
  • “Just send me some details over in an email or a message.” We emailed you that evening (25 August) with the audit, the demo and our details.
  • You said you do not decide on the spot: “I need time to have a think, have a look, check it out.”
  • A short follow-up call the next day with a free competitor analysis, and the offer of a client’s number to ring. That follow-up call did not happen, so this is our first proper catch-up since.
What today is for
  1. Hear what has happened in the ten weeks since: the new site, the hiring, and how busy you are.
  2. Go through what the new site does well now and the few gaps left (Audit Pt.2).
  3. Show where you now sit against the local firms we checked (Competitors Pt.2).
  4. Decide together whether anything else is worth doing now, or not.
