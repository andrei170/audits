What it means
1. You have gone from behind to ahead on the basics.

In August you were the only one of the four with no accreditation on the site, and one of two without tap-to-call. Of the three sites that load today, you have by far the most town pages (eight; EBN has since added one for Wakefield), you are the only one showing a review count, and your form asks the job type and location (EBN’s does too). For the wider local field from Google Maps, see Competitors.

2. Your credentials list is still the shortest.

Yorkshire Netting names CHAS and TG20, and EBN names TG20 and public liability. You name CISRS and “fully insured”. If you hold more, putting them on the site costs nothing.

3. You are now the only one of the three with nothing counting visits.

In August we found no tracking on any of the four sites. Today Yorkshire Netting carries Google Tag Manager and EBN has Google’s Site Kit plugin installed. We cannot see what they do with it, but they have the means to count, and you do not yet.

4. Nobody has a quote form on the homepage, and Maine’s site has gone.

All three live sites send people to a contact page to enquire. Maine’s website (mainescaffolding.co.uk) did not load on 4 November and again on 10 November because the address no longer resolves. In August we only read its Castleford page, and a directory listing places a Maine Scaffolding Ltd in Haslemere, Surrey, so it may never have been a local firm.

Sources and limits
  • Read on 10 November 2026 (first read 4 November): each firm’s homepage, sitemap and contact page (a-zscaffoldingltd.co.uk, yorkshirenettingandscaffoldingltd.co.uk, ebnscaffolding.co.uk). The August comparison was read on 25 August 2026.
  • mainescaffolding.co.uk was tried twice, including through Google’s public DNS, and did not resolve. The Haslemere listing is on construction.co.uk; we have not confirmed it is the same firm.
  • “Named on the site” means the word appeared on the pages we read. We have not checked any register, and a firm may hold things it does not publish.
  • We did not check Google’s or Meta’s ad libraries, search rankings or review platforms, so who is running ads is not assessed.
