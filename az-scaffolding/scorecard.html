How the grade works
  • Each check is scored 0 to 100 from what we measured on your site and on Google Maps on 10 November 2026, and each finding quotes the figure. The overall score is the average of the 13 graded checks.
  • Page speed is shown as not assessed and left out of the score, because we did not run a full speed test. Nothing is guessed.
  • “After” is the score these same checks would get if the fixes in the audit were made (and only if you hold the accreditations). It grades the website, not results: it is not a forecast of calls, jobs or rankings.
  • The August audit scored your old site 22 out of 100 on a different, 12-check list, so the two numbers are not like for like.
  • Grade bands: A 90+, A- 80+, B 70+, C 60+, D 45+, F below 45.
