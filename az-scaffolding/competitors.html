What it means
1. You own Pontefract and Knottingley in Google Maps.

First for “scaffolding Pontefract”, “scaffolders Pontefract” and “scaffolding Knottingley”, with 4.7 from 50 reviews. Only Ackworth Scaffolding shows more Google reviews (69) in the results we read.

2. save with scaffolding is the one to watch.

It appeared in all seven searches and was first for Castleford and Normanton, ahead of you in both. Its site says it is based in Pontefract, has town pages for Pontefract, Castleford, Wakefield, Leeds, Doncaster and York, and a quote form on its homepage.

3. Working Platforms is set up to advertise.

It is the only firm here with a Google Ads tag on its site, alongside Google Tag Manager and a reviews widget. We cannot see whether it is running ads or what it spends, only that it can measure them. You currently have no tracking at all.

4. Your credentials list is the shortest.

Four of the eight name TG20; Methley names NASC; Yorkshire Netting names CHAS; Leeds Scaffolding names SafeContractor. You name CISRS and “fully insured”. If you hold more, it costs nothing to say so.

5. Wakefield is the gap.

You do not appear in the 10 Maps results for “scaffolding Wakefield”, while save with scaffolding (8th) and Leeds Scaffolding (4th) do. Your Wakefield page exists but reads the same as the other town pages.

  • Ratings and review counts are as Google Maps showed them on 10 November 2026. Where Maps showed no rating we say so.
  • Websites read on 10 November 2026: each firm’s homepage and sitemap (savewithscaffolding.com, workingplatformsltd.co.uk, gorillaaccessltd.co.uk, ackworthscaffolding.co.uk, leedsscaffolding.com, methleyscaffolding.co.uk, yorkshirenettingandscaffoldingltd.co.uk, ebnscaffolding.co.uk).
