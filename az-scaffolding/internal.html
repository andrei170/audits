Questions to ask, in order
  1. “What has happened since August?” Let him talk. Busy season? Did the bigger jobs come?
  2. “The new site looks good. Who built it?” Footer says Kustom Dezign, but on the call he named Chris at Freedom Search. Same person or a different firm? Are they doing anything ongoing (SEO, ads, Google profile)?
  3. “Has anyone run ads for you since, or have you tried anything else?”
  4. “Did you get the two scaffolders you were hiring?” (Part 2, 2+ years.) Capacity decides whether 5 to 10 more jobs a week is real.
  5. “Has the new site brought you calls you can point to?” He will not know. That is the tracking opening.
  6. “Which accreditations do you actually hold, and your PL and EL figures?” (He has only ever confirmed insurance.)
  7. “What kind of jobs do you want more of now?” Bigger domestic vs commercial vs new build.
  8. “Who answers the phone when you are up a scaffold?”
  9. “Did you get a chance to look at what we sent in August, and ring Sam?”
