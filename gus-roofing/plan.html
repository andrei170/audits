The order we would do it in

The free things come first. The demo website shows Stage 2 working.

1Stage 1: the free fixes, this week

So the people who already hear about you can find you and trust you.

  • Set up (or claim and verify) your Google Business Profile as a Roofing contractor in Long Beach, with your photos, hours and service area.
  • Ask your last 10 to 20 happy customers for a Google review, with a direct link by text. You start from zero; local rivals have 29 to 113.
  • Put CA License #1154629, C-39 Roofing on every page of the site.
  • Fill in or remove the Legal Notice and Privacy Policy templates, and fix the spelling mistake on the home page.
2Stage 2: a website that wins the estimate

Shown working in the demo.

  • A page for each job: roof replacement, leaks and repairs, inspections, naming the roof types you do.
  • Your photos captioned with the city and the job, and your license and reviews on every page.
  • An estimate form on every page that asks the job, roof type, how soon and the ZIP, with an honest yes or no by area.
  • A page for each city you want more work in, and proper Google details in the page code.
  • Google Analytics and call tracking, so you can see how many people look and call, and from where.
3Stage 3: do not lose the ones who ask

So a request on a busy day still turns into a job.

  • Every enquiry gets a text back straight away, even when you are on a roof.
  • Every estimate gets a short follow-up a few days later and again in a couple of weeks, in your words, so nobody slips through.
4Stage 4: steady the slow months

Only once Stages 1 to 3 are working, and only if you want more work.

  • A small, local, measured test of Google ads (including Google’s Local Services listing for roofers) in the ZIP codes you choose, pointed at the jobs you want. You see what it costs per job before deciding to keep it.
What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of calls, jobs or a Google ranking. What this does is make sure that someone who hears your name, or searches for a roofer near them, can find you, see your license and your work, and ask for an estimate in a way that tells you straight away whether the job is worth the drive.
