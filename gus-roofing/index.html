Behind the pages
⚠ Code and set-up
  • Platform: IONOS "MyWebsite Now" (a WordPress builder). Domain registered October 21, 2026; pages last updated October 24, 2026. So the site is about four weeks old.
  • Tracking: no Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Meta pixel or Google Ads tag. Only the host’s SiteAnalytics, after a cookie banner that mentions "advertisements" although no advertising tools are on the site.
  • Template leftovers: a European-style consent box on the form, and Legal Notice and Privacy Policy pages still showing their "Please enter" placeholders.
  • Headings: Contact and Gallery have no main heading. Images: 19 of 19 without a description.
  • Google details: the business tag holds only the web address, not your name, phone, city or license.
