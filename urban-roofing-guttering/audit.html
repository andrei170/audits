The three things that matter most

You already have a working site, a page for every service and town, and a strong review base, so this is a short list on purpose. These three, in order, are what stands between a quiet spell and a lever you can pull to fill the diary.

1. Put measurement in (analytics and conversion tracking)

Before any money goes out on adverts, make every visit and every enquiry countable. That means a Google Analytics (GA4) property and tracking the enquiries as conversions, so you can see where work comes from and which advert or search produced a job. You already have a tag container on the site, so this is a short job, and it is what lets a paid channel be judged on booked roofs instead of spent blind.

2. Build one focused page for the big jobs

Your site is good for being found. A paid advert needs somewhere more focused to land: a single page about one high-value job (a new roof, a full re-roof) built to turn the click into an enquiry. It sits alongside your site and whoever looks after it, it does not replace anything, and there is a worked example of it in this pack.

3. Run a measured local campaign you can turn on and off

With measurement in place and a page to point at, a tracked local campaign brings in roofing enquiries you can measure to the pound, when you want more work. You see the cost per enquiry and how many become booked jobs, and you turn the spend up when the diary has room and down when it does not. That is the tap a firm with a ready team and a quiet spell actually needs.
