Two different races

How a Leeds or Bradford homeowner chooses a roofer

This page is not about which West Yorkshire roofer is the most skilled. It is about the fact that an emergency repair and a planned new roof are won in two different ways, and about one quiet place where even a good, well-reviewed site like yours still leaves work on the table. Understanding that is the whole reason a measured channel matters so much for you specifically, when the team and the gear are ready and the diary has room.

The pattern. A leaking roof in a storm is an emergency, so it goes to whoever shows up first and answers the phone, and your freephone number, same-day message and strong reviews do well there. A new roof or a full re-roof is a planned purchase of several thousand pounds, so the homeowner behaves differently: they look at two or three firms over days or weeks, they read reviews, they want to see real jobs, a guarantee and the trust marks, and they still reward a fast, confident reply. The quiet problem is this. Both of those depend on the customer finding you first, through search, word of mouth or the time of year. When that flow dips, and the setter says it has slowed lately, you have no way to make more of it appear. A rival who runs a measured advert can open the tap on a slow week and fill it, then turn it down when they are busy. You cannot, yet. On a quiet month, that difference is the whole month.

What this means for the quiet spell you are in

You are not short of work because the site is broken or the name is weak. It is live, well reviewed, and it wins the urgent repairs well. The quiet spell bites because every source of work you have depends on the customer finding you first, and when that flow dips you have no lever to pull. The fix is not a rebuild and it is not SEO, which your current provider already handles. It is to put tracking in so any spend can be judged on booked jobs, build one focused page for the big roofs, and run a measured local campaign you can turn up when the diary has room.
