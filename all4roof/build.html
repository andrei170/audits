The website
Every page in English and Spanish
  • Your own address. all4roofs.com, open to Google, with one clear language switch at the top of every page.
  • Tap to call on every page, and a call button that stays on screen on phones.
  • An inspection form that works, in both languages, asking the few things you need: name, phone, address, what is wrong, and photos if they have them.
  • A radio page at all4roofs.com/radio with its own number, so radio calls are counted.
  • Service pages: roof replacement, roof repair, hail and insurance claims (with who handles your inspections and supplements), gutters, and commercial and multifamily kept as they are.
  • Town pages for the places you want work, named by you.
  • About Grisel, in both languages: an owner who closes every deal herself and speaks her customers’ language.
  • Real reviews and real job photos only, added as they come in.
  • Everything Google reads set up properly: one main heading per page, titles with the town and job, a description that says what you do, and markup telling Google you are a roofer at your address.
The counting
  • Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager, set up to count calls and forms, not just visits.
  • A separate number for the radio, and later one per ad source.
  • A simple monthly view you can read in two minutes: calls, forms, jobs, and where each came from.
How we would work with you
  • We do the building, writing and translating. You check the Spanish reads the way you would say it.
  • About an hour a week from you: photos from jobs, a few answers, and pressing send on review requests.
  • Ads only start once the counting works, so from day one you can see what you get.
