What it means for you
⚠ Where you are behind
  • All three answer on their own web address and let Google read their site. Yours does neither yet.
  • All three have 5 to 7 tappable phone links on the home page. You have none.
  • All three have Google tracking of some kind, and Excel Roofing has a Google Ads tag, so they can count what their ads bring in. You cannot count your radio.
  • Excel Roofing, in your own ZIP code, says on its site it has 612 Google reviews.
⭐ Where you can win
  • Spanish. None of the three has a Spanish page, and in two web searches we found no Denver roofer advertising a Spanish website. You serve Spanish speakers yourself; your site can say so, in Spanish.
  • The radio. You already have a weekly voice in front of listeners. A radio page and a radio phone number turn that into something you can count.
  • You. An owner who closes every deal herself, in the customer’s language, is a story the bigger firms cannot tell.
⚠ Limits
  • We read home pages and checked for a Spanish address; a firm could have Spanish elsewhere on its site.
  • Review counts are self-reported by each firm and were not checked on Google.
  • We did not check their ad spend, which nobody can see from outside.
