Grisel, thank you for asking us to call you back. You told our colleague Josh that you are doing about two or three houses a week, that this year has been slower than last, and that you could take on a lot more. Your customers come from your Friday radio slot, and most of them are Spanish-speaking homeowners.

So this pack follows one of your radio listeners: they hear you on Friday, they look you up, and then what happens. Nothing here is priced.

⚠ What changed from our first pack

Our first pack (29 September) read your advertising as online ads and talked about commercial buyers. That was our mistake: your advertising is radio and your work is mostly homeowners. This version is rebuilt around that, and everything was checked again on 6 November.

⚠ Found today (6 November 2026)
  • Typing all4roofs.com sends people to all4roofs.wpcomstaging.com, a WordPress staging address.
  • The site asks Google not to read any of its pages (its robots.txt says “Disallow: /”).
  • Your number is on every page but cannot be tapped on a phone, on any of the 12 pages.
  • The Request inspection and Contact us buttons open a page with no form.
  • No Spanish anywhere on the site.
  • Nothing records which visitors or calls came from your radio slot.
⭐ Already working
  • Your radio slot. As you told us, it has brought your customers for about two years.
  • You close the deals yourself, and you serve the Spanish-speaking community directly.
  • Your phone, address and email are on every page, and there is a Meet the owner page about you.
  • You already do insurance work, with someone handling inspections and supplements.

⭐ Grade today: F, 47 out of 100, on 18 checks we could actually measure. See every grade.

The short version

Radio is your engine, and it works. The leak is what happens after the radio: a listener types your address and lands on a staging site Google has been told not to read, with no number to tap, no form behind the inspection button, and nothing in Spanish. Nobody can count which calls came from Friday.

Fix that, add a page and a phone number just for radio listeners, put the whole site in Spanish too, and then use Google and Facebook to reach the Spanish and English-speaking homeowners who never heard the show.

The order we would do it in
  1. Fix the front door. all4roofs.com shows your site on your own address, and Google is allowed to read it.
  2. Make it easy to reach you. A tappable number on every page and an inspection form that works, in English and Spanish.
  3. Count the radio. A page at all4roofs.com/radio and its own phone number, read out on air, so you can see the calls each Friday brings.
  4. Spanish version of the site. Every page in both languages.
  5. Google listing and reviews. Your Google Business Profile tidied, and every finished job asked for a review, in both languages.
  6. Then ads. Google search, the Google ads at the very top that charge per lead, and Facebook and Instagram, in English and Spanish, with every call counted.
