What you told Josh
In your words (as you told us in September)
  • “Working like two houses a week.” About two or three jobs a week.
  • Last year was very busy; this year is “kind of slow a little bit”. You want more work and can take on a lot more.
  • Your customers come from your advertisement, which is radio. Every Friday you talk on the radio, usually for 2 minutes, sometimes 5 or 6 when the host gives you more time. You have done it for about two years.
  • You tried Angie’s List about three years ago and did not like it.
  • You close the deals yourself, mostly with the Latino and Hispanic community. For other customers you send a bilingual colleague.
  • Someone else handles inspections and supplements, so you do insurance work.
  • You spend about one hour a week on marketing.
  • Mornings are best for you. And you said you do not know what SEO is, so this pack does not use the word.
The call
Call time to be arranged · about 45 minutes
  1. You first. How a normal week looks, what a good week looks like, and how many more jobs you want.
  2. The radio. What happens after a Friday slot: how many calls, and what listeners are told to do.
  3. What a listener finds online today. The audit and the other roofers.
  4. The plan. Fix the front door, count the radio, Spanish site, Google listing and reviews, then ads (the plan).
  5. The demo site. English and Spanish (demo).
  6. Questions and next step. Only if it makes sense to you.
Questions we want to ask you
1. Which radio station is your Friday slot on, and is the show in Spanish, English or both?
2. After a Friday slot, roughly how many people call you that day and the following week?
3. On air, do you give your phone number, your website, or both?
4. Of your two or three houses a week, how many are full replacements, repairs, or hail and insurance claims?
5. How many more houses a week could you and your crew take on?
6. Which towns and neighbourhoods do you want more work in?
7. What did you not like about Angie’s List?
8. Do you have a Google Business Profile (the listing on Google Maps), and who looks after it?
9. Roughly how many Google reviews do you have today, and do you ask customers for them?
10. Who built your website, and who has the login for it?
11. Who answers the phone when you are on a roof or with a customer, and what happens to missed calls?
12. How do Spanish-speaking customers prefer to reach you: a call, a text, or WhatsApp?
