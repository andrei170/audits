Freight in one minute
  • He is a freight forwarder: a travel agent for goods. He owns no ships, planes or lorries. He books space with the companies that do, handles customs and paperwork, and charges one door-to-door price.
  • A lead is a quote request (from where, to where, what, how much, by when). A job is a shipment. The value is in businesses that ship every month.
  • He keeps the gap between what carriers charge him and what he charges, roughly 10 to 25%, plus customs fees. Most of the money passes through to carriers.
  • Words: FCL / LCL (full or shared container) · customs clearance · spot vs contract rate · buy rate / sell rate · Incoterms (who pays for what) · demurrage (port fees for delays) · TEU (one container).
Your three openers
  1. The email link on every page opens benjamin@digiswift.nl, not his own address.
  2. His one Google ad runs from his hosting company’s account, has a typo, and lands on a homepage with no quote form or tracking.
