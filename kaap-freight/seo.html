Not named in any of three answers

We asked three questions the way a buyer would ask an AI assistant:

  • “Best freight forwarder in London for small businesses shipping to India”: named Mango Logistics Group, Alinnza Trading and John Good Logistics.
  • “Which freight forwarding company in London should I use for import customs clearance”: named Milky Way Logistics, AAA Freight Services and ST Express Cargo, and told the buyer to check the BIFA member directory.
  • “Who can ship my household goods from London to India”: named PSS Removals, John Mason and Globe Moving, and pointed to Trustpilot reviews.

⭐ The pattern: AI answers name firms that appear in directories, have checkable reviews and have pages that say plainly what they do and on which lanes. Those are all things you can add.

Three questions on one AI search tool on one day is a snapshot, not a measurement of every assistant.

What is holding the site back
  • Six pages. Nothing for customs clearance, air freight, sea freight, or any lane.
  • No page descriptions, generic titles (“Homepage - KAAP FREIGHT”), and 4 of 6 pages with no main heading.
  • No business details in the code (business type, address, phone, services) for search engines and AI to read.
  • No reviews anyone can check, and no memberships shown.
  • A leftover template page in the sitemap and no updates since August 2025.
What we would do
Pages that answer the exact question a buyer asks
  1. A page per service: import, export, relocation, customs clearance, air freight, sea freight.
  2. A page per lane you are strong on, e.g. China to UK, UK to India, UK to the Middle East, with real transit times and the documents needed.
  3. Proper titles, descriptions and headings on every page, and business details in the code.
  4. A Google Business Profile and a review request after every delivery, so reviews build on Google and Trustpilot.
  5. Listings where AI looks: BIFA (if you are a member), Clutch, GoodFirms and freight directories.
  6. Short answer pages for the questions buyers ask: “Do I need an EORI number?”, “How long does sea freight to India take?”

⭐ Why it matters alongside ads: ads stop the moment you stop paying. These pages keep bringing enquiries, and they make every ad cheaper because Google rates the landing page higher.
