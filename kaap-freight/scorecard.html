Digital presence scorecard
F
26 / 100
0 of 16 passed. 15 failed.
A real, registered London business with a site that looks the part but is not built to win quotes yet: it is hard to contact on a phone, invisible in search, and cannot measure ads.
FYour email link
🔴 The footer shows sales@kaapfreightservices.com, but tapping it opens an email to benjamin@digiswift.nl, a different domain, most likely left over from when the site was built. It is the same on every page we read. Anyone who emails you that way may never reach you.
FFirst screen on a phone
Checked in a 450-pixel-wide browser: the first screen is the big port photo and the logo. No headline saying what you do, no quote button and no phone number until well down the page.
FTap-to-call
⭐ WhatsApp is linked on every page, which is good. But neither phone number is a tap-to-call link on any of the six pages (0 found), so on a phone they have to be copied by hand.
FQuote form
The form asks only name, email, phone and a message, sits on the service and contact pages but not on the homepage, and says “Response within 48 hours on business days”. It never asks the lane, the cargo, the weight or the dates, so every quote starts with a back-and-forth.
FPage titles and headings
Titles read “Homepage - KAAP FREIGHT”, “Import - KAAP FREIGHT”. No page has its own description for Google, and 4 of the 6 pages have no main heading (homepage, Import, Export, Relocation).
FPages to be found on
Six pages in total: three services plus home, about and contact. No page for customs clearance, air or sea freight, or any lane (China, India, the Middle East), although the reviews on your own site mention all of them.
FShowing up for freight searches
🔴 Not in the first nine or ten results for any of nine freight searches we ran (DuckDuckGo, UK). First for your own name, which shows the site is indexed.
FNamed in AI answers
🔴 Not named in any of three AI-assistant style answers about London freight forwarders, customs clearance or moving goods to India. Competitors and directories were named instead.
FReviews a buyer can check
Five testimonials on the site, each a first name and initial. No link to a Google or Trustpilot profile, so a careful buyer cannot check them anywhere.
DTracking
⭐ Google Analytics 4 is installed (good). ⚠ No Google Ads conversion tag, no Meta pixel and no LinkedIn tag, so no ad can be told which clicks became quote requests.
FAds
One Google search ad found, run from an account named “1&1 IONOS SE”, not your own. Generic wording with a typo (“wheter importing”), pointing at the homepage. Nothing on Meta (searched “kaap freight”, UK).
FBusiness details for Google (schema)
Yoast adds basic website details. No business type, address, phone, opening hours or service list in the code, which is what search engines and AI assistants read.
FHousekeeping
A template page called “example-posts” is still in the sitemap, the Services menu link points to “#diensten” (Dutch for “services”), 7 of 12 homepage images have no description, and no page has changed since August 2025.
FWeight on a phone
The pages themselves come back in 0.7 to 1.3 seconds, but the homepage background is a video: 14.2 MB on phones and 42.7 MB on desktop. On mobile data that is the first thing a visitor waits for. ⚠ Google’s full speed test refused our requests, so this is not a Core Web Vitals score.
FGoogle profile and reviews online
🔴 Our searches found no Google Business Profile, Trustpilot page or review listing for KAAP (Trustpilot returned nothing for your domain). ⚠ Google Maps itself cannot be read by our tools, so check this together on the call.
FForm set-up
The form has reCAPTCHA (good, it stops spam), but it does not send people to your /thank-you/ page, which exists in your sitemap and is never used. That page is the usual way an ad platform counts a quote request.

⭐⭐ The good news: almost everything here is quick to fix. The business is real and the services are clear. What is missing is the machinery that turns a visitor into a quote request and tells you where it came from.

⚠ What this grade is not: Google rankings, Google reviews, your Google Business Profile and a full speed score could not be read today, so they are left out rather than guessed at.
