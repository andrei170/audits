What this means for KAAP
Do not fight the per-kilo parcel war. Win the business shipper.
  • The UK to India parcel market is a price race: Cargo Force and Sea Cargo to India lead with £ per kilo. Competing there on price is a race to the bottom, and those buyers rarely ship again.
  • Business freight is different. A company shipping pallets every month buys reliability, paperwork done right and someone who answers. That is what your site already says you do, so the ads and pages should be aimed squarely at them.
  • Every leader collects the shipment details up front with a calculator or a detailed form. Yours asks for a message.
  • Every leader shows reviews a buyer can check. Yours cannot be checked yet.
  • ⭐ The gap you can own: none of the five puts a named person and fast WhatsApp replies at the front. You already have WhatsApp on every page.
⚠ What this page does not contain

The Transparency Centre shows how many ads a firm has run, not how much it spent or when. We did not check every competitor’s Meta ads; PSS and Speedy Freight have the Meta pixel on their sites, which means they can advertise to past visitors there.
