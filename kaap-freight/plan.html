What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ Updated after our calls on 22 and 27 October. UK importers first, laid out over six months, and no price per kilo anywhere. The goal is more quotes from UK importers and exporters who ship regularly, answered fast, followed up every time, and traced to the ad that brought them. You told us capacity is not the limit, so the plan is built to bring in enquiries. So the order is: stop losing enquiries, give ads somewhere good to land, run the ads, follow up automatically, then build search so you rely less on paid clicks over time.

1. Fix what is losing enquiries now · Week 1

Done for you on WordPress: the email link, tap-to-call, a headline and quote button on the first phone screen, the quote form on every page, and conversion tracking. Move ads into an account you own.

And make the site something a new contact trusts at first look: always up, your real team and story. You told us a customer went quiet when the site went down.

2. Landing pages and the quote form · Weeks 1 to 2

One landing page per campaign and the three-step quote form that asks lane, cargo, weight and dates, as in the sample website.

So every ad click lands on a page that matches what they searched.

3. Ads, starting where intent is highest · From week 2

Google Search for UK importers first, China to the UK by sea and by air (the speciality), then the wider Far East and India, with LinkedIn alongside it for operations, purchasing and supply chain managers. Then exporters, relocation on Google and Meta as its own lane, and retargeting.

Budget moves weekly to the campaign with the lowest cost per qualified quote.

4. CRM and follow-up, connected · Weeks 2 to 3

Your two spreadsheets become one pipeline: new contacts, follow-ups and open quotes in one place, with reminders. Then instant replies, quote follow-up, shipment updates and review requests, all working with email, which is how your customers already deal with you.

Fast replies and steady follow-up win quotes that would otherwise go quiet.

5. SEO and AI answers · Month 2 onwards

Pages per service and lane, business details in the code, a Google Business Profile, reviews and directory listings.

Enquiries that do not stop when the ad spend does.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. There is no conversion tracking on your site today, so there is no baseline to forecast from. We would rather show you real numbers from your own account a few weeks in.
