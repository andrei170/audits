Why the full build exists
  • On our call you offered us your WordPress login so we can clean everything up: the email link, tap-to-call and the quote form. Those website fixes are part of the full build, and are not in the other two options.
  • You told us new contacts book far less often than existing customers, and that a customer went quiet when your site went down. Ads bring a new contact to the website first, so the website and the follow-up decide whether that click turns into a booking.
  • Without the CRM, every new quote still lives in the spreadsheets and depends on you finding time to chase it.
  • Without the SEO and AI search work, you pay for every visit. With it, the lane pages can bring in importers who search, or who ask an AI assistant, without you paying for each visit.
  • You asked for us to hold your hand for a little while and train the team properly, from Andrei or Randy and nobody else. The full build is the only option with unlimited training calls and direct 1-to-1 access to Andrei: we stay with your team until you are ready to take the training wheels off.
