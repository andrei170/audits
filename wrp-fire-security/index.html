The three things to fix first, all free
⚠ 1. Google describes you as a £299 home alarm firm

The search description on your home page, which Google and Facebook show under your link, reads “Home or buisness alarms starting from £299, Home or buisness cctv systems starting from £350”. A facilities manager choosing a contractor for a warehouse reads that before anything else.

⚠ 2. Your About page is called “New Page”

It lives at /blank, the menu calls it “New Page”, and it has no main heading. Yet it holds your story, your directors, your BAFE certification and your testimonial from Redrock Automation. Renaming it and linking it properly costs nothing.

⚠ 3. Your phone number cannot be tapped

Your mobile is on the home page as plain text, so a buyer on a phone has to copy it. Your landline only appears in the search description.

Your site, page by page
wrpfireandsecurityltd.co.uk: a Wix site, home page plus About, booking and 5 service pages
  • ⭐ Good: one main heading per main page, accreditation logos (BAFE, FIA, SSAIB, ECS), a real testimonial, and service wording that shows real technical depth.
  • No commercial, sector or case study page. “Commercial” and “industrial” appear once, on the home page.
  • Service pages are short (79 to 161 words) and have no form or phone number on them.
  • The form asks first name, email, address, phone and a message. Nothing about the site, the systems or a maintenance contract.
  • The photo slideshow is captioned with file names (“Screenshot 2026-07-07”), with no write-up of any job.
  • No local business markup, and no page for any town you work in.
  • No Google tag, Google Ads tag or Meta pixel, so nothing records where visitors or enquiries come from.
