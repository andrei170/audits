What this means for you
You hold the credentials. Two of them show theirs better.
  • Argus puts NSI Gold and BAFE in its headline, with a Google reviews panel and a page per sector. That is what a facilities manager scans for. Your BAFE certification is on a page called “New Page”.
  • Lookout, a few miles from you, has a page for each nearby town and a case studies page. A buyer searching “fire alarm St Helens” has a page from them to land on, and none from you.
  • Argus can follow up its visitors on Facebook and Instagram, and both it and Lookout count their visitors. You cannot see who visited your site at all.
  • ⭐ The two smaller firms are no better online than you. The gap is with the firms that look like commercial contractors on screen, and you already do the work they describe.
