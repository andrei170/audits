What carries over from roofing

What is different, and why it changes the pitch
  1. Demand is legal, not optional. The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 makes every business premises have a “responsible person” who keeps fire safety equipment working. Businesses have to buy servicing.
  2. The money is in recurring contracts. Commercial fire alarms are serviced at least every 6 months (BS 5839-1). Emergency lighting: monthly tests and a yearly 3-hour test (BS 5266). Plus intruder alarm maintenance and monthly monitoring fees. Roughly £300 to £2k+ per site per year, renewing. This is the fix for his winter slump: service visits happen whatever the weather. He told River he has 3 steady rolling contracts and wants 2 to 3 more.
  3. The buyer is a business, not a homeowner: facilities managers, landlords and letting agents (HMOs), schools, care homes, warehouses, offices, retail, main contractors. Longer sales cycle, site survey, quote, sometimes a tender. Contractors are often asked to be on Constructionline, CHAS or SafeContractor.
  4. £299 home alarms are the low-margin end, which is why residential runs under 10% and commercial targets 30%. His Google listing currently sells the £299 end.
Words to use so you sound like you know it
  • Fire: fire alarm panel, detectors, call points, “L1 / L2” and “P1” (how much of the building is covered), commissioning, servicing, false alarms.
  • Security: intruder alarm “grade 2 / grade 3”, CCTV, access control, monitoring through an “ARC” (alarm receiving centre), police response “URN” (needs NSI or SSAIB).
  • Certifications: BAFE SP203-1 (fire alarms), NSI Gold / Silver and SSAIB (security), FIA (trade body).
  • Brands he may fit: Advanced, Kentec (fire panels), Texecom, Pyronix (alarms), Hikvision (CCTV). Ask, do not assume.

  8. “What does MyBuilder cost you, and what margin are those jobs at?”
  9. “Is the 30% margin gross or net?”
  10. “Who does the quotes and admin, you or Rosie?”
Do NOT ask or say
  • Do not give fire safety advice or tell him what the law requires of his clients. Use the law only as the reason demand exists.
  • Do not call him a fire risk assessor unless he says he does risk assessments.
  • Do not pitch more £299 home alarms or more leads in general. He converts 90% already; the gap is the type of work.
  • Do not promise a number of contracts or a turnover.
  • Do not bluff on standards or kit. If he uses a term you do not know: “Walk me through that, I want to get it right for your pages.”
  • Do not knock MyBuilder or word of mouth. Keep them; add the commercial channel on top.
How the pitch changes from roofing
  • Every install should become a contract that renews. Sell installs plus service contracts, not one-off jobs.
  • Search terms are lower volume, higher value: “fire alarm servicing [town]”, “fire alarm maintenance contract”, “BAFE installer [area]”, plus a page per sector.
  • Case studies beat star ratings: a facilities manager wants similar buildings and certificate numbers.
  • Outbound can work here: email or LinkedIn to facilities managers and letting agents.

Objections
“We get our work from word of mouth and previous connections.”Agree: it got him to a record year at ~90% conversion. But word of mouth does not choose the work, it brings whoever rings. And the person who hears about WRP still Googles WRP, and reads “home alarms from £299” and an About page called “New Page”. We make the referral land and add a commercial channel on top, so he is less reliant on others for the big jobs.
“Ads can’t win contracts.”Agree first: a cold ad never closes an 18-month install. Ads get WRP on the shortlist of facilities managers by job title, and follow-up closes. Then the fallback reply below.
“We’ve no time for this.” (15 min booked)Agree, so keep it tight: one question on the goal, one on margin, show the £299 Google line, then book the full call. Don’t try to cover the pack.
“MyBuilder fills the diary.”At what margin? Diary-filling residential is the under-10% work. Good for small diary-filler jobs, and keep it. But MyBuilder buyers are mostly domestic and price-shopping; it does not bring a managing agent with a 40-building maintenance contract. Different job.
“We’re busy enough / hand to mouth, reinvest everything.”Then start with the free fixes and the follow-up on quotes already out, which costs time not ad spend. Point at the Nov to January lull: that is when the follow-up and renewal work can be set up.
“Send me something to look at.”Send the hub link (assets.html), but book the next call before hanging up.
