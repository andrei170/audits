Questions for the call
1. What are your biggest jobs, and who were they for?A warehouse, a school, a housing association, a main contractor? The answer decides who the pages and ads are aimed at.
2. How did those big jobs find you?A contact, a previous client, a tender? That is the route we would add to, not replace.
3. From the first enquiry to a yes, how long does a commercial job usually take?And how many quotes are out with someone deciding right now?
4. What happens after you send a commercial quote?Who follows it up, how often, and where it is all kept.
5. What do commercial buyers ask you for before they say yes?Certificates, insurance, RAMS, references? Whatever they ask for should be on the website before they have to ask.
6. Can you confirm your certification numbers?BAFE SP203-1, SSAIB, FIA and ECS are shown on your site. We would show the numbers and link to the registers.
7. Do you have a Google Business Profile and Google reviews?We could not check Google Maps ourselves.
8. Who built your website, and who looks after it now?It is on Wix. We would rather fix what is there than start again.
