What we would do, in order

⭐ The first three steps cost nothing and you will see them change on your own site. Everything after that is measured, so you can see what is working and stop anything that is not.

1. Fix what Google says about you · Week 1, free

Replace the £299 home alarm search description with one about commercial fire and security, BAFE SP203-1 and 24/7 monitoring, and tidy the page titles.

⭐ We can draft it with you on the call. It changes the first line a buyer reads about you.

2. Bring your About page out of hiding · Week 1, free

Rename “New Page”, give it a proper address and heading, and put the Redrock Automation testimonial and your certifications on the home page.

Your best proof stops being invisible.

3. Make the number tappable · Week 1, free

Mobile and landline as tap-to-call links on every page.

A buyer on site with a phone in hand can ring you in one tap.

4. A commercial page and a site-survey form · Weeks 2 to 3

One page for commercial and industrial buyers: certifications with numbers, sectors you serve, write-ups of past commercial jobs (with the client’s permission), and a survey form that asks site type, systems and contract renewal date.

This is the page every commercial ad and LinkedIn post would point to.

5. Measurement · Week 2

Tracking on calls, forms and survey bookings, so every enquiry shows where it came from.

So you can see what works before you spend anything on ads.

6. The follow-up and renewal system · Weeks 3 to 4

Every quote on one screen with its value, follow-ups you approve, and reminders before service visits and contract renewals.

Starting with the quotes you already have out. Useful in the quiet winter months, when there is time to chase them.

7. Small, commercial-only ads · When the page and tracking are live

Commercial searches only with domestic blocked, LinkedIn by job title, and retargeting, all pointing at the commercial page, with a budget you set.

⚠ Only once tracking is in, so you can see exactly what you are getting.

How we would work with you
  • Your website, your accounts, your data. Everything is set up in your name and stays yours.
  • You see the numbers: every enquiry, survey and quote, and where it came from.
  • Start small and grow it only when it is working.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. Commercial work is lumpy and depends on your capacity and the contracts already out there. Anyone giving you a forecast before knowing those would be inventing it.
