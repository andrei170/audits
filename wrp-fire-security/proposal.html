The plan, week by week

⭐ Week one costs nothing, and you will see it change on your own site. Each step after that is measured, so you can see what is working and stop anything that is not.

Week 1 · Free fixes
  • Rewrite what Google says about you: replace the £299 home alarm description with commercial fire and security, BAFE SP203-1 and 24/7 monitoring. We can draft it with you on the call.
  • Bring the About page out of hiding: rename “New Page”, give it a proper address and heading, and put the Redrock Automation testimonial and your certifications on the home page.
  • Make both numbers tap-to-call on every page.

Why: The first line a buyer reads about you changes, and your best proof is visible.

Week 2 · Measurement and the enquiry form
  • Tracking on calls, forms and survey bookings, so every enquiry shows whether it came from the website, Google, MyBuilder or a referral.
  • The commercial enquiry form: site type, size, systems on site, current provider and contract end date.

Why: You can see what works before anything is spent on ads.

Weeks 2 to 3 · The commercial page
  • One page for commercial and industrial buyers, with a section for each sector you choose.
  • Your certifications with their numbers and links to the registers.
  • Two or three short job write-ups (with the client’s permission): site type, system fitted, size of job.

Why: Every ad, LinkedIn post and email afterwards points here.

Week 3 · The maintenance contract offer
  • A clear contract page: what each level includes, visit frequency, call-out times, and one “ask for a maintenance quote” button.
  • A list of systems you have already fitted, so each client can be offered a contract before winter.

Why: Service work booked ahead, which fills November to January.

Weeks 3 to 4 · Follow-up and renewals
  • Every quote on one screen with its value and stage.
  • Follow-up messages you approve, starting with the quotes you already have out.
  • Reminders before service visits and contract renewals.

Why: Quotes stop going quiet, and the winter gives time to chase them.

From week 5 · Small, commercial-only ads
  • Commercial searches only, with home and DIY searches blocked.
  • LinkedIn by job title for facilities, estates and property managers in the North West.
  • Retargeting of people who visited the commercial page. All with a budget you set.

Why: ⚠ Only once tracking is live, so you can see exactly what you are getting.

How we would work with you
  • Your website, your accounts, your data. Everything is set up in your name and stays yours.
  • You approve every message and every page before it goes live.
  • Start small and grow it only when it is working.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. Commercial work is lumpy and depends on your capacity and the contracts already out there, so any forecast made before knowing those would be invented. Week numbers are a working order, to agree together.
