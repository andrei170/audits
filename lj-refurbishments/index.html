That is common and not a problem, but a commercial buyer may ask which entity they contract with, so it is worth having a one-line answer ready.

Your website, page by page
What is working
  • 19 pages and 12 blog posts, all updated on 21 September 2026, built on GoDaddy by Sync My Media.
  • Town pages for carpentry, bespoke furniture and decorating in Pinner and Harrow, and they rank.
  • Real photos of your work: alcove units, media walls, bookcases, under-stairs storage, loft wardrobes, murals, and your workshop.
  • Tap-to-call on every page, an email on your own domain, and a Google Analytics tag.
What is missing
  • Nothing about refurbishments. No page for full refurbishments, kitchens, bathrooms, garage conversions, extensions or commercial work, and no refurbishment project shown start to finish.
  • The home page sells furniture. Its title is “Bespoke Wooden Furniture by Expert Carpenters”.
  • The forms ask name, email and phone only: no job type, postcode, project size or timescale.
  • No structured data, and 6 pages without a meta description.
  • Two identical H1 headings on the home page, and the Pinner carpenter page’s heading says Harrow.
  • Your name and photo are not on the About page.
  • No Meta pixel or Google Ads tag, so ads cannot be measured.

The gap that matters

101 reviews sit on TrustATrader and 7 on Google. Google’s map results are decided largely by the Google profile and its reviews, and the profile is filed under Carpenter, so it has no reason to show you for “house refurbishment” or “builders”.

What we could not measure

  • Checkatrade and Houzz: not checked; Checkatrade blocks automated readers.
