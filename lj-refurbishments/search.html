Why they could not work as well as they should
  • Most ran for under three days. Meta needs time and steady spend to learn who responds.
  • No Meta pixel on the website, so nobody can see which ads produced an enquiry, and there is no audience of past visitors to show your work to again.
  • Mostly furniture and decorating. Only one ad showed a bigger project (the garage conversion in Pinner), and it ran for about a day.
  • The ads pointed to a site that does not mention refurbishments, even when the ad said “refurbishment and building company”.
What we would ask to see
  • Who set the Facebook ads up and what they cost.
  • Any enquiries you remember coming from them.
  • Access to the Google Business Profile, to check categories and services.
