Digital Presence Scorecard
D
50 / 100
4 of 18 checks passed. 5 failed.
The carpentry side is found, reviewed and well photographed. The refurbishment side, which is where the bigger jobs are, is close to invisible online.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (24 Oct 2026)
DEnquiry forms
Three forms (home, contact, recent projects). They ask name, email and phone, and the home form takes files. None asks what the job is, the postcode, the size of the project or the timescale, so a whole-house refurbishment and a single shelf arrive looking the same.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ 07703 504866 is a tap-to-call link on every page.
DPages and service depth
19 pages and 12 blog posts, with town pages for carpentry, bespoke furniture and decorating in Pinner and Harrow (good). No page at all for refurbishments, kitchens, bathrooms, garage conversions, extensions or commercial work, although your reviews and TrustATrader profile show you do them.
DPage titles and descriptions
The home page title is “Bespoke Wooden Furniture by Expert Carpenters”, with no business name and no town. 6 of the 19 pages have no meta description (the line Google shows under a result).
DHeadings
The home page carries two identical H1 headings. The Carpenter Pinner page’s H1 reads “Local Carpenter serving Harrow”, which works against the page it sits on.
FLocal business markup
No structured data on any page (no LocalBusiness or GeneralContractor schema), so Google and AI tools have to guess your services, area and hours.
DMeasurement
A Google Analytics 4 tag is on every page (good). No Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel, although 18 Facebook and Instagram ads have run since November 2025. Nobody can see which of those ads produced an enquiry.
CAccreditation shown
An Accreditations page shows the TrustATrader badge. Your TrustATrader membership number and the insurance TrustATrader verified on 16 September 2026 are not shown on the site.
CReviews
⭐ 4.98 out of 5 from 101 reviews on TrustATrader. The site shows nine testimonials. Google shows 5.0 from only 7 reviews, and Google is where most people look first.
CProof of work
Real photos of your carpentry: alcove units, media walls, bookcases, under-stairs storage, wardrobes. No refurbishment project is shown start to finish (before, during, after), which is what a big-job buyer looks for.
FWho you are
Your name does not appear on the About page. Jeff is named only inside customer reviews. There is no photo of you and no story of the business.
A-Showing up for carpentry searches
⭐ First in Google and first in the map for “carpenter Pinner”, first for “alcove units Pinner” (and named in Google’s AI answer), third for “painter decorator Pinner”, fifth for “carpenter Harrow”.
FShowing up for refurbishment searches
Not found in any of 6 refurbishment searches: house refurbishment Pinner, home refurbishment Harrow, builders Pinner, kitchen refurbishment Pinner, refurbishment company north west London, garage conversion Pinner.
FOne clear message
The business is called L & J Refurbishments, Facebook calls it a “refurbishment and building company”, and TrustATrader lists full refurbishments, kitchens and extensions. The website sells carpentry and decorating. Google has listed you as a Carpenter.
A-Professional email
⭐ info@ljrefurbishments.co.uk on your own domain.
A-Freshness
⭐ Every page in the sitemap was updated on 21 September 2026, and the blog carries 2026 posts. Small point: the footer still says 2025.
FPaid ads
18 Meta ads since November 2025, most live for under three days, all inactive now. No Google ads found for your domain. Short bursts with no pixel cannot build an audience or show results.
DSame phone number everywhere
Website and Google show 07703 504866. Your TrustATrader profile shows 07307 282487. ⚠ That may be a TrustATrader tracking number; worth confirming.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about the website and the listings, not about your work. 101 reviews at 4.98 is the hard part, and it is done.

After the build: the form, pages, titles, headings, markup, measurement, one-message and phone rows are fixed by the build itself. Google reviews and refurbishment search positions take months of steady effort, so we do not project them.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Checkatrade, Houzz, and anything inside your Facebook ad account.
