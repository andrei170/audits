Know the trade before you dial · damp proofing in plain English
The three kinds of damp (so you can follow him)
  1. Rising damp: ground water rising up a wall, tide marks and salts low down. Fix: a chemical DPC (he uses silicone injection), then replaster.
  2. Penetrating damp: water coming in sideways (failed pointing, gutters, render, below-ground walls). Fix: the source, or tanking (he uses epoxy resin) where a wall must be sealed.
  3. Condensation: moisture from living, black mould around windows. Fix: ventilation and insulation, not damp proofing. His site says he will tell people if they can fix it themselves, a real trust point.
Words and badges
  • PCA = Property Care Association, the trade body for damp and timber. CSRT (remedial treatment) and CSSW (structural waterproofing) are surveyor qualifications. None shows on his site; ask, do not assume.
  • Insurance-backed cover for 10, 20 or 30 years is common in this trade. His site mentions 10-year cover that transfers with the property.
  • Where the money is: tanking, injection plus replaster, and below-ground work. Condensation jobs are often small.
  • Other buyers who need damp work over and over: landlords, letting agents, housing associations, and buyers after a mortgage survey flags damp. Repeat work without a platform in the middle.
  • ⚠ General trade knowledge, not checked against his business. Let him confirm.
