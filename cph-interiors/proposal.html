The plan, step by step

⭐ Step one costs nothing and is a few small changes on a site you already have. Each step after that is measured, so you can see what is working and stop anything that is not.

Step 1 · Free fixes your web person can make
  • Show your scores on the home page and damp page: “5 out of 5 from 175 MyBuilder reviews” and “10 out of 10 from 92 Checkatrade reviews”, with links.
  • Put the survey form on the damp page and the home page, not only on the contact page.
  • Name yourself under “Meet the directors”, with a photo, and say when you reply (for example: within the hour, 7 days a week).

Why: Your best proof is visible where people decide.

Step 2 · Count what comes in
  • Tracking on calls and forms, so you can see how many enquiries come from your own site.
  • Tag every enquiry by where it came from (MyBuilder, Checkatrade, TrustATrader, Bark, website, Google, referral) and whether it became a survey and a job.

Why: For the first time, a number for each platform: what it costs, what it brings, what it pays.

Step 3 · One inbox, a reply in a minute, damp jobs first
  • Every enquiry in one list, whichever platform or form it came from.
  • An instant reply from your name, with three quick questions: the problem, the postcode, a photo.
  • Sorting rules you set: damp jobs in your area first, the rest answered politely without a call.

Why: Less time on enquiries that were never going to be a job, more on the ones that are.

Step 4 · Follow-up and reviews
  • Survey reminders the day before.
  • Every quote followed up on a schedule you approve.
  • A review request after each job, sent to the place you choose (your Google profile is the one you own).

Why: Quotes stop going quiet, and every job adds to your own reviews.

Step 5 · Damp pages for the towns you want
  • A page for each town you want damp work in (for example Tonyrefail, Porth, Pontypridd, Llantrisant, Bridgend), each with a real job from that town.
  • Your memberships on every page, if you hold PCA, CSRT or CSSW. If you do not, worth a conversation.
  • Two or three short job stories: what the homeowner saw, the cause you found, what you did.

Why: Your own site starts to win the search for your own town.

Step 6 · Small damp-only ads, once tracking is in
  • Google search ads for damp jobs only, within the area you choose, sending clicks to your damp page.
  • A budget you set, and stopped if the numbers from Step 2 say it is not working.

Why: ⚠ Only once Step 2 is live, so you can see exactly what you are getting.

Then · Decide which platforms to keep
  • With a few months of your own numbers, compare each platform’s cost with the jobs it brought.
  • Keep what pays, cut what does not. Your call, on your figures, not ours.

Why: Less spent on leads you share with other firms.

How an enquiry would be handled
  1. Every enquiry, from every platform and your website, lands in one list. No more checking five apps.
  2. A reply goes out within a minute, from your name and number, even at 8pm or on a Sunday.
  3. Three quick questions sort it: what the problem is (damp, mould, plastering, other), the postcode, and a photo if they have one.
  4. Damp jobs in your area go to the top. Small jobs or ones too far away get a polite reply, without you spending a call.
  5. The survey gets booked into a free slot, with a reminder the day before.
  6. Every quote is followed up on a schedule you approve, until they say yes or no.
  7. After the job, a review request goes out, to the place you choose.

How we would work with you
  • Your website, your accounts, your data. Everything is set up in your name and stays yours, and we work alongside whoever looks after your site now.
  • You approve every message and every page before it goes live.
  • Start small and grow it only when it is working.
