Digital Presence Scorecard
D
45 / 100
4 of 14 checks passed. 8 failed.
The site itself was built well and recently: clear titles, a real damp page, tap-to-call. What it does not do is prove you (267 platform reviews hidden, no memberships, no names) or count anything, so the platforms keep the customer and you cannot see what your own site brings in.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (3 November 2026)
A-Page titles and search descriptions
⭐ Every one of your 11 pages has its own title and search description, and the home page title leads with “Damp Proofing & Condensation Specialists South Wales”. That is done well.
BA damp proofing page
⭐ A dedicated damp proofing page (544 words) naming silicone DPC injection and epoxy resin tanking, with a free survey offer. It has no form on it: the button sends people to the contact page.
A-Local business markup
⭐ Your pages tell search engines you are a local business in Tonyrefail, with your hours, your areas and links to your Facebook, Instagram, Yell and MyBuilder profiles.
A-Tap-to-call
Your mobile is a tap-to-call link on every page, and there is a “Call CPH” bar at the bottom.
DEnquiry form
A good form, asking what the job is, a preferred survey date and the postcode. But it is only on the contact page. Every “Request a Free Survey” button is a link to it, so a visitor on the damp page has to load a second page to ask.
FReply promise
The form says “We’ll contact you to arrange a convenient time”, with no time given, and your hours are Monday to Friday, 9 to 5. Nothing on the site tells someone who enquires at 8pm when they will hear back.
FReviews shown on your site
Your public profiles hold 175 reviews at 5 out of 5 on MyBuilder and 92 reviews at 10 out of 10 on Checkatrade. Your home page shows five short quotes (repeated in a slider) and no score or count at all.
FTrust badges and memberships
The site lists NVQ, CSCS and yearly DBS checks in text. No Property Care Association (PCA), no CSRT or CSSW surveyor qualification, no TrustMark, and your Checkatrade and TrustATrader profiles are not shown or linked.
FWho you are
There is a heading reading “Meet the directors” with no names under it. Customers in your reviews name you personally, again and again. The site never does.
CProof of work
A gallery and a before-and-after slider of real jobs. ⭐ Your Checkatrade profile holds 237 photos. No job write-ups (the problem, the cause found, the fix, the town).
FTown pages
You list Tonyrefail, Porth, Pontypridd, Bridgend, Cardiff and further afield, but there are no town pages. Phrases like “Damp proofing Bridgend” sit in a long list with nothing behind them.
FFound in search (one Bing search)
On Bing, “damp proofing Tonyrefail” showed your Checkatrade profile at number 2 and your own site nowhere in the top 10. So the person who finds you is sent to Checkatrade, not to you.
FMeasurement
No Google tag, no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel in the page code. Nothing records how many people visit, which page they came to, or which enquiries came from the website.
FReady for paid search
A damp page exists to send a click to, which is a good start. But with no tracking and no form on the page, nobody could tell which clicks became a booked survey.

⭐ The hard part is already done: forty years of work and 267 top-scored reviews. Almost every low grade above is about showing that, and counting what comes in, not about rebuilding anything.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google Business Profile and Google reviews, Google search positions, your other platform profiles, and anything inside an ads account.
