The short version

Craig, you do not need more enquiries. You need more of the ones you already get to turn into damp jobs, without you and one other person spending your evenings on five apps.

  • What you told us: the platforms send you a lot of enquiries, only a small share become jobs, there are two of you, and damp proofing is the work you would rather do.
  • What we found: your website is new and well built, but it hides 175 five-star MyBuilder reviews and 92 ten-out-of-ten Checkatrade reviews, names no memberships and counts nothing. On Bing, your Checkatrade page beats your own site for “damp proofing Tonyrefail”.
  • What we would do: put every enquiry in one place with an instant reply and three sorting questions, so damp jobs in your area are answered first, then make your own site do more of the work the platforms do now.

Nothing here is priced and nothing forecasts a result.
