What this means for you
Nobody local looks better than you. The platforms just look louder.
  • ⭐ Your reviews beat everything on that page. 175 on MyBuilder and 92 on Checkatrade, at the top score on both. None of the local firms we read shows anything close.
  • The best local result belongs to Checkatrade, not to you. Someone searching your own town is sent to a page you pay for, where other firms are one tap away.
  • EBM Wales has a page for Tonyrefail and a form on it. You live and work there and have no such page.
  • The template sites are there to collect enquiries and pass them on. A homeowner cannot tell the difference, which is why showing your name, your reviews and your memberships matters.
⚠ What this page does not contain

Google results, Google Maps positions and Google reviews: our tools cannot read them, so none are reported. One Bing search is a snapshot, not a ranking report. We did not check the Property Care Association register for any firm. A tag on a site shows it is set up to measure or advertise, not how much it spends.
