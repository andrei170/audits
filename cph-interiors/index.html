The four things to fix first, all small
⚠ 1. Your 267 reviews are invisible on your own site

175 five-star reviews on MyBuilder and 92 at 10 out of 10 on Checkatrade. The home page shows five short quotes and the line “100% positive feedback on MyBuilder”, with no score and no count. A homeowner comparing you with a stranger’s site cannot see your best argument.

⚠ 2. The form is one page away from where people decide

The damp page, the home page and every service page send people to /contact to ask for a survey. Putting the same short form on the damp page itself is a small change.

⚠ 3. Nothing tells an evening enquiry when it will hear back

Your hours say Monday to Friday, 9 to 5, and the form says “we’ll contact you”. Someone who fills it in at 8pm, and who has probably asked three other firms on the platforms too, has no reason to wait for you.

⚠ 4. Nothing is being counted

No Google tag, no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel. You cannot see how many people visit, or whether a single job came from the website rather than a platform. That is the number that decides how much you need the platforms.
