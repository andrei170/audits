What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

We could not find Max Life Cooling and Heating anywhere a homeowner would look: no website, no Google listing, no reviews.

⭐ But the business behind it is real and properly set up. Your Oregon CCB licence (#246918) is on the active list, with a $25,000 bond and $1,000,000 of insurance on record. Those are the credentials of a firm people should be able to find.

🔴 Why word of mouth has a ceiling

You are based in Mosier, between Hood River and The Dalles. Word of mouth works, and it is why you have the jobs you do. But it only spreads as far as your existing customers' friends and neighbours.

⭐ Wasco County alone has about 26,700 people (2020 Census), before Hood River. The homeowner who has never met one of your customers is the one you cannot reach today.

⚠ The size of the gap, on your own numbers

You told our team you are doing about $18,000 a month on 2 to 3 jobs a week. That is roughly $1,700 a job on average.

⚠ So 2 or 3 more jobs a week is roughly another $15,000 to $20,000 a month at the same average. That is arithmetic on your figures to show the size of the prize, not a forecast.

⚠ Something worth knowing about Mosier

The City of Mosier's own site says there is no natural gas service in town. So homes there heat with electricity or propane, which is exactly where heat pumps and ductless mini splits make sense.

⚠ Oregon's heat pump incentive programme keeps a list of approved contractors. Two firms near you are on it and you are not. Homeowners chasing a rebate may go to them first. Worth asking what joining involves.

⭐ What is already strong
  • Licensed, bonded and insured in Oregon, since 2023
  • You do the work yourself. The customer deals with the owner, which the bigger firms cannot say
  • Based between both towns, so Hood River and The Dalles are both close

⚠ Not assessed: Facebook and Nextdoor, which are hard to search from outside. We have not guessed.
