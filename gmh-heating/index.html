What We Can And Cannot Tell You
⛔ We could not audit a website, because there is not one

Every other business we are speaking to this week got a list of things wrong with their site. You get something different, because there is nothing to look at.

⭐ That is not the disadvantage it sounds like. A bad website actively costs you money. No website costs you only the work you never hear about.

⭐⭐ The thing you said that matters most

You told our team that the type of job we target would decide how many more you could handle.

⭐⭐ That is the most commercially intelligent thing anybody has said to us this week. You are not asking for volume. You are asking for the right work, because you already know that the wrong work fills a one-man diary and earns nothing.

So the conversation is not how many more jobs. It is which jobs.

⚠️ What a one-man show can and cannot absorb

Two jobs a week with your own supplier run every morning means your time is the whole constraint. Every hour at the supplier, quoting, invoicing and driving is an hour not earning.

⚠️ So more enquiries is only useful if they are better enquiries. Ten more small call-outs would make your week worse. Two more of the right job would not.

The Arithmetic Of Being On Your Own

Your own numbers, at two jobs a week

~$3,600 a job

$380,000 across roughly 104 jobs. ⭐ Which is the number to test on the call: is that an average of lots of small ones and a few big ones, or are they all much the same? If the spread is wide, which jobs you take matters more than how many.

⚠️ Arithmetic on the figures you gave us, not a finding. Two jobs a week for 52 weeks against $380,000. If you work more weeks than that, or the year is not finished, the number moves.
⭐ What You Have Going For You
⭐ Said plainly

$380,000 single-handed. No premises overhead, no payroll, no agency fees, and a supplier relationship that works. ⭐ That is a high-margin operation and most firms we speak to with twice the revenue keep less of it.

And you know your own constraint, which is rare. Most people ask for more leads. You asked what kind.

What We Would Want To Ask
  • 1. What is the job you actually want more of? ⭐ Describe the last one you were pleased to get.
  • 2. And what is the job you would rather never do again?
  • 3. What is the spread? Smallest job this year, biggest job this year.
  • 4. How do people find you now? Word of mouth, trucks, suppliers, referrals from other trades?
  • 5. Could you do three a week? Four? And what would have to change for that.
  • 6. Have you ever had a website, or has it never come up?
  • 7. What do you do when somebody wants to check you out before calling? ⚠️ Right now there is nothing for them to find.
⭐⭐ Questions 1 and 2 are the whole call. You have already told us the type of work decides everything, so the job is to find out what that type is and then go and get it.

You did $380,000 on your own with nothing online.

The question is not how many more jobs. It is which ones.
