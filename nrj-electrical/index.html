What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

You want three more EV chargers and one more solar job a week. Those are the two jobs where buyers check credentials hardest, and your site shows the fewest.

A solar system is a five-figure decision. Somebody making it compares two or three installers, and looks for the certificate, the reviews and the warranty before the phone number.

🔴 1. Nothing a buyer can check
  • No certification body named: the site says "certified electricians" but never says certified by whom
  • Nothing for solar specifically. The solar installers below put MCS on their front page
  • One testimonial on the whole site, about a rewire, placed on the solar page
  • A one-year warranty, where competitors state 5 years (EV) and 25 years (solar)

⚠ You may well hold all of these. The site just does not say so.

🔴 2. The EV and solar pages have no way to enquire

The only form is on the Contact page, and it asks name, email, phone and a message. Somebody reading your EV page has to leave it to get in touch.

⚠ It also does not ask the job, the postcode or how soon, so every enquiry needs a call back before you know if it is worth going out for.

⚠ 3. Every page says Ballynahinch, and only Ballynahinch

All 12 pages are titled for Ballynahinch, which is good for your own town. But nothing is written for Lisburn, Downpatrick, Belfast or Newry, where most EV and solar buyers in County Down live.

⚠ 4. A Yahoo email address

nrjelectrical@yahoo.co.uk on every page. It works, but next to a polished site it is the one detail that looks small.

🔴 5. Two phone numbers

Every page shows 028 9568 3584. But the business details your site gives Google list 07929 608334, a mobile. Google can show a customer either one, and a mobile answered on a roof is a missed call.

⚠ 6. Small copy mistakes Google shows
  • The Maintenance Contracts description, which Google uses under your link, reads: "Regular inspection, maintenance, and testing to for safe, compliant properties"
  • The same page opens with "One-year manufacturer’s warranty. Fast and efficient service. Expert installation", copied from the EV page, on a page about service contracts
  • The homepage headline runs to 15 words in capitals: "Electrical Systems Installation Service, 24-Hour Emergency Call-Out, Ballynahinch". It never says EV or solar
⚠ 7. "Open 24/7", but no way to leave a job at night

The footer says Open 24/7. Somebody who finds you at 10pm can ring, or go to the Contact page. There is no form on the page they are reading, and nothing replies to them automatically.

⚠ Worth checking on the call

A construction directory describes a NICEIC-registered firm offering EV charging at 56 Cedar Hill, Ballynahinch, the address your site gives Google. If that is you, it belongs on every page of your website.

⚠ Your two targets, from your booking

You told our team you want 3 more EV chargers and 1 more solar job a week, with 5 on the team. That is about 13 more EV installs and 4 more solar jobs a month.

⚠ We do not know what each job pays you yet, so we have not turned that into money. It is the first thing we will ask.

⭐ What is already strong
  • A real, well-organised site: 12 pages, one per service, each titled for Ballynahinch
  • Tap-to-call on every page
  • An EV price on the page, from £949 fitted, which most electricians will not show
  • The myenergi Zappi, a charger that runs off solar panels. You sell both halves of that job
  • 24-hour emergency call-out and 20 years’ experience
  • Google Tag Manager is installed, so the groundwork for measuring is there

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, your reviews off-site, your social pages, and where you rank in search. We have not guessed.
