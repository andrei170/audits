What the call told us that the website could not

The first audit read your website from the outside. This one adds what you told me on Friday, because some of it changes the plan.

🔴🔴 1. Yell controls the website, the number and the data
  • About 18 months are left on the contract, and you cannot change the site yourself
  • 028 9568 3584 is Yell’s tracking number, which records your calls and forwards them to your mobile. That is why Google sees two numbers for you
  • Showing your accreditation logos costs extra per page, which is why none are on the site
  • You have not seen any reporting on where your enquiries come from

⭐ The answer is not to fight the contract. It is to build alongside it: your own pages, on a domain you control, with your own tracking, so every enquiry they bring is counted and yours.

🔴 2. The price on the site is not the price of the job

The site leads with “Ohme from £949 fitted”. You told me a standard job is closer to £1,200 to £1,500, and that Zappi is now 70% of your chargers. So the page attracts people looking for the cheapest Ohme, which is the price-shopper you said you do not want.

⭐ The new EV page leads with Zappi and a price range you set, so the enquiries already expect your price.

🔴 3. Three in ten leads go nowhere, and the rest are not sorted

Every enquiry reaches you the same way, whether it is a Zappi install next week or a fan in the hall. The form asks the job, the postcode and how soon, and scores it 1 to 4, so you ring the right ones first and the off-niche ones never take your time.

⚠ 4. Nothing follows a lead up

No CRM means a missed call or an unanswered form is simply lost. A text back to every missed call and a reminder for every open quote keeps them from ringing the next installer.

⚠ 5. One year of warranty, against 5 and 25

Cord EV states 5 years and EECO 25 years on their sites. If manufacturer warranties on the Zappi and the panels are longer than a year, they belong on the page.

A correction from Friday

On the call I said Google Tag Manager is not installed. It is (GTM-52LRX4ZS), but it is on Yell’s site and you have not seen what it records, so for you the result is the same: no numbers you can see.

⭐ What is already working for you
  • 40% of your work is word of mouth, which says the work itself is good
  • Solar and the EV charger from one electrician, which none of the five firms we looked at offers the same way
  • Room on the team for more work right now
