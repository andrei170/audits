  • You said it yourself: “the quicker you get on the ball, the quicker you get things going, the better for both”

The Website Plan
⚠ Why not a whole new website now

Yell runs nrjelectrical.co.uk for about 18 more months. Two full websites both called NRJ Electrical would compete with each other on Google and confuse your customers.

⭐ So, in stages
  • Now: your new pages on a web address you own. Your ads go there. The Yell site stays exactly as it is
  • When you get your domain back: the full website goes live on nrjelectrical.co.uk. It is already built, and the new pages carry straight over
  • The full website is £2,000, and whatever you paid for setup counts towards it. You never pay twice for the same page
Three things to ask Yell
  • Is nrjelectrical.co.uk registered in your name, or theirs? It renews on 28 October 2027
  • Who controls your Google Business Profile, and which number is on it?
  • What you pay them each month, to compare like for like
Why Not A Price Per Lead
How Yell, Rated People and MyBuilder work

They are paid whether the job is good or not, and the same customer is often sent to other installers too. That is the race to the bottom you described.

⭐ You still get the number you are after

Every 30 days the report shows how many enquiries came in, what each one cost, how many were EV or solar, and how many you booked. And every lead is yours alone.
