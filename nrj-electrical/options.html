Why not a price per lead

Price per lead is how Rated People and MyBuilder work, and you told me where that goes: price-shoppers and a race to the bottom. Your own ads, to your own page, mean every enquiry is yours alone. So you pay for the work that finds them, not per name.

How it works
⭐ Setup, one time: £2,000
  • Your EV and solar landing pages, in orange and black, leading with Zappi, running alongside the Yell site
  • The quote form that asks the job, the postcode and how soon, and scores every enquiry 1 to 4
  • A CRM: every enquiry in one place, a text back to every missed call, reminders on open quotes
  • Tracking on every call and form, so you see where each enquiry came from
  • Your Google and Facebook ad accounts, built in your name
Then each month
Starter£3,000 a month£1,500 of it is advertising, paid to Google and Facebook. EV for homes first, then solar
Growth£4,000 a month£2,000 of it is advertising. For when solar is working and you want two a week

⚠ Half of every month goes straight to the ad platforms, not to us.

The arithmetic, on your own numbers

You told me you walk away with about £120 to £150 on a charger after wages, and about £1,500 on a domestic solar job.

  • Two domestic solar jobs a month cover the Starter month
  • Your target of 4 more solar and 10 more chargers a month is roughly £7,200 to £7,500 more walk-away profit

⚠ This is arithmetic on your figures, to show what would have to be true. It is not a forecast of how many jobs this brings in.
