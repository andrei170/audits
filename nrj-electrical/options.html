Start small. Grow it when it is working.

You said you would rather start at 3 to 5 extra chargers a week and build from there, and that you do not want to spend a lot before you see it work. So this starts with one page and one campaign, and only grows when you decide it should. No long contract at any step.

Step 1: the EV pilot
⭐ What you get

  • The quote form that asks the job, the postcode and how soon, and scores every enquiry 1 to 4
  • A text back to every missed call, so a busy day does not cost you a job
  • Every call and form tracked, so you see exactly where each enquiry came from
  • One Google campaign: EV chargers for homes, the first one you picked

The website plan
⚠ Why not a whole new website straight away

Yell runs nrjelectrical.co.uk, and your contract has about 18 months left. Two full websites both called NRJ Electrical would compete with each other on Google and confuse your customers, which is the worry you raised.

⭐ So, in three stages
  • Now: the EV page, then the solar page, on a web address you own. Your ads send people there. The Yell site stays exactly as it is.
  • When you get your domain back (at the end of the Yell contract, or sooner if it is in your name): the full website goes live on nrjelectrical.co.uk. It is already built as a demo, and the pages from the pilot carry straight over
  • The full website is £2,000, and the £750 pilot setup counts towards it. You never pay twice for the same page
Three things to find out from Yell
  • Is nrjelectrical.co.uk registered in your name, or theirs? It renews on 28 October 2027
  • Who controls your Google Business Profile, and which phone number is on it?
  • What you pay them each month, so you can compare like for like
Why not a price per lead

Pay per lead is how Yell, Rated People and MyBuilder work. The lead platform is paid whether the job is good or not, and the same customer is often sent to other installers too. That is the race to the bottom you described.

⭐ You still get the number you are after. Every month the report shows how many enquiries came in, what each one cost you, how many were EV or solar, and how many you booked. And every lead is yours alone, from someone who searched for a charger and chose your page.
