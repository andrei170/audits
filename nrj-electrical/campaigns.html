Built around the two jobs you asked for

You told our team you want 3 more EV chargers and 1 more solar job a week, with 5 on the team to fit them. That is about 13 EV installs and 4 solar jobs a month. Every campaign below is aimed at one of those two jobs, and nothing else.

🔴 Before any advertising starts

Ads send people to your website. Today it gives them no form on the EV or solar pages, no accreditation named, and one review (see the audit). So these go first:

  • A quote form on every page, asking the job, the postcode and how soon
  • Your accreditations and Google reviews on the EV and solar pages
  • Every call and form counted, and where it came from
The campaigns, in the order we would switch them on
Campaign 1 · EV chargers for homes

Google search · switch on first

Who it reachesHomeowners in Ballynahinch, Lisburn, Downpatrick, Belfast and the County Down towns you cover, searching right now
What they search or see“EV charger installation” plus a town, “home EV charger fitted”, “Ohme installer”, “Zappi installer”
The ad saysYour price, which most installers will not show: Ohme Home Pro from £949 fitted, VAT included, from a local electrician with 20 years’ experience
Where it sends themYour EV charger page, and the “how much does a charger cost?” page
WhyThese people have already decided to buy. A competitor, All Charge NI, is already paying for these searches in Northern Ireland, which tells you they bring in work.
Campaign 2 · Solar panels

Google search · switch on first

Who it reachesHomeowners in County Down searching for solar
What they search or see“Solar panels” plus a town, “solar panel installers near me”, “solar and battery”
The ad saysSolar fitted by an electrician, with the board work done by the same firm. Your accreditation and reviews in the ad once they are on the site
Where it sends themYour solar page
WhySolar is a bigger decision, so people search and compare. Being there when they compare is the point.
Campaign 3 · Solar and EV together

Facebook and Instagram · switch on second

Who it reachesHomeowners within about 20 miles of Ballynahinch, including people interested in electric cars
What they search or seeA short video of a Zappi charging a car off the panels on the roof
The ad says“Charge your car off your own roof.” Panels, a Zappi charger and the board work, from one electrician in one job
Where it sends themYour “Solar and EV together” page, with the quote form
WhyThe job only you can do in one visit. The EV specialists do not fit panels and the solar firms are not electricians first.
Campaign 4 · People who visited but did not enquire

Facebook and Instagram · runs alongside

Who it reachesAnyone who looked at your EV or solar pages in the last 30 days
What they search or seeYour Google reviews, and photos of real installs
The ad saysThe proof they were looking for: reviews, your accreditation, your warranty
Where it sends themBack to the page they looked at
WhyMost people do not enquire on the first visit. This brings them back once they have compared.
Searches we would not pay for

“Free”, “DIY”, “how to install”, “jobs”, “course”, “cheapest”, and towns you do not cover. Each one is a click that will never be a job.

Later, once the first four are working
  • Workplace and fleet EV chargers for businesses, landlords and HMOs
  • Your Google Business Profile kept active with photos and reviews, so you show on the map for “EV charger near me”
⚠ How we would judge it
  • Daily budgets, so enquiries come in steadily and can be turned down in a busy week
  • Measured on booked installs, not clicks. Every month: enquiries, cost per enquiry, and installs booked, by campaign
  • Advertising spend is paid by you directly to Google and Meta, and you set the amount

⚠ This plan does not forecast a number of installs. What an EV install and a solar job pay you is the first thing we need to set the budget sensibly.
