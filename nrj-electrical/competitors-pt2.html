🔴🔴 The platforms you have tried

Yell, Rated People and MyBuilder all sit between you and the customer. The customer is comparing installers on price before they ever speak to you, which is why you called it a race to the bottom.

⭐ With your own ads and your own pages, the enquiry comes to you alone, from someone who searched for a Zappi or for panels and chose your page.

⭐ The gap nobody is filling
  • Solar and a Zappi charger fitted together by one electrician, so the car charges off the roof. The EV firms do not do solar, and the solar firms are not electricians first
  • Smaller domestic solar jobs, which the big solar firms do not lead with
  • A named local electrician, which only All Charge NI does, and with fewer years than you
