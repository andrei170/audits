What you told me
⭐ The jobs you want more of
  • EV chargers and solar, not general electrical work. “I’ve been doing that too long”
  • Today: about 20 EV chargers and 5 to 8 solar jobs a month, with 5 on the team
  • Room for about 10 more chargers a month, starting at 3 to 5 a week, and about 4 more solar jobs, one a week, then two a week if it works
  • Smaller domestic jobs, more of them. A standard charger with a board and a couple of hours’ work is about £1,200. Domestic solar runs £6,000 to £11,000. One big commercial job every three months is plenty
  • myenergi Zappi is about 70% of your chargers now, Ohme about 30%
  • You cover all of Northern Ireland for EV and solar, and at least 30 miles from Ballynahinch: Belfast, Lisburn, Newry, Newcastle
🔴 Where the work comes from today
  • About 40% Yell, 40% word of mouth, 20% social media
  • Out of every 10 Yell leads, about 3 are “dead in the water”, plus jobs you do not want, like fans and call-outs, and people who only want a price
  • You have not seen any reporting from Yell, and the contact is a call about every six weeks
  • Rated People and MyBuilder: “a race to the bottom”
  • No CRM today
What you asked
“If somebody fills the form out, how do you qualify it?”

Twice. First the ad itself, aimed at homeowners searching for a charger or panels. Then the form, which asks the job, the postcode and how soon, and scores every enquiry 1 to 4. Only the 3s and 4s are fed back to the ads, so the ads learn to find more of them. Not every lead will be perfect, and nobody honest will tell you otherwise.

“Can I run it parallel to the Yell site?”

Yes. The Yell site stays as it is for the rest of your contract, and the new pages run alongside it, with your own tracking. How that works →

“What does it cost?”

That is what today’s call is for. I said I would come back with it once I knew more about the business, and I will walk you through it.

What we agreed
  • The campaign order is right: EV for homes first, then solar, then solar and EV together, then Facebook to homeowners nearby, then retargeting people who visited but did not enquire. The campaign plan →

  • A landing page is enough to start. You do not need a whole new website
  • Orange and black, so it matches your branding. The demo is updated →
