Digital Presence Scorecard
F
41 / 100
3 of 17 checks passed. 11 failed.
The service pages, headings and your Which? score are a good base. But a customer on a phone cannot tap to call you, your pages disagree about where you are based, and Google is not being told about your town pages.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (2 November 2026)
FTap-to-call
Your phone numbers cannot be tapped on any of your 23 pages. 07787 760 808 and 01622 961042 are plain text, so a customer on a mobile has to copy the number by hand or give up.
FWhich number to ring
The top of every page shows 07787 760 808. The body and footer show 01622 961042. Two numbers is fine, but nothing says which one gets you fastest.
CReviews on your site
⭐ Your home page shows your Which? Trusted Traders score, 5.0 from 32 reviews, plus four named customer quotes. That is real proof. None of it appears on your service or town pages, where many visitors land.
FFresh reviews
Your Which? profile has 3 reviews posted since January 2025, and none in 2023. Most of the 32 are from 2017 to 2021. One local rival shows 124 reviews on its home page, with several posted this year.
FEnquiry form
A form (name, email, phone, message) on 21 of your 23 pages, but not on the home page. It does not ask the postcode, the type of job or for a photo, so every enquiry needs a call back.
FWhere you are based
Your contact page and Which? profile say Snodland. Your eight town pages say “Based in Orpington”. Your home page says “South London and Kent”, and the map link in your footer opens a map centred near Orpington, about 15 miles away. Customers and Google get three different answers.
DTown pages
⭐ Eight town pages: Snodland, West Malling, Larkfield, East Malling, Maidstone, Aylesford, Gravesend and “Burnham” (the village by Aylesford is spelled Burham). Each is about 500 words. No page for Orpington, Bromley or Sidcup, although your page titles name those towns.
FSitemap
The file that lists your pages for Google was last updated in July 2019, and it does not include any of your eight town pages.
FSecure address
Your site opens on http:// with no switch to the secure https:// version, and every link inside the site points to http://. Most browsers mark those pages “Not secure” in the address bar.
A-Search descriptions
⭐ 22 of your 23 pages have a search description. Small fixes: the complaints page has none, and the About page description spells Orpington as “Oprington”.
AHeadings
⭐ Every page has exactly one main heading. That is done right.
FLocal business markup
The code on your pages describes an “organisation” and an “article”. Nothing tells search engines you are an electrician, where you work or your hours.
BService pages
⭐ Nine service pages (door entry, CCTV, smoke detectors, electric showers, extra sockets, lighting, commercial, repairs, plus an overview), most around 850 to 1,100 words. A solid base.
DTrust shown on the site
⭐ Which? Trusted Trader, JIB contractor number and public liability cover are all shown. No NICEIC, NAPIT or Part P scheme is shown, and all four local rivals we read put NICEIC on their pages.
FFacts that disagree
Your About page says “Over 15 Years of Experience”; the home page and footer say “over 20 years”. The footer still reads © 2024.
FMeasurement
A Google Analytics tag counts visits, next to an old tag that Google retired. No Google Ads tag, no Meta pixel and no call tracking, so nobody can tell which calls came from the website.
FSearch: “electrician Snodland”
We ran this one search with the UK region set. Your site was not in the 11 results, although you are based in Snodland and have a Snodland page. The paid spot at the top went to a quote website, not an electrician.

⭐ Every failure above is fixable without rebuilding your site. The service pages and your Which? score are the hard parts, and you already have them.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google Business Profile and Google reviews, Facebook, trade registers, and anything inside an ads account.
