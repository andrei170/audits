What this means for you
You already have what most of them lean on. They just show it better.
  • All four have tap-to-call on their pages. Yours does not, anywhere.
  • JM Electric puts 124 reviews on its home page. You have a perfect 5.0 on Which?, but only 3 reviews posted since January 2025.
  • All four show NICEIC. Your site shows no scheme at all.
  • ⭐ None of the four had a Google Ads tag or a Meta pixel in its page code. On that search, the paid spot went to a quote website, not a local electrician. That space is open.
