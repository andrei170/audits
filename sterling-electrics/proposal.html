What we would do, in order

⭐ This starts with things you can check yourself. The first three steps cost nothing and you will see them change on your own site. Everything after that is measured, so you can see what is working and stop anything that is not.

1. Make every number tap-to-call · Week 1, free

Turn both numbers into tap-to-call links on every page, and say which one reaches you fastest.

⭐ A few minutes of work. Every mobile visitor can ring you in one tap.

2. One base, one story · Week 1, free

Snodland on every page, the map link fixed, the town pages corrected from “Based in Orpington”, one years-in-trade figure, and Burham spelled right.

So customers and Google get the same answer about where you work.

3. Tell Google about your pages · Week 1, free

Send every visitor to the secure https:// address, refresh the sitemap so it includes your town pages, and add electrician markup with your area and hours.

So the pages you already have can actually be found.

4. Keep your reviews fresh · Week 1

A short text after each job with your Which? (or Google) review link, and your score shown on every service and town page.

Your 5.0 is real. It just needs recent reviews next to it.

5. A form that sorts the job, and a text back · Week 2

Ask the job type and postcode first, let people send a photo, and send an instant text from your number when you miss a call.

So a customer waits for you instead of ringing the next electrician while you are mid-job.

6. Measurement from day one · Week 2

Tracking on calls and forms, so every enquiry shows where it came from.

You see every enquiry and its source before anything is spent.

7. Small, local Google Ads to your own site · When you want more work

Only the jobs you choose, only the towns you choose, sent to your own page about that job, ringing your own number, in an ads account in your name.

⚠ Only once tracking is in, so you can see exactly what each pound brings.

How we would work with you
  • Enquiries come to your website and your phone number. Not to a page we own, and never shared with other electricians.
  • Your website, your accounts, your data. Everything is set up in your name and stays yours if we stop working together.
  • You see the numbers: every call and form, and where it came from.
  • No ranking promises. We report what happened, not what might.
  • Start small and grow it only when it is working and you have room for the work.
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know which jobs you want, how much room you have or where your work comes from. Anyone giving you a forecast before knowing those would be inventing it.
