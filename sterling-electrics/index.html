What we checked, and where

Everything below was checked on 2 November 2026: all 23 pages of sterling-electrics.co.uk and its sitemap, your Which? Trusted Traders profile, the Companies House register, one Google-style search for “electrician Snodland”, and the websites of the four electricians that came up.

The business behind the name
⭐ A Which? Trusted Trader with a perfect score
  • Which? Trusted Trader since April 2019, with 5.0 from 32 reviews. Customers mention you by name, turning up when you say you will and tidying up after.
  • Shown on your site: Which? Trusted Trader, JIB contractor number, public liability cover up to £2m.
  • Nine services, from repairs and extra sockets to door entry, CCTV, showers, lighting and commercial work.
  • Companies House: no limited company is registered under the name Sterling Electrics today, so there are no filed accounts to read. That is normal for a business run in your own name.
The three things to fix first, all free
⚠ 1. Nobody can tap to call you

Across all 23 pages, neither of your phone numbers is a tap-to-call link. Most people looking for a quick electrician job are on a phone. They have to copy your number by hand, or they tap the next electrician instead.

⚠ 2. Your site cannot agree where you are based

Snodland on the contact page and Which?. “Based in Orpington” on all eight town pages. “South London and Kent” on the home page, and a footer map link centred near Orpington. Google uses these signals to decide where to show you, and right now they point two ways.

⚠ 3. Google is being handed an out of date list of your pages

Your sitemap was last updated in July 2019 and leaves out all eight town pages. Your site also opens on the older http:// address, which browsers mark “Not secure”.

Your site, page by page
sterling-electrics.co.uk: 23 pages, About and Contact edited May 2026
  • ⭐ Good: nine service pages with real content, one main heading per page, search descriptions on 22 of 23 pages, and your Which? score on the home page.
  • No tap-to-call anywhere, and two different numbers with no hint which is fastest.
  • The form asks name, email, phone and a message, and is missing from the home page. No postcode, job type or photo.
  • Reviews only on the home page. Service and town pages show none.
  • Eight town pages on the Maidstone side, none for Orpington, Bromley or Sidcup, although page titles name those towns. “Burnham” is likely meant to be Burham.
  • No NICEIC, NAPIT or Part P shown, and “15 years” on one page against “20 years” on another.
  • No Google Ads tag, Meta pixel or call tracking, so nobody can see which calls came from the site.
Search

We searched “electrician Snodland” on DuckDuckGo with the UK region set, after a test search confirmed the tool was working. Your site was not in the 11 results. The paid spot at the top went to myjobquote.co.uk, a quote website. Below it: Snodland Electrician, Checkatrade (its listing says 54 electricians in Snodland), CJA Electrical, JM Electric (twice), Thomson Local, Yell, Sigma Electrical, Ebden Electrical and Able Group.

⚠ What we could not measure
  • Further searches: the search engine blocked our tool on the second one, so we have one search, not a full picture.
  • Google itself, Google Maps and your Google Business Profile: our tools cannot read them, so we do not know your Google reviews or map position.
  • Trade registers: we did not check the NICEIC or JIB registers.
  • Facebook and Twitter: linked from your site, not read.
  • Ads: we did not check Google’s or Meta’s public ad records, and nobody can see ad spend from outside.
  • Page speed: not tested, so not graded.
