What this means for you

1Air-On is the one to beat, and it is three miles from you.

Air-On, also in Victorville, has built eleven commercial refrigeration pages: walk-in coolers, walk-in freezers, ice machines, reach-ins and prep tables, maintenance, and a separate page for Victorville, Hesperia, Apple Valley, Adelanto, Oak Hills and Barstow. Each one names restaurants, stores and food service. Your site has none, so when a restaurant searches, Air-On has a page that matches the search and you do not.

Source: aironac.com, read November 23, 2026.

2Air-On is paying for those searches.

Its site carries a Google Ads tag, which means it can run paid search and measure which clicks become calls. It also shows 68 Google reviews on the page. You run no tracking at all, so even good work you win today cannot be traced back to where it came from.

Source: aironac.com page code, read November 23, 2026.

3Two licensed refrigeration shops near you have no website we could find.

Gandarilla Refrigeration & HVAC and Crestline Refrigeration are both listed with refrigeration (C-38) licences in the High Desert, and we could not find a website for either. A restaurant searching online cannot pick them. That leaves room: a well-built commercial site would put Air Pro alongside Air-On as one of the few local answers.

Source: Public contractor directory and web searches, November 23, 2026.

4The national firms show what a commercial buyer expects.

Source: coolsys.com and techoncallhvac.com, read November 23, 2026.
