The three fixes that matter

Eleven of these are housekeeping. Three are quietly costing you jobs.

1. You cannot see where your calls come from

No analytics and no ad tag means every inquiry is a mystery. You do not know whether a job came from Google, a van wrap, or word of mouth, so you cannot double down on what works or stop paying for what does not. This is the first fix because it makes every later decision measurable.

2. Google cannot read where you are or what you do

There is no local business markup and no structured data on the site. For a trade that lives and dies on "near me" searches across the High Desert, this is the difference between appearing in the local pack and not. It is also one of the quickest fixes on this list.

3. Your site is residential, your work is both

Air Pro is a refrigeration company, and your About and Refrigeration Services pages mention commercial work, yet the homepage never uses the word commercial and reads as a residential AC and heating firm. There is no page for walk-in coolers, freezers or restaurants. A restaurant manager searching for walk-in cooler or freezer repair in Victorville will not recognize you as the answer. The commercial refrigeration work you can do is invisible to the people who need it most.

What is already working

  • Lead capture is in place. Forms on the homepage and contact page plus tap-to-call links mean a ready buyer is not left stranded.
  • Clear trust basics. Your CSLB license number and "Licensed, bonded & insured" are right on the homepage.
  • Depth is there. 49 pages is a solid base to build local search on once the markup is added.
  • Financing is offered (12 months, 0 percent), which is a genuine differentiator on larger installs. It deserves to be louder.
