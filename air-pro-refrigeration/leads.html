You told our team you paid a lead company for three months of HVAC leads and closed none of them: mostly price shoppers. This page explains why that happens, and how leads that come only to you are different.

Why lead companies send price shoppers

1The same request often goes to several contractors.

Many lead services sell one homeowner’s request to more than one company. By the time you call, the customer has three or four quotes coming and is choosing on price. You are not losing on your work; you are losing a race you were entered into with everyone else.

Source: How shared-lead services commonly work; ask your old provider whether yours were exclusive.

2They were HVAC leads, not the work you want.

A residential AC request is the most price-compared job there is. A restaurant with a warm walk-in cooler is the opposite: it needs someone today and cares more about speed than price. Buying HVAC leads put you in the wrong market for the jobs you want.

Source: Your own words to our team, November 2026.

3Nobody asked the questions that matter before you called.

A lead with a name and a phone number tells you nothing about the business, the equipment or the urgency, so you spend your time finding out the job was never yours.

How leads that come only to you work

  • Your own pages answer the search. A restaurant searching "walk-in cooler repair Hesperia" lands on your walk-in cooler page or your Hesperia page, not a directory.
  • Your own form does the screening. Before you pick up the phone you know the business type, the equipment, how urgent it is and the town. A home AC request is marked as residential so you can price it separately.
  • Your own adverts on commercial searches only. Paid search can be limited to commercial terms such as walk-in cooler repair, freezer repair and ice machine repair across your towns, so you are not paying for residential AC clicks.
  • Nobody else gets the lead. It comes from your site to your phone. There is no list of other contractors calling the same restaurant.
  • Every call and form is tracked. You see which pages and searches produced real jobs, and stop paying for the ones that did not.

The form on the demo

Open the request service page on the demo. It asks for the type of business, the equipment, how urgent it is, the city, the business name and how many units are affected. That is the filter between a price shopper and a restaurant that needs you today.
