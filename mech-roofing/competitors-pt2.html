🔴 Finding 1 · They have a page for every town and every job. You have one per town.

RuFR has 14 pages for Rutherfordton alone (replacement, repair, insurance claims, hail damage, metal roofing and more). Hayes has roofing, gutters and siding pages for Rutherfordton, Columbus, Tryon, Landrum and Hendersonville. You have one page for each of 10 towns, and none for Mill Spring.

When somebody searches “roof replacement Rutherfordton”, Google looks for a page about exactly that.

🔴 Finding 2 · Two of them can see which ads bring jobs. You cannot.

RuFR and Woot both have a Google Ads tag and Google Analytics. You have neither. That is very likely part of why Google Ads “did not work” for you: without the tag, Google cannot learn which clicks became roofs.

⚠ Finding 3 · Woot has a Forest City page, and Hayes shows jobs near you

Woot, from Morganton, has a page for Forest City, Rutherfordton and Shelby. Hayes’s job gallery lists finished roofs in Mill Spring, Lake Lure and Columbus. Firms from outside your area are already building pages for your towns.

⭐ Finding 4 · Your reviews are the thing they would want

Hayes has 372 reviews and you have 34, but yours talk about exactly what homeowners worry about on an insurance job: the paperwork, the clean-up and not being subcontracted. Ask every finished customer by text and that count grows every week.

The short version

You are not losing on the work. You are losing on how many towns and jobs Google can match you to, and on measuring what the ads bring in. Both are fixable on your existing website.
