The three things holding you back
⚠ 1. You cannot see where your calls come from

There is no Google Analytics 4, no Google Ads tag and no Tag Manager on any page. Your RealWork plugin counts clicks on the phone and the form, and your Facebook pixel is live, but nothing tells you which search, ad or post sent a customer. That is the same gap that lets an agency tell you whatever it likes about results.

⚠ 2. Your Google listing needs tidying before anything else

The Review Us button and map link on your site point to a different Google listing from the one Google shows with your 34 reviews. Your address reads Forest City on your site and Mill Spring on BBB and Yelp. And the reply to your newest review (1 star, 2 November) thanks the reviewer for great service, which reads badly to anyone who looks. These are free to fix, and Google leans on all of them.

⚠ 3. The other roofers have far more Google reviews

Hayes Roofing shows up for Rutherfordton with 372 Google reviews and Woot Roofing has 80. You have 34. Your customers clearly like you; most of them have simply never been asked.

Your site, page by page
mechroofandgutter.com: 24 pages, built on Duda
  • ⭐ Good: a page for every service, ten town pages linked from the home page, titles that name the job and the town, a local tap-to-call number and a Book Now button on every page, and 31 real Google reviews on the site.
  • Home page: the main heading is “Roofs Built to Last” with only 2 sub-headings, so Google gets little from it about the jobs and towns.
  • Town pages: about 2,000 words each, but no photos of jobs in that town and no reviews from that town, though your job log has both.
  • Markup: only the home page tells Google you are a roofing contractor.
  • The quote form asks name, phone, email and message, with no job type, town or photo. Its hidden labels read “Field label”, which screen readers will read out.
  • Copy: the services say “5 years of experience” several times and the siding page says “We have 5 five years of experience”. Small, but worth a tidy.
Search

We ran two searches on DuckDuckGo with the US region set before it stopped answering. Positions count the free results only; one paid ad sat above them each time.

  • “roofing contractor Forest City NC”: directories first (Yellow Pages, Yelp, Angi, BBB), then Sunrise Roofing Services, J & C Roofing and EF Home Improvement. Your site was not in the top 10.
  • “roof replacement Rutherfordton NC”: RuFR USA, Hayes Roofing (twice), All Of Us Roofing and Sunrise Roofing Services, all with a page for that town. Your own Rutherfordton page was not in the top 10. ⭐ You were named in a Yelp list of roofers near Rutherfordton that came up 9th.

⭐ Searching Google Maps for your name finds you straight away, with your 34 reviews. Customers who already know you can find you. The work is in being found by the ones who do not.

⚠ What we could not measure
  • Your Google map position for roofing searches, and anything inside your Google Business Profile.
  • Facebook, Yelp, Angi and BBB pages: they blocked our tool.
  • Ads: nobody can see another firm’s ad spend from outside, and we did not check the Meta Ad Library or Google’s ad centre.
  • Page speed: Google’s speed test refused our requests that day.
