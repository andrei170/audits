Getting the enquiry · 75 / 100

⭐ Tap-to-call on all 24 pages and a Book Now button on every page. Most roofers make people phone.

⚠ The quote form is on 11 of 24 pages and on none of the 10 town pages. Someone who lands on your Rutherfordton page from a search has to scroll for the number or click away to find a form, and the form they find asks nothing about the job. Fix: a short form at the top of every page asking job type and zip first, then insurance claim, how soon, and a photo.

Knowing where calls come from · 20 / 100

🔴 0 of 24 pages carry Google Analytics, Tag Manager or a Google Ads tag. Your Facebook pixel is on all 24 and your RealWork plugin counts clicks on the phone and form, but nothing records which search, ad or post sent a customer, and none of it reaches Google.

This is the same gap that lets an agency tell you whatever it likes about results, which is how you described the last two. Fix: tracking on every call, form and booking, in your name, that you can read yourself.
