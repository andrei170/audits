What the grades say
⭐ You are not the weakest roofer here

On these five checks you grade B, 71, ahead of O&T (D, 53) and Sunrise (F, 12). Your enquiry route and your business markup are as good as anyone’s on the list.

🔴 The two roofers ahead of you are ahead on tracking and pages

Woot Roofing (A-, 88) and RuFR USA (A-, 84) both carry Google Analytics and a Google Ads tag, so they can see which ads bring jobs. Woot has 55 pages and RuFR 140, against your 24, and Competitors Pt.2 shows how many of those are town pages, including Woot’s page for Forest City.

⚠ Hayes is the one to watch on reviews

Hayes Roofing has 372 Google reviews at 4.9 and 153 pages in its sitemap. Its home page is behind a bot check, so its tracking, markup and form were not read and are not graded. Two of five checks only.

What changed since 5 November
  • Your Google reviews went from 34 at 4.6 to 35 at 4.7.
  • Sunrise Roofing’s own sitemap now lists only 2 addresses (its home page and a default “hello world” post). On 5 November we noted Forest City and Rutherfordton pages for it seen in search results; we could not find them in its sitemap today.
  • RuFR USA’s Google rating and review count still do not show in Maps search results.

The short version: your reviews and your site are a decent base. The gap to Woot and RuFR is tracking and pages per town, and both are fixed on your existing website. See it done on the demo site.
