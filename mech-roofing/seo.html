AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and similar)
Not named in any of three answers

We asked three questions the way a homeowner would ask an AI assistant:

  • “Who is the best roofing company in Rutherford County NC for a full roof replacement?” Named Elite Roof & Solar and Dunlop Roofing (commercial). Not you.
  • “Which roofer should I call for hail or storm damage near Forest City NC who works with insurance?” Said it could not find a roofer based in Forest City, and sent the homeowner to firms in Charlotte, Asheville and Raleigh. Storm and insurance work is 70 to 80% of what you do.
  • “Who is a reliable roofer near Mill Spring or Columbus NC?” Named Carolina Roofing Siding Painting (66 reviews), Ridge Roofing (39), Secure Roofing (18), Carolinas Exteriors and Ricardo’s Alpha Roofing, and pointed to the GAF contractor directory. Not you, in your own town.
⭐ The pattern

AI answers named roofers with reviews on directories it can read (Angi, BBB, GAF) and pages that say plainly what they do and where. Your 34 Google reviews are not reaching those places: HomeAdvisor shows one review from 2021. Every one of these is fixable.

Three questions on one AI search tool on one day is a snapshot, not a measurement of every assistant.

What is holding the site back (from the audit)
  • The home page heading, “Roofs Built to Last”, names no job and no town.
  • No business markup on your service and town pages, so Google and AI cannot read who you are, where you are and which towns you cover.
  • Two addresses: your site’s business details say 162 E Main St, Forest City; you told us Mill Spring is right.
  • The Review Us button goes to a second Google listing, so new reviews are split.
  • No Google Analytics, Ads tag or Tag Manager, so nothing tells Google which visits became roofs.
  • Storm and insurance work has no page of its own that a homeowner (or an AI assistant) would land on.
What we would do
Build on the 24 pages you have
  • A storm damage and insurance claims page, and one per town you want storm work from.
  • Business markup on every page, one address (Mill Spring) everywhere, and one heading per page that names the job and the town.
  • One Google listing, with Review Us pointed at it.
  • Directory profiles brought up to date: HomeAdvisor, Angi, BBB, Yelp, and the GAF or other manufacturer directory if you hold a certification.
  • A review request after every roof, so reviews build where AI looks.
  • Short answer pages for what homeowners ask: “Will insurance pay for my roof after hail?”, “Metal or shingles in the foothills?”

⭐ Why it matters alongside ads: Local Services Ads and Roof Nuts stop the moment you stop paying. These pages keep bringing calls you own, and they make every ad cheaper because Google rates the landing page higher.
