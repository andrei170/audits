The site itself is better built than most: a page per job, ten town pages, a local number and online booking. What holds it back is that nothing tells you which calls came from where, and your Google listing, reviews and address need tidying before any money goes into ads.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (5 November 2026)
BPages on the site
⭐ 24 pages in your sitemap: a page for each service (roof installation, roof repair, gutters, siding, decks, windows, pressure washing, insurance work, storm damage), plus reviews, recent projects, gallery and contact. Most roofers we look at have far fewer.
B-Town pages
⭐ 10 town pages, all linked from your home page: Forest City, Rutherfordton, Shelby, Kings Mountain, Columbus, Tryon, Saluda, Hendersonville, Asheville and Landrum SC. ⚠ Several towns where your own job log shows work (Weaverville, Black Mountain, Mooresboro, Mill Spring, Spindale, Lake Lure) have no page, and the town pages carry no photos or reviews from that town.
A-Page titles
⭐ Every service page names the job and Forest City (“Roof Repair | Forest City, NC”), and every town page names its town. This is done right.
CMain headings
Service and town pages have one clear main heading each. But the home page’s is “Roofs Built to Last”, which names no job and no town, and the contact, reviews, gallery and recent projects pages have no main heading at all. The home page has only 2 sub-headings.
BSearch descriptions
⭐ Every page has one and most name the job and the town. The siding page’s just says “Elevate your home”.
CLocal business markup
The home page tells Google you are a roofing contractor, with your address, phone, email and hours. ⚠ None of the service or town pages carries it, so Google is not told which jobs and towns each page is about.
FGoogle tracking
No Google Analytics 4, no Google Ads tag and no Tag Manager on any of the 24 pages. If you ran Google Ads tomorrow there would be no way to see which ads brought calls.
BFacebook (Meta) pixel
⭐ Live on every page. So Facebook can already build audiences of people who visited your site.
DKnowing where each call came from
Your RealWork plugin counts clicks on the phone number, email, quote form and Book Now button. That is a start. But nothing records which search, ad or post sent that person, and none of it reaches Google.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ 828-829-6750, a local number, is tap-to-call at the top and bottom of every page.
A-Online booking
⭐ A Book Now button (Housecall Pro) on every page. Most roofers make people call.
DQuote form
Name, phone, email and a message. It does not ask the job type, the town or zip, whether it is an insurance claim, or for a photo, so every enquiry needs a call back to sort it.
BGoogle reviews
4.6 stars from 34 Google reviews. ⚠ Hayes Roofing, which shows up for Rutherfordton, has 372 and Woot Roofing has 80.
A-Reviews on your site
⭐ 31 real Google reviews shown on your site, with towns and names, and many naming you. That is better than most.
DReplies to reviews
The newest review (1 star, 2 November 2026) has a reply that thanks the reviewer for great service from “Travis”, which does not match what they wrote. Three of the reviews from November and December 2025 have no reply.
DOne Google listing
The Review Us button and the map link on your site point to a different Google listing from the one Google shows today with your 34 reviews. Worth checking which one is yours.
DSame address everywhere
Your site says 162 E Main St, Forest City. Your BBB and Yelp web addresses say Mill Spring. Google puts your pin in a third place. Google matches a business across sites partly by its address.

⭐ Nothing here needs a new website to fix. The pages, the services and the reviews are already there.

⚠ Not graded, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed (Google’s speed test refused our requests that day), your Facebook page, Yelp, Angi and BBB pages, and anything inside your Google Business Profile, ads, Housecall Pro or RealWork accounts.
