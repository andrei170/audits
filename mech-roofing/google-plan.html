From 3 jobs a week to 6

⭐ You told us you do about 3 jobs a week and want 6, and you have the crew. That is about 13 extra jobs a month (3 more a week, times 52 weeks, divided by 12).

How many extra leads that takes depends on how many of your quotes you win, which only you know. As examples:

If you winExtra leads a month to get 13 extra jobs
If you win 1 in 2 quotes26 extra leads a month
If you win 1 in 3 quotes39 extra leads a month
If you win 1 in 4 quotes52 extra leads a month

⚠ These are arithmetic, not a forecast. Your real close rate, and which jobs you want (full replacements, repairs or insurance work), decide the real number. Month one replaces these examples with yours.

Why this time can be different
You see every number yourself
  • Tracking goes in first, before any money is spent. Every call, form and booking shows where it came from.
  • Everything is in your name: Google listing, ad accounts, tracking. You can log in and check.
  • We agree with you what “working” means before we start, in numbers you can check: leads, booked inspections, jobs won.
  • Start small in the towns you choose, and grow only what brings jobs.
1. Your Google Business Profile · free, and first
What we would do
  • Find out which of the two Google listings is yours, and make sure there is only one, in your control.
  • One address everywhere: your site, Google, BBB, Yelp and Facebook.
  • Main category roofing contractor, with gutters, siding and the rest as extra services, and your service area set by town.
  • Add job photos every week. Your RealWork job log already takes them.
  • Fix the reply on the 2 November review, and reply to the ones without a reply.
2. Reviews · closing the gap
Where you stand

34 Google reviews against Hayes Roofing’s 372 and Woot’s 80. Your rating is good; the count is what is behind.

What we would do
  • A text with your Google review link on the day each job is finished, from your name and number.
  • A friendly reminder a few days later.
  • Ask past customers from the last year, starting with the ones who already said nice things in person.
  • Reply to every review, in your words, about what they actually wrote.
3. Google Local Services Ads · pay per lead
What it is

The ads at the very top of Google with a green check badge, which Google gives after checking a business’s licence, insurance and background. You pay when a homeowner calls or messages you, not for a click. Google offers it for roofers; ⚠ we have not yet checked that it is open in your towns, and would do that first.

What we would do
  • Apply under roofing for your towns and get the checks done.
  • Pick the jobs you want, so you are not paying for leads you would not take.
  • Answer fast: these ads rank partly on how quickly you pick up, and your reviews count a lot.
  • Mark each lead as booked or not, so the ones you do not want stop coming.
4. Google Search Ads · people ready to book
What it is

Text ads for searches like “roof replacement Forest City NC”, “roof leak repair Rutherfordton” or “hail damage roof insurance claim”. You choose the searches, the towns and the daily budget.

What we would do
  • Lead with full replacements, metal roofs and storm and insurance work, where one job is worth the most.
  • Each ad sends people to the page about that job and that town, not the home page.
  • Only switch on once tracking is in.
  • Start in the towns closest to Forest City, then add the outer towns that bring jobs.
5. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads · planned re-roofs
Why it fits a roofer

Your pixel is already live and one of your reviewers found you through a Facebook ad. Meta puts you in front of homeowners who are not searching yet: good for planned replacements, metal roofs and gutters, using your own before and after photos. Weaker for emergency leaks.

What we would do
  • Ads built from your own job photos and your customers’ words, aimed at homeowners in your towns.
  • A short form that asks the job, the town and whether it is an insurance claim, then a fast reply.
  • Every lead counted the same way as Google, so you can compare the two side by side.
Underneath all of it: tracking
So you can see what brings jobs
  • Google Analytics 4, the Google Ads tag and Meta’s conversion events on every page.
  • Call tracking on the website, Google listing and every ad.
  • A quote form that asks the job type, town, insurance claim yes or no, and a photo.
  • A text back within seconds if you miss a call while you are on a roof.
  • One short monthly report: leads, booked inspections, jobs won, and where each came from.
