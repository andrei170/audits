The “burned before” objection
“I’ve worked with companies before and they didn’t get me what they promised.”Agree, and ask what they promised and what he got. Then: “We will not promise you a number of jobs. What we will do is put tracking in first, in your name, so you can see every lead and where it came from yourself. You judge us on that, not on what we say.”
“How do I know you’re different?”Show the pack: built from his own site, Google listing and reviews, with what we could not check marked plainly. Offer the free fixes on the call (Google listing, the 2 November reply, the address) whether he works with us or not.
“Word of mouth is enough.”It got him to 3 a week and it is his best source. The plan adds to it: reviews and the Google listing make word of mouth findable.
“I tried Facebook ads.”One reviewer found him that way, so it worked at least once. Without tracking nobody could tell how often. Ask who ran it and what it cost him.
“Send me something.”Send the hub link (assets.html), but book the next step before hanging up.
