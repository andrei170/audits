What this means for you
1The engineer you want is being offered a lot, in writing.

Employed domestic roles in London on Reed today run from about £44,000 to £60,000 basic, usually with a company van and fuel card. Self-employed and contract roles quote £55 an hour, £200 to £220 a day, or £5,000 to £7,000 a month. We do not know what you offer; it is the first question for the call, because an advert without it gets ignored.

Source: Reed.co.uk adverts, read 17 November 2026. These are advertised figures, not what anyone is actually paid.

2Your closest rival for this engineer is a family firm in Parsons Green.

Established 25+ years, high-end homes in south-west London, self-employed, "a consistent flow of work". That is very close to what you offer. The difference is that they have written it down and put it where engineers look.

Source: Reed advert, posted 30 October 2026.

3The adverts compete on the things your notes say matter: travel, hours and a local patch.

"Monday to Friday only, no weekends", "all work within the M25", "within 10 miles of Staines", "local patch". You told us good engineers will not commute into London. Firms that name a small patch and fixed hours are answering exactly that worry.

Source: Reed adverts, 17 November 2026.

4Almost no local heating firm has a careers page. That is your opening.

Of the 20 North and East London heating and plumbing firms whose websites we could read, only Pimlico Plumbers and Aspect (two large multi-trade firms) have a careers page, and neither shows pay. None of the 18 independents had one. A clear page that says what the job is, who it suits and how to apply would stand out.

Source: Each firm’s website, 17 November 2026.

5There is a large pool of social housing engineers, and you need to filter them out, not exclude them by accident.

Social housing repair roles advertise at £20 to £21.50 an hour. Engineers from that work will apply to a better-paid private role. A screening question about where their experience is (private homes, social housing, new-build or commercial) sorts this before you spend a minute on the phone.

Source: Reed advert, Westminster, posted 3 November 2026.

Where you are ahead
  • The kind of work many engineers want: private homes, long-standing customers, no social housing (from your notes).
  • 229 reviews at 4.96 on TrustATrader that praise the engineer who came. That tells an applicant the standard and that good work gets noticed.
  • A company since 2012 and a base in Islington, with a set of customers who come back and refer others.
  • No volume pressure like a national installer: you want one good person, so the advert can be specific and honest.
What this page does not contain

What any of these firms actually pays, how many applicants they get, or how long their vacancies stay open. Nobody can see that from outside. Adverts change daily; these are the ones live on 17 November 2026. We could not read Indeed, where you told us you have advertised.
