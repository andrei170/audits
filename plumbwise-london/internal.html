Objection notes
  • "We tried agencies, they failed." Agree. An agency sends a shortlist and takes 15 to 20% whether or not the person is right; you never see the ones they rejected. Here you see every applicant, the must-haves are checked in the form, you check the Gas Safe number and references yourself, and nobody goes near a client before a trial day. You own the advert and the list.
  • "We tried Indeed: lots of applicants, none good enough." That is the problem the application fixes. Indeed collects everyone; the nine questions turn away anyone without Gas Safe, Level 3, 5 years in homes or a van before you read a name. Show the demo with a social-housing answer.
  • "Good people won’t commute into London." So we do not ask them to commute from far away: the adverts go to engineers who already live near the work, the advert names the patch and the hours, and every application shows a home postcode.
  • "Can’t you just find us one?" That is the aim, but no one can honestly promise a date. What we can do is put the right advert in front of the right people this month and filter properly. Never promise a hire.
  • "We’ll wait for word of mouth." Five years of it has not found one. Keep it, and add a channel that runs every day.
