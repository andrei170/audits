Costs, setup and how long it takes.

In short
  • Setup takes about a week. Adverts can be live in week two.
  • Realistically 4 to 8 weeks to someone starting with you, depending on whether he is self-employed or has notice to work.
  • You can start small: a test from roughly £800 to £1,250 all in, before committing to anything ongoing.
  • An agency usually charges around £7,500 to £12,500 for one engineer on £50,000.

All of our figures on this page are rough ranges for discussion. The exact price follows once we know how many engineers you need and how quickly.

Most of these adverts also lead with Monday to Friday, no weekends, and a local patch. The pay and the van are what an engineer reads first, so they are the first thing to settle.

Three ways to start (rough ranges)
1A small test
  • Careers page and application form: about £500 to £750, one-off.
  • Adverts for 2 to 3 weeks: about £300 to £500, paid by you directly to Facebook and Instagram.
  • About £800 to £1,250 all in. You see who applies before deciding anything else.
2A hiring sprint
  • One fixed fee of about £1,000 to £1,500: setup plus four weeks of running the adverts and chasing applicants.
  • Advert spend on top, on your own card: about £500 to £1,500 for the month.
  • It stops when you have hired. Nothing monthly afterwards.
3Ongoing, if you are hiring more than one
  • Setup: about £1,000 to £2,000, one-off.
  • Running it: about £1,000 to £1,500 a month, month to month.
  • Advert spend: about £500 to £1,500 a month, on your own card.

Whichever you choose, the careers page, the adverts and every applicant are yours to keep, and can be used again the next time someone leaves. Splitting the setup, half to start and half once you have seen applicants, is also an option.

What one more engineer could be worth

As a rough illustration only: a domestic plumbing and heating engineer in London might bill around £300 to £500 a day. Across a working year that is roughly £65,000 to £110,000 of work, before his pay. Your own figures will differ: how many jobs a week you turn down or push back, and what an average job is worth to you, are the numbers that matter here.

Christmas

Fewer people move jobs in December. Starting now means good engineers can be talking to you before Christmas, with a start in January. Starting in January means the same steps begin then.
