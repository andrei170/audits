The order we would do it in

Fix the leaks, then capture, then measure, then grow. The demo website shows steps 2 and 8 working.

1Fix the leaks first (week one)

Check where 0800 744 7002 rings and point every call button at a number you answer. Claim the Google listing at your address, set the category to paving or driveway contractor, add your phone, website, photos and service area. These cost nothing and stop enquiries going nowhere.

2Ask the postcode first

Every form asks the job and the postcode before anything else. Your patch gets a yes and a visit time straight away; further out gets "we check first"; outside the area gets an honest no. You see where every job is before you pick up the phone. The demo shows it working.

3Measure everything

Google Analytics 4, Tag Manager and the Facebook pixel on the site, with every call, form and postcode recorded. Then you can see which towns, pages and adverts bring real enquiries, and which bring nothing.

4Adverts that only reach your area

Facebook and Google adverts limited to the postcodes you choose, each landing on a page for one job (for example resin drives in Market Harborough) with the postcode form. The pixel tells Facebook which enquiries were real and local, so it finds more of those. This is the direct answer to the far-away jobs.

5Make TrustATrader work harder, without relying on it

Keep TrustATrader: it clearly works for you. Show its 5.0 and real reviews on your own site, and after every job ask the customer for a Google review as well, so the Google listing catches up. That way a quiet month on one channel is not a quiet month for the business.

6Know what flyers and boards bring

Give flyers and ad boards their own number or web address, so you can see how many enquiries each one brings and spend where it works.

7Follow up every enquiry fast

An instant text back to every missed call and form, so a homeowner who reaches you while you are on a job hears back in minutes. You set what it says; a real person still calls.

8Town pages that are genuinely local

One page per town you want work in (Market Harborough, Kettering, Corby, Lutterworth first), each with a recent job, a photo and the postcode form, instead of near copies.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of enquiries, jobs or a ranking. What these steps do is make sure the enquiries you pay for are from your own area, that every one is answered quickly, and that you can see what works. Many of the website changes could be done by whoever looks after your site now; the demo is a blueprint, not a demand to switch.
