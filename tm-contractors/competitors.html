What this means for you
1On Google, every one of them looks stronger than you, and none of them is better than you on TrustATrader.

Kingswood shows 4.9 from 92 and Approved 4.9 from 65 on Google. Your listing shows 2.3 from 3 as a "Corporate office". Your 37 TrustATrader reviews at 5.0 would put you among the best rated in the area, but a homeowner on Google never sees them.

Source: Google Maps and trustatrader.com, 16 November 2026.

2Three of the six measure their visitors or adverts. You measure nothing.

Approved runs Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager, JK Paving runs Google Analytics 4, Rutland has a Google Ads tag and StyleSeal runs Tag Manager. They can see which pages and adverts bring enquiries. None of the six has a Facebook pixel either, so the firm that gets Facebook right first has the field to itself.

Source: Home page code of each site, 16 November 2026. A tag shows a firm is set up to measure or advertise, not how much it spends.

3Approved puts its review score and its years on the first screen.

"Est. 2001", "4.9 rated across TrustATrader & more" and a written promise sit at the top of its home page. You have more years in the trade (30 plus, from your TrustATrader profile) and a 5.0, and neither is on your site.

Source: approveddrivesandpatios.co.uk home page, 16 November 2026.

4Rutland has built a page for nearly every service in nearly every town, including yours.

490 pages, 164 of them naming a town, including Market Harborough, Kettering and Corby. That is how a firm based in Rutland comes up for Market Harborough searches. Your 12 town pages are mostly copies of each other.

Source: rutlanddriveways.co.uk sitemap, 16 November 2026.

5Nobody we read asks for your postcode before the details.

Not one of the six forms we saw checks the area first. A form that tells a homeowner straight away whether you cover them, and tells you before you ring back, would be a first in this patch.

Source: Each firm’s home page, 16 November 2026.
