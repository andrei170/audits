Why the old ads brought far-away jobs, from what we can see
⚠ Three things on the site that make it likely
  • No Facebook pixel. Without it, Facebook cannot see who went on to send an enquiry, so it cannot learn which people (and which places) turn into jobs. It optimises for cheap clicks instead.
  • No postcode on the form. Every enquiry arrives without saying where it is, so the far ones are only found out on the phone, after the money is spent.
  • The site names six towns from Market Harborough to Peterborough and Northampton, so an advert pointed at "the areas we cover" covers a wide stretch of the East Midlands.
  • We cannot see the old ad account, so we cannot say how it was targeted. These are the gaps on the site that would have let it happen.
