Objection notes
  • "The last ads guy got me jobs outside my area." Agree, and show why: no pixel on the site, so Facebook could not learn what a real local enquiry looks like, and no postcode on the form, so he only found out on the phone. We fix both before an advert runs, limit adverts to his postcodes, and he sees the postcode of every enquiry. Demo the postcode check live.
  • "TrustATrader already works." Agree: 5.0 from 37 is excellent and we would keep it. But it is one channel he does not own, and he said the last 1 to 2 months have been quiet. Ask: "If TrustATrader is the main source and it goes quiet, what else is there?" Our job is to add channels he owns (Google, his own site, local adverts) and put his TAT reviews to work on them.
  • "Flyers and boards work for me." Good; keep them, and give them their own number or link so he knows how many each brings.
  • "I tried ads, they don’t work." The ads ran with nothing measuring them. Ask what he spent and what he got; we start with the free fixes (the call button, the Google listing) before any spend.
  • "I’m too busy / it is the quiet season." Quiet months are when the setup gets done, so it is ready when people start planning drives. Do not claim seasonality as fact; ask him when his busy months are.
  • "I need to think about it." "Of course. What specifically would you want to think over?" Then deal with that.
