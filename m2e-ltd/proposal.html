The order is the argument. The first fix costs nothing and unblocks everything after it. Each step builds on the one before.

🔴 1. Unblock Google, this week

Remove the noindex tag, submit a sitemap that includes the homepage, and settle on one address. Until the homepage is listable, nothing else can work.

🔴 2. Show the proof

Three to five projects: the sector, the problem, what you built, the standard. Client names only with permission; “a UK airport baggage system” works without one. Name the standards you build and test to.

🔴 3. Count everything

Tracking on the site and the form, so every enquiry is traced to where it came from: Google, an AI assistant, a referral or an ad.

4. Become the answer: a page for each service, sector and question

Control panels, electrical design, functional safety, installation. Conveyors, food and pharma, machinery. An FAQ. Business details marked up for Google and AI tools. Each page answers a question a buyer types or asks. The demo site shows the shape of it.

5. Google Business Profile and LinkedIn

Set up or tidy the profile, matching the website exactly, and link them both ways.

6. Then reach buyers directly, locally

Google search ads on the exact searches engineers make, shown only within the distance you choose around Tamworth, landing on the matching page. Every enquiry counted.

⚠ Nothing here forecasts a number of jobs. Which work, which sectors and how far you will travel is the first thing we need from you.
