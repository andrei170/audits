In the order we would do it

The order is the argument. The first fix costs nothing and unblocks everything else.

🔴 1. Remove the noindex tag, today

One setting in your website builder. Until it is gone, Google will not show your homepage.

🔴 2. Show the proof

Three to five projects: the sector, the problem, what you built, the standard. Client names only with permission; “a UK airport baggage system” works without one.

🔴 3. Name your standards, and count your visitors

The standards you build and test to, on every page. And tracking, so every enquiry is traced to where it came from.

4. A page for each service and each sector

Control panels, schematics, functional safety, installation documentation. Conveyors, food and beverage, pharma. Each one is a search an engineer makes.

5. Then reach the buyers directly

Targeted search ads and LinkedIn aimed at engineering and maintenance managers in the Midlands, sent to the matching page.

⚠ Nothing here forecasts a number of jobs. Which sectors and job sizes you want more of is the first thing we need.
