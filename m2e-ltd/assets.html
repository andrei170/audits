⚠ Questions that would make the call far more useful
  • The ChatGPT enquiries: roughly how often, what did the person ask for, and what did they say when they rang? That tells us which questions to build pages around.
  • Which work do you want more of? Full design and build, drawing-only work, safety assessments, or installation packs. And which sectors?
  • What counts as local? How far will you travel for a job, and which towns would you most like to be the first name for?
  • Who buys from you? Engineering or maintenance managers at the factory, machine builders, or main contractors? The answer changes the pages and the ads.
  • What can we show? Projects, photos of panels, sectors, even without client names.
  • Which standards do you build and test to, and do you hold any certificates such as ISO 9001?
  • Google Business Profile and LinkedIn: do you have them, and who has the login to the current website builder?
  • Which address should Google use? Wilnecote on the website or the Shenstone registered office?
  • Capacity: you said you can take on as much as comes in. If enquiries doubled, what would you need first: another panel builder, an engineer, or nothing?
