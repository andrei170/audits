How AI assistants and Google find you
⭐ Why this page exists

You told our team some of your enquiries already come through ChatGPT. This page measures what an AI tool can learn about M2E from your own website, and what would make it recommend you more often.

🔴🔴 The finding

We ran a live AI-style search on 15 October for “control panel design and build company Staffordshire Tamworth Lichfield”. The answer began: “I didn’t find a control panel company based in Tamworth or Lichfield.”

🔴 You are exactly that company. With the homepage set to noindex and no business details marked up, search and AI tools cannot see it. The ChatGPT enquiries you already get are coming in despite the website, not because of it.

⚠ What would change it
  • Remove the noindex tag from the homepage
  • Mark up your business details: M2E Ltd, address, phone, services, and the areas you cover
  • A page for each service and sector, each answering the questions an engineer asks before buying
  • Three to five project write-ups, which are what AI answers quote
  • One address everywhere: website, Google, Companies House and LinkedIn
  • A proper llms.txt listing your services, sectors, standards and contact details

⚠ Not measured: what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini answer on any given day (it changes), rankings, and page speed. We have not guessed.
