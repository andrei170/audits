🔴🔴 What it shows

Six questions about the work you do, near where you do it. M2E was named in none. On 15 October a similar question got: “I didn’t find a control panel company based in Tamworth or Lichfield.”

⭐ Three answers said, in effect, “nobody local does this”: no panel builder in Tamworth, no baggage and parcel panel specialist in the Midlands, no West Midlands firm offering Sistema calculations. M2E does all three. Where it named anyone, it reached for firms further away. It is not that it ranks you lower; it cannot see you.

⚠ How to read this: we used an AI web-search assistant, which searches the live web the way ChatGPT does when it looks something up. It is not ChatGPT itself, and answers change by day and by wording. That some enquiries already reach you through ChatGPT suggests it sometimes finds you; the test shows how often it does not.

How we would make M2E the answer
  1. Unblock Google: remove the noindex tag and submit a sitemap that includes the homepage
  2. One identity everywhere: the same name, address and phone on the website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories
  3. Mark up the business: name, address, phone, services, software, sectors and areas, in the code Google and AI tools read
  4. A page for each service and sector, each written around the question a buyer asks: “who builds conveyor control panels near Tamworth?”
  5. An FAQ page answering what engineers ask before they call: software, safety, standards, lead times, distance
  6. Three to five project write-ups: AI answers quote specifics, and a project is the most quotable thing you can publish
  7. A proper llms.txt that lists the services, sectors, software and contact details (the demo site has an example)
  8. Re-run these same six questions every month and show you which ones name M2E

⚠ Nobody controls what an AI assistant says. These steps give it the facts to find and repeat; we will not promise a position.
