🔴🔴 The headline

Your homepage has a noindex tag. It is an instruction to Google: do not show this page in search results. We found it on 15 October and it was still there on 20 October.

It is usually left on by accident when a site is built. Removing it is a five-minute change, and until it goes, the most important page you own cannot appear on Google or in the search results AI assistants draw on.

1. Google and search
🔴 Hard for Google to find, and nothing local about it
  • Homepage set to noindex, and missing from your sitemap, which was last updated in July 2022
  • Page titles say “UK”. No page names Tamworth, Lichfield or the Midlands, though you prefer local work
  • Google is given no address, phone, services or business type, only the site name
  • Five short pages, so there is little for a search to match against

⚠ We could not run reliable Google ranking checks: Google blocks automated searches. With the homepage set to noindex, it cannot rank for anything.

2. AI assistants (ChatGPT and the like)
🔴 Asked six times, it named other firms every time

On 20 October we asked an AI web-search assistant six questions a buyer might ask, including “control panel design and build company near Tamworth” and “who builds control panels for baggage and parcel conveyors in the Midlands”. M2E appeared in none of the answers. Asked about M2E by name, it said it “returned nothing about M2E Ltd in Tamworth”.

⭐ The good news: three of the answers said it could not find exactly what you do, near where you are. That is a gap waiting for you. See all six questions and answers →

3. What a buyer sees when they land
🔴 No proof and no standards

An engineering manager choosing a panel builder wants to see what you have built, for whom, and to what standard. Your site shows no projects, clients, photos of panels or testimonials, and names no standard.

⚠ The competitors we read lead with exactly this: case studies by sector, named projects, ISO 9001, BS EN 61439. See the comparison →

⚠ The homepage on a phone

In our phone-width test, the headline and the opening paragraph run off the right-hand edge of the screen. Many first looks happen on a phone.

⚠ The enquiry form asks almost nothing

Name, email, phone, message. It does not ask the type of work, the sector or the timescale, so the first call has to collect all of it.

⚠ Nothing is being counted

No Google Analytics, no tags of any kind. Nobody can say how many people visit, from where, or which enquiries came from Google, ChatGPT or a referral.

  • Google: the Google Ads Transparency Centre shows 0 ads for m2eltd.com in the UK, at any time (checked 20 October). Check it yourself
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): your site has no Meta pixel and links to no Facebook page. ⚠ Meta’s ad library would not load for our tools, so we have not confirmed it there. Open the Meta ad library search

None of the competitors we read showed a Google Ads tag on 15 October.
