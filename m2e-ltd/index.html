What we found
🔴🔴 The headline

Your homepage has a noindex tag. It is an instruction to Google: do not show this page in search results. We confirmed it in the page code on 15 October 2026.

🔴 It is usually left on by accident when a site is first built. Removing it is a five-minute change, and until it is gone, the most important page you own cannot be found on Google.

🔴 1. No proof for buyers spending up to £100,000

An engineering manager choosing a panel builder wants to see what you have built, for whom, and to what standard. Your site shows no clients, no projects, no case studies and no testimonials.

⚠ Competitors lead with exactly this: SM Control Engineering shows case studies by sector, Controlsforce shows projects and a customer quote, Towerglens shows its panel standards.

🔴 2. No standards named

Buyers in food, pharma and baggage handling check standards before they shortlist. Your site names none. Every competitor that lists one shows ISO 9001, and Towerglens shows BS EN 61439, the standard for low-voltage switchgear assemblies.

⚠ If you build to a standard, it belongs on every page. If you do not hold one, it may be worth a conversation.

⚠ 3. Nothing is being counted

No Google Analytics, no tags of any kind. So nobody can say how many engineers visit, from where, or what they look at.

⚠ 4. It has not changed since 2022

The sitemap was last updated in July 2022, pages run 100 to 300 words, and the footer still shows the Tamworth address, though your registered office moved in May 2026. To a buyer, an unchanged site can read as a business that is not busy, when the opposite is true.

⭐ What is already strong
  • A real specialism: panel design and build, EPLAN and AutoCAD Electrical schematics, and an in-house functional safety engineer, from Sistema calculations to performance levels
  • Panels built and tested by your own team
  • Named sectors: baggage and parcel conveyors, food and beverage, pharmaceutical, special purpose machinery
  • Trading since 2015, according to Companies House, though the site never says so
  • Tap-to-call on every page

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, where you rank, and page speed. We have not guessed.
