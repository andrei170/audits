What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ The aim is to make twenty years of good work visible, make it easy for the right customers to reach you, and make sure no enquiry or quote goes quiet while you are on the tools.

1. Decide the work you want · On the call

The job types worth your time (domestic, landlord EICRs, commercial, industrial), the areas you will travel to, and how much more you can take on.

⭐ Everything below is sized to that answer.

2. Make it easy to call and send a job · Week 1

Your number on every page and tap-to-call on a phone, a quote form on every page asking job type, urgency, postcode and photos, and a site-survey form for commercial buyers.

This is the fastest fix and it does not depend on anything else.

3. Answer every enquiry while you are working · Week 1

A text back within seconds on a missed call, an instant reply to every form, and every enquiry saved in one place with its source.

⭐ This is what stops jobs going to the next name on the list.

4. A page for each job you want more of · Weeks 1 to 3

EICRs and landlords, fuse boards and rewires, fire alarms and emergency lighting, commercial, industrial, EV charge points, and the towns you want, with your name, NICEIC approval, real reviews and real photos.

⚠ If you want to keep the current site and its host, this becomes a checklist.

5. Google profile and reviews · Weeks 1 to 4

A complete Google Business Profile with a service area, and a review request by text after every job, so new reviews land on Google as well as Checkatrade.

Reviews decide the map results and what AI answers quote.

6. Let AI tools and Google read you properly · Week 2

Full Electrician markup, readable accreditation logos, page titles that name the service and the area, and a decision with you on the robots.txt blocks.

Nothing changes on the robots file without your say.

7. Measurement · Week 2

Analytics, call tracking and conversion tracking, and a source on every job.

So you can see which source, Checkatrade included, really brings the work.

8. Commercial: shortlist and follow-up · From month 2

Commercial-only Google searches, LinkedIn by job title, retargeting, and the quote follow-up pipeline with retest reminders.

⚠ Only if commercial is the work you want more of. We start small and measured.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your job values, how much work you want, or what Checkatrade costs you. Anyone giving you a job forecast before seeing those would be inventing it.
