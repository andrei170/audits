Digital Presence Scorecard
F
43 / 100
2 of 16 checks passed. 9 failed.
Twenty years of trading, NICEIC approval and 80 perfect reviews are real. The website hides most of it, and makes it hard to ring you or send a job.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (24 Oct 2026)
FEnquiry form
One form on the whole site, on the contact page only. It asks Name, Email, Message and Domestic, Commercial or Industrial. No phone number field, no postcode, no job type, no photos. The home page and all three service pages have no form at all.
FPhone number and tap-to-call
07930 209 725 appears only on the contact page, as plain text. It is not on the home page or any service page, and nowhere on the site can a mobile visitor tap to call.
FPages and service depth
7 pages in total (per the site's own sitemap). The three service pages list about 60 services between them as bullet points. EICRs, fuse boards, rewires, fire alarms, emergency lighting and EV charge points have no page of their own, and no town has a page.
FPage titles and descriptions
Every page has a description (good). But the home page title is “Stenic Electrical Contracting Ltd -” and ends on a dash, no title names Dartford or a service a customer searches for, and the home description reads “Domestic,, commercial”.
FHeadings
One main heading per page, but they read “STENIC ELECTRICAL CONTRACTING LIMITED”, “DOMESTIC”, “COMMERCIAL”, “INDUSTRIAL” and “gallery”. None says what the service is or where.
FLocal business markup
There is basic Organization markup (name, phone, email, founded 2006), added by your SEO plugin. It does not say you are an electrician, where you work or your hours, which is what Google and AI tools look for.
FMeasurement
No Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads tag or Meta pixel on any page. A visitor counter plugin is installed, but nothing records which visits became calls or enquiries.
CAccreditations shown
⭐ NICEIC, TrustMark and Domestic Installer logos sit on the home page. But they are images with no text description, so Google cannot read them, they are not linked to anything a customer can check, and they do not appear on the commercial or industrial pages, where a buyer looks for them.
CReviews
⭐ Three written reviews on the home page and a link to Checkatrade, where you hold 10 out of 10 from 80 reviews. But Checkatrade shows only 1 review in the past 12 months, and no Google review count could be read.
DProof of work
A gallery of 27 captioned photos (garden lighting, EV charge points, fuse boards, kitchen lighting). Almost all of it is domestic. Only a stage lighting repair and sensory lighting in a suspended ceiling hint at other settings, and no caption says what kind of building it was, while the site lists about 36 commercial and industrial services.
FWho you are
The site says it was “founded by an experienced electrician” but never names you. Your name only appears inside customer reviews. There is no About page (both /about and /about-us return “not found”).
BProfessional email
⭐ enquiries@stenic.co.uk, on your own domain. It is plain text rather than a tap-to-email link.
A-Facts that match the public record
⭐ “Established in 2006” matches Companies House exactly (incorporated 5 September 2006), and NICEIC is confirmed on your Checkatrade profile as well as your site.
DAI and ad crawler access
Your robots.txt file opts the site out of AI training: OpenAI’s training crawler (GPTBot), Google-Extended (Gemini) and Common Crawl are told to stay out. It does not block Google search, ChatGPT’s search crawler or Perplexity. It also blocks a crawler named “AdsBot”, which is worth clearing up before any Google Ads run. See the search page for the detail.
FImage descriptions
On the home page, 3 of 5 images have no description (alt text), including all three accreditation logos.
DFreshness
The footer still says ©2024. The service pages were last changed on 5 August 2025; the home page on 12 January 2026.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about the website, not about your work. The things that take years to earn (twenty years of trading, NICEIC approval and 80 reviews at 10 out of 10) are already done.

After the build: the form, phone, pages, titles, headings, markup, measurement, accreditation, who-you-are, image and freshness rows are all fixed by the build itself. Google reviews, your Google profile and search positions take weeks of steady effort, so we do not project those.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google review count, Google Business Profile, Meta ads and anything inside an ads account.
