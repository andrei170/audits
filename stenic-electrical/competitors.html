What this means for you
Your record beats most of them. Your website does not show it.
  • You have 80 reviews at 10 out of 10, NICEIC approval and twenty years of trading. That is the hard part, and it is already done.
  • The firms that show up have a page or a listing per job type and per area. REA has separate listings for commercial, testing and emergency work. ESI has a page called “Fire Safety in Dartford”. Your fire alarm, emergency lighting and EICR work sits in bullet points on one page each.
