Questions for the call
1. What does a good month look like for Stenic right now, and what would you like it to look like?So everything we suggest is sized to the work you want, not just more of it.
2. Where does your work come from today?Checkatrade, repeat customers, landlords and agents, contractors you work for, or Google?
3. Is Checkatrade still bringing you enquiries, and what does it cost you a year?Your profile shows 80 reviews but only one in the past 12 months.
4. When you are on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?Voicemail, call back in the evening, or does someone else pick up?
5. Which jobs do you want more of: domestic, landlord EICRs, commercial or industrial?And which would you rather turn down?
6. What are the biggest commercial or industrial jobs you have done, and who were they for?An office, a care home, a factory, a caravan park? That decides who any pages and ads are aimed at.
7. How did those bigger jobs find you?A contact, a main contractor, a tender, a search? That is the route we would add to, not replace.
8. What happens after you send a commercial quote?Who follows up, how often, and where it is kept. This is usually where big jobs go quiet.
9. What do commercial buyers ask you for before they say yes?Insurance level, RAMS, accreditations, references? Whatever they ask for should be on the website before they have to ask.
10. Who looks after the website now, and are you happy for it to change?Your robots file opts the site out of AI training. If that was a deliberate choice, we would like to know why before touching it.
