What the public record says, which you may not realise is visible
🔴 Nobody searching for "Iron Hammer Roofing" can find your company

Your Who We Are page says it plainly: "Iron Hammer Roofing operates as a DBA (Doing Business As) of Roof Pup, our parent company and legal business entity."

We looked Roof Pup up, and there are three registrations:

⭐⭐ Texas is the domestic entity. Oklahoma and Kansas are foreign registrations of it. In plain terms, Texas is the company and Oklahoma is a branch of it operating out of state — which is the clearest possible corroboration of what you told our team about which location suffers.

⚠ And the practical problem: a homeowner, an adjuster or a commercial client who checks the state register for "Iron Hammer Roofing" finds nothing at all, because the filing is under a different name. On a business built entirely on being trusted with somebody's insurance claim, that is a bigger gap than it sounds.

The thing we think is actually wrong

You publish four phone numbers across four cities — Oklahoma City, Dallas, Austin and Wichita — spanning roughly 550 miles.

🔴🔴 And there is not one enquiry form anywhere on the site. No analytics. No advertising tag. Every enquiry therefore depends on a person answering one of four telephones, and nothing records the ones nobody answered.

⭐⭐ That is the Oklahoma problem, written in your own website. Oklahoma sits in the worst hail belt in the country, so it is not short of demand. It is short of attention — and there is no system underneath to catch what falls through while attention is somewhere else.

🔴 Which is why we will not open by offering you more leads. In storm work the roof goes to whoever documents the damage first. More enquiries into a branch already answering late produces slower callbacks and worse reviews, not more jobs.
