The call, in order
  1. Open: "Thanks Dan. Ben said you are flat out with 4 to 5 jobs a day. What made you take the call, and what would make this half hour worth it?"
  2. The mix: "Of those jobs, how many are leaks and call-outs, and how many are installs? What would your ideal week look like?"
  3. Installs: "Boilers or bathrooms, which do you want more of? What does each come to, and how long does it take?"
  4. What he built: "You did the site and SEO yourself. What has worked? Ever tried adverts or Checkatrade?" Credit him.
  5. Hiring: "Ben said you want to take people on in the new year. Who, and when? What do you expect from Indeed?"
  6. Pain check: "1 to 10, how much do you want to get off the leaks and onto installs next year?"
  7. Summary back, wait for a clear yes, then share the audit and scorecard, then the plan.
  8. Show: credit the site, then the homepage leading with emergencies, no installation landing pages, no tracking, no careers page.
Finding capacity and revenue without sounding salesy
  • "How many installs could you fit in a week if you dropped some of the leaks?"
  • "Are you turning work away now, or picking and choosing?"
  • "When you hire, how many more jobs a week does that free up?"
  • Then let HIM do the sum: "So swapping a few leaks a week for one boiler install is worth roughly what?"
How to pitch it
  • Frame: "You do not need more work, you need different work. Your SEO keeps the leaks coming. We would build pages and adverts aimed only at installations, so the bigger jobs come in alongside, and you can see which ones pay."
  • Then the bridge: "When you hire in the new year, the same approach finds engineers, without paying Indeed and hoping."
  • Fit: Starter for installation ads; Growth if Google is in. Recruitment as phase 2. Figures are in the brief. Say them only if asked, never in writing.
  • No promise of enquiries, jobs or dates.
Close
  • "If we had installation pages live, tracking in, and adverts running for boilers and bathrooms around Ashton Vale, is there any reason not to start before the new year?"
  • "Then I need your best install photos and the jobs you want most."
  • Not ready? Book the follow-up before you hang up. Recap by email, no pricing.
