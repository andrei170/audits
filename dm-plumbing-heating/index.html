The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A proper website: services, pricing (£75 call-out covering the first hour), online booking, FAQs and an enquiry form.
  • Gas Safe registered and City & Guilds qualified, shown clearly near the top.
  • Real reviews: 5.0 from 10 Google reviews, with names and dates, shown on the homepage.
  • Pages for the areas you cover: Bedminster, Bishopston, Clifton, Keynsham, Portishead, Redland.
  • Your work on show: bathroom before-and-afters, walk-in showers and tiling.
⚠ What is missing
  • No analytics and no Meta pixel found in the page code, so you cannot see where enquiries come from, and no advert could learn who becomes a customer.
  • The enquiry form is at the bottom of the page, and it asks for your full address before the job. A customer on a phone has to scroll past everything to find it.
  • Nothing is bringing new people in beyond word of mouth and Google finding the site. Your own About page says the business has grown through word of mouth.
  • Bathrooms, your biggest jobs, sit alongside dripping taps. There is no page built to win a bathroom refit on its own.
