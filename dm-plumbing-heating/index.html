The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • You are busy, and you built that yourself: your own website and SEO are already bringing 4 to 5 jobs a day. Most trades pay someone for that.
  • A proper website: services, pricing (£75 call-out covering the first hour), online booking, FAQs, a page for every area you cover.
  • Gas Safe registered and City & Guilds qualified, shown near the top.
  • Real reviews and real work: 5.0 from 10 Google reviews, plus bathroom before-and-afters and walk-in showers.
⚠ What is missing
  • The site brings in the wrong mix. It leads with "2-hour emergency response" and leaks, so that is what comes in. Installations, the work you want, sit in the same list as dripping taps.
  • No page built to win a boiler install or a bathroom refit on its own, the pages adverts would send people to.
  • No analytics and no Meta pixel found in the page code, so you cannot see which enquiries turn into installs, and no advert could learn who becomes a customer.
  • Nothing in place for hiring yet. When you take people on in the new year, there is no careers page or way for an engineer to apply.
