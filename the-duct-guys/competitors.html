🔴🔴 The pattern

Three of the four have Google Ads tags set up. Three of the four have a Meta pixel. The Duct Guys has neither.

⚠ A tag being present is not proof of what anyone spends. It does mean they are ready to measure advertising, and you are not yet.

⭐ Where you win
  • You cannot out-review Efficiency or Sky on volume (they claim 1,500+ and 5,300+ across bigger teams). You do not need to: your reviews name you and Tyler, and an owner who answers questions is something a 5,300-review company cannot offer
  • Licensed since 2007, against a duct cleaning competitor registered in February 2026
  • Based in Sandy, on the east side the big Portland firms serve from further away
  • Your no-scare-tactics duct cleaning stance and fraud article are a ready-made advert in a trade known for upselling
