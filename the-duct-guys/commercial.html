The honest answer first

One ad does not sell a full system or a duct replacement. These are considered purchases: people read, ask two or three companies to quote, and take days or weeks to decide. Ads put you on the shortlist and keep you there. What wins the job is how well the quote is followed up, and today that is you, by hand, alongside Housecall Pro.

Who buys the bigger work
  • Homeowners replacing a whole system: heat pumps, furnace and AC together, older ductwork that needs replacing
  • Landlords and property managers of rental and multifamily homes, which your own site says you take on in limited numbers
  • Other HVAC companies that do not build custom ductwork and pass it to you, which you put at roughly a tenth of your work

Only what you confirm goes on the site: no warranty, financing terms or accreditation is added that you do not hold.

Where ads fit
  • Google search, large jobs only: searches like heat pump installation or duct replacement in the towns you choose, with small jobs such as dryer vent cleaning excluded so they do not use the budget
  • Each ad lands on the matching install page, with the project form on it and photos of a similar job
  • Reminders for months, not days: people who read an install page see your finished jobs on Facebook and Google while they decide
  • A page for property managers and landlords, only if you want more of that work
  • A page for HVAC companies that sub out ductwork, so the referrals you already get have somewhere to land
  • A daily budget you can turn down when large projects are booked six to eight weeks out
The follow-up is where the money is
⭐⭐ What it would look like
  • Every large inquiry on one board: inquiry, site visit booked, quote sent, following up, won or lost, with the value of each quote on it, so you can see the total waiting on a decision
  • A follow-up schedule after every quote, written by us and approved by you before it goes live, so no quote goes quiet because the day got busy
  • Seasonal nudges before heating and cooling season to people who got a quote and did not go ahead
  • After each job: a photo of the finished work for the site and Facebook, and a review request
  • Where each job came from, recorded every time, so you know which source pays
  • ⚠ Housecall Pro already does some of this. We would add only what is missing, and confirm which on Monday
What we would measure

Large-job inquiries by source, site visits booked, quotes sent and their total value, quotes won, and the time from inquiry to yes. No forecasts: nothing on your site counts any of this today, so there is no starting number to forecast from.

Questions for Monday
1. Where did your last few large jobs come from?A referral, an HVAC company, AI, the website, or a repeat customer.
2. How many quotes are out now, and what happens after one is sent?That is the first thing the follow-up would cover.
3. Do you want more rental and multifamily work, or mainly homeowners?Your site says you take a limited number.
4. How do HVAC companies find you for ductwork today?Whether a page for them is worth building.
5. How far ahead can you book a large project right now?That sets how much to spend, and when to turn it down.
