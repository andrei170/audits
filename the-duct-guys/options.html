The money, in your own numbers

You told us your large jobs are $15,000 to $50,000, and you aim for about 37% on each.

Setup plus your first month with us$2,500
Your share of one $15,000 job at 37%about $5,550
Your share of one $50,000 job at 37%about $18,500

So one large job at the low end covers the setup and the first month, with about $3,050 left over towards your ad spend. After that, each month only has to cover $1,000 plus whatever you choose to spend on Google, and the tracking shows you whether it does before you decide to carry on.

Compare that with Angie’s: about $15,856 spent over a contract year to get about $15,720 back, on shared leads that sometimes had another company on site before you could call. Here the calls come only to you, and you can stop the first month it is not working.

This is your own maths, not a forecast. We are not promising a number of leads or jobs.
