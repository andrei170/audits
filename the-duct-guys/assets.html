Questions for the call
1. Oregon, Texas, or both?Which market do you want more large jobs in first? This pack covers theductguys.com, the Oregon site.
2. What is your Google Business Profile’s primary category?If it says duct cleaning, that is the quickest fix there is.
3. How many quotes are out right now?And what happens after one is sent: who chases it, and when?
4. What do you offer in writing?A workmanship warranty, financing. AAA, Efficiency and Sky each put one or both on their homepage.
5. What is the smallest job worth a site visit?That decides how hard the project form filters.
6. Can Kevin send us the Texas site address?So we can read it the same way, without disturbing what AI is already sending you.
The one sentence this comes down to

You do work the big Portland names do not advertise, and the listings Google reads still describe a duct cleaner. Fix what is said about you, count what comes in, then test ads on the large jobs only.
