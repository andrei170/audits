Read first: what The Duct Guys actually do, in plain English
What “HVAC” means

Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning. Everything that heats, cools and moves air around a house: the furnace or heat pump that makes the heat, the air conditioner, wall-mounted mini-splits, and the ducts, the metal pipes hidden in walls, ceilings and under floors that carry the air to each room.

Words you will hear: heat pump (heats and cools in one unit, very common in Oregon) · mini-split (a ductless unit on the wall, no ducts needed) · furnace (the heater) · ductwork / sheet metal (the pipes) · full system (replacing the whole lot) · CCB (Oregon’s contractor licence, Kevin holds it).
