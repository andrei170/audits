In the order we would do it

⭐⭐ This order is not arbitrary. Counting goes first, because every decision after it, including whether ads are worth it, depends on knowing what already comes in. Anything that needs a decision from you is marked.

1. First, and measured in days: count what already comes in

Make every inquiry a result you can see

In the Google Analytics you already have: a result recorded for every form sent, every tap on your number and every “Book Online” click. Where Housecall Pro allows it, the completed booking too.

⚠ Your decision: whether to add a tracked phone number, so calls from the website can be told apart from calls from your van and your regulars.

2. Next: ads tags installed, not switched on

Google Ads tag and Meta pixel, tested and ready

Installing a tag is not starting to advertise. It costs nothing to have ready, and it is the difference between measured spend and guesswork. It also starts building an audience of people who have already visited you.

3. Then: make the pages people land on able to take an inquiry

A short request form on every service and town page

Job type and zip code first, then name and phone. On the Gresham page, the Duct Cleaning page and every other page someone might arrive on from Google or an ad.

Tell Google what you are, on every page

HVAC business type, hours, service area and CCB license written into the page code, and your Reviews page pointed at your Google reviews.

4. Only then: advertising, on the jobs you choose

Google first, for people already searching

Search ads for the jobs you want more of, in the towns you want them from, each landing on the matching page you already have. ⚠ Your decision: which jobs and which towns. We will not pick for you.

Meta for the seasons, not all year

Short campaigns around moments that already drive calls in Oregon: furnace checks before winter, and smoke season for duct cleaning and filtration. Your honest duct cleaning stance is the message.

5. Alongside: follow up every inquiry

No inquiry left waiting

A text back to every missed call, a reminder to anyone who started a request and did not finish, and a review request after each job. ⚠ To confirm on Monday: what Housecall Pro already does for you here, so we add only what is missing. For large jobs, the quote follow-up is set out on Bigger jobs.

What we would not touch

  • Your pages and town pages: more depth than any competitor needs to beat
  • Your blog: 32 articles, kept current
  • Your review widget and tap-to-call: both already work
  • Your website itself: it does not need rebuilding
⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. Nothing on your site counts inquiries today, so there is no starting number to forecast from. Anyone giving you an inquiry forecast before that would be inventing it.
