What we found
⭐⭐ First, what is already strong
  • 63 pages in your sitemap: 30 site pages and 33 blog entries, last updated 29 September 2026
  • A page for 8 towns: Portland, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Hillsboro, Gresham, Troutdale, Sandy and Beaverton, plus 27 communities listed on the homepage
  • A separate page for every service, from heat pump installs to duct replacement and air duct cleaning
  • 67 Google reviews shown on the homepage, several naming Tyler and one naming you as the owner
  • Your number is tappable: 6 tap-to-call links on the homepage, and online booking through Housecall Pro
  • Licensed and checkable: Oregon CCB #179150, active since November 2007, and a BBB profile
  • An honest duct cleaning page: “without scare tactics or unrealistic health claims”, plus an article on duct cleaning fraud in Oregon. That is a real reason to choose you
🔴🔴 1. Nothing is set up to measure advertising

There is no Google Ads tag and no Meta (Facebook) pixel in your page code. We also read the Google tag your site loads, G-J9GF4DQ64G, and it carries no Ads link either.

🔴 So if ads started tomorrow, nobody could say which ad, keyword or town produced a call or a booking. Three of the four competitors we read already have Google Ads tags in place.

🔴 2. Your best inquiries leave the site before they can be counted

Every “Book Online” button goes to book.housecallpro.com, a different website. Google Analytics on your site sees the click, not the booking.

⚠ Whether your Analytics records form sends and taps on your number as results could not be seen from outside. Worth opening together on the call.

⚠ 3. The pages people land on have no form

The homepage and Contact page each have a request form. The Portland, Gresham, Duct Cleaning, Reviews and About pages we read do not. Someone who lands on your Gresham page from Google can call or leave for Housecall Pro, and that is it.

⚠ The form asks for your phone number before the job and the town. Asking the easy question first gets more forms finished.

⚠ 4. Smaller things Google and homeowners notice
  • No business type in the page code on the homepage, service or town pages. The code has your address and map coordinates but never says “HVAC business”, your hours or your license
  • No photos of finished work: no gallery, no before and after
  • Your Reviews page links to Yelp and Facebook, but not to Google, where your 67 reviews are
  • No careers page. Your CCB record shows you employ staff; if you are hiring, there is nowhere for a tech to apply

⚠ Not assessed: your Google Business Profile, where you rank, site speed, your Housecall Pro account and call volumes. We have not guessed at any of it. What you told us about past ads is in Audit Pt.2.
