The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A real craftsman’s website: your story, your standard, real jobs and six blog posts written in your own voice.
  • Your standard is written down: "Quality means everything", "High Finish", and "we can not hire the best without paying what they are worth".
  • Guild of Master Craftsmen named and explained, four customer testimonials, own-domain email.
  • A tap-to-call link on all 12 pages, and a Facebook page that is still active.
⚠ What holds it back for hiring
  • Nothing on the site is aimed at a tradesperson. No careers page, no vacancy line, no way to apply, on any of the 12 pages.
  • The only form is a customer contact form, with no option for someone wanting work and no way to send photos.
  • No tracking of any kind, so an advert could never learn who visited or applied.
  • Loose ends that make it look older than it is: template headings on Projects, a 2022 footer, a blog that stops in July 2023, and an About-page email link to an address that does not exist.
  • No Google listing we could find, and no Guild listing in the Guild’s public search.
