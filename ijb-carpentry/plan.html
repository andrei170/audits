The plan, in order

Open the door, filter hard, then test on a real job. The demo careers page shows steps 3 and 4 working, including what you would receive for each applicant.

1Decide the offer (on Monday)

Employed or self-employed, the rate or salary, van or travel, tools, hours and the kind of jobs. We do not guess any of these; you set them, and the page says them plainly. Your own line, "we can not hire the best without paying what they are worth", is the right starting point.

2Tidy the shop window first

Fix the About-page email link, remove the template headings from Projects, update the footer, and set up or claim a Google Business Profile. Small jobs, but a good carpenter checks you out the way a customer does.

3One page that opens the door

A "Work with us" page on your own site, in your own words: the work, the finish, you, and exactly who you are looking for. The demo shows it. Left open all year, it does the looking while you work.

4An application that does the first sift

Five minutes on a phone. A driving licence, the right to work, can they reach East Kent jobs, what finish work they have done, two questions that show attitude, then photos of their own finish work. Everyone gets an instant, honest reply. You only see the ones worth a call.

5Fix the tracking

Add the Facebook pixel and Google Analytics to Wix, so an advert learns which people actually apply, not just who clicks.

6A small advert left running, not a burst

Facebook and Instagram adverts shown to people within reach of Canterbury who follow carpentry, joinery and woodworking. Your own photos and a short clip of you on a job. Run small and continuously, so the right person sees it when they are ready to move. Aimed by area and interest, never by age or gender.

7A screen that protects your evenings

We read the answers and photos first. You get a short list, each with their photos on one page, then one 15-minute call per person, booked into one slot in your week.

8A paid trial day on a real job

One day alongside you: hang a door, scribe a skirting, set an architrave, and see how they leave the room. A day shows you their finish and their attitude before you rely on them.

9Bring them in steadily

A few jobs working with you, then pieces they finish alone that you check. Then the jobs you are turning down today become jobs you can say yes to.

10See it working

Each week: applications, how many passed, photos reviewed, calls held, trial days booked. You can see it working without chasing anyone.

⚠ One thing to think about together

Your homepage says: "You’d have to go a long way to find a team more experienced, reliable or skilled than IJB." Customers choose you for you. When someone new joins, it is worth deciding how you say it: for example, that every job is still priced, set out and signed off by Ieuan. The careers page in the demo is written that way. It is also a decision for both of you, because a new person changes the shape of the week at home as well as on site.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise a hire, a number of applicants or a date. Good finish carpenters are scarce, which is the whole problem. What this plan does is put your work in front of good carpenters who are not looking, turn away the wrong ones before they cost you an evening, and test the right one on a real job before you rely on them. On employed versus self-employed status, your accountant is the right person to ask.
