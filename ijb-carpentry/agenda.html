  1. Your situation first (10 minutes). The people you want, what happened with your adverts, the jobs you turn down, and what growing would look like for you both.
  2. What we found (5 minutes). The audit, the grade and who is hiring, the short version.
  3. The demo (10 minutes). The work-with-us page, the application tried as a good carpenter and as someone without a licence, and the page you would see for each applicant.
  4. Next steps (5 minutes). Only if it makes sense to you both. If now is not the right time, that is fine too.
Questions we would like to ask you
  • Who works with you now, and what does a normal week look like?
  • Employed or self-employed: which would you offer, and roughly what would the right person earn?
  • Would they need their own van and tools, or would you supply them?
  • What did your Facebook and LinkedIn adverts show, and where did they send people? What was the one bite like?
  • What does "the finish I need" look like in practice? What would you check on someone’s first day?
  • How many jobs have you turned down this year, and what kind?
  • When you say you could push a lot more if you had a start, what would that look like a year from now?
  • Who looks after your website, Facebook page and Google listing today?
