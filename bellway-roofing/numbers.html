The sum, in plain numbers

At about £55 a lead, 5 to 7 leads a week is roughly £1,200 to £1,650 a month in ad spend, paid to Google. If you book 1 in 3, that is 7 to 10 jobs a month. If a lead costs £100 rather than £55, the same leads cost nearer £2,200 to £3,000 a month. That is why the first two weeks are about getting the cost per lead down, not spending more.

Then the one sum that matters

Cost per job = (ad spend + running cost) ÷ jobs won. Compare that with what one job is worth to you after materials and labour. If one re-roof pays for a month of adverts, the question is only how many of those you want.
