The idea

Stop paying an agency to choose your installers. Reach them directly, and let a short application do the first check. Certified fire door installers are a small group, so the message has to reach the right people and the application has to screen them out before you spend time on a call.

Step by step
  1. A "work with us" page on your website. What the work is (hotels, councils, HMOs), where it is, what you pay or the range, and what makes Essex Fire Doors good to work for. Photos of real teams on real jobs.
  2. A short application form on that page that asks the qualification question first: which certification do you hold, and can you send a copy? Anyone without one can still apply as a trainee, but is marked separately, so carpenters never reach your shortlist by accident.
  3. Adverts aimed at installers, not customers. Facebook and Instagram adverts shown to carpenters, joiners and fire door installers in Essex and London, plus job boards. Separate adverts for whole teams and subcontractors.
  4. Every applicant lands in one place. The CRM idea from July, used for hiring: a board showing new applicants, checked, interviewed and started.
  5. An instant text and email to every applicant, asking for their certificate and offering a time to speak to you.
  6. A short call with you, then a trial day. You only spend time on people who have already shown the paperwork.
  7. Then the work side. Once teams are in place, the repeat inspection list from July becomes the steady work that keeps those teams busy between big jobs.
How you can check we are doing what we say

You have dealt with agencies that promised one thing and delivered another, so you should not have to take our word for anything. This is how we would set it up so you can see for yourself:

  • Everything sits in your own accounts. The advert account, the page the adverts run from and the applicant board are set up in your name, with your card on the advert spend. You can log in and see every penny and every applicant at any time, and you keep all of it if we ever stop working together.
  • Start small and judge it on applicants. A short first run with a capped advert budget that you set, then a plain report: how many people applied, what each one cost to find, and how many had the right certificate. If the numbers are not there, you stop.
  • Nothing is hidden behind us. Applicants come to you, not to us. We never stand between you and an installer the way an agency does.
  • You have already seen how we work. In July we built your audit and the CRM mock-up before you had paid anything, and the July pages are still live for you to look back at.
What we need from you first
  • The qualification you require.
  • What you pay, or a range.
  • Photos of your teams at work, if you have them.
  • Where the hotel and council jobs are.
What we are not saying

We cannot tell you how many certified installers will apply, because there are not many of them and we have not run this for you yet. What we can do is show you every applicant, what each one cost to find, and how many had the right paperwork, so you can decide quickly whether it is working.
