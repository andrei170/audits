Who we are looking for
  • Certified fire door installers, holding the scheme or qualification you require (to confirm on the call).
  • Whole teams and subcontractors who could take on a hotel or council job.
  • Good carpenters and joiners willing to get certified, kept on a separate trainee list so they never reach your shortlist by mistake.
Adverts, written for installers
Draft 1: certified installers

"Certified fire door installer in Essex or London? We are Essex Fire Doors, fully booked into the new year with hotel and council work, and we are building new teams. Steady work, proper jobs, paid on time. Apply in two minutes, and have your certificate ready."

Draft 2: teams and subcontractors

"Running a fire door team? We have hotel and council work in London and Essex that needs certified installers. Tell us about your team and your certification, and we will call you back."

Draft 3: carpenters who want to certify

"Carpenter or joiner thinking about fire doors? It is skilled, regulated work that is not going away. Register your interest, and tell us if you are working towards certification."

Wording to agree with you before anything runs. Pay, location and the qualification are placeholders until you confirm them.

The application asks, in this order
  1. Which fire door certification do you hold? (a list, plus "none yet")
  2. Upload a photo of your certificate or card.
  3. Are you applying on your own, or as a team? How many people?
  4. Employed, self-employed or limited company?
  5. Where are you based, and how far will you travel?
  6. Your recent fire door work: type of building and number of doors.
  7. When could you start?

Anyone who answers "none yet" goes to the trainee list automatically.

How you can see it working
  • Everything runs in your own accounts, on your card, and you can log in any time.
  • A weekly report: how many applied, the cost of each one, and how many sent a valid certificate.
  • You speak only to people who have already shown their paperwork.
