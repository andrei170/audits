Handling the trust question
  1. Agree with him first. "You are right, we do not post much. We put the time into client work." Do not argue that social media does not matter, and do not claim followers or posts that are not there.
  2. Point at what he can check, not what we say. In July he got the audit and CRM mock-up before paying. Now: his own ad account, his own card on the spend, his own applicant board. He sees every applicant and every penny live.
  3. Make the first step small and measurable. A short capped test, judged on applicants with the right certificate and cost per applicant. He decides whether to continue on the numbers.
  4. Contrast with the agencies, honestly. An agency stands between him and the installer and chooses for him. This puts the installer in front of him with the paperwork already asked for.
  5. Only offer a reference call if you have a client lined up who has agreed to it. Do not promise one on the call and then look for one. Do not quote the $200k / $101k results to him: they are not trades businesses and he will check.
  6. Do not over-promise on hires. Certified installers are scarce. Overselling now repeats exactly what the agencies did to him.
  7. For later (not for this call): the thin AG1 social presence is costing trust with established trades buyers. A few posts showing real client work (with permission) would answer this before the call instead of on it.
