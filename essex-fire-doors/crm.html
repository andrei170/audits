Board 2: enquiries (as in July)

Domestic and smaller jobs keep the July set-up: every enquiry in one place, an instant reply, a text back on missed calls, and follow-ups on day 2 and day 5. The difference is that each quote now shows the first date a team is free, so nobody is promised a start you cannot meet.

Board 3: hiring

Every installer who applies lands here and moves from Applied to Certificate received, Call with James, Trial day and Started. See the sample hiring board.

Repeat inspections

Every door you fit goes on a list with its next check date. Under the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022, communal fire doors in buildings over 11 metres need checking every quarter and flat entrance doors every year. The system sends the building manager a reminder and books the visit, which gives new teams steady work between the large jobs.
