What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ The aim is not volume. It is a small number of good jobs a week that you own, and a setup that answers, filters and books them so you are not on the phone after a long day on site.

1. Decide the work you want · Today

The two or three job types worth your time, the towns you will drive to, and how many a week. Everything below is built around that answer.

⭐ Fewer, better jobs starts with saying no to the rest in writing.

2. Google Business Profile and reviews · Week 1

A verified profile under your licensed name, with a service area instead of a home address, your services and real photos. Then a review request by text after every job, Angi customers included.

This is what decides the map results and what AI answers quote.

3. A site that filters and books · Weeks 1 to 2

A page per service you want, your area named, your licence shown, and a booking form asking job type, urgency, zip and photos.

⚠ If GoDaddy stays as the host, this becomes a checklist. The sample site shows every item.

4. Answer every enquiry without picking up · Weeks 1 to 2

Missed-call text back within seconds, an instant reply to every form, online booking into the slots you choose, and follow-up on every quote until it is a yes or a no.

⭐ This is the part that saves time when you are on the tools all day.

5. Measurement · Week 2

Call tracking, a Google tag and conversion tracking, and a source on every job in the CRM.

So we can see what each lead source really costs per job, Angi included.

6. Rebuild the Google Ads, small and tight · Before November

Only your chosen job types and towns, negative keywords, a schedule that matches when you can answer, and a budget sized to your capacity.

⚠ We look inside your current account first. If it is working, we keep what works.

7. Turn Angi down as your own leads arrive · From November

Based on cost per booked job from each source.

Not before the numbers say so.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your ticket sizes or what your ads and Angi cost. Anyone giving you a job forecast before seeing those would be inventing it.
