Questions for the call
1. When do you want to be fully independent, and what has to be true before you leave the main job?This sets the timeline for everything else, including when the site and ads need to be live.
2. What does a good job look like for you?Which work do you want more of (EV chargers, troubleshooting, commercial, something else), and which do you want less of?
3. What is a typical ticket for the jobs you like, and for the ones you would rather skip?So we aim at the high-value work and filter out the trips that are not worth it.
4. Roughly how many of your side jobs come through Angi today, and what does each lead cost you?And how often you actually win a lead you paid for.
5. How good are the Angi leads?Do you compete with other pros on the same request? Do they pick on price?
6. What do your Google Ads cost a month, and how many jobs can you trace back to them?And who set them up: you, GoDaddy, or someone else? We could not find any ads pointing to your site.
7. When you are on a job all day and the phone rings, what happens to that call?Voicemail, call back at night, or does someone else pick up?
8. How do customers find you when it is not Angi?Referrals, contractors you work for, repeat customers, or Google?
9. Licence and insurance: is everything you want to show current, and are you happy for your licence number to be on the site?Your L&I licence is active until May 2027. We would like to show it, plus your insurance if you want.
10. Is the 24/7 emergency service on your site something you actually want to offer right now?With a full day job, a promise you cannot keep costs more than leaving it off.
