The licence is real and active, and a search for your name finds you. Almost everything a stranger needs in order to pick you, book you and trust you is missing from the site.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (24 Oct 2026)
FEnquiry form
One short form on the home page (Name, Email, attach files) and one on the contact page (Name, Email or Phone). Neither asks what the job is, how urgent it is, or the zip code, so every enquiry needs a call back before you know if it is worth driving to.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ (425) 599-0502 is a tap-to-call link on all 3 pages.
FPages and service depth
3 pages in total (Home, Services, Contact, per the site's own sitemap). All six services share one page, a short paragraph each. No page for EV chargers, troubleshooting or commercial work on its own, and no page for any town.
FPage titles and descriptions
Titles are “Practical Electric LLC”, “Contact” and “Services | Practical Electric LLC”. No page title names a service or a city, and no page has a meta description. These are the lines Google shows in results.
FHeadings
The home page has two H1 headings, both “Practical Electrical Solutions Near You”. No city, no service. “Near you” tells Google nothing about where you work.
FLocal business markup
No structured data at all (no Electrician or LocalBusiness schema), so search engines and AI tools have to guess your services, area and hours.
FMeasurement
No Google tag, Google Ads conversion tag or Meta pixel found in the page code of any page. If ads send people to the site, nothing on the site reports back which clicks turned into calls or forms.
DLicence shown
The site says “fully licensed” but shows no licence number. The licence is real and active (L&I #PRACTEL755JJ), so this is free credibility being left off the page.
FReviews
No reviews on the site. Your Angi listing appears to show one review (from a search snapshot; Angi blocked our reader). No Google review count could be found.
FGoogle Business Profile
No business profile panel appeared on a Google search for “Practical Electric LLC” Seattle. ⚠ Google Maps itself could not be read by our tools, so confirm this on the call.
FShowing up in local searches
Not found in any of 5 local searches run from Seattle: electrician Seattle, electrician north Seattle, electrician Shoreline WA, panel upgrade Seattle, EV charger installation Seattle.
A-Brand search and AI answer
⭐ A search for your business name puts your site first, Angi second, and Google's AI Overview correctly describes you as a licensed electrical contractor in Seattle, owned by you. It got that from the state register and Angi, not from your site.
FWho you are and proof of work
One real photo of you on a job (good). Your name, your story, project photos and the jobs you specialise in are not on the site.
A-Professional email
⭐ info@practicalelectricllc.com on the contact page, on your own domain.
FClaims that match each other
The site promises “24/7 Emergency Services” and “visit during normal business hours”, while the contact page lists 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday and the Angi listing (search snapshot) says no emergency service. A homeowner who reads all three gets three answers.
FFreshness
Every page in the sitemap was last changed on 24 May 2025, two weeks after the licence was issued, and the copyright line still reads 2025.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about the website and the listings, not about your work. The thing that takes years and money to get, an active electrical contractor licence, is already done.

After the build: the form, pages, titles, headings, markup, measurement, licence and claims rows are all fixed by the build itself. Reviews, your Google profile and search positions take weeks of steady effort, so we do not project those.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, Google review count, Angi review details, and anything inside your Google Ads account.
