A homeowner with no power at 9pm calls, gets voicemail, and calls the next electrician. Pick one promise you can keep, and say it the same way everywhere.

Why you do not show up for the searches that matter
  • No page per service. Google matches “EV charger installation Seattle” to a page about exactly that. Every firm that ranked has one.
  • No town or area named. “Near you” and “King County” do not match a search for Shoreline or north Seattle.
  • No Google Business Profile found and almost no reviews. The map results above the normal results are decided by the profile and its reviews.
  • No markup. AI answers and Google both read structured data for services, area and hours.
