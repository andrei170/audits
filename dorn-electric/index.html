Michael, we read every page your sitemap lists (60 of them) on November 10, 2026, plus your Google listing. Your website grades D, 48 out of 100 (see the scorecard). In one line: the site is built to rank and to take enquiries, but nothing on it counts what happens, and it does not yet show a homeowner who you are or prove you are licensed.

1. What is already working
⭐ Better than most trade sites we read
  • A quote form on 58 of 60 pages and tap-to-call on all 60.
  • One headline and a meta description on every page. Most sites miss this.
  • Service pages for panel upgrades, electrical repair and wiring, EV charging, lighting and ceiling fans, plus town pages and 17 blog posts.
  • 4.8 stars from 148 Google reviews. Reviewers name you, describe answering the phone yourself, fair prices and clean work.
2. Counting: nothing is recording visits, calls or forms
🔴 No Analytics, no ad tag, a broken Tag Manager reference
  • Google Analytics 4: not found on any of the 60 pages.
  • Google Ads conversion tag (AW-): not found. Meta pixel: not found. Call tracking (CallRail or similar): not found.
  • The one tracking reference is a Tag Manager iframe with ID GTM-17918830900. A real Tag Manager ID is GTM plus 7 or so letters and numbers; an 11-digit number is the shape of a Google Ads account ID. We found no Tag Manager loader script. It looks like someone meant to install ads tracking and put the number in the wrong place.
  • What it means: you told Drew most of your work comes from Google. Today nobody can tell which part (your listing, the website, an ad) sends the jobs, or what a job costs from each.
3. Trust: licence, years, awards, reviews, email
⚠ The things a homeowner checks before calling
  • Licence: no CSLB number on any page. The site says "licensed electricians" on many pages but never gives the number to check.
  • Years: pages say "25+ years" and "over 25 years" consistently. Good. (Our first read on November 9 took the home page's "15+" for years; it is actually "15+ Win Awards".)
  • Awards and counters: the home page shows "2250+ Happy Customers", "2635+ Finished Projects" and "15+ Win Awards". No award is named anywhere. The Locations page counters display "0 +" in the page code until the animation runs.
  • Reviews widget: says "39 Google reviews"; Google shows 148. The second review it shows, on the home, About and Reviews pages, is a long negative review naming you. We have not reproduced it anywhere in this pack.
  • About page: 1,227 words and it never names you or anyone on the team.
  • Email: a Gmail address on every page.
4. Enquiries: the form asks the wrong questions first
⚠ Five fields, none about the job
  • Fields on every form: first name, last name, email, phone, comment.
  • Missing: what job (panel, EV charger, repair, lighting) and which zip code. Those two answers decide whether you call back today or not.
  • No form on: /faqs/, /services/.
  • We did not submit the form, so we cannot say where it goes or how fast it is answered.
5. Towns: the pages point at Orange County more than at Corona
⚠ Where your town pages are
  • Town pages exist for: Corona, Murrieta, Temecula, Irvine, Anaheim, Lake Forest, Newport Beach, San Clemente. Five of those eight are Orange County towns.
  • No page for the towns closest to you: Norco, Eastvale, Riverside, Jurupa Valley, Chino Hills, Lake Elsinore, Menifee.
  • 3 of the 8 town pages are copies of the home page with the town name swapped (/newport-beachca/, /san-clementeca/, /temeculaca/): same 1,450 words, headline "Your Trusted Electrical Experts in temecula,Ca". Google tends to treat copies like this as one page.
  • Irvine has two wiring pages and two EV charger pages, which compete with each other.
  • Your Google listing's categories: Electrician, Building inspector, Electric vehicle charging station contractor. "Building inspector" is worth a look; it can show you for the wrong searches.
