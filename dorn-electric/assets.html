Michael, thanks for making time. Here is what you told Drew, as we understood it:

  • You do 6 to 10 jobs a week, at around $1,000 a job, and you have room for another 5 to 10 a week.
  • Most of your work comes through Google. You have tried Angi and Yelp and they did not work well.
  • The business took a big hit some years back and is not yet where you want it.
  • The goal: grow, bring in another electrician, get to the point where it runs without you on every job, and maybe sell it one day.

So this pack asks one question: how do you add 5 to 10 jobs a week from Google, in a way that keeps working when you are not on the tools? Nothing here is priced.

⭐ What is already working
  • Google already sends you work, which means homeowners near you are finding you.
  • 39 Google reviews, and most of them praise you by name.
  • A website with a quote form on every page and pages for the towns you cover.
⚠ What is holding you back
  • The business is built around you. The reviews say "Mike", the email is your Gmail, and you are on the jobs. A business that needs its owner is hard to grow and hard to sell.
  • We could not confirm that your website is counting calls and forms, so you cannot see which Google searches turn into jobs.
  • No licence number on the site, and a negative review showing on your own reviews page.

⭐ Website grade today: D, 59 out of 100. See every check.

The short version

5 to 10 more jobs a week at about $1,000 is roughly $5,000 to $10,000 more a week, before costs. Google is already where your work comes from, so the plan is to do more of what works, count it properly, and set it up so the calls get answered and booked whether or not you are on a ladder. That is also what makes room for a second electrician, and what makes the business worth something to a buyer.
