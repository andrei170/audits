Where you are, as you told us
  • About 6 to 10 jobs a week at around 1,000 dollars a job, with room for another 5 to 10 a week (as you told us).
  • Most work comes through Google. Angi and Yelp did not work well (as you told us).
  • The goal: grow, add an electrician, run the business without being on every job, maybe sell one day (as you told us).
Part 1: more jobs from Google
  1. Count everything first. Google Analytics, a proper Tag Manager container, a Google Ads conversion tag and tracked call numbers, so every call and form is counted by source.
  2. Google Local Services Ads and Google Ads for the jobs you want most (for example panel upgrades and EV chargers) in the towns closest to Corona. The lead comes to you only, not to five electricians at once.
  3. Your Google listing: recent job photos, services listed, a look at the "Building inspector" category, and a review request after every job.
  4. Website fixes (shown in the demo): licence number on every page, a form that asks job type and zip first, real pages for Norco, Eastvale and Riverside, Electrician markup on every page, the widget showing your real review count, a company email, and an About page that introduces you.
Part 2: a business that runs without you on every job
  1. Every call gets answered or texted back, even when you are on a job, and booked into a calendar.
  2. Every lead in one place, so a second electrician or an office person can see the job, the address and what was said.
  3. A careers page so the next electrician can find you.
  4. Follow-up and review requests happen automatically.
  5. A record of where every job came from and what it cost. That record is what a buyer looks at.
What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of jobs, a ranking or a sale price. What we can do is set it up so you see the cost of every booked job by source, every week, and decide with numbers whether it is working.
