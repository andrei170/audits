What we found before the call
🔴🔴 There is no website, and we checked properly before saying so

We looked for a site on six likely domains. We also found electratek.co.uk and checked whether it was yours. It is not — it belongs to a different Electratek doing smart home and AV installation, registered in Oldham, Lancashire, with no mention of Lincoln, of you, or of solar.

⚠ We mention that because we nearly audited the wrong company, and you should know that the name is not unique. Somebody searching for you today can very easily find them instead.

⭐⭐ The arithmetic is the whole conversation here

You told our team: one client a week, at £12,000 to £16,000 a job, entirely from word of mouth, with no marketing ever attempted.

⭐ So one additional job a week would be between £624,000 and £832,000 a year. ⚠ That is your two figures multiplied, nothing more — it is not a forecast and it says nothing about whether we could deliver it. We show it because it sets the scale: at your job value, very small changes in enquiry volume are worth a great deal, which is unusual and it is in your favour.

🔴 Nineteen years of reputation, with nowhere for it to live

Companies House shows Electratek Lincoln Ltd, company 06296108, incorporated 28 June 2007 and active since. That is nineteen years.

⭐⭐ Nineteen years of satisfied customers is the single most valuable asset you own, and none of it is visible to anybody who has not already met you. Every review, every finished job, every referral that happened and was never written down — a competitor three years old with a decent website currently looks more established than you do.

⚠ And solar makes this urgent rather than optional

You are moving into solar. 🔴 Solar is not a word-of-mouth purchase in the way electrical work is. Somebody who needs a fuse board replaced asks a neighbour. Somebody considering £12,000 of solar researches for weeks, compares installers, and checks accreditations before they ever make contact.

⚠ For solar specifically, MCS certification is normally required for the export tariff and most grant schemes. We do not know whether you hold it, and it is one of the first things we would ask.

What is genuinely strong, and we checked it

Verified on Companies House, not taken from a website, because there isn't one:

  • ELECTRATEK LINCOLN LTD, company 06296108, active, incorporated 28 June 2007.
  • Registered at 22 Hawthorn Road, Cherry Willingham, Lincoln LN3 4JX.

⭐⭐ Nineteen years, a £12,000 average job, and you have never advertised once. That is a rare position to be in. Everything that normally has to be built — the trade skill, the reputation, the referral base — you already have. What is missing is the cheapest part.

What we would want to ask
  • Do you hold MCS certification for solar? ⭐ It normally gates the export tariff and most grant schemes, so it changes what can honestly be advertised.
  • How many more jobs a week could you actually service? You are at one. Two is double. Selling you demand you cannot deliver would make things worse, not better.
  • Is it you on the tools, or do you have a team? Companies House lists you and Claire as officers, and that is all we know.
  • What does a solar job pay against an electrical one? You gave us £12,000 to £16,000 for domestic. We do not know which that refers to.
  • Do you have reviews anywhere at all? Google, Checkatrade, Facebook. Nineteen years of goodwill may already be written down somewhere we have not found.
  • How far around Lincoln do you work? It decides whether this is a Lincoln play or a Lincolnshire one.
