Who A Good Roofer Compares You With
🔴🔴 How a working roofer decides where to go next

A good roofer who is already in work does not answer job adverts. He hears a name, then he looks the firm up.

So the comparison that matters for hiring is the same one a customer makes: what each firm's website says when somebody looks.

⭐⭐ We read six other Lancaster and Morecambe roofers' own websites on 9 October. Not one of them has a careers page, a vacancy, or a line saying they are hiring.

⭐ Where the ground is open
  • Nobody local is visibly hiring. A careers page would make you the only roofer in the area a working roofer can find recruiting
  • Nobody shows a real team. Every site says "time-served" and names nobody. A page with you on it, and the work you do, stands out on its own
  • Hardly anyone mentions training. Only you (your NVQ and Bolton College apprenticeship) and Jamie Nicholson (his apprenticeship) talk about how they learned the trade. That is exactly what a qualified roofer wants to see

⚠ Where you are behind: trust marks and reviews. T & J show Checkatrade, Best Choice show 186 Google reviews, and you show neither. That matters for customers more than for hiring.
