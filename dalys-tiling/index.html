What we found before the call
🔴🔴 Your website does not mention a single one of those things

We read the whole site. It is 446 words. Here is what it does and does not contain:

  • “Smethwick” appears eight times.
  • “Swimming pool”: zero. “Care home”: zero. “Commercial”: zero.
  • Nothing about travelling for work, and nothing naming any area beyond Smethwick.

⭐⭐ So the work you want more of is the work your website never mentions. A facilities manager at a care home group, or a contractor pricing a pool refurbishment, reads that page and sees a local domestic tiler in one town. They do not see you.

⚠ This is the whole audit in one finding. Everything else below is smaller.

🔴 Nothing is measuring anything

No Google Analytics. No Tag Manager. No advertising tag. No pixel. Nothing.

⭐ So you have no way of knowing whether anybody is reading the site, what they searched for to get there, or which enquiries came from it. It is the cheapest item on this page and everything else depends on it.

🔴 No local business markup, and no second page to rank

There is no address, no coordinates and no trade category in the code where search engines read them, and no sitemap we could find. For a commercial buyer searching something like “swimming pool tiling contractor” there is no page on your site that could answer them.

⚠ No reviews, no gallery, and no past-work section anywhere

On a trade where somebody is choosing who to let into a care home or a leisure centre, photographs of finished commercial work are the single most persuasive thing you could publish, and there are none.

⚠ Two small things worth knowing

Your site is dalystiling.co.uk but the email it publishes is richard@dalystiling.com, a different domain. Worth checking that address actually receives, because a commercial enquiry going nowhere is worse than no enquiry.

And the published number is a mobile. Fine for domestic. ⚠ For a care-home group or a main contractor it reads smaller than the work you are describing.

What is genuinely strong, and we checked it

We verified the company on Companies House rather than taking the site's word for it:

  • DALYS TILING LTD, company 11288490, active, incorporated 3 April 2018.
  • Registered at 4 Crystal Drive, Smethwick B66 1QG.
  • Richard Paul Daly, sole director. No other officers, none resigned.

⭐⭐ And the thing that makes this an easy conversation: the hard part is already done. You already win commercial pool and care-home work, you already travel for it, and you already have the references. None of that needs building. It needs saying.

What we would want to ask
  • What does a commercial job pay you, against a domestic one? Even a range. You travel the country for the commercial work, so we assume the gap is large, but we are not going to put a number on this page that you did not give us.
  • How many pools or care homes have you done, and can we name any of them? ⭐ On commercial work, naming a site you have completed is worth more than every other thing on your website combined.
  • How far will you actually travel, and is there a job size that makes it worth it? That single answer decides whether this is a national play or a Midlands one.
  • Who do the enquiries come from now? Main contractors, architects, facilities managers, or direct from the end client? They are found in completely different places.
  • Do you have photographs of finished commercial work? And are you allowed to publish them? Some sites restrict it, which is worth knowing before we plan around it.
  • Is richard@dalystiling.com actually monitored? It is on a different domain from the website and we would rather ask than assume.
