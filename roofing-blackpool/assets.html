Today's call plan (for Andrei)
Discovery questions
  1. What did you spend on Google and on Meta, roughly per month, and for how long?
  2. Who ran them: you, your website guy, or an agency? Do you still have access to both ad accounts?
  3. When someone filled in a Facebook form, what happened next? How fast did they get a call, and how many picked up?
  4. How did you know which jobs came from ads? Was anything written down per lead (booked, quoted, won)?
  5. Out of 10 people you quote, how many say yes? Is it different for repairs and full roofs?
  6. What is a typical job worth to you, low end and high end? Which jobs do you want more of?
  7. What is a good lead for you? Which towns, which jobs, and which ones you would rather not drive to?
  8. Where should the centre of the patch be: Chorley, or Blackpool and the Fylde? How close is “closer”?
  9. What does your website guy look after, and would he be happy working alongside us?
  10. If you had 20 to 30 leads next month, could you and the team handle them? Who answers the phone when you are on a roof?
