The problem, stated plainly

You told our team you ran Google ads and Meta ads, that they did not work, and that you have put several figures into marketing that is not really working. You also said you are sceptical about the cost, which is a reasonable place to be standing.

🔴🔴 Here is the part that should annoy you: on the setup your site had while that money was being spent, nobody could have told you whether those ads worked or not. There is analytics on the site, but no conversion event on your enquiry form and no tap-to-call link anywhere on fifteen pages - so a click that became a phone call left no trace at all.

⭐ That is not the same as the ads having failed. They may have failed. They may have worked and nobody attributed it. Both look identical from where you are sitting, and that is the actual problem to solve before any more money goes anywhere.

What we are deliberately not selling you

⭐ You said you have a website guy, so we are not pitching you a website. Your site is fifteen pages with TrustMark shown and analytics already installed - whoever built it did a reasonable job. The gap is not the site, it is that nothing on it records an outcome. ⚠ Two things worth passing to him either way: there is not one tap-to-call link on the whole site, and nobody is named anywhere on it - we searched for Sam, Walter and Lee and got no hits.
