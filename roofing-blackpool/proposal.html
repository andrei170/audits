Fix the leaks, then spend, then prove it every week.

Step 1: stop the leaks (first two weeks, before any new ad spend)
  • Fix or redirect the two Google ad landing pages that return “page not found”.
  • Meta pixel and lead events on both sites, plus a way to tell Facebook which leads booked and which paid.
  • One phone number per channel (Google, Meta, website), so every call is counted against the thing that caused it.
  • Every lead into one simple pipeline: new, contacted, inspection booked, quoted, won, lost.
  • An instant text back to every new enquiry and every missed call, so nobody waits while you are on a roof.
  • roofingblackpool.co.uk: tap-to-call, one brand, real reviews, no other firm’s name. The demo shows exactly what we mean.

Most of the website items are a clear list your website guy can do. We would rather work with him than around him.

Step 2: spend in a tight patch
  • Agree the patch with you: the towns you want, drawn on a map, not “Lancashire”.
  • Google search ads on people already looking for a roofer, each pointing at a page that matches the search.
  • Two Meta campaigns (repairs and new roofs) with your video and your before-and-afters, left to run long enough to learn.
  • Town pages and service pages so Google and AI assistants can match “roofer in Poulton” to you.
Step 3: prove it, every week

A weekly one-page report: spend, leads, cost per lead, inspections booked, jobs won, cost per job, split by Google, Meta and website. If something is not paying, you see it within a week and we change it or stop it.

That is the thing that was missing last time. Not effort, and not your roofing: a way to see which pound turned into which job.

We do not promise a number of leads or jobs. What we will commit to is that every pound is counted, and that you will know within weeks, not months, whether it is working.
