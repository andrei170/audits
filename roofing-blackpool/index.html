⛔ We are going to start with the thing that is least comfortable for us, not for you. You have spent real money on advertising that did not work, and you are right to be sceptical of somebody else turning up asking for more.
🔴 The Finding, And It Explains The Money You Lost
🔴🔴 There is no tracking on your website. None at all.

We checked for every standard tool. No Google Analytics, no Tag Manager, no Google Ads conversion tag, no Meta pixel. Nothing.

⭐⭐ So when you say the ads did not work, the honest position is that nobody could have known either way. Without a conversion tag, Google and Meta cannot see which clicks turned into enquiries. Which means they cannot optimise, and you cannot measure.

🔴 That is not a small technical detail. It is the difference between advertising and guessing, and it is very likely a large part of why several figures produced nothing you could point at.

⚠️ We are not saying the ads would definitely have worked with tracking on. We are saying nobody can currently tell you, including whoever ran them.

🔴 There is not one tap-to-call link on your site

Fifteen pages, and no clickable phone number on any of them. Your mobile appears as text.

⚠️ Most people looking for a roofer are on a phone. Asking them to copy a number out by hand loses a share of them every time. ⭐ And if you were paying for those visitors, you were paying for the ones who gave up too.

🔴 One enquiry form across fifteen pages

A reader on any other page has to navigate away to make contact. Cheapest fix on this list after the phone links.

⚠️ Your site says 25 years. The company was registered last year.

The website claims 25 years. The limited company was incorporated in July 2024.

⭐ That is almost certainly completely straightforward — twenty-five years on the tools and a recent decision to incorporate. Thousands of tradesmen do exactly that.

⚠️ We raise it only because a careful customer can check it in thirty seconds, and at the moment nothing on the site explains it. One line — twenty-five years in the trade, the company formed in 2024 — removes the question entirely.

⚠️ Nobody is named anywhere on the site

We searched the whole site. No names appear on it at all. There is a team section with no people in it.

⭐⭐ For a roof, that matters more than it does in most trades. A homeowner handing over several thousand pounds wants to know who is turning up. Names and faces are the cheapest trust you can add, and you have none.

⚠️ Also worth knowing: a mobile number and no landline, on a company trading under a year, compounds the same impression.

What You Are Actually Asking For

From 8 jobs a month to 20–30 leads a month

8 jobs → 20–30 leads

⚠️ Those are two different units and it matters. Twenty to thirty leads is not twenty to thirty jobs. At a typical trade conversion rate it might be six to twelve jobs, which would roughly double you. We would want to know what you convert at before promising anything.

⭐ And here is the part that should reassure you: you cannot currently answer that question, because nothing is measuring it. Fixing the measurement is cheap, it happens before any ad spend, and it is what makes the next attempt judgeable rather than another several figures you cannot account for.
⭐ What Is Already Right
⭐ Genuinely good, and worth keeping

TrustMark shown on the site. Fifteen pages, a portfolio, reviews, a team page, a guarantee, insurance mentioned, and commercial work named.

⭐ And somebody has done proper structured work on your FAQs — they are marked up so search engines can read them as questions and answers. That is more than most roofers have, and it suggests your website guy knows what he is doing.

⚠️ Which makes the missing tracking stranger, not worse. It is the sort of thing that gets forgotten rather than skipped.

What We Would Want To Ask
  • 1. How many enquiries do you get a month now, and how many become jobs? ⭐ Even a rough figure.
  • 2. Who ran the Google and Meta ads, and what did you spend? Not to criticise. To find out what is salvageable.
  • 3. Did anybody ever show you a cost per enquiry? ⚠️ If not, that tells us a lot.
  • 4. What is an average roof worth to you?
  • 5. Could you do more than eight a month? With who?
  • 6. What does your website guy do, and is he still involved? ⭐ We would rather work with him than around him.
  • 7. What would make this worth trying once more? You have been burned, so say what the bar is.
⭐ Question 3 is the tell. If nobody ever showed you a cost per enquiry, then nobody was measuring, and the money did not fail because advertising does not work.

Several figures spent, and nothing was counting.

That is the finding, and it is also the reason to be less sceptical than you are.
