What this means for you
Your reviews already beat most of them. Your website does not
  • Three of the five sites we read carry a Google Ads tag (Bath Property Maintenance, Heads Apart, Bristol Property Maintenance). Heads Apart was the only firm paying to appear on “property maintenance Bristol”. Nobody in this market is spending heavily on that search.
  • The firms that rank have a page for the work: Bristol Handyman Team has a landlord page and 121 pages in all; Dowry sells planned maintenance packages with a client portal. You do the landlord work and have no page for it.
  • Your 29 Google reviews at 5.0 put you ahead of most of the map results we saw, and first in Keynsham. JSM (57) and the larger Bath firms are the ones with more.
  • Bath is open: three Bath firms hold the map results there, and you are not in them.
