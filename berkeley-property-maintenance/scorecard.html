Digital Presence Scorecard
D
48 / 100
4 of 18 checks passed. 8 failed.
Your reviews and your map listing in Keynsham are genuinely strong. The website underneath them is four pages that do not explain the landlord and agent work you are best at, and a stranger searching your own name may not find it.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (24 Oct 2026)
A-Reviews
⭐ 16 written reviews on your home page, and Google showed 5.0 stars from 29 reviews beside your listing. A letting agent (Gregorys) says you are one of their main contractors. This is the strongest thing you have. One review (Hannah Godwin) appears twice on the page.
BGoogle map listing
⭐ First in the map results for “property maintenance Keynsham” and third for “property maintenance Bristol”. Not in the map results for Bath, which your site names as half of your area.
A-Tap-to-call
⭐ 07469 253 878 is a tap-to-call link on all 4 pages, plus a floating call button.
A-Professional email
⭐ info@bpmsw.co.uk, on your own domain.
FEnquiry form
One form on the whole site, on the contact page (name, email, phone, message). The home page button sends people there. It does not ask the job type, the postcode, whether the property is let, or for a photo, so every enquiry needs a call back before you know what it is.
FPages and service depth
4 pages in total (home, services, gallery, contact, per your own sitemap). About 17 services, from decking to mould removal to vinyl flooring, share one services page. No page for landlords and letting agents, even though they are who the site says it is for, and no page for Bath or any town.
DPage titles
Titles name Bristol, which is good. But the services page and the gallery page carry the identical title (“Handyman Services Bristol”), and the gallery's main heading reads “Property Maintenance Bath” over a page of 86 words. The services description talks only about painting.
DHeadings
One main heading per page (good), but no second-level headings on any page, so Google cannot see where one service ends and the next begins.
FLocal business markup
No LocalBusiness markup. The only structured data is what the SEO plugin adds by default (web page, breadcrumb). Nothing tells Google or AI tools your services, area, phone or hours in a form they read directly.
DMeasurement
A Google Analytics tag (G-0J9GJCH7JR) is on every page. No Google Ads conversion tag and no Meta pixel, so if ads ever run, nothing reports which clicks became calls or forms.
FAds
0 ads pointing to bpmsw.co.uk in the Google Ads Transparency Centre (UK, any time; control checkatrade.com shows about 3,000), and no ads under your name or domain in the Meta Ad Library.
FShowing up in Google (beyond the map)
In 8 live searches, your website itself appeared once: 8th, for “property maintenance Keynsham” (the services page). Not in the top 10 for Bristol, Bath, landlord maintenance, letting-agent maintenance or handyman searches.
FBrand search
A Google search for “Berkeley Property Maintenance SW” and for “Berkeley Property Maintenance Keynsham” did not show your website in the top 9 results. Other Berkeley firms (Berkeley Estates & Management, Berkeley Property Management Bristol) did. A letting agent told about you who searches your name can land on someone else.
CAccreditation shown
SafeContractor and SSIP badges, “DBS-checked staff” and “Property Maintenance Company of the Year 2025/2026” are all on the site. None is linked to anything a buyer can check: no awarding body is named for the award, and the badges are images, not links to your listing.
DProof of work
11 job photos in the gallery (roof, bathrooms, decking, fencing, carpet). Real and good. No captions: no job type, no area, no before-and-after, and the page carries 86 words.
FThe landlord and agent offer
The site says it is for landlords, estate agents and property managers, but does not say how you work with them: key collection, tenant liaison, photo reports, invoicing, response times, end-of-tenancy turnarounds. A letting agent's review says you do all of this. The site does not.
FWho you are
Reviews name you (“Gary”) again and again. The site never introduces you: no photo of you, no story, no “who you will deal with”.
DFreshness
Home and gallery changed in July 2026; services last changed October 2024 and contact August 2024. End-of-tenancy cleaning is announced in a scrolling banner but has no page of its own.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about the website and how it is found, not about your work. The hard part, a five-star reputation with the agents and landlords you already serve, is done.

After the build: the form, pages, titles, headings, markup, measurement, the landlord and agent offer, who you are, proof of work and the accreditation rows are all fixed by the build itself. Search positions, the Bath map results and review counts take steady work over months, so we do not project those.

⚠ Not assessed, and left out of the score rather than guessed: page speed, the content of your Google and MyBuilder listings, Checkatrade, and Facebook.
