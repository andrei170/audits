What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ The aim is to make your reputation visible to the agents and landlords who would give you regular work, and to make sure the ones who find you can send a proper job request, get a fast answer and get followed up.

1. Agree the customers and the work you want · On the call

Which agents, landlords and jobs are worth most to you, which towns, and how much more the team can take on. Everything below is built around that answer.

⭐ It is easier to say yes to the right work when the website is written for it.

2. A website that explains what you already do · Weeks 1 to 2

A page for letting agents and landlords, a page per service, a Bath page, an about page with you in it, captioned job photos, your reviews in one place, and a request form that asks the job, the postcode, whether the property is let, the tenant contact and a photo.

The sample site shows every one of these.

3. Make your name findable · Weeks 1 to 2

Unique page titles, LocalBusiness markup, the award’s awarding body named, your accreditations linked, and your Google profile, MyBuilder and site all saying the same thing.

So someone searching “Berkeley Property Maintenance” finds you, not another Berkeley.

4. Answer and follow up without picking up the phone · Weeks 1 to 2

An instant reply to every request, a missed-call text back while you are on a job, quote follow-ups on a schedule you approve, and a photo report to the agent when a job is done.

⭐ This is what turns a trial job from a new agent into a regular account.

5. Measurement · Week 2

Conversion tracking on the form and calls, and a source on every job, so you can see what each channel brings in.

Groundwork before a penny goes on ads.

6. Small, measured ads · When you are ready

Only the searches and audiences you choose (agents and landlords in Bristol and Bath first), pointed at the right page, with a budget sized to your capacity.

⚠ Only once the pages and tracking are live.

7. Keep the reviews coming · Ongoing

A review request after every job, aimed at Google, and a push on Bath.

Reviews are already your strongest asset; this keeps them growing.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your job values, your capacity or where your work comes from today. Anyone forecasting jobs before knowing those would be inventing them.
