Questions for the call
1. Who are your best customers today?Letting agents, private landlords with several properties, block or property managers, or homeowners? Roughly what share of your work comes from each?
2. What work do you want more of, and what would you rather do less of?End-of-tenancy turnarounds, planned maintenance, refurbishments, bathrooms, or the small reactive jobs?
3. How do new agents and landlords find you now?Referrals, MyBuilder, Google, Facebook, or a letting agent passing your name on? The reviews suggest referrals are a big part.
4. How many agents use you regularly, and how did you win the first one?That route is usually the one worth repeating.
5. What does an agent need from you before they put you on their list?Insurance, accreditations, response times, photo reports, invoicing on account? Whatever they ask for should be on the website before they ask.
6. When a job request comes in, what happens next?Who answers, how fast, and what you need to know before you can quote.
7. How much more work can you and the team take on?So anything we build matches what you can deliver.
8. What happens to a quote after you send it?Who follows it up, and how many are out with someone deciding right now?
9. Have you run ads or paid for leads before?MyBuilder, Checkatrade, Google or Facebook: what worked and what did not?
10. Does anyone else help run the website or the marketing?So we know who else would be involved.
