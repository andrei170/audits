3. Discovery questions, in this order
1. Who are his best customers: how many letting agents use him regularly, which ones (Gregorys? House and Co?), and roughly what share of work is agent vs private landlord vs homeowner?
2. How did he win his first agent? (That is the route to repeat: referrals, walking in, a tenant who worked at an agency, per the Monica review.)
3. What is a typical job worth, and what is a good one (end-of-tenancy turnaround, bathroom refit, refurb)? Keep the answer off client pages.
4. Average employees on the accounts is 1. Who is 'the team' / 'the boys': employees, subcontractors, labour? How many can he put out on a busy week?
5. Capacity: is he turning work away or wanting more? If full, the pitch is better work and agents, not volume.
6. What do agents ask him for before using him: insurance level, SafeContractor (is it current?), DBS, photo reports, invoicing on account?
7. Who gave the 'Property Maintenance Company of the Year 2025/2026' award? (Site does not say.)
8. How do jobs come in today: agent emails, phone, MyBuilder (40 reviews there), Facebook? Does he pay MyBuilder or Checkatrade?
9. What happens after a quote? Who chases it? How many quotes are out right now?
10. Does he want Bath? He is not in the Bath map results at all.
11. Who built the site, and does anyone still look after it? (WordPress + Beaver Builder, last services edit Oct 2024.) If there is a web person, pitch the system and pages, not a rebuild.
12. Is the end-of-tenancy cleaning a real growth line or a side add-on?
13. Decision: just Gary, or a partner or business partner involved? (Second company Berkeley Property Investments SW Ltd, letting own property, same director.)
4. Likely objections and answers
“Most of my work is word of mouth, I don’t need ads.”Agree: word of mouth is why he has 5.0 from 29 and a top spot in Keynsham. Then: word of mouth sends people to Google to check him, and a search for his own name showed other Berkeley firms, not his site. Step one is making the referral land, not ads.
“Agents don’t find contractors from ads.”Correct, mostly. Ads get him on the shortlist and keep him there; the trial job and the follow-up win the account. The agents page plus photo reports is the bit that closes them. Use the commercial page.
“I already have a website.”Do not knock it. Facts: 4 pages, 17 services on one page, no landlord or agent page, two pages share one title, services last updated Oct 2024, site not in the top 10 for 7 of the 8 searches. Keep the domain and host if he wants; the sample site is the checklist.
“I’m busy enough.”Then it is about better work: more agents on regular accounts, bigger turnarounds, fewer small odd jobs, and less time on the phone chasing quotes. Ask what he would drop if he could.
“I use MyBuilder / Checkatrade.”Keep them. Ask what each lead costs and how many he wins. Shared leads go to several trades; an agent who finds Berkeley directly is his alone.
“Send me something to look at.”He has the hub link. Book the follow-up on the call with a date.
5. Close and next step
  1. Summarise in his words: the customers he wants, the gap he agreed with, what it costs him today (lost agents, quotes that go quiet, Bath).
  2. Ask directly: “Based on what you have seen, do you want us to build this for you?” Then stop talking.
  3. If yes: price live on the call (nothing is priced in the pack). Agree start date, who supplies photos, the accreditation and insurance details, and a photo of Gary for the about page.
  4. If he needs to think or talk to someone: book the follow-up there and then, with a date and time in the calendar before the call ends. He already has the hub link; do not promise to send anything new.
  5. If no: the fallback offer: start with the agents page, the job request form and quote follow-up, then search, then ads.
  6. After the call: note his answers to the placeholders (hours, insurance, reply times, towns, award body) so the demo can be updated for the follow-up.
Do not
  • No prices in writing, and no forecast of jobs, enquiries or revenue.
  • Do not quote headcount, net assets, his date of birth or the registered office back to him.
  • No promise words about results, and no first-person picking of an option for him.
  • Do not knock whoever built the current site; ask first (Q11).
