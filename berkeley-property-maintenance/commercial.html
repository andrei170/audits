The honest answer first

Agency and portfolio work is not won by one ad. A letting agent or a landlord with twenty doors does not see an advert and hand over their maintenance that afternoon. They try a contractor on a small job, watch how fast you reply, how you deal with tenants and keys, and whether the invoice and photos turn up. Then they give you more.

So ads do a different job here. They put Berkeley in front of the people who hand out this work, and keep you there for the weeks or months it takes them to try someone new. The follow-up and the way you report back are what turn a trial job into a regular account. Your Gregorys review shows you already do that part well.

Who buys this work around Bristol and Bath
  • Letting agents and estate agents with a lettings department: the property manager or lettings manager.
  • Private landlords with several properties, often investors who live elsewhere.
  • Block managers and residents’ management companies: communal repairs, decorating, fencing.
  • HMO and student landlords: turnarounds between tenancies, in a short summer window.
  • Housing associations and councils: usually through an approved-contractor list or a tender.

Which of these are your best customers is the first thing to agree on the call, because it decides every page, ad and message below.

What they check before they use you
  • Insurance: public liability, and the level of it.
  • Accreditations: your site shows SafeContractor and SSIP badges and DBS-checked staff. Agents who need approved contractors look for exactly these, and want to be able to check them.
  • Speed: how fast you reply and attend, and whether you handle emergencies.
  • How you report back: before-and-after photos, a written job report, an invoice the agent can pass to the landlord.
  • Tenant handling: collecting keys, booking access, being polite in someone’s home.
  • References: another agent or landlord who will vouch for you.

Your reviews prove most of this. Your website does not say any of it in one place, so an agent who finds you has to ring to find out. The sample site has an agents and landlords page and a request form built for them.

Where ads fit
1. Google search, for landlord and agent searches

Searches like “landlord maintenance Bristol”, “end of tenancy cleaning Bristol” and “property maintenance Bath” are typed by people with work to give out. In our checks only a handful of firms were paying to appear on them. We would test a small set, sending clicks to the agents page, not the home page.

2. LinkedIn and Facebook, aimed at the people who hand out the work

Lettings and property managers can be reached by job title in Bristol and Bath. The aim is not an instant enquiry; it is that when their usual contractor lets them down, Berkeley is a name they already recognise.

3. Staying in front of people who already looked

Anyone who visits the agents page keeps seeing your before-and-after work afterwards. That matches how slowly an agent changes contractor.

4. A page that does the shortlisting for them

One page with your agent reviews, your accreditations, how you work (keys, tenants, photos, invoices) and a request form that asks the property address, the job, the tenant contact and the urgency.

The follow-up is where accounts are won
Every enquiry and quote on one screen
  1. New enquiry
  2. Visit booked
  3. Quote sent, with its value
  4. Following up
  5. Won and scheduled
  6. Job done, report sent
  7. Repeat work due

So at any moment you can see every open quote, what it is worth, and which ones need a nudge today.

What the system does for you
  • Replies within a minute to every request, from your name and number.
  • Follows up every quote on a schedule you approve until it is a yes or a no.
  • Sends the agent a photo report when a job is finished, and asks for a review or reference.
  • Reminds landlords before the summer tenancy changeover and before seasonal work (gutters, fences, decorating).
  • Tells you which enquiries came from which source, so money goes where the work comes from.
What stays with you

The visit, the price and the relationship. Every message is written with you and approved by you before it goes out.

What we would measure

Requests from agents and landlords, visits booked, quotes sent and their total value, quotes won, and repeat jobs per agent. No number of jobs or accounts is forecast here.
