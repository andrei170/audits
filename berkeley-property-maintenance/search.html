What it means
⭐ The map listing is working

First in Keynsham and third in Bristol, with a perfect 5.0 from 29 reviews. That comes from real customers, and it is the base everything else builds on.

Why the website does not show
  • No page per service and no landlord page. Google matches “maintenance contractor for letting agents” to a page about exactly that. Bristol Handyman Team has one; you do not.
  • No Bath page and no Bath map presence. Your site names Bath as half your area; three Bath firms hold those results.
  • Two pages share one title, and there is no LocalBusiness markup telling Google what you do and where.
  • Your name is shared with other Berkeley firms. Searching “Berkeley Property Maintenance Keynsham” showed Berkeley Estates & Management and Berkeley Property Management Bristol, not you. An agent who is given your name and searches it can end up ringing someone else.
⚠ Limits

Searches vary by location, device and time. We did not test AI answer tools separately for this pack. These snapshots show the pattern, not a fixed position.
