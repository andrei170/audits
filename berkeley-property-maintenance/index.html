What we checked, and where

The business behind the name
⭐ A real, active company with a strong word-of-mouth reputation
  • BERKELEY PROPERTY MAINTENANCE SW LTD, company number 16294021, active, incorporated 5 March 2025. Companies House record.
  • Director and majority owner on the register: Gary Stutt.
  • First accounts filed (micro-entity accounts, year to 31 March 2026). Next accounts due by 31 December 2027.
  • Your site says you are based in Keynsham, between Bristol and Bath, and works for landlords, estate agents and property managers.
⚠ Two things worth knowing
  • There are several other “Berkeley” property firms in and around Bristol (Berkeley Estates & Management, Berkeley Property Management Bristol Ltd). They show up when people search your name. See the search page.
  • The site names an award, “Property Maintenance Company of the Year 2025/2026”, without saying who gave it. Naming the awarding body (and linking to it) turns it from a claim into proof.
Your website, page by page
Four pages carrying a business that does a lot more
  • 4 pages in the sitemap: Home, Services, Gallery, Contact. Services last changed October 2024; home and gallery July 2026.
  • About 17 services on one page: routine and emergency repairs, upkeep and inspections, painting and decorating, wallpapering, minor repairs, flatpack, fitting shelves and rails, carpentry and joinery, decking and fencing, roofing repairs, windows and doors, mould removal, wall repair, plumbing repairs, carpet and vinyl flooring. Plus end-of-tenancy cleaning in a banner.
  • One enquiry form, on the contact page, asking name, email, phone and message only.
  • No page for letting agents or landlords, no page for Bath, no “about Gary” page.
  • ⭐ Good: tap-to-call everywhere, an email on your own domain, a Google Analytics tag, 11 real job photos, 16 written reviews, and accreditation badges on show.
⭐ The reviews are the best asset on the site

Sixteen reviews, several from landlords and one from a letting agent (Gregorys) who says you are “one of our main contractors that we use on a daily basis” and praises the way you handle quotes, keys and tenants. Another came through a referral from House and Co. That is exactly the story an agent wants to read, and today it is buried in a scrolling list on the home page.

Ads and tracking

No ads found. The Google Ads Transparency Centre shows 0 ads pointing to bpmsw.co.uk in the UK at any time, and 0 anywhere (the same check on checkatrade.com shows about 3,000, so the check works). The Meta Ad Library shows no ads for “berkeley property maintenance” or bpmsw.co.uk. Google’s record.

The site has a Google Analytics tag, but no Google Ads conversion tag and no Meta pixel, so the groundwork for measured ads is not in place yet.

What we could not check
  • Checkatrade, MyBuilder and Facebook blocked our reader. Google’s results show your MyBuilder profile at 5.0 from 40 reviews; we could not open it.
  • Your Google Business Profile itself: we saw it in the map results (5.0, 29 reviews, listed as property maintenance, with your mobile) but could not open the listing.
  • The SafeContractor and SSIP badges: we did not check them against the schemes’ own registers.
  • Page speed: not lab-tested, so not graded.
  • Searches change by location and by the hour. These are eight snapshots, not a ranking report.
