What your own reviews say about your jobs

Trusted Trader asks every reviewer roughly what the job was worth. Across your 21 reviews:

13jobs up to £1,000

So most of what reaches a review is repairs and emergency work, with a steady share of bigger jobs. That matters for adverts: a £400 repair and a £6,000 re-roof cost about the same to win, so the aim is more of the bigger ones without losing the repairs that feed them.
