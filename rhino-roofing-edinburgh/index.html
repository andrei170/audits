Your website, page by page
Home page: rhinoroofingedinburgh.co.uk

What it does well: a clear headline (“Edinburgh’s Leading Roofing Company”), the mobile number at the top, four services explained, a 24/7 emergency section, the Marley guarantee line and a review widget. It loads quickly (the page came back in about half a second when we read it).

What a homeowner with a leak runs into:

  • The phone number is text, not a link. On a phone they cannot tap it to ring you. There are 0 tap-to-call links on the page.
  • Five separate forms (one per service), each asking for name, email and a message. Phone is optional, and nothing asks what the job is or where it is.
  • Eleven services in the footer (stone roofs, lead work, roof cleaning and coating, UPVC, roughcasting, rendering, scaffolding, guttering and more) are plain text with nowhere to click.
  • The review widget is from 2023 (6 reviews, newest 1 July 2023).
  • The footer copyright says 2023, and the footer email reads info@rhinoeroofingedinburgh.co.uk, with an extra “e”.
Contact page: /home/contact-us

A form (name, email, message), the email, the mobile as text, the hours (24/7) and the same 2023 reviews. Again no tap-to-call. Last edited January 2024.
