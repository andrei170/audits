The enquiry plan, in three steps
1A website that wins the quote

Put a real site on manplumbing.co.uk: your kitchens, the BIFIS badge, the area you cover, and a quote form at the top of every page that asks the job type and postcode first. See the demo website for how it would look.

2Get found and get reviewed

Set up or tidy your Google Business Profile with photos of real kitchens, ask recent customers for reviews, and give each nearby town its own page (Leigh-on-Sea, Southend, Westcliff, Benfleet, Rayleigh, Hadleigh).

3Small, targeted adverts, only when you want more work

Once the site is counting enquiries, run adverts aimed at homeowners near you who are planning a kitchen, and see which ones turn into real jobs. Only as much as your diary can take.

What we need from you
  • Photos of 5 to 10 kitchens you have fitted (phone photos are fine).
  • The towns you want work from, and the ones you do not.
  • Your best type of job: full kitchen installs, kitchens plus plumbing, boilers, or a mix.
  • Any reviews customers have already left you, and where.
Questions we will ask you
Where does most of your work come from today?
How far ahead are you booked?
What does a typical kitchen job look like for you, and how long does it take?
If more of the right enquiries came in, how many more jobs a month could you take on?
