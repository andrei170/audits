Three ways to start

All three are one-off fees. Built once, yours to keep, switched on whenever you need people. No fee per hire, no monthly fee, and everything sits in your name.

Option 1: Hiring System

£1,500 one-off · no monthly fee

  • A careers page on manplumbing.co.uk with your jobs, vans, team and the pay
  • An application that screens for you: years qualified, tickets, licence, area, a job they ran alone, and a reference
  • Three advert campaigns built from your own photos: engineer, bathroom installer and a reminder
  • The first hiring round run for you for six weeks: we read every application and send you only the ones who pass, with a note on each
  • Ad account, adverts and applicant list all in your name

Less than a quarter of one agency fee.

Site and hiring together
Option 2: Hiring System + Website

£2,500 one-off · no monthly fee

  • Everything in Option 1
  • The full website from the demo, replacing the parked page on manplumbing.co.uk

  • Ready for the bathroom line and new areas when you have the people

Less than half of one agency fee. Engineers look you up before they apply, and right now they find a parked page.

Option 3: Hiring System, Website + CRM

£3,500 one-off · no monthly fee

  • Everything in Option 2
  • Every applicant and every enquiry in one place, so you and your sister can see who is where
  • An instant text back to every new applicant and enquiry
  • Review requests sent after each finished job

Still less than one agency hire, for a system you use on every future hire.

Terms
  • Ad spend is separate and goes on your own card, in your own account. About £300 to £500 a month while a round is running, and nothing when it is switched off.
  • Up to two payments on any option, so it does not land in the same week as wages.
  • Running another round later: a small one-off fee, agreed in writing before it starts (about £500).
  • Your trial process stays yours: trial day, three-month trial, then the van.
Timing
