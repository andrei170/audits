The plan in one line

A hiring system you own: a careers page on manplumbing.co.uk, adverts aimed at qualified engineers across south Essex, an application that screens out the wrong people before you see them, then a trial day, a three-month trial and a van. Built once, switched on when you need people.

Improver (optional)0 to 1Recently qualified, for the quieter summer refurbishment work. Only if you want one; we would not lead with it.

Full time on all roles, as you asked. Pay goes on every advert; we need your figure on Friday.

How a hire happens, step by step

1The careers page. manplumbing.co.uk is currently a parked page. It becomes your site, with a careers section: who you are, the work, your vans and jobs in your own photos, the pay, and the trial process laid out honestly.

2The application screens for you. Short questions before anyone can apply: years since qualifying, Gas Safe (if you need it), full driving licence, right to work, where they live, a reference, and one question about a job they ran on their own. Anyone who fails gets a polite no straight away.

3The adverts. Facebook and Instagram plus Indeed, aimed at south Essex only (Southend, Leigh, Basildon, Rayleigh, Benfleet, Canvey, Chelmsford). Details on The campaigns.

4We read every application and send you only the ones who pass, with a short note on each. You do a 10 to 15 minute phone call with the best.

5Trial day or trial week, alongside one of your experienced lads, on a job where a slip will not cost you a client relationship.

6Three-month trial, then the van. Your own process, unchanged. We set up simple check-ins at weeks 2, 6 and 12 so you see early if someone is slipping.

7Switch it off. Once you have your people, the adverts stop. The page, the application and the list of good applicants you did not hire all stay with you for next time.

Protecting what you have built
  • New people start on your own jobs, alongside an experienced lad, before they ever go alone to a contract client.
  • The application asks how they handled a job on their own, so confidence is tested before day one, not after.
  • References are required to apply. Fewer applications, but more of the right people.
After the team is in place

Once you can back up more work, the same site can start bringing it in: the bathroom installs your suppliers already refer, and towns you do not cover yet. That is a second step, not part of the hiring system, and only when you want it.
