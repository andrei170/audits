The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • A real, established firm: a limited company since May 2012, filings up to date.
  • A recognised trust mark: BIFIS registered installer, specialism kitchens. Most local fitters cannot say that.
  • The domain is already yours: manplumbing.co.uk and your email on it. Half the work of a website is done.
  • Kitchens plus plumbing and heating in one firm: the customer does not need a separate plumber, which is a selling point most kitchen fitters lack.
⚠ What holds it back
  • Your website is a parked GoDaddy page. Anyone who looks you up finds nothing.
  • No way to ask for a quote online, and no tap-to-call.
  • None of your kitchens are visible, and a kitchen is bought with the eyes.
  • Nothing is measuring anything, so no advert could run properly today.
