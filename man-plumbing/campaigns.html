The three campaigns in one line

Experienced engineers first, a bathroom installer second, and a reminder for anyone who looked. Each has its own advert set and its own form, so you can see which brings people worth a trial day. All of them point at the careers page on manplumbing.co.uk, and every applicant answers the same knock-out questions before you see them.

Campaign by campaign
1The qualified plumbing and heating engineer
  • The goal: two full-time engineers, three or more years past their apprenticeship, confident running a job on their own.
  • Who sees it: engineers in south Essex only: Southend, Leigh, Westcliff, Rayleigh, Benfleet, Canvey, Basildon, Wickford and Chelmsford. Aimed by area and trade interest, never by age or gender.
  • Where: Facebook and Instagram, plus a sponsored Indeed advert, because some engineers do search there.
  • What it shows: your own vans, kit and finished installs in your own photos, the pay, and a short line from you about the firm: 14 years, working directly with the owner, proper installs.
  • Where it sends them: the engineer role on the careers page. Years since qualifying, Gas Safe if you require it, driving licence, right to work, area, one question about a job they ran alone, and a reference.

Why this one: this is the role that unlocks the Luton work and the next areas. The good engineers are not on job boards in winter, so the advert has to reach them in their feed.

2The bathroom installer
  • The goal: one or two installers for the bathroom work your suppliers already send you.
  • Who sees it: fitters and tilers in the same south Essex area.
  • Where: Facebook and Instagram.
  • What it shows: finished bathrooms you have fitted, the type of work (supplied fittings, steady referrals), and whether it is employed or a day rate.
  • Where it sends them: the bathroom role: years fitting, tiling, plumbing ability, own tools, area and a reference.

Why this one: you said the bathroom referrals could become a line of their own once you have the people. This campaign is how that starts.

3A reminder for anyone who looked
  • The goal: bring back engineers who opened the careers page but did not apply.
  • Who sees it: only people who have already visited the page or watched the clip.
  • Where: Facebook and Instagram.
  • What it shows: the trial terms in plain words, a finished job, and “the role is still open”.
  • Where it sends them: back to the same role and form.

Why this one: a good engineer thinks about a move for a few days. This is the cheapest part to run because the audience is small and already interested.

Your card, your account.

You set the budget, on your own card, in an ad account in your name. After the first fortnight we move it towards whichever campaign brings people who pass the questions, not whichever gets the most clicks. When you have your people, it switches off.

What one applicant is likely to cost
A rough guide, not a promise. Your real figure shows within the first few weeks.

There is no published cost per applicant for plumbers and heating engineers in Essex, so this is built from what is published and from what makes your roles harder to fill.

What we are not saying

No hire, number of applicants or date is promised. Good engineers are busy, which is the problem you came to us with. What the campaigns do is put M.A.N in front of engineers who are not looking, turn away the wrong ones before they take your time, and keep a list of good people for the next round.
