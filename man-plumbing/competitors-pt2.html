What this means for you

1The going rate is about £40k to £46k with a van and fuel card. That is what a qualified domestic plumbing and heating engineer near you is being offered right now. Your advert needs a clear pay figure to compete; an advert without one gets skipped.

2Most of them give the van from day one. You give it after a three-month trial, which is sensible for you but will put some good people off. Two honest ways round it: offer a van from day one with the same three-month trial, or say plainly on the advert that the van comes at three months and why. Your call.

3The big contractors are hiring the same people (Mitie, Morgan Sindall, Sureserve). What they cannot offer is working directly with the owner of a 14-year-old local firm on proper installs instead of a ticket queue. That is the line your advert leads with.

4Your local competitors are not advertising at all. We found no careers page or job advert for T M Hughes, Cannon or Marine Bathrooms. A small Leigh firm that shows its vans, its jobs and its pay would be the one visible option for an engineer who wants to work local.

5Bathroom fitters are mostly self-employed on day rates, around £250 to £350 a day in the adverts we saw. Worth deciding whether your bathroom role is employed or self-employed before it goes out.
