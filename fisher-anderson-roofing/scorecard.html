The business is busy and well run: booked into February off word of mouth alone. But online, Fisher & Anderson barely exists, so anyone who does not already know your name finds another roofer.
GradeWhat we checkedWhat we found (27 Oct 2026)
FA website of your own
No website. The only place we found Fisher & Anderson online is your Facebook page. You told River you had a site through Yell about ten years ago and stopped because it did not bring anything in.
FShowing up in local searches
Not in the top 10 for “roofers Dunfermline” or “roofer Dunfermline”. Those results are directories (Checkatrade, TrustedTrader, Yell) and local roofers with their own sites. R&J Roofing was 5th.
FFound on your own name
Two web searches for “Fisher & Anderson Roofing” returned no page about you. Your Facebook page exists, but someone who hears your name from a neighbour and searches it has nothing else to land on.
FDirectory listings
Not on TrustedTrader’s Dunfermline roofers list, where 20 local roofers each show between 9 and 201 reviews. These are the pages that sit at the top of the searches above.
FA way to ask for a quote
No quote form anywhere. A customer has to find your number or message the Facebook page, and nothing asks them the job, the postcode or the type of house before you call back.
FMeasurement
With no website there is nothing to measure: no way to see how many people look you up, or which jobs came from where.
DName and details that match
Your Facebook page says Fisher & Anderson Roofing Ltd; the company is registered as Fisher & Anderson Ltd. Small, but a customer or a factor checking you on Companies House finds a different name.
FReady for ads
Nothing for an ad to send people to and no tracking, so an ad today could not be measured.
A-Company record
⭐ Active limited company since 2019, accounts filed on time every year. That is the kind of thing bigger customers and factors check, and it is clean.

⭐⭐ Every failure above is about being found, not about your roofing.
