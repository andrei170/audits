What we would do, in order

⭐⭐ The aim is not just more enquiries. It is the right ones: bigger roofs, closer to Dunfermline, spread through the year instead of piling up in summer, with every enquiry answered while you are working.

1. Google Business Profile and reviews · Week 1

Set up or tidy your Google profile with Dunfermline as your base, your services and your photos, and start asking every finished customer for a Google review by text.

⭐ This is what shows on the map when someone searches “roofers Dunfermline”, and it costs nothing.

2. A website that sells bigger roofs · Weeks 1 to 3

A page for each kind of roof work, a page for each town you want work in, your photos and reviews, and a quote form that asks the job, the postcode and the type of house.

The sample site shows every page. Bigger roofs are first on purpose.

3. Answer and follow up without coming off the roof · Weeks 2 to 3

Missed-call text-back within seconds, an instant reply to every form, and follow-up on every quote until it is a yes or a no.

⭐ With 6 to 10 enquiries a week already, this is where time goes today.

4. Fill the winter · From the first cold snap

Small, local ads on repairs and storm damage when the weather turns, and a “book your spring re-roof now” offer so the diary fills before the season starts.

Only the towns you choose, only the jobs you choose.

5. Measure everything · From day one

Every call and form tagged with where it came from.

So you can see which jobs, towns and months actually pay.

6. Step up when you are ready · When you have the crew

If doubling the jobs means more people on the roofs, the enquiries can be turned up as you take them on, not before.

⚠ We would never push more work than you can fit.

⚠ What this page deliberately does not contain

No prices, and no numbers about what any of this would produce. We do not yet know your job values or how many more jobs your team can take on.
