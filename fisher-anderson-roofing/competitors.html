What this means for you
None of them is better at roofing than you. They are just easier to find.
  • R&J Roofing is the one to watch. 83 pages, including pages for Rosyth, Cowdenbeath, Dalgety Bay, Kinross and Kirkcaldy, a page per kind of roof work, and a Google Ads tag on the site. It was 5th for “roofers Dunfermline”.
  • Every one of the six has 27 to 83 reviews on TrustedTrader alone. A homeowner who has never heard of you compares that with nothing.
  • Most of their sites are small: 5 to 8 pages for four of the five sites we could count. A proper site with a page per job and per town would be one of the best in Dunfermline, not one of many.
  • Two of the five sites have no quote form on the home page at all.
