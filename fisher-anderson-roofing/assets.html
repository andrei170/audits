Questions for the call
1. What kind of roof do you want more of?Full re-roofs on bigger houses? Slate? Commercial? And which jobs would you rather pass on?
2. Which towns do you most want to work in?You told River you would like more local work. We have started with Dunfermline, Rosyth, Inverkeithing and Dalgety Bay, Cowdenbeath and Kelty, Kinross and Kirkcaldy.
3. What does a quiet winter look like?How many weeks, and what kind of work would fill them: repairs, storm damage, quoting spring re-roofs?
4. Doubling the jobs next year: what would that take?More enquiries, or more people on the roofs as well?
5. What happened with the Yell website?What did it cost, what did it bring in, and what would you need to see this time?
6. When you are up a roof and the phone rings, what happens?Voicemail, Scott, call back in the evening?
7. Does Scott want to be part of this decision?You are both directors.
8. Do you have a Google Business Profile, and Google or Facebook reviews?We could not see either from outside.
9. Do you have photos of jobs you are proud of?They are the most important thing on a roofer’s website.
