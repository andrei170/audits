  7. Pain. "How many calls do you think you miss in a week when you are on a job? What happens to them?"
  8. Pain. "When a call comes in, can you tell whether it came from the website, Google Maps or a referral?"
  9. Pain. "What happened last storm season? Did you turn generator or emergency work away?"
  10. Pain. "If a panel upgrade inquiry goes to someone else because nobody called back in time, what does that cost you?" Let him put the number on it.
  11. Pain. "How long has it been like this? What have you tried? Why sort it now? On a scale of 1 to 10, how big a priority is it?"
  12. Tie-downs after each pain answer: "So it is costing you jobs, not just time, isn’t it?" / "You would want to know what the SEO is actually bringing in, wouldn’t you?"
  13. Summary back. "So let me make sure I have this right: [work comes from X], [calls get missed when Y], [you cannot see what the site brings in], and what you want is [his words]. Is that right?" Wait for a clear yes.
  14. Demo. Share the demo site on a phone view: form in the first screen, ZIP check, the emergency path, Generators page, Lake Jackson page, TECL on every page. Then the scorecard and competitors.
  15. Close. "If we could get every visitor an easy way to ask for you, get every missed call answered in seconds, and show you exactly what brings jobs in, is there any reason you would not want to go ahead?" Then book the next call with Mekala on it.
