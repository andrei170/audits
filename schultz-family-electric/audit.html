How a visitor gets in touch today
⚠ The path from reading to booking
  • Call: easy. (979) 264-9024 is a tap-to-call link on every page.
  • Schedule now: every page’s button opens a separate booking page on l.schultzfamilyelectricllc.com, a GoHighLevel calendar called "service callback". The visitor leaves your site to use it.
  • Form: only on the Contact page. It asks name, phone, email and a message; it does not ask what the job is, the ZIP code, home or business, or whether it is an emergency.
  • Google listing: a "Book online" button that runs through Housecall Pro, a different system from the one on the site.
  • So a homeowner reading your Panel Upgrade page at 9pm, who does not want to call, has no form on that page and two different booking systems depending on where they found you.

TDLR’s rules for electricians ask licensed electrical contractors to show their contractor licence number on advertising, and three of the five local competitors we read (Buchanan, F&D and Ivy Leach) show theirs on the home page. Worth confirming the exact wording with TDLR rather than taking our word for it; either way it is a two-minute fix.

Behind the pages
⚠ Code and set-up
  • Platform: WordPress with the Kadence theme and Kadence Blocks Pro; Google reviews shown through WP Social Ninja. Footer credit: "A ScaleSuite Design". The site’s images were uploaded from June 2026, so the site is new.
  • Tracking: none. No Google Analytics 4 ID, no Universal Analytics ID, no Google Tag Manager container, no Google Ads tag and no Meta pixel on any of the 92 pages.
  • Business markup: the main pages are described as web pages, articles and a person, with an address; the "Electrician" business type is only on the 9 blog category pages. Service pages carry FAQ markup.
  • Headings: one main heading on the home page; two on each of the 30 service pages ("Customer Testimonials").
  • Descriptions: written for the main pages; missing on the 9 category pages, including "Uncategorized".
  • Images: 22 on the home page; on the Services page, 26 of 35 have no alt text.
  • Size: the home page code is 566 KB, heavy for a phone on a weak signal. Your server answered our requests in 1.0 to 1.6 seconds.
  • Security and addresses: loads over HTTPS; the http:// and www. versions redirect correctly; robots.txt and the sitemap are in order; privacy and accessibility pages are live.
