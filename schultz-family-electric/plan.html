The order we would do it in

Measure first, then capture, then follow up, then grow. The demo website shows steps 2 and 6 working.

1Measure what already happens

Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager on the current site, with every call, form and booking counted as a conversion, and call tracking so you can see which pages and searches bring calls. Nothing else changes; this comes first so every later step can be judged.

2A form on every page

A short form in the first screen of every service and town page, asking the job, the ZIP code, home or business and when. It feeds one inbox, in whichever system you run jobs from.

3One place for every lead

Calls, forms, the booking page, Google’s "Book online" and Facebook messages land in one list, so nothing sits in two systems.

4Fast follow-up

An instant text back to every missed call and form, so a homeowner who reaches you while you are on a job hears back in seconds. A real person still makes the call; you set the rules for what goes out automatically.

5Reviews after every job

A review request by text when a job is signed off, and the Reviews page updated to show your real count.

6Tidy the site

Your TECL number on every page, business markup on the main pages, one main heading per page, descriptions on the category pages, a tappable email address and a readable address for the /2747-2/ article.

7Grow with ads, if you want more work

Google Ads and Local Services Ads for the jobs you want more of (panels, generators, EV chargers), each landing on its own page with its own form, measured from day one.

8Storm-season generator push

A generator page and campaign ready before hurricane season, the biggest job type competitors lead with.

What we are not saying

We cannot promise a number of calls, jobs or a ranking. What these steps do is make sure the visitors your site already gets have an easy way to ask for you, that every request is answered quickly, and that you can see what works. Your current web designer can make many of the site changes; the demo is a blueprint, not a demand to switch.
