The short version
⭐ What is already working
  • On the public record since 1988: Angle Builders Ltd incorporated on 6 June 1988, you a director throughout.

The gap in one line
⚠ 18 quotes waiting, and a gap from March to June

You do not need more strangers first. You already have 18 people who wanted the work enough to get a quote, and a reputation that your history since 1988, the FMB, the Guild and your reviews back up. The fastest way to fill March to June is to bring some of those 18 back, with a website that shows them the quality and makes saying yes easy. The plan starts there.

How a homeowner gets in touch today
⚠ The path from looking to a site visit
  • Phone: 01992 715614 at the top of every page, tap-to-call. Good.
  • Form: only on the Contact page (first name, last name, phone, email, subject, message) and a call-back box on the Bathrooms page.
  • Nothing asks what the job is, the postcode, the rough size or when they want to start.
  • After sending: a line saying "Thank you for your message. It has been sent." Nothing about when you will reply.
  • Someone who had a quote and is thinking it over has nowhere to go on the site except the same general form.
Proof of your work: the pages are there, the projects are not
  • Your sitemap lists 23 project pages: extensions, loft conversion, refurbishments, kitchens, bathrooms, en suites, cloakrooms, utility rooms, staircases, Velux windows, roofing, brickwork, driveways, paving, fencing and more.
  • Every one of the 23 is empty: a heading, and no projects or photos.
  • In 2017 the site had real project pages, for example a home extension in Epping, one in Loughton and an extension, loft and refurbishment in Woodford. Those addresses now show "Oops, This Page Could Not Be Found!".
  • Your best photos are in the home-page slideshow, with no captions to say what the job was or where.

  • Social: 4 social icons on every page that link to nowhere.
  • Headings: 4 main headings on the home page. Images: 40 of 110 without a description.
