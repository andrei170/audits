What this means for you
1You have the longest record by a distance. Nobody would know from your site.

Adior says over 15 years and Gemini says trading since 2003. You have been incorporated since 1988, are FMB listed and a Guild member since 2011. Of the five, only DWM mentions the FMB on its home page. Your history is the strongest thing a nervous customer can be shown.

Source: Each firm’s home page; Companies House; FMB and Guild profiles, 18 November 2026.

2Review counts are where you look smaller.

DWM shows 5.0 from 52 on Google, Adior 4.9 from 39 and BHM 5.0 from 33. You show 4.7 from 12. After all these years you have far more happy customers than that; asking the recent ones for a Google review is free and closes the gap fastest.

Source: Google Maps from central Waltham Abbey, 18 November 2026.

3One competitor has built a page for almost every town.

DWM’s sitemap holds 2,995 addresses, 289 of them naming a town. You have none. You do not need hundreds; six honest pages for the towns your reviews come from would do.

Source: Each firm’s sitemap, 18 November 2026.

4Nobody asks the postcode or the start date, and nobody is tracking visitors.

Two of the five have a form on the home page, none asks where the job is, and we found no tracking code on any of their home pages. The bar locally is low: a site that asks the right questions and follows up properly stands out.

Source: Home page code of each site, 18 November 2026.

Where you are already ahead
  • Since 1988, the FMB and the Guild: none of the five can match that combination.
  • Reviews that name you and talk about finishing on time, at the quoted price, with a tidy site.
  • Real photos of high-end extensions and kitchens, better than several of the five show.
